# Leg 268 — ROUTE-PUB2R — novelty / prior-art pass

**Written before any edit was made.** This leg builds no solver module, runs no computation,
and produces no new magnitude. Every number it moves into PUB2 was measured by **leg 249** and
is transcribed with attribution. The novelty question therefore has an unusual answer, and it
is worth stating plainly rather than performing the usual literature sweep at a claim that
does not exist.

## 1. What this leg claims

Nothing mathematical. It is a **rework leg on submission-track prose plus one correction
artifact**. Its deliverable is that PUB2 stops printing three things the repository's own
verified data do not support:

1. `‖T⁻¹‖_X = 4.026` as a **truncation-independent** value (it is one rung of a still-rising
   sequence converging to ≈ 4.0318);
2. `σ_min = 0.0908047` at `N = 512` (the 7th significant figure is **proved wrong**);
3. two smaller nits inherited from the queued-and-absorbed leg 259.

None of these is a new result. A claim of novelty here would itself be the error.

## 3. External prior art: checked, and correctly empty

Leg 249 established the point this leg inherits: **neither `0.0908` nor `4.026` appears
anywhere in Xu arXiv:2607.19762, in any form.** Proposition 4.6 gives only a qualitative
majorant `‖R₀(z)‖_X ≤ C(R,d)α^{−3/2}` with `C` unevaluated; Lemma 4.5's `‖T_z‖ = 1/α` is a
different operator's norm. There is consequently **no external number this leg could be
checking PUB2 against**, and no literature search over "the correct value of `‖T⁻¹‖_X`" is
meaningful — the quantity is a property of a discrete realization this repository built.

That is why leg 249's independence had to mean *re-derivation from the definitions*, and why
this leg's correctness condition is **faithful transcription of leg 249**, not agreement with
anyone.

## 4. Two things that would have been novelty claims, and are deliberately not made

* **The corrected limit ≈ 4.0318 is not presented as a result.** It is presented as what a
  monotone sequence with geometrically shrinking decrements extrapolates to — i.e. as float64
  evidence, the same grade leg 176 gives its other number. PUB2 now says that, and nothing
  stronger.
* **Leg 249's two findings that make leg 176's case *stronger*** (the Cholesky whitening stays
  monotone through `N = 2048`, so the reliable window is wider than claimed; the Xu
  reproduction is 7.1× better than leg 176's own residual metric reports) are recorded in the
  correction artifact and one of them is footnoted in §3.5, but **neither is converted into a
  strengthened claim in PUB2's argument**. Upgrading a claim on the strength of a verification
  leg's by-product is the failure mode this note exists to avoid.
