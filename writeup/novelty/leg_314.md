**Object.** The **finite-unstable-spectrum (FUS) condition**: that the spectrum of the operator
obtained by linearising about a self-similar blow-up profile, in similarity variables, contains
only **finitely many** points in the right half `µ`-plane. `arXiv:2509.14185` p.19 calls this
*"desirable"* and **assumes** it, for CCF, IPM (2D, with boundary), 2D Boussinesq and 3D Euler
with boundary. Leg 175 banked that neither `2509.14185` nor `2511.22819`'s precision fix touches
it: **it is spectral, not a precision question.**

**Calibration applied.** The §0a steer weights this pass toward **old** prior art. That was the
right instruction: the decisive machinery here is 1960s–2000s operator theory, and the decisive
*on-object* paper (Xu) was already in this repository.

## 1. The decisive prior art, and it is inside this repository

### 1a. Xu, *The spectral picture of self-similar collapse in the Constantin–Lax–Majda equation*

<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19762> — arXiv:2607.19762v1, 22 Jul 2026, 41 pp, physics.flu-dyn,
cross-listed math-ph. **Already banked here** (legs 171 / 173 / 249 / leg 70's Route-RC;
`test_energy_coercivity.py`, `test_literature_gates_selfconsistency.py`,
`experiments/p2_route_xul_v1_lit.py`, `experiments/p2_route_xum_v1_scoping.py`,
`experiments/p2_route_rc_v1_realization_audit.py`, `experiments/p2_route_xun_v1_convert.py`).

What it establishes, for the `a = 0` CLM profile `Ω(y) = −y/(y²+1/4)`:

* **A realization dichotomy.** The *"in-strip smear"* seen in generic discretizations is the
  spectrum of the **maximal `L²` realization**; the **origin-`H²`** realization eliminates it.
  **Same PDE, same profile, two different spectral pictures.**
* In the origin-`H²` realization the **essential spectrum in `{Re λ ≥ −1/2}` is the single
  vertical line `{Re λ = −1/2}`**, produced by a **log-widening Weyl sequence**; a
  **Hardy–Mellin resolvent bound** clears the rest of the half-plane.
* The **point spectrum over all of `ℂ` is exactly `{0, 1}`** — scaling and time-shift symmetry
  modes — **with no embedded eigenvalues**; quotienting them out gives a spectral gap of `1/2`.

**This is prior art for the mechanism this leg classifies, and this leg does not re-claim it.**
Xu covers `a = 0` CLM. It says nothing about CCF, IPM, Boussinesq or 3D Euler with boundary.

### 1b. The repository's own banked measurement of the smear

`writeup/data/p2_route_i_v1_driven.json`, `i5_stability` (Route-I, gCLM `a = 1/2`, `p = 3`,

of Xu's dichotomy. **These are banked numbers; this leg does arithmetic on them and runs no new
gCLM measurement** (see §3).

**Classical background** (named for the argument, not claimed as this leg's find): Weyl's theorem
on invariance of the essential spectrum under relatively compact perturbation (Kato, *Perturbation
Theory for Linear Operators*), and the fact that a discrete eigenvalue set in a half-plane can
still accumulate at `|Im µ| → ∞` unless a resolvent bound forbids it. **That accumulation is the
whole content of the FUS condition**, and it is the only part no bounded computation sees.

## 5. Verdict of the pass

* The FUS condition **has not been discharged** for CCF, IPM, 2D Boussinesq or 3D Euler with
  boundary, by anyone, as of 2026-08-11. `2511.22819` does not touch it (instrument-checked).
* The **machinery** that would discharge it is old and is not this leg's invention: Weyl +
  a high-frequency resolvent bound, realised as **maximal dissipativity** (BCG 2022, CGSS 2023,
  GHJS 2025) or as a **Hardy–Mellin resolvent bound** (Xu 2026).
* The **realization-dependence** of the answer is **Xu's published dichotomy**, already banked
  here at legs 70 / 171 / 173 / 249. This leg **cites** it; it does not claim it.
* **What is genuinely open, and therefore what this leg classifies**, is the *status of the
  condition for the four models `2509.14185` names* — which nobody has stated, and which is the
  gate this leg answers.

**No link of the L1→L4 chain moved. Clay odds stay ~0.05%.**
