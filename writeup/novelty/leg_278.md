## 4. The one contradiction, and the 2/2 split

`verify_276` §9's finding reproduces exactly. The certificate-closure activity is **legs 51–57 —
seven** — anchored by two independent banked sources. `BLOG_P2_PUB2_V1.md:14` is the
*certificate-building* claim, near-verbatim its companion `BLOG_P2_PUB1_V1.md:9`, with the number
mistyped as `seventy`. It contradicted PUB1 at both PUB1 sites, PUB2's own technical companion at
`TECHNICAL_P2_PUB2_V1.md:35`, and both banked leg-range sources.

**The fix, in full — one word, no argument touched:**

> *"We spent about **seventy** work-legs failing to build a computer-assisted proof for a
> one-dimensional fluid model."*
> → *"We spent **seven successive** work-legs failing to build a computer-assisted proof for a
> one-dimensional fluid model."*

`TECHNICAL_P2_PUB2_V1.md:627` and `BLOG_P2_PUB2_V1.md:52` were **not** touched: they carry the
broader *obstruction-span* claim, which `verify_276` judged defensible and distinct. A repo-wide
sweep of `writeup/`, `experiments/journal/` and `reports/` found **zero** banked anchors for the
seventy-leg span in either direction, against **two** for `seven`/51–57. That asymmetry is banked
as `CORRECTIONS.md` §7 with both locators rather than deferred to an eighth pass.

**Note that the fix makes BLOG structurally mirror TECH**: both documents now carry `seven` for
the certificate attempts and `seventy` for the obstruction span, in the same two places. Before
the fix BLOG was internally uniform and externally wrong; it is now consistent with every other
document in the family.
