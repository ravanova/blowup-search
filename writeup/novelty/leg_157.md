## 3a. THE NOVELTY PASS CAUGHT ITS OWN LEG. Recorded in full, because that is the point.

The deep-mine's headline candidate was going to be arXiv:2504.05066, reached as Cadiot's
backward citation `[15]`, and written up in the first draft of this file as *"never consulted
by this repository."* **That was false, and the sweep above caught it before the construction
was built.**

`grep -rln "2504.05066" .` returns exactly two files: this one, and
[`reports/REPORT_2026-08-05.md`](../../reports/REPORT_2026-08-05.md) lines 219–223, which
records, verbatim:

> Leg 62 (CP) answered its gate **NO** mid-cycle (Cadiot arXiv:2505.03091 does not pre-empt or
> refute NG's no-go) with an important nuance relayed directly to leg 58 (NG): a "zero diagonal"
> framing would be falsifiable against Breden–Payan–Reisch–Tang arXiv:2504.05066; the correct
> framing is quantitative (off-diagonal row-sum growth vs. diagonal growth).

So leg 62 **did** find this paper, **did** draw from it the operative conclusion — that a bare
"zero diagonal" framing is falsifiable and the framing must be quantitative — and **did** relay
it to the leg that needed it. Everything this leg would have claimed as new about *why the paper
matters* was already banked, one cycle ago, by the leg it set out to read deeper than.

**What is genuinely left, and it is smaller than the first draft claimed:**

* The paper was **never read at full text** — leg 62's contact with it was an identification and
  a one-sentence reading, recorded in a *report*, not in `CADIOT_SCOPE`, not in `CP_FORWARD`,
  not in any executable ledger. A check that is not executable decays at the rate of memory
  (lesson 68), and `Papers/MANIFEST.md` has no row for it.
* Its **Theorem 2.6 and its two changes of basis have never been located** with section
  numbers and verbatim hypotheses.
* Its §2.1 **describes Farid–Lancaster [FL91]'s hypotheses explicitly** — and `CP_NOT_OBTAINED`
  records FL91 as `"NOT OBTAINED -- paywalled (Elsevier)"`. A secondary source that states the
  hypotheses of the primary source this repository could not buy is worth banking on its own.

**Bearing on the gate.** The gate asks for a construction *"that has not already been tried in
this repository."* A construction whose operative reading was already extracted and relayed a
cycle ago is not that. This leg proceeds to **locate and bank**, and it will answer its gate
against what the full text actually contains — not against the excitement of the first draft.
