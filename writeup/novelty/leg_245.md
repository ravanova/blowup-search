## Method: author LISTINGS first, keyword nets only as a cross-check

A follow-up with an unexpected title cannot be missed by a *listing* the way it can be missed by
a *keyword search*. So the candidate set is closed by enumerating complete arXiv career listings
entry by entry, and only then running a keyword net — which here doubles as the leg's live-probe
control. Leg 240 established this shape, leg 242 confirmed it; this leg follows it and **hit
legs 240's and 242's exact failure mode for a third time**, on a third author line (Q3).

## Q7 — the parent itself, read at full text, and the sharpest thing in this leg

Two locators from `arXiv:2404.04054v2`'s own extraction.

**First, this repository's ledger characterization is confirmed correct.** The paper's title and
framework are stated on ℝ^d, which could have made "1D viscous Burgers" look like an error. It
is not. Line 1669:

> We focus on a generalised viscous Burgers equation on ℝ₊ with Neumann boundary condition

with `∂_t v + v²∂_x v = ∂_xx v`, `∂_x v(t,0) = 0` (eq. 53), similarity `v(t,x) = t^{−1/4}u(x/√t)`,
profile equation `Lu − u/4 + u²∂_x u = 0` (eq. 54). **The framework is d-general; the viscous
example is not.**

**Second — Remark 40, line 1687, and this is the finding.**

> **We deal with a one-dimensional example here for simplicity, but terms like (u·∇)u could in
> principle also be handled in dimension d ∈ {2,3}, as u ∈ H²(μ) is then still enough to
> guarantee that (u·∇)u ∈ L²(μ) since u ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d).**

`(u·∇)u` **is** the Navier–Stokes/Euler nonlinearity. The precedent's own authors state that
their Grade-A weighted-Sobolev machinery reaches it in d = 2 or 3 *in principle*, and give the
reason (the `H²(μ) ↪ L^∞` embedding).

So leg 174's empty Grade-A/fluid cell is **not merely unoccupied — it is flagged as
tractable-in-principle by the very group best placed to fill it**, and **28 months later it is
still empty**, with that same group's most recent paper calling a far smaller extension
"non-trivial ... future work". The gap between what is reachable in principle and what has been
done is the finding, and **the authors have documented it at both ends themselves.**
