## 3. The verbatim quotes the gate turns on

All from arXiv:2607.19762v1, `pdftotext -layout` extraction of the fetched PDF.

**(Q1) — §1, the abstract's own framing of the `a_c` recomputation as a cross-check:**
> "For a > 0 we prove a conditional two-line inclusion for each admissible smooth focusing
> profile, recompute the branch cl (a) of Lushnikov, Silantyev, and Siegel as a cross-check
> (matching their critical advection ac to 0.04%)"

**(Q2) — §1, the method actually used, and its declared accuracy:**
> "We recompute cl (a) independently by a lower-order Newton continuation on a compactified
> real-line grid (Newton residual 10−13 -10−10 , solution accuracy ∼ 3 × 10−4 at the a = 0
> floor), as an implementation cross-check and to supply the operator coefficients (Ω, cl )
> that Proposition 1 and the exponent s∗ below need at a > 0; it reproduces the branch
> endpoint against the published ac to 0.04% (Section 2). The a = 0 results above do not use
> it: there the profile and cl (0) = 1 are exact."

**(Q3) — §2, LSS declared the reference, Xu's the cross-check:**
> "The full high-precision branch cl (a) on (0, ac ) was already computed by Lushnikov,
> Silantyev, and Siegel [4] (complex pole dynamics), and we take theirs as the reference;
> the recomputation here is an independent lower-order cross-check that also supplies the
> operator coefficients (Ω, cl ) needed by Section 3."

**(Q4) — §2, the endpoint itself, and how it is obtained:**
> "the computed cl (a) decreases monotonically from 1 at a = 0 and crosses zero at a∗ ≈
> 0.6888 (linear interpolation between the computed branch values cl (0.68) = 0.0185 and
> cl (0.72) = −0.0659), to be compared with the published ac = 0.6890665 of [4]. The two
> agree to 0.04% relative error (Figure 1), which is the one quantitative check on the
> recomputed branch."

**(Q5) — §2, Xu's own error bar on his branch, stated as *not* a bound:**
> "the two-grid difference is |cl (N =1024) − cl (2048)| ≲ 5 × 10−4 (about three significant
> figures), a self-consistency measure rather than a certified continuum error bound (it can
> understate the true error against the high-precision branch of [4], which at nonzero a
> agrees only to about two or three significant figures; we do not tabulate a node-by-node
> comparison, and only the endpoint ac is checked against [4] quantitatively)"

**(Q6) — Table 1 caption, `c_l(1/2)` declared exact BY CITATION, not computed:**
> "cl ( 12 ) = 13 is exact ([20]; also [4, Thm. 2])."

**(Q7) — §6, the same again, in the text, and the decisive sentence about validation:**
> "The branch value is tested at a = 1/2, where s∗ = 3 exactly (cl ( 12 ) = 13 by the exact
> solution of [20]; also [4, Thm. 2]) and J. Chen [20] proved blow-up at s = 2 < 3
> (subcritical), consistent with persistence. **The only quantitative validation of the
> branch itself is the ac cross-check of Section 2.**"

**(Q8) — §6.1, what `s∗` *is*, in Xu's own words — a formal diagnostic, not a threshold:**
> "s∗ is a formal scaling (dissipation-relevance) threshold read off the self-similar
> exponent … and s∗ is not the sharp critical dissipation curve separating blow-up from
> global regularity, which for this family remains unknown."

**(Q9) — §3.2, what the spectral machinery returns across the advection branch:**
> "On the parameter values we ran it returns ndisc (a) = 0 for a ∈ [0, 0.65]; we report this
> as exploratory evidence only."
and, on the same strand:
> "This strand is exploratory: the computation records a per-point phase-increment and
> integer-defect diagnostic and anchors the a = 0 value to the proven empty strip of Theorem
> 2, but it does not enforce these diagnostics as hard gates, and its run outputs are not
> retained."

**(Q10) — §8, Xu's own statement of what is his versus LSS/ALS's:**
> "the pole-dynamics program of Lushnikov, Silantyev, Siegel, and Ambrose [4, 14, 21]
> developed the complex-pole continuation machinery (and the value ac ) … the difference is
> real-line versus periodic, a linear-stability spectral gap versus exact pole trajectories,
> and a continuous fractional-s scaling-relevance map versus isolated integer orders"

## 5. The novelty claim of this leg, stated narrowly

Not the value of `a_c`; not the value of `alpha(1/2)`; not Xu's 0.04% — all three are
already banked and are re-quoted here with locators rather than re-claimed. What is new is
the **provenance audit of the third source**: which of Xu's two distinct machineries
(spectral §3 vs. profile-continuation §2) produces each number, whether either is
independent of LSS/ALS, and at what precision — read off Xu's own sentences (Q1–Q10) rather
than off this repository's memory of him. The answer, and whether it triangulates or
escalates, is the leg's gate.
