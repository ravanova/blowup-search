# Leg 271 — ROUTE-PUB2R2 — novelty / prior-art pass

**Written before any edit was made.** This leg builds no solver module, runs no computation,
introduces no number that was not already banked, and moves no link of the `L1 → L4` chain.
It is the **second** rework iteration on the same two submission-track documents, commissioned
because leg 268's own verifier (`writeup/novelty/verify_268.md`) found that leg 268 corrected
some sites of a claim family and left others standing — two of which then contradicted leg 268's
own new sentences inside the same files.

## 1. What this leg claims

Nothing mathematical. Its deliverable is that **PUB2 no longer asserts, at any site, that
`σ_min` or `‖T⁻¹‖_X` has a value independent of the truncation**, and that the two companion
documents agree with the actual record about whether the independent check has reported.

Every replacement sentence is built from wording leg 268 already established at the sites it
*did* fix (TECHNICAL §3.2's "no §2-class obstruction" row, §3.5's ladder paragraph, §5(3)) or
from magnitudes leg 249 already certified. **No new framing is invented here.** A claim of
novelty at this leg would itself be the error.

## 2. Why a prior-art sweep is not the right instrument

There is no mathematical proposition to sweep. The three assertions removed are:

1. `σ_min` is "independent of the truncation" (BLOG §"the other room");
2. origin-`H²` has a truncation-independent `σ_min` at the `1e−4` level (TECHNICAL §3.2's
   contrast table, where the evidence offered was a *quadrature-window* sweep);
3. "Here the operator does have one" / "does have a floor" (TECHNICAL §5(1), BLOG's **no**
   paragraph) — a proved floor asserted as fact.

Each is *weaker* after this leg than before it. A rework that only ever removes strength cannot
create a novelty claim, and the surviving contrast — **this ladder flattens where `ℓ¹_w`'s
decays** — is exactly the contrast leg 249 independently states survives, and is the only thing
§§3–5 ever use the number for.

## 3. The one judgement call, recorded

TECHNICAL §5(1) and its BLOG mirror carry what leg 176 calls "its most useful output": the
attribution of the `Z₁` failure to the **shape** of `A` rather than to the operator or the space.
The escalation clause asks whether repairing the wording changes the **argument**.

It does not, and the reason is asymmetric evidence on the two sides of the contrast:

- the `ℓ¹_w` side is a **theorem** (leg 127: `σ_min(L_M) = c_s M^{−(1−s)} → 0`, hence `Z₁ ≥ 1`
  for every bounded `A`);
- the origin-`H²` side was never more than a **float64 ladder**, and leg 268 had already
  restated it as such at §3.2 and §3.5.

So the repaired sentences assert that leg 127's mechanism is **measured absent** rather than
proved absent, and mark the shape-attribution as holding *on that reading — the strongest the
data support*. The inference (leg 127's mechanism does not apply here, so the failure is
elsewhere) is untouched; only its epistemic strength is stated correctly. **The escalation clause
did not fire**, and the reasoning above is recorded so that a reviewer can disagree with the call
rather than have to reconstruct it.

## 4. Ceiling

No ban lifted, no route promoted, no GA compute, no solver run. Nothing is claimed about
`HL_S2_nonsymmetric` or about Navier–Stokes. The object throughout is still the `a = 0` CLM
steady linearisation, and every magnitude in PUB2 is still float64 at a stated truncation except
the leg-249 figures explicitly marked exact. Clay odds unchanged at ~0.05 %.
