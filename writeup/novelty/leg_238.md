is carried into PUB1 with the citation.

## 4. One premise of this leg's dispatch, corrected against the record

The dispatch states leg 176 has *"been independently verified (leg 192)."* **It has not, at this
writing.** Leg 192's only commit on any ref is `483b0d7`, its pre-registered novelty / prior-art
pass (`writeup/novelty/leg_192.md`, 116 lines, one file) — **no verification runner, no
re-derivation, no verdict**, and it is not an ancestor of `main`. Leg 176's magnitudes are
therefore written into PUB2 as **one leg's own float64 measurements**, and PUB2 says so in three
places (§0, §3.5, §7). Presenting them as independently confirmed would have been the easier and
more flattering sentence; it is not available.
