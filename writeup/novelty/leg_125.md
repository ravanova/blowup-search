## 1. The narrow question this pass asks

Not "has anyone studied dissipative gCLM" (they have, extensively). The narrow question, which
is the only one that prices this leg's output:

> **Does a computer-assisted proof / interval-arithmetic / validated-numerics certificate of a
> self-similar blow-up profile of gCLM with FULL LAPLACIAN dissipation (γ=2) exist?**

and its immediate widening,

> **Does a CAP certificate of ANY dissipative self-similar blow-up profile exist for a 1D
> fluid transport model of this family?**

## 3. Two near-misses, fetched and read rather than assumed

* **2509.14185, "Discovery of Unstable Singularities."** Fetched. Models: incompressible porous
  media and 3D Euler **with boundary**. gCLM: absent. Dissipation: the abstract gestures at
  "the boundary-free Euler and Navier–Stokes cases" as expectation, not result. CAP: the
  precision *"meets the requirements for rigorous mathematical validation via computer-assisted
  proofs"* — i.e. enabling, not completed. **Not a hit.**
* **2207.07548, Lushnikov–Silantyev–Siegel.** Fetched. gCLM with `-Λ^σ` dissipation, real line
  vs periodic; global existence for `σ ≥ 1` (periodic, small data, all real `a`); on the real
  line, **exact analytical self-similar singularity solutions at `a=0` and `a=1/2` for various
  `σ`, including `σ=2`**, plus accurate (non-rigorous) numerics tracking complex-plane
  singularities. **Not a certificate** — no interval arithmetic, no CAP. It is, however, the
  single most load-bearing neighbour for this leg, because it is a second, independent
  analytic source for the same `(a, γ) = (1/2, 2)` corner. It is already in this repository's
  `Papers/MANIFEST.md` Tier 1; leg 125 re-reads it at construction depth rather than citing it.

## 4. The one genuine CAP-under-dissipation precedent, and why it does not collide

**Dahne–Figueras arXiv:2410.05480** — verified branches of self-similar singular CGL/NLS
solutions **in the dissipation parameter**, in interval arithmetic. This repository already
re-derived it (leg 48: their Tables 1/2 to 1.8e-07, their Fig. 1a branch to 3.0e-06, their
fold to 3.8e-07) and it is what **closed stage V for non-novelty**. It is a real CAP under
dissipation — but of a **complex Ginzburg–Landau ODE profile**, continued in the dissipation
parameter. It is not a fluid transport model, carries no Hilbert transform, no `a`-advection,
and no `|X|^{-1}` far field. So it pre-empts V's *question* (margin survival in float) and does
not pre-empt this leg's *object*.

## 6. Verdict

**NOVEL, on the narrow question, with two qualifications that are part of the verdict.**

1. **No computer-assisted / interval-arithmetic certificate of any dissipative self-similar
   blow-up profile of a 1D fluid transport model was found** in 7 queries plus 2 full fetches.
   The entire CAP corpus for this family (Chen–Hou–Huang, Hou–Qin–Wang–Wei, Chen–Hou I/II,
   Buckmaster–Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano) is **inviscid**. This reproduces leg 63's finding at
   one level more depth and does not overturn it.
2. **Qualification A — the blow-up itself is not novel and this leg never claims it is.** Chen
   1908.09385 proved it analytically; LSS 2207.07548 gives exact analytic self-similar
   solutions in the same corner. What would be novel is a *certificate*, and what this leg
   actually produces is one rung lower still: the *first measured `Y_0`* against a
   radii-polynomial budget for such a profile.
3. **Qualification B — Dahne–Figueras is a real CAP under dissipation.** Any novelty claim
   this repository makes must be phrased against the *object* (1D fluid transport, Hilbert
   transport, `|X|^{-1}` far field), never as "no one has ever certified anything dissipative."
   That phrasing was available and is refused here.

**Honest ceiling, pre-committed and repeated from the brief:** this is **not** movement on
L1→L4 and **not** Clay progress — odds stay ~0.05% — regardless of how the gate answers. Only
the novel-output sub-goal is in play. The tripwire in §0 was checked at the end of the leg and
was not crossed: no dissipation parameter was floated against any certificate's margin, at any
point.
