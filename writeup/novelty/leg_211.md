### 2.2 What is therefore genuinely unasked

Three things, and the runner measures all three:

1. **The sign question itself.** Does Xu anywhere admit `ν ≤ 0`? No pass has enumerated the
   places in Xu where the sign of the dissipation coefficient is *stated as a hypothesis*.
2. **The collocation.** Is Xu's stated `s = 2` sub/supercritical boundary the *same locus* as
   legs 125/185's `a* = 0.3864963972206034`, and at what magnitude of agreement — measured
   against **Xu's own** declared resolution, not against a wish.
3. **The mechanism.** Is the agreement structural or coincidental — i.e. is Xu's `γ := 1 − s c_l`
   at `s = 2` algebraically the same object as this repository's own
   `Δ := 2 c_l/|c_ω| − 1` (`solver/dissipative_profile.py` lines 35–44), the condition
   leg 185 *imposed*?

None of 1–3 appears in any banked note, any JSON, or any of the seven prior Xu reads.
Leg 185 could not have asked them: its own novelty pass never opened Xu past the abstract.

this leg's gate forbids it from attempting.

## 5. The novelty claim of this leg, stated narrowly

**Not** the value `0.386` (leg 145 banked it). **Not** `s*(a) = 1/c_l` (banked at leg 45 and
audited at leg 189). **Not** leg 185's `a*` (leg 185's). What is new is the **join**: that
Xu's stated `s = 2` sub/supercritical boundary and this repository's `a*` are the same
locus; that the identity behind it is exact (`Δ = −γ|_{s=2}` under Xu's own time
normalization); and that Xu nevertheless **stipulates `ν > 0`** at both places he writes the
dissipative equation, so his framework contains the *threshold* but no *sign-changing branch*.
Whether that counts as "bearing on" is precisely what the gate adjudicates.
