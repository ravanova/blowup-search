### Q4 — is (FI) already in HQWW24, i.e. does priority run back to 2024?
Read `2305.05895` §3 at full text. Its map (p.8) is

> `R_a(f)(x) := ( T_a(f)(x)^{1/a} · exp( ((1-a)/a) ∫_0^x (T_a(f)(y) - T_a(f)(0)) / (y T_a(f)(y)) dy ) )`

**This is the same integration trick but it is NOT (FI)'s algebra.** The extra exponential
factor is forced by HQWW24's ansatz `Ω(x) = -x f(x)`: they integrate the **self-similar** profile
equation, where the `c_l X Ω_X` term contributes a `1/y` kernel that (FI) does not have.
`2305.05895` p.7 carries the intermediate line explicitly:
`f(x) = g(x)^{1/a} exp( ((1-a)/a) ∫_0^x (g(y)-1)/(y g(y)) dy )`.

**Conclusion of the priority question: the object in the project's exact algebraic form —
no exponential factor, `(1 - a u / r)^{1/a}_+` — is the TRAVELING-WAVE specialization, and its
first published appearance is `2603.25104` §7 (v1 2026-03-26 / v2 2026-06-16), attributing the
method to HQWW24 (ARMA 2024).** Either date puts the project (§23, 2026-08-01) second.

## What the pass settles about R1 and R2

Full quotes, page references and the algebraic identification live in
`experiments/journal/leg_112.md` and `writeup/data/p2_route_as2_v1_lit.json`. In one line each:

* **R1 CONFIRMED, with one qualifier §24 did not carry.** `2603.25104` abstract, p.1, verbatim:
  *"For 𝑎 > 0, we observe one-scale self-similar blowups with regular profiles that have not
  been found in previous studies. In contrast, for 𝑎 ≤ 0, we discover a novel two-scale
  self-similar blowup scenario…"* The qualifier: the whole dichotomy is **conditional on
  degenerate initial data** (`ω′₀(0) = 0`), which is the paper's stated subject (p.3: *"The main
  purpose of this paper is to find out whether and how self-similar blowups can develop from
  degenerate initial data"*). It is **not** a general statement about gCLM at `a > 0`.

* **R2 CONFIRMED, in the strongest available form.** `2603.25104` p.38 **defines** its map as
  `R_a(ω)(x) = (1 − a T(ω)(x)/r(ω))^{1/a}_+`, and p.39 **derives** it exactly as §23 did:
  *"𝜔′/𝜔 = 𝑢′/(𝑎𝑢−𝑟). Solving this ODE and writing 𝑢 = 𝑻(𝜔) gives …"*. Under
  `(ω, u, r) = (−Ω, −U, c)` this is (FI) character for character, **including the anchor
  constant**: their p.37 `ω = 1/(1+x²), u = ½ln(1+x²), r = ½` is §23's measured anchor
  `Ω = −1/(1+X²), U = −½log(1+X²), c = ½` (§23 residual **1.11e-16**).

**Therefore this leg claims nothing.** §23's hedge and §24's retraction were both correct, and
they are now correct **at full-text depth with locators** rather than at search level.
