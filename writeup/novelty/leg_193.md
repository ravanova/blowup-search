## Why this pass is light, stated before the work rather than after

This leg makes **no new claim about the world**. Its whole deliverable is a re-derivation of
a claim this repository landed nine hours earlier (leg 187, `b92031f`), from that leg's own
transcribed constants. Nothing it produces could be scooped, because nothing it produces is
new mathematics: if the re-derivation succeeds, the credit is leg 187's and, upstream of that,
Chen's and Xu's; if it fails, the finding is a defect in this repository, not a result.

The route brief says "novelty pass (light — this is a re-derivation task, not new
literature)", and that instruction is followed literally: **no new search was run.** What is
recorded instead is the attribution ledger this leg is bound by, because a verification leg
can silently launder a citation into a claim by re-deriving it and forgetting where it came
from. That is the failure mode this file exists to prevent.

## What this leg does add, and it is not a claim about the literature

One thing in this leg is stronger than what it verifies: leg 187 proves the orbit identity in
`Q[X]` at **five pinned rational values of `g`**, and argues `DF φ = 0` in prose backed by a
float residual of `3.27e-06`. This leg proves both in **`Q[X, b]` with `g = b²` symbolic** —
an identity in two variables, true for **every** `g > 0`, with `DF[φ]` the **exact** zero
polynomial rather than a small float.

That is a strengthening of the *evidence*, not of the *result*. The mathematical content —
"the dilation orbit is exact, so its tangent is a kernel element" — is unchanged, and its
attribution is unchanged: the symmetry is elementary and the zero mode is Xu's. No novelty
question arises, and none is opened.
