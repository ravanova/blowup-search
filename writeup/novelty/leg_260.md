## 2. External prior art: has anyone searched for a DSS blow-up as a periodic orbit?

The whole of Entry B rests on an object — *a DSS Navier–Stokes blow-up realised as a
periodic orbit of the dynamically rescaled flow, found by a global unseeded search*. Eight
arXiv nets, run live against the arXiv API this leg (`export.arxiv.org/api/query`,
`abs:` fields, HTTPS, counts read from `totalResults`):

| net | n |
|---|---|
| `abs:"periodic orbit" AND abs:"self-similar" AND abs:"Navier-Stokes"` | **0** |
| `abs:"discretely self-similar" AND abs:"periodic orbit"` | **0** |
| `abs:"periodic orbit" AND abs:"rescaled" AND abs:"blow-up"` | **0** |
| `abs:"periodic orbit" AND abs:"blowup" AND abs:"Navier-Stokes"` | **0** |
| `abs:"log-periodic" AND abs:"Navier-Stokes"` | **0** |
| `abs:"recurrent flow" AND abs:"Navier-Stokes"` | 3 |
| `abs:"unstable periodic orbit" AND abs:"singularity"` | 6 |
| `abs:"dynamic rescaling" AND abs:"periodic"` | 1 |

**Five independent phrasings of the intersection return zero.** The three non-zero nets
were opened and **0 of 10 hits are on the question**: the `recurrent flow` hits are
2D Kolmogorov flow and surface-flow decompositions; the `unstable periodic orbit` hits are
quantum criticality, delay embeddings, Klein–Gordon oscillons, cycle expansions and two
Bianchi-IX cosmologies; the `dynamic rescaling` hit is a deep-transformer normalisation
paper. **Links, not counts** — the on-topic-adjacent ones are `arXiv:1406.1820`,
`arXiv:2310.12561`, `arXiv:1703.05501`, `arXiv:2411.13103`, `arXiv:2206.00499`.

**So the object is genuinely unattempted, and that is a finding with two faces.** It is not
a crowded field where we would be re-deriving; it is also not a field with a method waiting
to be picked up.

**The one paper that matters is `arXiv:1406.1820` (Lucas & Kerswell 2014), and it is the
method Entry B would have to use.** Read at abstract this leg: it extracts *"simple
invariant sets (recurrent flows) **directly from chaotic/turbulent flows**"* on 2D
Kolmogorov flow. That phrase is the load-bearing one for §6's price — the method's
substrate is an **ergodically visited chaotic trajectory**, and the journal's price
argument turns on whether the rescaled 3D NS flow supplies one. Recorded here as
`read_at_abstract`, not `read_at_source`; the full PDF was not obtained, and §6 uses only
the abstract's own sentence.
