## Family A — hypothesis-violation / fabrication-acceptance in Y0/Z0/Z1/Z2

**This family's novelty was already settled by leg 79's own novelty pass** (committed
`5020fc8`, `writeup/novelty/leg_79.md`), which this leg treats as standing prior art rather
than re-deriving: van den Berg–Lessard (AMS Notices 62(9):1057, 2015) and Hungria–Lessard–
Mireles James (Math. Comp.) establish that Y0/Z1/Z2 are upper bounds on norms *within the
hypotheses of the imported theorem*; the NaN-bypasses-a-comparison-guard hazard is documented
in *Verifying Floating-Point Programs in Stainless* (arXiv:2601.14059); adversarial batteries
against trusted-base components are the routine QED-at-Large method (arXiv:2003.06458).

This leg's own queries reinforce and extend that finding with older, more specific sources:

- Query: `radii polynomial theorem rigorous numerics Y0 Z1 hypotheses nonnegative validation
  software`. Hits: [Rigorous numerics for analytic solutions of differential equations: the
  radii polynomial approach](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/jplessard/ODEs_files/final_draft.pdf)
  — states plainly that Y, Z0, Z1, Z2 "are norms or suprema of norms, hence automatically
  nonnegative," i.e. the theorem's own statement makes non-negativity a *hypothesis*, not an
  output property the code can assume-and-skip. Same family: [Global bifurcation diagram of
  steady states via rigorous numerics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.01414), [Rigorous numerics
  for NLS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.6531).
- Query: `radii polynomial theorem hypothesis violation input validation "garbage in" interval
  arithmetic ill-formed IEEE 1788`. Hits: [Introduction to the IEEE 1788-2015 Standard for
  Interval Arithmetic](https://inria.hal.science/hal-01559955/document) — the decoration
  system exists specifically because "an enclosure f(x) ⊆ x alone does not establish a fixed
  point" when the hypotheses (continuity, everywhere-defined) are not checked; this is the
  general-purpose, decades-old answer to exactly Family A's mechanism, already the anchor of
  leg 98's own novelty pass. [Validated numerics (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Validated_numerics)
  as a general orientation.
- No source was found that names this repo's specific pattern (Y0/Z0/Z1/Z2 accepted without a
  guard, producing `closes=True` on fabricated input) as its own named hazard — but the
  *general* pattern ("a computer-assisted-proof theorem is only as good as its verified
  inputs, and verified-numerics tooling exists precisely to make that checkable") is squarely
  folklore, decades old (the radii-polynomial theorem itself dates to Day–Lessard–Mischaikow
  2007 / Gameiro–Lessard 2010 lineage; IEEE 1788 to 2015, itself formalizing older interval
  arithmetic practice).

**Verdict for Family A: folklore.** No entry survives as literature-novel.

## Family B — scale-invariant convergence test blind to scaling-family escape

This is the family the assignment specifically flagged as possibly generalizing. The pass
here is the load-bearing one.

- Query: `pseudo-arclength continuation phase condition scale invariance symmetry Newton
  convergence blind to family`. Hits: [Condition Estimates for Pseudo-Arclength Continuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603716)
  (Dickson–Kelley–Ipsen–Kevrekidis) — establishes that pseudo-arclength's extra equation
  removes exactly the *fold* degeneracy, not a continuous-symmetry degeneracy: "each
  continuous symmetry (time-shift phase, spatial translation, scale invariance) needs its own
  orthogonality/phase constraint... if Newton stalls with a rank-deficiency equal to the group
  dimension, that is diagnostic, not a bug." This is precisely leg 237's C3 criterion
  (equation admits a scaling degeneracy) stated as general numerical-continuation folklore.
- Query: `freezing method relative equilibria self-similar profile phase condition
  normalization Beyn Thummler`. Hits: Beyn & Thümmler, *Freezing solutions of equivariant
  evolution equations*, SIAM J. Appl. Dynam. Syst. 3(2):85–116 (2004); Beyn & Thümmler,
  *Phase Conditions, Symmetries and PDE Continuation* — the abstract framework is: a Lie group
  acts on the solution set (translation, rotation, *or scaling*), and a "phase condition"
  (⟨u − u_prev, ∂_θ u_prev⟩ = 0 for each generator) is required precisely because, without it,
  Newton converges to an arbitrary member of the group orbit rather than the intended one —
  the exact mechanism leg 202/237 measured. This is 2004, pre-dating this repository by two
  decades: squarely OLD prior art per the §0a calibration.
- Query: `dynamic rescaling method self-similar blowup normalization condition scale
  invariance numerical profile equation`. Hits: the dynamic-rescaling / modulation technique
  (Merle, Raphael, Martel, Zaag, Soffer, Weinstein lineage, 1990s–2000s onward; recent
  instances [arXiv:2603.25104](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25104),
  [arXiv:2604.01868](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01868)) states in so many words: "the scaling
  parameters are not free — they must be fixed by normalization conditions that remove the
  scale-invariance degeneracy of the profile equation... the stability of the dynamic
  rescaling equation may heavily depend on the concrete choice of normalization conditions."
  This is the self-similar-blowup-specific instance of exactly the 202/237 claim, and it is
  standard methodology in that literature, not a novel observation.
- Query (Navier–Stokes-specific, spelling variants `Navier--Stokes` / `Navier-Stokes` / `self
  similar` both tried): [ON SCALE-INVARIANT SOLUTIONS OF THE NAVIER-STOKES
  EQUATIONS](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~sverak/Krakow-6ECM.pdf) (Šverák), [Forward
  self-similar solutions to the 2D Navier–Stokes
  equations](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03161v1) — the latter's continuation strategy tracks
  linearization eigenvalues explicitly because a scale-invariant residual alone cannot
  distinguish branches; no hit named the general hazard as its own contribution — it is
  treated as routine continuation hygiene, consistent with folklore status.
- Query: `"scale-invariant" residual convergence criterion pitfall relative tolerance masks
  wrong branch numerical`. Hits: general numerical-solver literature (MOOSE, OpenMDAO,
  ADMM/Wohlberg arXiv:1704.06209) converge on the same warning independently of self-similar
  PDEs at all: "a relative residual criterion answers 'did the residual shrink relative to
  where I started?', not 'am I near the solution I want?'... a converged, verified solution
  may still be on a branch you didn't intend." This is domain-general numerical-analysis
  folklore, older than and broader than this repository's occasion for rediscovering it.

**Verdict for Family B: folklore, and older/broader than expected.** The 202/237 claim that a
scale-invariant test can't detect scaling-family escape is not merely "already known
somewhere" — it is the *named justification* for an entire standard methodology (phase
conditions since Keller-era pseudo-arclength continuation, formalized by Beyn–Thümmler 2004,
and independently re-derived as the dynamic-rescaling normalization-condition requirement in
the self-similar-blowup literature). C1/C2/C3 (leg 237's own criteria for a "hit": verdict is
a function of a scale-invariant quantity ONLY, no absolute-scale companion, and the equation
admits a genuine scaling degeneracy) is a precise operational restatement of the same
diagnostic the phase-condition literature already uses ("rank-deficiency equal to the group
dimension is diagnostic, not a bug").

## Gate answer

already in the literature or folklore. Both mechanisms are old (IEEE 1788 formalized 2015 but
built on older interval-arithmetic practice; radii-polynomial hypothesis structure dates to

underlying pseudo-arclength technique older still; dynamic-rescaling normalization is a
1990s–2000s methodology). Per the pre-committed NO branch: bank the prior-art map above
(links, not counts — every claim in this file is a URL plus the specific sentence it
supports, not a tally), and do not draft `writeup/CAP_SILENT_FAILURES.md` as a taxonomy.
