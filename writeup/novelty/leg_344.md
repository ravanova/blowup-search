## §2 full-text reads (not abstract-only, not snippet-only)

- **`arXiv:2506.19243`** (Wang, Liu, Li, Anandkumar, Hou, 24 Jun 2025, v1 only). Full HTML
  fetched and searched specifically for "KAN"/"Kolmogorov-Arnold": found ONLY as a Related
  Works citation to other authors' papers ("Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) [29,28] that
  leverage nonlinear learnable activation functions... further investigated in the PINN
  setting [56,48,51]") — **KAN is not used anywhere in this paper's own experiments.** The
  architecture actually used is a standard MLP (4×20 tanh for Burgers, 7×30 SiLU for
  Boussinesq) trained with Adam then self-scaled BFGS (SSBroyden1). This directly corrects
  leg 342's label of this paper as "Hou's own PINN/KAN machinery" — the KAN half of that
  label is not supported by the source.

- **`arXiv:2210.07191`/`2305.05660`** (Chen-Hou I/II, Boussinesq/axisymmetric-3D-Euler stable
  nearly-self-similar blowup). Abstracts fetched in full. Confirmed: rigorous
  functional-analytic proof + computer-assisted numerics, not machine learning at all — this
  is the certification layer that `2506.19243`'s PINN profile feeds precision into, read here
  to establish what validation discipline a completed retarget would eventually have to meet.

- **`arXiv:2604.16842`** (Y. Wang PhD thesis, 18 Apr 2026). Abstract fetched in full,
  specifically re-checked against leg 342's prior read (which flagged it "located, not a
  candidate"). Confirms and sharpens: the thesis's KAN architecture is demonstrated on
  Nonlinear Heat, Complex Ginzburg-Landau, and 3D Keller-Segel with logistic damping — never
  on Navier-Stokes or Euler. This is the ONLY place in the literature found this leg where KAN
  is actually applied to a blowup problem, and it is not the target equation.

- **`arXiv:2509.14185`** ("Discovery of Unstable Singularities", Wang/Martens/Racanière/
  Cao-Labora/Buckmaster/Georgiev/Gómez-Serrano/Lai et al, DeepMind + academic collaborators,
  17 Sep 2025, v1 only — `v2` checked and returns HTTP 404, confirming no update). **Load-bearing
  find, not in leg 342's net.** HTML full text fetched and read for architecture, precision,
  failure modes, and stated open problems (not just the abstract, which truncates under the
  tool's quote-length limit). This is a new, independently-evolved successor/competitor line to
  Hou's PINN work, combining DeepMind ML engineering with two of the field's leading rigorous
  fluid-singularity authors (Buckmaster, Gómez-Serrano). Full detail in
  `writeup/data/p2_route_pklr_v1.json` and `experiments/journal/leg_344.md`.

- **`arXiv:2410.04096`** (Sinc-KAN). Abstract fetched in full. General-purpose KAN variant for
  PDEs with singularities, not fluid-dynamics-specific (subject classes cs.LG/cs.AI/cs.NE/
  math.NA/physics.comp-ph, no physics.flu-dyn). Flagged as an unverified, off-the-shelf
  candidate component, not existing art on the target problem.

- **`arXiv:2406.11045`** (KINN). Located via search snippet only, not fetched to full text this
  leg — background context, not load-bearing, not classified inherit/rebuild.
