### R3. What did legs 307 and 335 actually settle, so this leg does not redo it?

* **Leg 307 (Route-TSCX, `401a5f7`)** adjudicated leg 221's *flagged two-scale counterexample* —
  the `r cos(b)[exp(-r^2) + exp(-400 r^2)]` field read as `1.1351209034484022` against truth

  with its own `5e-4` basis (~865x is right), which leg 352 (`aa9c5cd`) then corrected in the
  two sites that carried it. This is an adjudication of a *documentation magnitude and a
  contrived field*, not a re-run of either of leg 221's gate clauses.
* **Leg 335 (Route-S1GR, `cc725d1`)** adjudicated the `spike1_stepC_gate.json` reproducibility
  gap as **REPRODUCIBLE_AS_BANKED**: the regeneration harness had passed `argv=["--logged"]`

  gap was a harness argv defect, not a solver defect. The `BANKED` registry entry on `main` now
  reads `argv=["--logged", "--steps", "2500"]`. Again: an adjudication of *one moving artifact*,
  not a re-run of the sweep that found it.

Neither leg is a substitute for either clause of this leg's gate. That matches the DM's cycle-11a

Established mechanically before any construction, because the answer bears on what a re-run can
even mean. `solver/boussinesq_rescaled.py` was last touched by `aa9c5cd` (leg 352), *after* the

repaired ones. Full magnitudes go in the curated JSON.
