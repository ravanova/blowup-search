## R2 — external prior art: is the discriminant itself novel?

**No, and it is not claimed to be.** The quantity being repaired is the ratio of relative GMRES
residuals across a Krylov-dimension ladder, read as *flat ⇒ continuum in the spectrum* versus
*bending ⇒ finite ill-conditioning*. That reading is textbook — it is the standard convergence
picture for GMRES on operators with essential spectrum (Greenbaum–Pták–Strakoš on GMRES
residual behaviour; Nachtigal–Reddy–Trefethen on how badly non-normality decouples convergence
from the spectrum), and Route-K's own module docstring already attributes the operator-splitting
move to Chen–Hou (arXiv:2210.07191). **No external novelty is claimed by this leg at any
strength.** The repair is a correctness property of *our implementation* of a standard
diagnostic, so there is no external literature that could be prior art to it: "select the
ladder's endpoints by the ladder's parameter rather than by array position" is a coding
invariant, not a result.

## R3 — the one thing a reader might mistake this for

This is **not** a claim that any published number was wrong, and it must not be cited as one.
Leg 243 established that none were, and this leg's G1 re-establishes it independently under
`==` on raw float64: **largest absolute difference across all 10 published quantities is
exactly `0.0`**. What changes is the *reason* they are right. A leg that repairs a latent
defect and moves no number is the weaker, better outcome — cf. leg 166, which closed a
`critical_radius` gap and moved **0** clean-input floats.

**Novelty claimed: NONE.**
