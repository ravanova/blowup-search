**Committed before adjudication of the gate**, per ORCHESTRATION.md §0a. B2's gate is an
internal instrument-construction question about THIS repository's own compactified basis

far-field block in a Navier-Stokes search context — is already in the literature, not whether
mapped-Chebyshev bases exist at all (they manifestly do, confirmed by the positive control
below).

**Verdict: genuine controlled absence, not banked as a discovery.** No title/abstract on arXiv
combines a mapped/rational Chebyshev basis with either "discretely self-similar" or
"log-periodic" far-field structure. This is expected and not informative either way: Boyd-type
algebraic/logarithmic maps for endpoint singularities are decades-old general spectral-methods
technique (not scoped to Navier-Stokes or to DSS objects at all), so a title/abstract search
naturally returns nothing when the query is narrowed to this repository's specific object. No
claim is made that the general TECHNIQUE (log-map removes a branch point; algebraic map
compactifies a semi-infinite variable) is novel — it is not, and is not presented as such
anywhere in `solver/dssp_basis.py`'s docstring. What this leg measured is a NEW NUMBER (the
enrichment factor on leg 313's specific target function), not a new numerical-analysis
technique.
