## What this leg would have to be careful not to claim

Three separable things get measured downstream, and only the third is a candidate for
"novel". Naming them apart is the point of doing this pass first.

1. **Breden–Chu's own framework and its Remark 40.** Entirely theirs. Every hypothesis,
   constant, bound-name (`Y`, `Z¹₁`, `Z¹₂`, `Z²₁`, `Z²₂`, `Z₂`, `Z₃`) and the
   `A = diag(A_n, I)` convention is read out of `arXiv:2404.04054v2` and cited to a line
   number in a pinned extraction. **Nothing here is banked as this repository's.**
2. **The Nečas–Růžička–Šverák exclusion.** A 1996 theorem (and Tsai's 1998 extensions). This
   repository already records it — `plan_of_record.py` line 45, `CLAY_ROADMAP.md` line 334,
   `CONTINUATION_PROMPT.md` line 63 — as the screen constraining stage P0's ansatz. **Not
   novel, not this leg's, and its exact hypothesis boundary is leg 253's (ROUTE-NRSX) job, not
   this leg's.** This leg uses only the L³ form the repository itself already records, and
   says so wherever it is used.
3. **The composition of (1) and (2)** — i.e. whether Breden–Chu's space, applied to the
   backward-self-similar 3D Navier–Stokes profile that Remark 40's nonlinearity points at,
   lands inside or outside NRS's hypothesis set, with an explicit embedding constant. **This
   is the only thing the pass below is trying to pre-empt**, and it is a scoping observation,
   not a theorem.

## Q1 — is anyone already doing computer-assisted proofs of self-similar Navier–Stokes?

`all:"self-similar" AND all:"Navier-Stokes" AND all:"computer-assisted"` — **3 entries.**

* https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09949 — *Stable Finite-Time Singularity Formation for 3D
  Navier–Stokes via 5D-Lifted Axisymmetric Reduction* (2026-04-10). **Already in this
  repository's `Papers/`** (`MANIFEST.md` Tier 3). Lifts the dimension to evade NRS rather
  than working in a Gaussian-weighted space; not Breden–Chu's framework.
* https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25116 — *Nonuniqueness of Leray–Hopf solutions to the unforced
  incompressible 3D Navier–Stokes* (2025-09-29).
* https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14185 — *Discovery of Unstable Singularities* (2025-09-17). The
  PINN/Gauss–Newton line; not a weighted-Sobolev CAP.

**None uses `H²(µ)`, `H²(e^{|x|²/4})`, or a Hermite–Laguerre eigenbasis of
`L = −Δ − (x/2)·∇`.**

## Q4 — the Leray/self-similar literature, to place the NRS screen

`all:"self-similar" AND all:"Navier-Stokes" AND all:"Leray"` — **19 entries**, read
title-by-title. The relevant fact is the *shape* of the set: every nontrivial-profile paper in
it works in a **scaling-critical or slowly-decaying** class — Morrey
(https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15776), Marcinkiewicz
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.08177), anisotropic Lebesgue
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01510), `L²_loc` DSS
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08060). **Not one works in a Gaussian-weighted space.** That is
the prior-art shape the downstream computation is about, and it is *evidence the observation
below is unsurprising to experts*, not evidence it is banked anywhere.
