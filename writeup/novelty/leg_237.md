# Leg 237 — Route-SIRC novelty pass (light; census family)

**Date:** 2026-08-06. **Branch:** `leg/237-sirc-v1`. **Role:** LEG.
**Verdict: `PROCEED_AS_CENSUS`.**

This leg builds no new mathematics, sharpens no bound, measures no physics and claims no
novel mechanism. It takes a defect *class* that leg 202 (Route-PNA) established on one
module and asks, by construction rather than by inspection, whether any **other**
`solver/` module carries it. Precedent shape for a census/audit leg with no
`BLOG_`/`TECHNICAL_` pair and no registered figure: legs 107 (FIA, `5e03e13`), 122 (ASA),
202 (PNA), 179, 186, 214.

Per `writeup/novelty/README.md`: **links, not counts.** The external-literature pass on the
underlying *mechanism* is not repeated here — leg 202 ran it, and leg 223 (Route-PUB3)
separately recorded the general thesis ("a scale-invariant convergence test cannot see a
scaling degeneracy") as **NON-NOVEL against four external literatures**. Nothing in this
leg's result depends on that thesis being new; what is new, if anything, is the *census over
this repository's own surfaces*, which no external literature can supply.

## R3. Does the census claim to be exhaustive, and on what basis?

It claims to be **exhaustive over `solver/`'s convergence and closure verdict sites**, at
47 `.py` files, 20 classified verdict sites, and 27 modules recorded as carrying no verdict
site at all. The enumeration basis is named in the runner: every site that produces a
`converged`/`closes`/verdict boolean, found by grep and then **read**, with the boolean's
own expression transcribed into the artifact so the classification can be checked without
re-running anything. It does **not** claim exhaustiveness over `experiments/` runners or
root-level `test_*.py`, which were searched only for call sites.

**One honest limit, recorded before the result.** A census can only be as good as its
criteria, and criterion C3 (the equation must admit a scaling degeneracy) was added *during*
the pass, when `spectral_certificate.tail_norm_ladder` satisfied C1 and C2 and still was not
the defect — `l^1_w` membership is genuinely scale-invariant, so there is no wrong number to
hide. C3 is therefore not a criterion this leg pre-committed; it is one the pass discovered
it needed. Stated here rather than presented as foresight.
