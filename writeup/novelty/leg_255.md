## 1. The reach clause, quoted from the source rather than paraphrased

Screen (iv) is **Breden–Chu's own words**, located by leg 245 at line 1687 of the md5-pinned
extraction `ff7a34b776bfe5edf97397e5eabdbb7a` of `arXiv:2404.04054`
(<https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04054>, Breden & Chu, *Numer. Math.*, DOI
`10.1007/s00211-025-01504-4`), **Remark 40**:

> We deal with a one-dimensional example here for simplicity, but terms like **(u·∇)u** could in
> principle also be handled **in dimension d ∈ {2,3}**, as u ∈ H²(μ) is then still enough to
> guarantee that (u·∇)u ∈ L²(μ) since u ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d).

**The reason is the whole clause.** The reach is exactly what the embedding `H²(μ) ↪ L^∞(ℝ^d)`
buys: a **local, polynomial** nonlinearity in `u` and its first derivatives, over a **Gaussian
weighted** Sobolev space `H²(μ)`, `μ = e^{|x|²/4}`, with the profile operator built from the
Laplacian / Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operator. What Remark 40 does **not** name, and what this census
therefore records as *outside the stated reach* rather than silently admitting:

* nonlocal operators in the nonlinearity — Hilbert transform, Biot–Savart, **Leray projection**
  (the pressure), Fourier restriction, averaging;
* nonlocal dissipation — `(-Δ)^α`, `Λ^γ` for non-even `γ`;
* state spaces that are not `H²(μ)` on `ℝ^d` — sequence/shell ladders, free boundaries;
* quasilinear principal parts (geometric flows).

Leg 245 also located the *other* end of the same group's own honesty, `arXiv:2603.27198`
l.273: extending merely from an interval to a higher-dimensional **rectangle**
*"is non-trivial but will be studied in a future work."*

**And the caution the dispatch built into the gate is in this same paper.** Breden–Chu's own
worked example, l.1669: *"We focus on a generalised viscous Burgers equation on ℝ₊ with Neumann
boundary condition"*, `∂_t v + v²∂_x v = ∂_xx v`. Viscous Burgers is **parabolic and
certifiable and does not blow up** — screens (i) and (iv) are independent and both must pass.
Viscous Burgers is carried in the census as a row, and it is killed by screen (i).

## 3. What the Biernat–Donninger hit does and does not change

**It does not contradict leg 174.** Leg 174's empty cell is `fluid_adjacent = True, grade = A`.
Biernat–Donninger is `fluid_adjacent = False, grade = A` — a cell leg 174 already recorded as
**occupied** (by `arXiv:2410.05480`, DF-CGL, 2024). The empty cell stays empty.

**What it changes is the date and the strength of that occupancy.** The Grade-A/non-fluid cell
has been occupied since **2016**, not 2024, and by a *second, independent* author line
(Donninger's, disjoint from Dähne–Figueras, which leg 242 closed). Two independent groups have
enclosed a dissipative equation's own self-similar blow-up profile in interval arithmetic. The
method half of Phase 1 is therefore better evidenced than the record showed; the target half is
not.
