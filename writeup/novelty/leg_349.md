## 4. Already-in-repository prior art, re-surfaced and read in this leg's frame (not new, correctly labelled)

Grep of the checked-out worktree at the start of this leg located two records **already banked by
prior legs**, directly relevant to this leg's subject and re-read here for the specific question

* `writeup/data/p2_route_dssp_v1.json:284` and `writeup/novelty/leg_334.md:189` — leg 334's own
  novelty pass, query `"Newton-Krylov" AND "periodic orbit" AND turbulence` → **2**:
  `arXiv:1705.03720` (*Relative periodic orbits form the backbone of turbulent pipe flow* — RPO
  extraction in 3D NS at research resolution) and `arXiv:1705.03838` (*The Openpipeflow
  Navier–Stokes Solver* — a published Newton-Krylov-with-hookstep code). This is prior art for the
  *technology* (NK-RPO extraction is mature, published, working engineering on 3D NS, just not on

  is a well-behaved GA *fitness signal*, which is this leg's actual question and is not addressed
  by either paper (neither paper uses NK convergence as an evolutionary-search fitness at all; both
  use it as a direct root-finder from a hand-supplied recurrence).
* `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEDSSP_V1.md:722-726` (leg 334's own plan document, not a
  novelty-pass link but a prior first-party finding in this repository) — already states, of a

  **B5 (DSSP-NKBASIN)**, drafted (not dispatched) to measure the basin radius of NK+phase-condition
  extraction on a *published, known* 2-D Kolmogorov-flow RPO before committing 3-D cost. This is
  directly load-bearing for §5 of this leg's journal: the repository's own prior plan already
  flags NK-basin behaviour as an open, unmeasured empirical question, which is exactly the kind of
  fact a six-property gate analysis needs and cannot assume away.

## 6. Reading against this leg's own thesis

The thesis does not claim zero prior art; it claims the *proposed fitness* is, for the first time in
this project's history, not a Tier-1/2 blow-up proxy. **This pass finds no prior art at all — in
either the "GA fitness = NK convergence" combination specifically, or in the narrower "GA ∘
Newton-Krylov" combination generally (Q1 = 0, controlled genuine)** — for using Newton-Krylov (or
Newton's-method) convergence/basin-membership as an evolutionary-search fitness signal, anywhere,
in any domain, fluid-dynamical or otherwise. Every adjacent hit found (Q2, Q5, the Q3 pairwise
control) is either off-topic by object-kind or uses a metaheuristic merely to drive a downstream
deterministic solve, not to select on that solve's convergence as the fitness itself. **The novelty
claim in the thesis is not contradicted by this pass, and no scooping risk is recorded**: nothing
located targets this specific fitness-design question, in this or any other domain.
