## 3. The fourteen findings

### E1 — the highest-priority rework item: `0.0908` is convention-relative and PUB2 never says so

**15 sites** print `0.0908` / `0.090804` (TECH L46, 206, 219, 221, 312, 315, 318, 325, 376, 382,
383, 391, 543, 582; BLOG L91, L117). **None carries the convention.**

Leg 249 §10 (W5), carried into
[`p2_route_h2c_v1_construction_correction_leg268.json`](../data/p2_route_h2c_v1_construction_correction_leg268.json)
as `convention_caveat_recorded_by_249`: the `X ⊕ ℂ` Gram puts unit weight on the border amplitude
while the `X` block carries the bare Laguerre normalization. Sweeping that weight over `10⁻²…10²`
moves `σ_min` across **0.01086 … 0.13580, a factor of 12.5**; adopting Xu's own `y`-space
normalization (carrying the `2π`) gives **0.0420** instead of 0.0908. Leg 249's own sentence:
***"PUB2 quotes the digits without the convention."*** Neither leg 268 nor leg 271 addressed it;
`grep -c` for `convention|normaliz|0.0420|2π` over both files returns **0**.

**Why this is a rework item and not an escalation.** Leg 249 certifies in the same paragraph that
the **gate property — bounded away from zero uniformly in the truncation — is invariant** under
that weight, since a positive weight cannot send a positive limit to zero. PUB2's argument uses
only the *contrast in ladder behaviour* (flattens here, decays like `M^{−(1−s)}` there), which is
convention-invariant. No argument moves. What is missing is one disclosing sentence at first use.

Worth recording: PUB2 spent four legs (250, 268, 271, 274) removing over-determined *adjectives*
from this number. The remaining over-determination is in the *digits*.
