# Leg 318 — Route-DECR — §0a novelty / prior-art pass

**Run and committed BEFORE any mathematics**, per contract §0a. Runner
`experiments/p2_route_decr_v1_lit.py`; machine-readable record
`writeup/data/p2_route_decr_v1_lit.json`. Date of the search: **2026-08-11**.
No ban touched, no apparatus built, `plan_of_record.py` untouched.

**The question the pass has to serve.** Leg 240 measured a distinction and gave it two
names: the viscous term of a self-similar blow-up certificate is **DOMINATED** (the
certified object is the `ν = 0` system, viscosity is admitted as a decaying error under a
scalar inequality on the scaling exponent) or **ENCLOSED** (`ν` sits *inside* the
certified problem, so the certified object itself depends on `ν`). This leg asks whether a
**general structural criterion** exists for which of the two is available. The prior-art
pass therefore has to separate three literatures that a naive keyword sweep merges:

| cell | what is in it | expected |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | the classical **criticality classification** of a dissipative term against a scaling (sub / critical / supercritical) | old, populated, **folklore** |
| **B** | **computer-assisted / validated** constructions of self-similar profiles with dissipation *inside* the certified equation — the ENCLOSED instances | populated, small, recent |
| **C** | any statement of a **criterion** connecting A to B — a general rule saying which criticality class admits an enclosure | the only cell where this leg could be new |

### The fault that *is* real: HTTP 429, and why it looks exactly like a zero

**Measured this leg.** At a 3 s politeness delay the export endpoint began returning
**HTTP 429** after roughly a dozen queries; the first execution of this runner died on the
*first* control query with 429 after the earlier ad-hoc probes had used the lane. The
delay was raised to 6 s and explicit exponential backoff (15/30/45/60/75 s) added; the
first control still needed **five** backoffs before it answered, and then returned its
correct 84.

**This is the important part.** A client that swallows the exception, or that returns an
empty feed on 429, reports the query as **zero results** — indistinguishable from a clean
negative. That is the single most plausible origin of leg 315's six all-zero ANDed
queries, which were banked as evidence of a *general* endpoint defect that leg 314 could
not reproduce and that this leg has now measured working. **The generalization was wrong;
the underlying rate limit is real.** Recorded here as a finding in its own right:
`query()` in the runner never lets a 429 become a 0, and this is the mechanism any future
leg's literature instrument should be checked against first.

### Cell D — the compressible-implosion cell, and TWO NEW PAPERS THAT BEAR ON THE ANSWER

`all:"implosion" AND all:"compressible Navier-Stokes"` (both spellings, same 2 hits):

- **`arXiv:2603.10141`** — *Development of Implosions of Solutions to the Three-Dimensional
  **Degenerate** Compressible Navier–Stokes Equations.* Density-dependent viscosity with a
  **power-law** exponent, and the paper identifies a **threshold in that exponent**,
  depending on the adiabatic exponent, below which implosion still occurs — its own words:
  *"in this regime, the degenerate viscous terms are **not sufficiently strong to suppress
  the convective mechanism** driving the implosion."*
- **`arXiv:2512.18545`** — *Global Regular Solutions of the Degenerate Compressible
  Navier–Stokes Equations with Large Initial Data of Spherical Symmetry.* At the
  shallow-water degeneracy (viscosity linear in density) solutions are **globally regular
  and cannot implode**, all `γ ∈ (1,∞)` in 2D and `γ ∈ (1,3)` in 3D.

**Both are new to this repository's bank** (leg 240's enumeration was of `2208.09445`'s
author group and closed on 2026-08-06; these are a different group and one of them
postdates it). Neither refutes leg 240 — neither carries any certification apparatus, and
`all:"implosion" AND all:"computer-assisted"` is a verified **0**. They matter to *this*
leg for a different reason, recorded here so the mathematics that follows cannot be
accused of having been fitted to them: they are the **two outer branches** of the very
trichotomy this leg's criterion is about — dissipation too weak to matter (implosion
survives, viscosity is an error term) and dissipation strong enough to win (no blow-up at
all) — measured on a *different* dissipation operator (`μ(ρ) = ρ^δ`) from BCG's constant
viscosity, by an author group with no computer in the argument.
