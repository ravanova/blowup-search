## Relationship to the neighbouring legs, stated so this leg cannot be read as a repeat

Leg 116 is the **measurement**; this leg is the **repair**, and it is dispatched as a leg rather
than a bench one-off on the leg-76 precedent for exactly the reasons DIRECTION.md gives: it is
multi-file, it touches certifying-bound code, and it must invert leg 116's GAP-PIN gates in the
same commit. Nothing in the repair is allowed to re-argue leg 116's finding; leg 116 already
established, at primary source, that the constants are nonnegative-and-finite **by hypothesis**
of the imported theorem, and that this is 2015-vintage textbook material.

Legs 86 and 105 are the **post-repair regression checks** for the two already-repaired siblings.
Their test files (`test_port_certification_regression.py`, `test_interval_certificate_postrepair.py`,
`test_port_certification_postrepair.py`) are the instruments this leg's gate clause (b) is
measured with. They are **run, never edited** — a repair that has to edit the test that checks
it has not been checked.

An important asymmetry, read from the source before construction and not assumed: the two
existing copies of `_hypothesis_violations` are **not** textually identical, and the differences
are load-bearing, not cosmetic.

* `solver/port_certification.py` **accepts `None`** in any slot and skips it — `None` means NOT
  MEASURED and is the whole point of that module's kill-switch (`BLOCKED_AT_STEP_ONE`).
* `solver/interval_certificate.py` **raises `TypeError` on `None`** — its `radii_verdict` has no
  not-measured branch, and its docstring says so explicitly.
* The two NaN messages differ in which comparison they cite as the one a NaN slips past
  (`NaN >= 1.0` vs `NaN < 1.0`), because the two modules guard the contraction in opposite
  senses.
* `nk_bounds.budget` has a **fourth** slot the other two do not: `Z_0`. Its contraction
  condition is `Z_0 + Z_1 < 1`, not `Z_1 < 1`.

`interval_certificate.py`'s own comment already names the hazard this leg is closing:

> *"Mirrors `solver.port_certification._hypothesis_violations` (leg 79's repair) so the two
> pipelines cannot drift apart on what 'outside the theorem' means."*

So the shared guard is not this leg's idea — it is the **in-repo comment's own stated intent**,
implemented. That comment is prior art against this leg, and is cited as such.

**On-topic finding for (N1) — established, and it also sets this leg's scope line.** The
refactoring is textbook and is claimed as nothing else. The Rule of Three is satisfied *exactly*:
this is the third call site, and the three sites are not coincidentally similar — they implement
the hypotheses of the **same imported theorem**, which is the definition of genuine shared
knowledge rather than accidental resemblance. But the WET counter-argument binds the design: the
four differences enumerated above (`None` policy, NaN message, `Z_0` slot, contraction sense) are
**real** differences and must survive the consolidation as *parameters of the shared guard*, not
be flattened into it. A shared guard that silently changed `port_certification`'s `None` handling
would be the wrong abstraction, and it would show up immediately as a clean-input result moving —
which is this leg's own stop condition.

## Verdict and the scope line this leg is bound to

**`PROCEED_AS_SHARED_GUARD_REPAIR`.** Every question comes back established prior art or
"no external design to copy". Therefore:

1. **No novelty is claimed** — not for the hypotheses (textbook, 2015), not for the refactoring
   (DRY / Rule of Three), not for the zero-regression method (golden master, plus leg 105's
   instrument choice), and not for the absence of an equivalent layer in RadiiPolynomial.jl /
   CAPD / Arb / INTLAB, which is recorded as an unaudited README-level absence.
2. **The four real differences between the three call sites survive as parameters**, not as
   flattened behaviour. The WET counter-argument (Q1) is the reason this is written down before
   construction rather than discovered during it.
3. **Any clean-input result moving AT ALL is a stop**, per the gate's own no-branch. The shared
   guard is a *rejection* layer: it may only turn things that used to be accepted into refusals.
   It may never change a number that a hypothesis-satisfying input already produced.
4. **Nothing here can be read as movement on the `L1→L4` chain.** `nk_bounds.py` is Route-D
   infrastructure and `port_certification.py` / `interval_certificate.py` are the port's; what a
   validation layer restores is the **guarantee**, not the margin. Leg 116 measured every defect
   in `nk_bounds.py` as LATENT (every in-repo caller passes `Y0 = 0.0` literally and stays inside
   `α < 2`, `X ≤ 3.2e7`, worst ref/module 0.99997), and legs 79/98's repairs moved no banked
   number. This one must not either — that is clause (b).
