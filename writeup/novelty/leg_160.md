### 2.2 The mechanism — and here is the pre-emption

Query: `objective function evaluation noise floor limits finite-difference linearity
verification optimization arXiv`

Query: `More Wild estimating computational noise ECnoise finite difference interval lower bound
noise level`

Returned, and these are the ones that matter:
- **<https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Estimating-Computational-Noise-Mor%C3%A9-Wild/a23428b804001bc40bce46e634295cfee4731519>**
  / <https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/2557.pdf> — Moré & Wild,
  *Estimating Computational Noise* (ECnoise). Quantifies the noise level `ε_f` of a computed
  function and states plainly that **`ε_f` is a lower bound on the roundoff error in `f`**.
- **<https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09762>** — Berahas, Byrd, Nocedal et al., *On the Numerical
  Performance of Derivative-Free Optimization Methods Based on Finite-Difference
  Approximations.* The differencing interval is set **by the noise level and a bound on the
  second/third derivative** — i.e. the usable interval is bounded **below** by the noise.
- **<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06380>** / <https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/21M1452470> —
  Shi, Xie, Xuan, Nocedal, *Adaptive Finite-Difference Interval Estimation for Noisy DFO.* A
  bisection that **balances truncation error against measurement error** to pick the interval.
- <https://arxiv.org/html/2401.10757> — Estimating computational noise on parametric curves.
  Same programme, curves instead of lines.
- <https://arxiv.org/html/2405.01731v1>, <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09606>,
  <https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00112>, <https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19720> — smoothing /
  mixed-difference / survey work in the same DFO-with-noise literature.

**C2 IS PRE-EMPTED AS A GENERAL PRINCIPLE, and this leg must say so in every artifact.** That a
finite-difference probe of a noisy function has a *two-sided* usable window — capped above by
truncation error and **floored below by the function's own noise level** — is standard,
40-year-old numerical-optimization knowledge with a named algorithm (ECnoise) and a named
bisection (Shi et al.). This leg did not discover it. What is new here is *only its
application*: this repository's frozen P3 probe was repaired at the **upper** end only (leg 50's
`DEFECT_WINDOW_C = 0.1 / ‖A‖_w`, explicitly derived from truncation/nonlinearity) and has **no
lower cut at all** — the grid runs to `ε = 1e−11`. Moré–Wild says that is exactly where a
probe stops measuring the function and starts measuring its noise. The claim this leg is
entitled to make is therefore: *the standard two-sided window criterion, applied to this gate's
frozen probe, predicts P3's residual — and predicts that no weight-box repair can move it.*
Not: *we found that noise floors the window.*

This also **retro-fits a banked lesson rather than a new one**: standing lesson 84 in
`CONTINUATION_PROMPT.md` is already "**a known-answer probe has a WINDOW**", and lesson 86 is
"**a rigorous bound dominated by its own EVALUATION error is a statement about the code**".
Leg 50 applied 84 to one end of the window. This leg applies 84 and 86 to the other end. Both
lessons were already in the record; neither is claimed as new.

### 2.3 C3 — the P1/P5 vs P3 tension

### 2.4b CORRECTION, appended before the runner produced a number, and it costs this leg a claim

After committing this log I read `experiments/p2_weight_repairs_v2_evidence.py` and leg 59's
`D_probe_window` block in full, rather than only its `properties`. **Leg 59 already banked the
C2 diagnosis, and banked it in almost the words I had planned to use.** Its JSON carries, per
resolved weight, `eps_min_noise_floor = Y_0 / max_i w_i |d_i|` and `eps_max_linearity =
C/‖A‖_w`; it reports `Spearman(window width, |slope−1|) = −0.8779220779220779` over 21 weights;
and its own `reading` field says verbatim:

> "the fitted slope is a function of the probe WINDOW's width, not of the weight:
> `defect_ladder` bounds eps above (leg 50's repair) and not below, so weights whose window is
> narrow are probed under their own noise floor. **This is a defect in the FITNESS's
> definition, not in the box the wall model draws** — which is exactly what the gate's
> no-branch says a future B proposal has to change. NOT applied to the gate above; the gate is
> frozen."

So **C2 is pre-empted twice over**: as a general principle by Moré–Wild/Berahas/Shi, and *for
this exact object and this exact probe* by leg 59, which measured it and deliberately left it
out of the frozen gate. This leg neither discovered the mechanism nor is the first to name it.

What survives as this leg's own, restated narrowly:
- **(a) acting on it.** Leg 59 diagnosed and stopped, by design — its territory was the wall
  model. This leg makes the definitional change leg 59's `reading` field asks for, and puts it
  through the frozen gate. That is the part that can answer the ban's lift condition.
- **(b) a stronger form of the evidence.** Leg 59 *correlated* window width with slope error
  (ρ = −0.878). This leg *reconstructs the ladder outright* from `ρ = A F(z*)` with no PDE
  evaluation at any perturbed state, and predicts each weight's fitted slope. A correlation of
  −0.878 is consistent with several mechanisms; a reproduction of the slopes is consistent with
  one. It can also fail, which is the point.
- **(c) C3, the P1/P5-vs-P3 tension**, which nothing in the record states.

`knee_eps` as computed in this leg's `floor_census` is algebraically identical to leg 59's
`eps_min_noise_floor` (`max_i w_i|ρ_i| = Y_0` at the base state). That is a **check, not a
coincidence**, and the evidence script asserts the two agree — if they do not, this leg has
mis-implemented its predecessor's diagnostic and says so.

## 3. What the pass changed about the plan, before the plan ran

Three things, all recorded here so they cannot be retrofitted:

1. **The mechanism claim is demoted to an application of Moré–Wild.** Every artifact cites
   ECnoise and Berahas et al. and states that the two-sided-window principle is theirs.
2. **A mechanism CONTROL is added, and it can report the other answer.** If P3's residual is the
   evaluation floor, then re-evaluating the *same* fitness definition with the module's existing
   higher-precision residual (`_F_longdouble`, ~3.5 extra decades of mantissa) must move
   `|slope−1|` **down**; if P3's residual is definitional, it must not move. Leg 59's own result
   (a box repair moving P3 by exactly `0.0000000000`) is the negative control that already
   exists. Per lesson 90 I asked what would have to change in the code for this control to come
   out the other way: the answer is "the floor would have to not be in `F`", which is a real
   possibility the run can falsify.
3. **An identity control is added first.** The candidate fitnesses are injected by substituting
   the engine class, so the frozen predicate itself is executed **unmodified**; running it with
   an identity subclass must reproduce leg 59's `P2 = 0.975` and `P3 = 0.3421493449940881`
   bit-for-bit before any candidate is scored.
