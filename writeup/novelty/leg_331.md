## §3 The prior art that matters, and exactly what it pre-empts

**(A) `arXiv:2403.18718v3` — Matthieu Cadiot, "Constructive proofs of existence and
stability of solitary waves in the Whitham and capillary-gravity Whitham equations"
(27 Mar 2024, rev. 29 Sep 2024).** This is the load-bearing find. The Whitham equation's
linear part *is* a nonlocal Fourier multiplier, and Cadiot proves existence **and** spectral
stability for it by an explicitly **Newton–Kantorovich** approach, approximating the
multiplier operator and building an approximate inverse for the linearization. His own
closing sentence states the generalisation claim: the methodology *"provides a new approach
for addressing existence and spectral stability of solitary waves in **nonlocal** nonlinear
equations."*

**What this pre-empts:** the *generic* question "can Newton–Kantorovich / radii-polynomial
certification handle a nonlocal operator at all?" is **already answered YES in the
literature**, and this leg must not claim otherwise. Any result of mine is not the first
nonlocal computer-assisted proof and will not be described as one.

**What it does NOT pre-empt, and this is the whole of my leg's remaining room:** Cadiot's
realization is **Fourier series analysis** — the nonlocal operator is *diagonal* in the very
basis the space is built on, which is what makes an approximate inverse constructible. This
repository has already measured the hypothesis that comes with that: leg 304 (Route-CADX,
landed `b319449`) read `arXiv:2505.03091` Assumption 1, p. 6 — *"there exists `l_min > 0`
such that `|l(ξ)| ≥ l_min` for all `ξ ∈ ℝ^m"`* — where `L` is a Fourier multiplier by the
class definition. Breden–Chu's space is **not** a Fourier space: it is `H²(µ)`,
`µ = e^{|x|²/4}/Z`, on the **unbounded** domain, diagonalised by the half-Hermite/Laguerre
eigenbasis of `L = −Δ − (x/2)·∇`, in which a Fourier multiplier is **not** diagonal and not
even banded. That is lesson 87 exactly — *a certification method has a SHAPE, and the shape
is a property of the operator: multiplier or shift?* — and it is the untouched question.

**(B) `arXiv:2607.15256v1` — Jiajie Chen, Thomas Y. Hou, "Analytic finite-rank corrections
for singularly weighted estimates in a computer-assisted proof of 3D Euler singularity"
(16 Jul 2026).** Nonlocal (stream-function/Biot-Savart) fluid CAP with **weighted** energy
estimates and singular weights; abstract closes on *"broader applicability to
computer-assisted stability analysis for **nonlocal PDEs**."* **Not** radii-polynomial /
Newton–Kantorovich — it is a low-rank-correction + weighted-energy method, a different
machinery from Breden–Chu's. It is the strongest evidence that the *fluid nonlocal* cell is
being worked in a *different* realization, and it is the correct citation for "weighted
estimates for nonlocal fluid operators are not new." It does not touch `H²(e^{|x|²/4})`.

**(C) `arXiv:2404.04054v2` — Breden–Chu**, the machinery itself, already reproduced end to
end by leg 256 (`solver/bc_weighted_sobolev.py`). Its **Remark 40** — *"terms like `(u·∇)u`
could in principle also be handled in `d ∈ {2,3}`"* — is what screen (iv_a) has been reading
as a *silence* about nonlocality across 21 kills. Remark 40 is a statement about what the
authors *name*, not a measurement of what the machinery *does*. Nothing in the paper
measures a nonlocal operator on `H²(µ)`.

**(D) `arXiv:2501.13672v2` — Breden et al., "Numerical analysis of differential equations on
weighted Sobolev spaces: beyond classical orthogonal polynomials."** Same author group, same
family of spaces, one year later. Located and flagged; it is the single most likely place
for a pre-emption of this leg and is checked in the construction's §0 before any headline is
written.

**(E) Also located, not pre-emptive:** `arXiv:2504.05066v1` (Turing instability, nonlocal
heterogeneous reaction–diffusion, CAP — nonlocal *convolution* kernel, bounded domain, not a
singular integral operator, not a weighted Gaussian space); `arXiv:2607.28194v1` (mass-critical
half-wave blow-up — nonlocal `|∇|`, but an analytic virial argument, no CAP);
`arXiv:1403.5668v1` (fractional Schrödinger spectral problems, no CAP framework);
`arXiv:2308.01528v3` (Hou–Luo model exact self-similar blowup, nonlocal, but not on `H²(µ)`).

### Verdict

**`PROCEED`.** The exact cell — *Breden–Chu's `H²(e^{|x|²/4})` weighted-Sobolev machinery
pointed at a nonlocal operator* — has **no located prior art**, and the four zeros that
carry that claim are built from high-recall phrases with a passing ANDed-phrase control.
The *adjacent* cell — Newton–Kantorovich certification of a nonlocal operator in a **Fourier**
realization — is **occupied by Cadiot (`arXiv:2403.18718v3`)** and this leg's prose will say
so. My leg's contribution is therefore explicitly **not** "nonlocal CAP is possible" but
**"what happens when the nonlocal operator is not diagonal in the space's own basis, and the
space's weight is forced to be Gaussian by self-similarity."**

## §5 The construction, PRE-COMMITTED HERE BEFORE IT WAS BUILT OR RUN

So that no number below can be read as assembled backwards.

**The object.** Breden–Chu eq. (54), `L u − u/4 + u² ∂_x u = 0` on `ℝ⁺` with Neumann data at
0, with **exactly one letter changed**: `∂_x ↦ Λ^{2α} = (−Δ)^α`. The `α = 1/2` case is
`Λ = H∂_x`, the **Hilbert-transform** realization the gate names first. The local original is
therefore the **control that runs through the identical code path**, with nonlocality as the
single varied ingredient (lesson 90: the control can come out differently, and does).

**The primitive.** `Λ^{2α}ψ_m` is computed **exactly**, not by a discretised transform:
`ψ_m(x) = c_m H_{2m}(x/2) e^{−x²/4}` and the Fourier transform of that is
`2√π(−1)^m e^{−ξ²}H_{2m}(ξ)`, so `Λ^{2α}ψ_m` is a single Gaussian-damped cosine integral. For
`α = 1/2` there is a second, fully closed form via the Hermite functions of the second kind
`G_k(s) = (1/π) p.v.∫ H_k(σ)e^{−σ²}/(s−σ)dσ`, `G_0 = 2·Dawson(s)/π`,
`G_{k+1} = 2sG_k − 2kG_{k−1} − (2/√π)δ_{k0}` — and the two routes are cross-checked against
each other and against `solver/line_hilbert.py`. The recurrence is contaminated by the
growing `H_k` solution, so it is run in **`decimal.Decimal` at high working precision**, which
is the "precision binds" contingency the directive anticipated.

**The five pre-committed measurements**, each reported as a magnitude:

- **M1 — does the nonlocal image stay in the space?** `‖Λ^{2α}ψ_m‖²_{L²(µ)}` truncated at
  radius `R`, as a function of `R`, against the local control `‖∂_xψ_m‖²_{L²(µ)}` on the same
  integrator. Prediction recorded in advance: the nonlocal image has an **algebraic** tail set
  by the vanishing-moment order of `ψ_m`, against a weight `e^{x²/4}` that grows, so this
  diverges for every `m` while the control converges.
- **M2 — does the nonlinearity survive anyway?** The nonlocal image never appears bare in the
  problem: it appears as `ū²·Λ^{2α}ū` and, in the linearization, as `ū²·Λ^{2α}ψ_m`, both
  carrying a Gaussian-decaying companion factor that may kill the algebraic tail. Prediction
  recorded in advance: **these are finite**, and M1's divergence is therefore *not*
  automatically fatal. Which of M1/M2 governs is exactly what is unmeasured.
- **M3 — is the machinery's own quadrature still exact?** Every Breden–Chu bound rests on
  products of `ψ` and `∂_xψ` being polynomial × Gaussian and hence **exactly** Gauss–Laguerre
  integrable; `Λ^{2α}ψ` is not in that class. The induced error of the six/four-product rules
  is measured against a refined independent quadrature, and specifically whether the tail
  terms — computed by Breden–Chu as a **difference** `‖full‖² − ‖P_n‖²` of two nearly equal
  numbers — survive it or go negative.
- **M4 — the sup-norm route.** `sup|Λ^{2α}ψ_m|` and its growth exponent in `m`, deciding
  whether `Σ_m sup|Λ^{2α}ψ_m|²/λ_m²` (what `Z̄22`, `Z2`, `Z3` need) converges.
- **M5 — the bounds themselves.** `Y, Z̄11, Z̄12, Z̄21, Z̄22, Z1, Z2, Z3` and Corollary 21's
  radii polynomial for the nonlocal problem, at named `n`, beside the local control's own
  values at the same `n` — the gate's actual question.

**Ceiling, stated before any number.** float64 for the bounds, `Decimal` where precision
binds, **not** interval arithmetic. Nothing this leg produces is a rigorous enclosure and
nothing it produces may be cited as one. The realization is named in the gate answer per
lesson 91: *even half-Hermite/Laguerre basis, `H²(e^{|x|²/4})`, `d = 1`, even (Neumann)
sector, `Λ^{2α} = (−Δ)^α`.*
