# Leg 320 (Route-MTSC) — novelty pass: is there validated/rigorous numerics in a Malmquist–Takenaka (MT) basis, and has the target's nonlinearity in that basis been touched by anyone?

**Pass date 2026-08-11.** Run before any construction, per the leg contract. Links, not counts
(`writeup/novelty/README.md`). Per the 2026-08-07 calibration note (recorded 2026-08-11), this
cell is measured-vacated ground (four author groups already censused and gone) — weight goes to
locating OLD prior art over an active-scooping check, and that is what turned up the one load-
bearing find below.

---

## 0. What this pass is for

Leg 301's own counterweight (b) states plainly: *"no rigorous-numerics or computer-assisted-
proof work in a Malmquist–Takenaka / Christov basis anywhere."* That claim is re-tested here,
not re-quoted, and a second, narrower question is added that 301 did not ask: has anyone built
**numerical (not necessarily rigorous) analysis of a quadratic nonlinearity coupled to a
non-smooth-symbol Hilbert-type multiplier in the MT/MTC basis** — because that is exactly this
leg's clause (c)/(d) territory, and if it exists it changes the build-cost estimate.

## 1. Confirms 301's claim, does not weaken it

Re-checked Cadiot arXiv:2505.03091 directly (abstract-level; full text already quoted verbatim
by leg 304 in `plan_of_record.py`'s CADX annotation) — its spectral method is **Fourier on a
box** (`Ω_d = (-d,d)^m`) with a pseudo-diagonalization / generalized Gershgorin argument, not
MT/MTC. Nothing in it or in Breden–Desvillettes–Lessard (arXiv:1503.06315, already on file)
touches a rational Hardy basis. A search for `"Malmquist-Takenaka" "radii polynomial" OR
"computer-assisted proof"` returns **no rigorous-numerics or CAP hit of any kind.** 301's claim
stands: **no validated MT transform exists anywhere.**

## 2. What 301 missed, and it matters for clause (d)

`arXiv:1904.10755` — **Shindin, Parumasur & Aluko, "Analysis of Malmquist–Takenaka–Christov
rational approximations with applications to the nonlinear Benjamin equation"** (2019,
ScienceDirect / J. Comput. Phys. companion). This is exactly the situation this leg is scoping:
the Benjamin equation couples a **quadratic nonlinearity** to a **Hilbert-transform-type
multiplier whose symbol is not smooth at the origin** — structurally the same pairing as
`HL_S2_nonsymmetric`'s `S·∂_X` plus `H(Ω)` terms. Their own framing: MTC approximations are
*"particularly suitable for solving semi- and quasi-linear problems containing Fourier
multipliers, whose symbols are not smooth at the origin"* and the paper delivers **rigorous
convergence and stability analysis of a collocation MTC scheme** for that nonlinear equation,
comparable in efficiency to spectral Fourier/hybrid Fourier-FEM methods.

Two things to be precise about, because "rigorous" is being used in two different senses in this
sentence and conflating them would be a lesson-91-shaped error:

- **Not** computer-assisted / interval / enclosure numerics (MSC classes are 65M12/65M15/65M70,
  no 65G tag; no interval arithmetic, no enclosure, nothing self-validating). Classical float
  convergence/stability *theorems*, proved by hand, tested in float.
- **Is** a real, load-bearing, already-published convergence theory for *the exact structural
  pairing* (quadratic nonlinearity × non-smooth Hilbert-type multiplier) in the MTC basis. That
  is not nothing: it means clause (d)'s costed build does not start from zero on the collocation/
  convergence side, only on the interval-arithmetic/CAP side.

A third, narrower hit — Molla & Shindin, *"A spectral method for fractional nonlinear
transmission lines"* using MT basis functions for a nonlinear ODE — is the same genre (classical
float spectral method, no rigor claim beyond convergence) and is not separately load-bearing
once Shindin–Parumasur–Aluko is on file (same first author, same method family).

A fourth hit, `arXiv:2407.04013` ("Approximation of rogue waves using Malmquist–Takenaka
functions", NLS), is **linear approximation theory of a nonlinear PDE's *solution*** (fitting a
known rational NLS soliton in the MT basis), not a certification or even a discretisation of the
nonlinear operator itself — noted, not used.

## 3. What this changes about this leg's scope

Nothing about clauses (a)/(b)/(c) — those are float measurements on the actual target, and no
paper above touches `HL_S2_nonsymmetric` or any of its three death mechanisms. **Clause (d)
changes**: the validated-MT-transform cost is not "green field decay theory plus CAP wrapper"
but "an already-published collocation convergence theory for the *matching* nonlinear-times-
non-smooth-multiplier structure, plus a CAP/interval wrapper that does not exist for it either."
That is a real, citable reduction in the (d) estimate, stated below with the number attached to
it, not left as a vibe.

**No link of the L1→L4 chain moved by this pass.** Clay odds stay ~0.05%.
