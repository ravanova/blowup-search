**Date:** 2026-08-06. **Agent:** LEG. **Branch:** `leg/204-tna2-v1`.
**Verdict: `PROCEED_AS_AUDIT`, WITH THE DISPATCH'S PREMISE CORRECTED.**

This leg builds no new mathematics, measures no new physics, and claims no novel
mechanism. It adversarially audits `solver/target_norm.py` along four axes named in the
dispatch — NaN/Inf, degenerate/zero-norm input, boundary parameters, planted wrong-value
pass-through — and asks one gate: *under adversarial and degenerate inputs, does
`target_norm.py` ever silently return a wrong value rather than reject or visibly
propagate the defect?* The module is **read-only** to this leg under either branch.
