## 2. Outside prior art: has anyone published this target selection?

The nearest published objects are surveys of self-similar solutions and of computer-assisted
fluid proofs. Neither performs a selection under a certification-feasibility constraint.

* Bradshaw & Tsai, *Self-similar solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations: a survey of
  recent results* — https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00038 — **read at full text**. It is a
  survey of *constructions and exclusions*, organised by forward/backward and SS/DSS. It
  states the open classes (see §3 below) but proposes no target and mentions no
  computer-assisted certification.
* Pineau & Vicol, *On rotated backwards self-similar solutions of the incompressible 3D
  Navier-Stokes equations* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09619 — **read at full text**.
  Narrows one of the open classes. Analytic; no certification programme.
* Constantin, Ignatova & Vicol, *On putative self-similarity for incompressible 3D Euler* —
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17570 — **read at full text**. Constrains the *exponent*, not
  the target list.

This repository's own literature legs already establish the negative on the
certification-feasibility side, and I re-checked rather than assumed it:

* **Leg 174**'s occupancy matrix — `experiments/journal/leg_174.md:43-58`, `:230` — the
  Grade-A × fluid-adjacent cell is **empty**, *"for want of a TARGET, not for want of a
  method."*
* **Leg 242** — `experiments/journal/leg_242.md:244-252` — gate **NO** on both clauses,
  matrix reprinted with that cell **"— STILL EMPTY —"** (12 nets, 7 subsequent works, 0
  joint, 0 fluid, 668 days).
* **Leg 245**, **leg 240**, **leg 197** concur from three other directions.

So: nobody has named the target that fills the empty cell. That is exactly this leg's job,
and it is why the finding is a *target*, not a *method*.

## 3. The live check that decides the leading candidate

My leading candidate is an **upgrade of the certified object's grade**, so the whole
candidate collapses if somebody has already done the upgrade. Two independent live searches,
run 2026-08-07:

**Query A** — *"computer-assisted proof interval arithmetic self-similar profile compressible
Navier-Stokes implosion viscosity enclosed 2026"*. Result: the state of the art is stated as
*"Computer-assisted proofs have been employed to establish implosion for compressible
Navier–Stokes by showing that the Euler self-similar profile dominates the dynamics in
appropriate parameter regimes, **with viscosity treated as a perturbation**."* No hit
encloses the dissipative term.

**Query B** — *"Cao-Labora Gomez-Serrano imploding singularity Navier-Stokes computer
assisted proof adiabatic exponent range 2026 improvement"*. Result: the current published
state is CGSS, *Non-radial implosion for compressible Euler and Navier-Stokes in T³ and R³*,
**Cambridge J. Math. 13(4) 753–885 (2026)** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05325) — non-radial,
still Euler-profile-driven, viscosity still not enclosed.

Sources consulted for these:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09445 (BCG) — **read at full text**; the constraint is stated
  by the authors themselves at §7's opening: *"in the Navier-Stokes case we need to
  restrict the parameter r to a regime where the self-similar profile dominates the
  dissipation."*
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05325 (CGSS) — **read at full text**; §3.4.2 is a
  *"Dissipation term"* energy estimate, i.e. the same analytic-domination architecture.
- https://sites.brown.edu/jgs/papers/ — the author's own paper list, checked for a newer
  entry closing this. None.
- https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12758 — 1D compressible Euler with far-field cutoff, 2026 —
  inviscid, so it does not close the gap.

**Determination: the gap is open.** The dissipative term in every published imploding
CAP is *dominated by a parameter restriction*, never *enclosed*. This matches leg 174's and
leg 240's banked readings, re-verified live rather than inherited.
