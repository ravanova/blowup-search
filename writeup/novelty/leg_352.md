two are different papers by an overlapping but non-identical author list):

- arXiv:2509.14185 (`USC.cite`, already fixed by leg 338): 22 authors, including **Javier
  Gómez-Serrano**, none named "Leger" — re-confirmed by this leg, reproducing leg 338's result.
- arXiv:2511.22819 (`USC2.cite`, this leg's dispatched site): **Yongji Wang, Tristan Léger,
  Ching-Yao Lai, Tristan Buckmaster** — four authors, **Tristan Léger present, no Gómez-Serrano

person's name from an unrelated paper (Gómez-Serrano). **Not applied.** This is a resister
under the gate's own NO branch, and is reported rather than forced, per the leg spec's explicit
instruction to do exactly that when verification fails to reproduce the claimed correction.
