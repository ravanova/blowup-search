# Leg 172 — Route-W2L novelty pass / literature query log

**Pass date: 2026-08-06.** Question surveyed: has validated/interval numerics ever
certified a **genuinely 3D** PDE singularity, in **any** field — not just in this
repository's usual corpus?

**Gate (verbatim).** "Does any published work — in fluid dynamics, PDE theory, or any
other computer-assisted-proof tradition — establish a rigorous (interval-arithmetic,
validated numerics, or otherwise machine-certified) singularity/blow-up result for a
genuinely 3D PDE model (not an ODE, not a 1D/2D reduction)?"

**Answer: NO.** Every machine-certified singularity result located — across fluid
mechanics, dispersive PDE, parabolic PDE, chemotaxis and the wider CAP literature —
certifies an object of **at most two spatial variables**. Where a *theorem statement*
names a 3D PDE, the 3D-ness is supplied by **analysis layered on top of** a certified
low-dimensional object (a symmetry reduction, or an ODE profile), never by the
certificate itself. **Wall 2 is field-wide, not an artifact of this repository's
narrower literature scope.**

This log records links and query strings, not counts, per this directory's README.

## Part 2 — primary-source reads, verbatim

### R1 — Chen–Hou, arXiv:2305.05660 (Part II), fetched
Abstract, verbatim: *"This is Part II of our paper in which we prove finite time blowup
of the 2D Boussinesq and **3D axisymmetric** Euler equations with smooth initial data of
finite energy **and boundary**."*

What the computer certifies, verbatim from the same abstract: *"sharp stability estimates
of the linearized operator by constructing space-time solutions with rigorous error
control"* and *"sharp estimates of the velocity in the regular case using computer
assistance"*, verifying *"that the stability constants obtained in Part I indeed satisfy
the inequalities in our stability lemma."*

**Dimensionality of the certified object: 2.** The linearized operator acts on the 2D
Boussinesq system in the meridional half-plane; the 3D Euler statement is obtained
**through the axisymmetric reduction** — exactly the 2D↔3D correspondence
`DIRECTION.md`'s Status note names. Published: Multiscale Model. Simul. (SIAM),
`10.1137/23M1580395`; the smooth-data 3D Euler statement also appears as Chen–Hou, PNAS
2025, `10.1073/pnas.2500940122`, whose title itself carries the hypothesis: "Singularity
formation in 3D Euler equations with smooth initial data **and boundary**".

### R2 — Buckmaster–Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano, arXiv:2208.09445, **full PDF read**
This is the sharpest data point, because the paper states in its own words exactly what
the machine did. Section 1.4, verbatim:

> *"In order to perform rigorous, error-free calculations, **interval arithmetic** will be
> used as part of the proof whenever needed."*

> *"In our concrete case, we will use the computer in two different parts of our strategy:
> 1. Computing (with rigorous bounds) a high amount of Taylor coefficients of a solution
> of an **ODE** at a singular point (Lemma A.27, Lemma A.28).
> 2. Validating the sign of polynomials of degree 7-11 with coefficients depending on
> **2 parameters** using a branch and bound method (Proposition 3.2, Proposition 3.5,
> Proposition 4.5)."*

The reduction is stated explicitly at the head of §1.5, verbatim: *"Let us begin by
rewriting (1.2) **under spherical symmetry**, and in terms of the rescaled sound speed
σ"* — after which the system is a pair of equations in `(R, t)` alone.

**So: a paper whose title says "3D compressible fluids", whose certified objects are (i)
an ODE and (ii) a two-parameter polynomial sign condition.** This is the Tucker-shaped
pattern `DIRECTION.md`'s thesis anticipated, occurring **inside fluid mechanics itself**,
in one of the field's flagship recent results (Forum of Mathematics, Pi, January 2025).

The same section carries the field's own self-description of its reach — the best
available third-party statement of where Wall 2 sits, quoted verbatim:

> *"Very early computer-assisted proofs were mostly constrained to finite dimensional
> problems [34, 85]. Slowly, more and more computational power has enabled to tackle
> harder problems, including partial differential equations. We mention the pioneering
> papers of Plum [76, 77] and Nakao [71, 72] in this context, and more recent advances
> done by Fazekas–Pacella–Plum [33] for the Lane Emden equation, van den Berg–Hénot–
> Lessard [88] for semilinear elliptic equations, Dahne–Gómez-Serrano–Hou and
> Gómez-Serrano–Orriols [28, 40] for inverse spectral problems, Jaquette–Lessard–Takayasu
> [50] for the non-conservative NLS equations, Dahne–Gómez-Serrano [27] for the
> Burgers-Hilbert equation, Takayasu–Lessard–Jaquette–Okamoto [82] for the complex in time
> nonlinear heat equation and Breden–Engel [11] for chaos in stochastically perturbed Hopf
> systems. We make no claim that this list is exhaustive, but we would like to emphasize
> the broad directions of the problems that the field (computer-assisted proofs in PDE)
> has been able to undertake over the last few years."*

> *"In the context of fluid mechanics we highlight the following authors and equations:
> Kobayashi [57] and Stokes' extreme waves; Chen–Hou–Huang [21] and De Gregorio;
> Castro–Córdoba–Gómez-Serrano [19] and SQG; Enciso–Gómez-Serrano–Vergara [31] and
> Whitham; Arioli–Koch, Figueras–De la Llave, Gameiro–Lessard, Figueras–Gameiro–Lessard–De
> la Llave, Zgliczynski, Zgliczynski–Mischaikow [5, 35, 37, 36, 92, 93] and
> Kuramoto–Shivasinsky; van den Berg–Breden–Lessard–van Veen, Arioli–Gazzola–Koch,
> Bedrossian–Punshon-Smith [87, 4, 8] and Navier-Stokes."*

**Every equation in that fluid-mechanics list is 1D or 2D.** The survey the authors point
to for more (Gómez-Serrano, *Computer-assisted proofs in PDE: a survey*, SeMA J. 76(3),
459–484, 2019) adds no 3D singularity result.

### R3 — Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano–Shi–Staffilani, arXiv:2310.05325, fetched
Abstract, verbatim: *"In this paper we construct smooth, **non-radial** solutions of the
compressible Euler and Navier-Stokes equation that develop an imploding finite time
singularity. Our construction is motivated by the works [Merle, Raphaël, Rodnianski, and
Szeftel, Ann. of Math., 196(2):567-778, 2022, Ann. of Math., 196(2):779-889, 2022],
[Buckmaster, Cao-Labora, and Gómez-Serrano, arXiv:2208.09445, 2022], but is flexible
enough to handle both periodic and non-radial initial data."*

This is **the closest thing in the literature to a YES**, and it is worth being precise
about why it is not one. The *theorem* is genuinely 3D and genuinely non-symmetric
(`T^3` and `R^3`, non-radial smooth data, finite-time implosion for compressible Euler
**and** Navier–Stokes) — but the paper introduces **no** interval arithmetic and **no**
validated numerics of its own (the fetch found none). Its machine-certified content is
**inherited** from arXiv:2208.09445 — i.e. from R2's spherically-symmetric ODE profile.
The 3D non-radial part is **pure analysis built on top of a 1D certified object**.
Accepted, Cambridge Journal of Mathematics.

This is the single most instructive case for this repository: it shows the field's actual
route to a genuinely-3D, genuinely-non-symmetric blow-up theorem, and that route puts the
*certificate* at the bottom of a 1D object and does the dimensional lifting **by hand**.

### R4 — the false positive, re-cleared
arXiv:2604.09949 (Shahmurov) is the only search hit asserting a machine-certified
singularity for 3D Navier–Stokes. Its own title — *"Stable Finite-Time Singularity
Formation for 3D Navier–Stokes **via 5D-Lifted Axisymmetric Reductions**"* — states a
symmetry reduction, so it fails the gate's "not a 1D/2D reduction" clause **even taken at
face value**, before any question of validity arises. Independently, this repository
already classified it `CLAIMED_UNUSABLE` (legs 90, 93: no verification package released;
the reconstructed exactly-backward self-similar solution is excluded by
Nečas–Růžička–Šverák; the author has since published six papers claiming the logically
opposite result in the same 5D lift, none citing it). Q10/Q11 re-confirm that no
independent confirmation, refutation or retraction has appeared.

## Part 3 — what this leg found that the EXT family could not have

The EXT legs (74, 77, 82, 90, 93, 123) asked "has anyone certified **this repository's
target object**." That question cannot see the following, which this broader pass
establishes:

1. **The 3D barrier is specifically about *time-dependent singularity formation*, not
   about three spatial variables per se.** Validated numerics **has** reached genuinely 3D
   objects: van den Berg & Williams, *Rigorously computing symmetric stationary states of
   the Ohta–Kawasaki problem in three dimensions*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 51(1):131–158
   (2019), gives *"the first existence proofs for both the double gyroid and body centered
   cubic packed sphere solutions"* in three space dimensions — achieved by *"preserving
   the relevant symmetries"* for *"an enormous reduction in computational cost."* So 3D CAP
   exists; it is **elliptic/stationary**, and it still buys its 3D-ness with symmetry.
   The honest form of Wall 2 must therefore say *singularity*, not *dimension*.
2. **The field names the obstruction in its own terms.** The high-dimensional CAP
   literature works by *"generat[ing] sharp **one-dimensional** analytic estimates which
   are then used to produce high-dimensional estimates"* (Lessard et al., "Efficient
   rigorous numerics for high-dimensional PDEs via one-dimensional estimates"), and the
   direct tensor-product route is *"deemed to be practically infeasible beyond a
   relatively small number of dimensions (three or four)"* — the curse of dimensionality,
   stated by the CAP community, not inferred by this repository.
3. **The pattern is universal, and it now has a name.** In every case located the shape is
   identical: **machine-certify a ≤2D object, then reach 3D by analysis.** Tucker's Lorenz
   proof (3D **ODE**) is the ancestor; Chen–Hou (2D Boussinesq profile → 3D axisymmetric
   Euler) and BCG → CGSS (1D ODE profile → 3D non-radial compressible Euler/NS) are the
   modern fluid instances. **No located work certifies a three-spatial-variable,
   time-dependent, singularity-forming object directly.**

> **Wall 2, sourced form.** No published work machine-certifies a singularity for an

> computer-assisted ingredient obtains its 3D-ness from a symmetry reduction or from an
