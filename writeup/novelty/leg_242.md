## Method: author LISTINGS first, keyword searches only as a third net

A follow-up with an unexpected title cannot be missed by a *listing* the way it can be missed by
a *keyword search*. So the candidate set is closed by enumerating both authors' complete arXiv
career listings entry by entry, cross-checking each against an independent net, and only then
running keyword queries to catch anything outside arXiv. Leg 240 established this shape; this
leg follows it and, as recorded below, **hit leg 240's exact failure mode on a new author line.**
