## 4. External prior art

The hazard classes here (absolute-vs-relative tolerance on unscaled input; NaN losing every
comparison so a stop criterion silently ceases to exist; `max()` swallowing NaN) are textbook
floating-point engineering, not research contributions — Goldberg 1991 and IEEE-754's
non-signalling quiet-NaN semantics cover all three. **Nothing in this leg is claimed as novel
against the literature**; the claim is a *reproduction and regression* claim about this
repository's own code, and it is capped there. This mirrors legs 86, 87 and 94, whose
post-repair closes were likewise recorded as internal verification, not as findings.
