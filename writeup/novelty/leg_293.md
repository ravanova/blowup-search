2. **Mention census.** A regex scan of `experiments/JOURNAL.md` for every occurrence of
   `leg[s]? N` (case-insensitive, word-boundary, `100 <= N <= 400`), recording every line

landed `experiments/journal/leg_N.md` file with **zero** occurrence of their own leg number
anywhere in `experiments/JOURNAL.md` — not a formal entry, not an incidental mention, nothing.
