**Honesty note on NRS, stated rather than papered over.** Four routes were tried and all four
returned HTML landing pages or 404s, not PDFs: `intlpress.com` fulltext path (404),
`link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF02551584.pdf` (HTML), Project Euclid `.full` (1161-byte
stub), Project Euclid `journalArticle/Download?urlId=…` (HTML). This matches
`LITERATURE_CHECK.md` l.197's standing record that NRS is unobtainable from this environment.
**What replaces it is not a paraphrase but a full-text primary restatement by a direct party:**
Tsai's 1998 paper (Šverák's student, acknowledged as such at l.1145–1146) restates NRS's theorem
and its hypothesis at `leray.txt` l.79–80 and re-derives NRS's decay rates at l.157–160. Every
NRS hypothesis banked by this leg carries **that** locator, and is labelled `restated_by_tsai`,
never `read_at_source`. Leg 251 gets the distinction, not a laundered claim.
