for *self-consistency* — does every row cite a real, actually-checked source, and does its

## 3. What would make this leg's result non-novel

If the audit comes back clean, the result is a *banked clean bill* and its novelty is
procedural, not mathematical — that is stated plainly rather than dressed up. If it comes
back dirty, the finding is novel only in the sense that it is the first executable check of
a claim-bearing ledger in this repo; the *content* of any drift may already be known
(leg 65's L599–600 note is exactly such a case, and is credited above rather than
re-discovered).
