directly from leg 302's already-banked `writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json`. No new

re-running of leg 302's runner (`experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1.py`) or its inlined term

The only thing produced is a rendering choice, exactly per the leg brief's framing: which

edited. The underlying claim the figure illustrates — leg 302's gate answering NO because
KA8's `k(1)` evaluation is ill-conditioned in IEEE double at r=1 while the transcription

is drawn analytically over a plotting grid rather than read as a pre-sampled array, because
the curated JSON stores this as a verified closed-form fact (probe KA2, residual 6.66e-16)
rather than as a stored curve of points. Evaluating an already-verified closed form at plot
resolution is not a new measurement; the endpoints of the drawn line are checked in-script
against the JSON's own banked window endpoints (self-check G5) so the plot cannot silently
drift from what leg 302 actually measured.
