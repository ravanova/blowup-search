## 6. Frozen in advance

**The classifier is leg 202's, taken verbatim, not re-derived.** `diagnostics()` and
`verdict()` are copied from `experiments/p2_route_pna_v1_adversarial.py` (lines 84–137 at
`b2a70b8`) with their thresholds unchanged: far-field inflation `> 1e2` against the anchor's
own far field with `relres < 1e-10`; gauge residual `> 1e-6`; `iterations >= 40` with
`relres > 1e-10` as SILENT_DEGRADED. Freezing them here means this leg cannot tune a threshold
to reach a preferred branch.

**`solver/profile_newton.py` is read-only and must be byte-identical to the merge base at the
end of this leg, under every gate branch.** This leg repairs nothing; that is leg 226's job.

**Pre-declared: the re-run will not be bit-identical to v11's banked JSON**, and that is not
itself a finding. Item (6) measured this module's stalled residuals moving 2.31 decades across
BLAS backends. A first probe here already shows it: cold `solve(a=0.5, n=401)` returns
`relres = 6.476482078299978e-08` against v11's banked `6.476603153401411e-08` — agreeing to 6
significant figures, on a *capped* (40-iteration) row. **So the leg must separate two effects
and report them apart:** (i) reproduction drift, which is expected and carries no claim, and
(ii) a verdict changing because a row is classified off-branch. Only (ii) can move a banked
number. Any "moves" claim that cannot be shown to survive (i) will be reported as
indeterminate, not as a move.

**Pre-declared expectations, so the result can disappoint them.** From the trace done before

`continuation`) covers far less of v11 than leg 202's "DIRECT AND MATERIAL" grading implies —
and this leg will report the wider `solve`-dependent set as well rather than hide behind the
narrow wording, because the gate's purpose is to say which banked numbers can be trusted.
