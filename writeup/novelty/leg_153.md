## 2. Is the *mathematical* content novel? No — it is Plemelj–Privalov, and this leg says so

The mechanism leg 119 found is that the sup-account envelope `cS → 2cos^α(inner/2) ≠ 0` as the
pair separation `dφ → 0`, so only the seminorm envelope `cT ~ dφ^γ → 0` tames the near-pole
endpoint — which needs `γ > 0`. That the conjugate-function / Cauchy singular integral operator
preserves Hölder classes exactly for `0 < γ < 1` and fails at the endpoint `γ = 0` is the
**Plemelj–Privalov theorem** — Plemelj for smooth curves, Privalov for the circle, which is
precisely this module's setting. Links, not counts:

* Karapetyants–Samko, [*The Plemelj–Privalov theorem for generalized Hölder classes*](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1070/SM1993v075n01ABEH003378), Sb. Math. **75** (1993) 1 — the modern generalized-Hölder statement.
* [*Boundedness of the Cauchy singular integral operator under Hölder-type conditions*](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47437) — the boundedness statement with its hypotheses written out.
* [*The Plemelj–Privalov theorem in variable exponent Clifford analysis*](https://www.academia.edu/23273816/The_Plemelj_Privalov_theorem_in_variable_exponent_Clifford_analysis) — variable-exponent extension.
* [*Characterizations of Lyapunov domains in terms of Riesz transforms and the Plemelj–Privalov theorem*](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19143) — **new since leg 130's pass**; a converse-direction characterization. Checked: it strengthens the hypothesis side, and changes nothing about the `γ > 0` endpoint.
* [*Finite-sheeted Cauchy operator at rational corners*](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12722) — **new since leg 130's pass**; corner geometry, not the endpoint exponent. Not applicable.

**No novelty is claimed for the `γ > 0` condition.** This leg's guard is an executable
enforcement of a classical hypothesis the module never checked, and the guard's docstring is
written to say exactly that. What the repo contributes is not the theorem but its enforcement at
the one call site where a violated hypothesis silently produced a finite, plausible number.

A second search — for a published *validated-numerics* treatment of a weighted-Hölder seminorm
bound on the conjugate operator with a domain guard of this kind — returned nothing on point
(the hits are generic validated-numerics and weighted-Hölder-space references:
[ICMS](https://icms.ac.uk/activities/workshop/validated-numerics-for-computer-assisted-proofs/),
[vncap](https://vncap.org/), [Schauder estimates for discrete PDEs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.13973)).
That absence is **not** claimed as novelty for this leg: the object is a repair, and its
novelty question is §1's, which answers *no*.

## 3. Is the failure mode novel in this repo? No — it is the second instance of the sibling's

Leg 106/130's `hilbert_pointwise.py` endpoint divergence is the same mechanism one dimension
down (a single-point pole rather than the two-point `E_N` pole). Nothing here is a new class of
bug and the journal will not present it as one.

One sub-family **does not** recur, and saying so is part of this pass. The repo's IEEE-754
unordered-NaN thread (legs 100/106/116/117/130) turns on a payer comparison `rho*cT <= cS`
being silently `False` for non-finite `rho`. **`hilbert_holder.py` has no `rho`** — its payer
rule is the bare `cT <= cS`. Non-finite `γ`/`α` still poison it, but by making `cT` itself `NaN`,
not by a ratio underflowing. The instance count for the IEEE-754 thread is therefore *not*
incremented by this leg.

## 4. What the pass CHANGED about the plan — the part that was not known before

Leg 119's escalation prescribes the repair as: *"a guard on `increment_pair_bound` and
`hilbert_holder_constant` — reject `gamma <= 0` (and non-finite `gamma`/`alpha`) … The soundness
predicate is exact: `gamma > 0`."* This pass **confirms that predicate** — unlike leg 130, whose
pass falsified its own predecessor's prescription — but it falsifies three *implementation*
assumptions around it, all measured before any code was touched.

**(a) Leg 130's guard cannot be transplanted verbatim: its `rho` clause is vacuous here.**
Leg 130's threshold-free predicate is *"the payer crossover `s* = (2cos^γ(θ/2)/ρ)^{1/γ}` is a
representable positive float"*, which is the clause that catches its `ρ = 1e300` violator. With
`ρ ≡ 1` the crossover is `s* = 2^{1/γ}·cos(inner/2) ≥ cos(inner/2) > 0`, and `2^{1/γ}` was
measured finite over `γ ∈ {1e-3 … 10}` (`1.072e301` at `γ = 1e-3`, `4` at `γ = 0.5`). The clause
can never fire. Transplanting it would ship a dead branch that no test could exercise —
lesson 90's tell, caught before it was written rather than after.

**(b) Leg 119's ε-drift *diagnostic* is NOT a usable predicate, and its reported clean gap does
not survive extending the γ ladder.** Leg 119 reports the diagnostic separating the regimes with
"a clear gap (max `1.34×` when sound — `gamma = 0.05` is the closest approach — vs. min `1.98×`
when not)". This pass re-ran leg 119's own sweep at its own pair (`θ₁ = 1.0, σ = 0.05,
α = 1.5`), reproducing its `γ = 0` column exactly (`16.7098 → 43.8938`, the same `16.71/43.89`
leg 119 tabulates), and then extended the ladder **below** `γ = 0.05`, which leg 119 did not:

| γ | −1.0 | −0.5 | −0.1 | 0.0 | 1e−6 | 1e−3 | 0.01 | 0.05 | 0.15 | 0.5 | 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `u(1e−16)/u(1e−6)` | 2.633 | 2.634 | 2.776 | 2.627 | **2.382** | **2.359** | **2.168** | 1.606 | 1.129 | 1.001 | 1.000 |

The bolded columns are **sound** (`γ > 0`, Plemelj–Privalov applies, the head provably vanishes)
and they sit **above** leg 119's "min `1.98×` when not sound". The gap leg 119 reported is an
artifact of its ladder's floor at `γ = 0.05`; the drift is continuous through `γ = 0⁺`, as it
must be, since `s0^γ/γ → ∞` as `γ → 0⁺`. **Adopting the diagnostic as the guard's predicate would
reject sound configurations** and break gate (b). This is a correction to leg 119's *diagnostic*
commentary, not to its verdict — its verdict rests on the exact predicate, which stands.

**(c) The right predicate is exact, threshold-free, and simpler than the sibling's.** The
charged envelope is `min(cS, cT) ≤ cT` **always** (the payer rule takes the smaller), and
`|cot(t/2)| ≤ 2/|t|` on each one-sided near piece, so the head the `E_N` quadrature discards
over `(0, s0]`, `s0 = ε·lo`, is bounded in closed form by

    head ≤ Σ_pieces 2 · cmax^(α−γ) · s0^γ / γ ,

which **vanishes as ε → 0 if and only if `γ > 0`**. No `ρ` clause, no separate head-vanishing
clause: the predicate *is* `γ > 0` (plus finiteness of the arguments and `ε ∈ (0,1)`). Because
the split of the head between the `S` and `T` coefficients is unknown, the `"inflate"` policy
must add the head to **both** coefficients — valid since `h_S S + h_T T ≤ head·(S + T)`.

**(d) A configuration that must be WARNED, not rejected — and it is the production optimum.**
Not in leg 119's escalation, and it decides a free constant. With the closed-form head above,
measured over the shipped pair grid at default `ε = 1e-10`:

| configuration | worst head / returned value, over 481 pairs |
|---|---|
| shipped `(α, γ) = (1.5, 0.5)` | **1.393e−05** (at `θ = 3.0688`, `σ = −1.19`) |
| production `(α, γ) = (1.4, 0.15)` | **5.080e−02** (at `θ = 3.1305`, `σ = −1.19`) |

The production optimum's exact majorant is **finite** (`γ = 0.15 > 0`), so this is
**under-refinement, not divergence** — rejecting it would be a claim about a sound object, and
leg 119 separately measured true/bound `0.1081` there, a ~9× margin the 5% head does not close.
It must therefore be closed by a warning that **changes no returned value**, which is exactly
what keeps gate (b) true. A `HEAD_WARN_TOL = 1e-3` sits `72×` above the worst shipped
configuration and `51×` below the worst production one — both margins measured, both going in
the JSON. This is the leg's one free constant; it governs a warning only, never a rejection and
never a number.
