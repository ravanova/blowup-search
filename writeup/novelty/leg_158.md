## FINDING 1 (Q1) — the even/odd regrouping is CLASSICAL and IS NOT CLAIMED

Eliminating odd-indexed unknowns from a tridiagonal system and regrouping into 2×2 blocks is
**cyclic reduction / odd–even reduction**, and it is explicitly a *permuted block Gaussian
elimination*, i.e. red–black ordering:

- Gander–Golub, *Cyclic Reduction — History and Applications*,
  [people.inf.ethz.ch/gander/papers/cyclic.pdf](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/gander/papers/cyclic.pdf)
  — the history; the first parallel tridiagonal algorithm.
- [Cyclic reduction (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_reduction) — states the
  equivalence to block Gaussian elimination on the permuted system `(PAP^T)(Pu) = Pf` with `P`
  a red–black ordering.
- Heller, *A Cyclic Reduction Algorithm for Solving Block Tridiagonal Systems of Arbitrary
  Dimension*, [SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 14 (1977)](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0714048).
- [Cyclic Reduction overview, ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/computer-science/cyclic-reduction).

**Consequence for this leg.** The regrouping itself is 50-year-old numerical linear algebra and
is claimed by nobody here. What is *not* in these sources is any statement about what the
regrouping does to a **radii-polynomial dominance hypothesis on an infinite-dimensional
weighted sequence space** — the stability results quoted are for symmetric positive definite or
**diagonally dominant** tridiagonal systems, which is the hypothesis at issue. So this leg may
report only the **measured value of the block dominance ratio for this repository's operator**,
and must present the regrouping as classical.

## FINDING 2 (Q2) — no follow-up covering a vanishing diagonal was located

Searches for extensions/citations relaxing BDL's assumptions returned the authors' own pages
and a survey but no follow-up on a vanishing diagonal:

- [Maxime Breden CV](https://sites.google.com/site/maximebreden/CV);
  [BIRS talk, "Rigorous numerics for a tridiagonal dominant operator"](https://www.birs.ca/events/2014/5-day-workshops/14w5098/videos/watch/201409221514-Breden.html)
  (the talk predates the preprint; same construction).
- Survey of current practice: [arXiv:2502.20644](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.20644) (fully
  spectral Fourier–Chebyshev rigorous integration) — no tridiagonal-dominance relaxation.
- Adjacent method sources re-checked and unchanged from legs 57/127:
  [arXiv:2302.12877](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.12877),
  [arXiv:2403.10450](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10450),
  [arXiv:2505.03091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03091) (Cadiot — the *stronger* reason the
  leg-51 ban stays up; its standing hypothesis is a Fourier multiplier bounded below).

**Consequence.** The gap leg 57 found is still open in the literature. This leg does not get to
claim the gap is novel — leg 57 banked it — only to report what the source's own construction
does inside it.

## FINDING 3 (Q3) — BDL's rank-one tail-feedback correction is THEIRS, and is quoted, not claimed

The `K = D - β_{m-1} λ_m · (w_I)_0 · e_{m-1} e_{m-1}^T` correction (BDL p. 6, unnumbered
display before (17)) is a Schur complement of the infinite tail onto the finite block,
condensed into **one scalar**. Low-rank/Schur corrections to approximate inverses are of course
a large literature
([Saad et al., hierarchical low-rank Schur complement preconditioner](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~saad/PDF/ys-2017-02.pdf),
[arXiv:1505.04341](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.04341)), so the *shape* is not novel either;
BDL's specific closed form for a tridiagonal tail is theirs and is quoted verbatim with its
page/equation reference wherever this leg uses it.

## FINDING 4 (incidental, and it is the reason the leg is worth running)

Both BDL's **introduction** (§1, p. 1) and its **conclusion** (§5, p. 25) name *differentiation
in frequency space of the Hermite functions* as the motivating tridiagonal operator. In the
normalized-Hermite basis that operator's recurrence is
`ψ_n' = sqrt(n/2)·ψ_{n-1} − sqrt((n+1)/2)·ψ_{n+1}`, i.e. **its diagonal is identically zero** —
the class assumption (4) excludes. Corroborating statement of the same structural fact:
[Hermite Spectral Analysis](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/hermite-spectral-analysis)
("the Hermite function differentiation matrix … has zero diagonal entries"). This is an
observation *about* the source, quotable verbatim, not a new mathematical claim.
