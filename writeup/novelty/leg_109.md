# Leg 109 — Route-RCA novelty pass (run and committed BEFORE construction)

**Date:** 2026-08-06. **Branch:** `leg/109-rca-v1`.
**Verdict: `PROCEED_AS_ROBUSTNESS_AUDIT`.**

This leg builds no new mathematics, sharpens no bound, and claims no novel mechanism. It
audits one *software* property of `solver/reduced_certificate.py`: whether the module can
report **internal self-consistency on a case that is not actually self-consistent**, under a
battery of degenerate or NaN-poisoned inputs. `solver/reduced_certificate.py` is **READ-ONLY**
under this leg, under either branch of the gate.

**Explicit non-claim, stated first.** Nothing here reopens the Route-D v16 rehearsal the
module carries (`writeup/data/p2_route_d_v16_rehearsal.json`), nothing re-measures `Y_0`,
`Z_0`, the `a <= 1/2` threshold on `sup|N''|`, the step adversary's `+0.499` slope, or the
Hölder threshold `gamma >~ 0.35`. The plan-of-record ban *"another Route-D bound-sharpening
leg"* is respected literally: **no bound is sharpened, no constant is improved, no new solver
is built.** The battery calls the module's own public entry points on hand-built adversarial
inputs and compares the module's *reported* self-consistency against an independent
recomputation. The question is arithmetic and reporting hygiene of one module, not the
viability of the certificate it rehearses.

## 1. What the claim is, and where the hazards live

`capabilities.py:354-357` validates this module as *"internally self-consistent across the
reduced space; explicitly NOT a proof — float64 throughout."* The module's single assembled
entry point is `rehearsal(a, K, Xc0)`, which returns a dict carrying `converged`,
`Y0_interpolant_defect`, `Z0`, `N2_sup_by_cutoff`, `N2_finite`, `Z1: None` and a prose
`verdict`. "Self-consistency" is therefore a property of *that dict*: the numbers it reports
and the sentence it reports alongside them must agree.

Five places in the implementation are where that agreement could silently fail, and they are
the five the battery targets. All five are declared here, before construction.

| # | site | the shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | `rehearsal`, lines 230-237 | the `verdict` is a **hardcoded string literal**, identical for every input. It asserts *"Y_0 is at machine level and Z_0 is roundoff"* without ever reading `Y0_interpolant_defect` or `Z0` from the dict it is being placed into. Nothing in the function can make that sentence come out differently — **lesson 90's exact shape**, applied to a claim rather than to a control. |
| **H2** | `rehearsal`, line 220 | `finite = bool(rp.p >= 2.0 and max(npp)/min(npp) < 1.01)` is a *relative* flatness test over **three** cutoffs. A ratio is undefined at `min(npp) = 0` and is `nan` under NaN poisoning — and `nan < 1.01` is **False** (IEEE-754 §5.11), so a poisoned case reports `N2_finite = False`, which is the *same* value a genuinely divergent case reports. A False here does not distinguish "measured divergent" from "the measurement failed". |
| **H3** | `interpolant_defect`, line 102 | `Y_0` is a **sup over a function**, estimated by `np.max` over a finite grid whose size `n=997` is a default argument the caller may change. A sup estimated by sampling is unbounded below by the sample: nothing in the function certifies that the grid resolves the residual, and the docstring's own defence ("coprime to the node count") is a property of the *default*, not of the argument. |
| **H4** | `z0_defect`, lines 107-109 | `A = np.linalg.inv(M)` on a Jacobian that is never conditioned-checked, and the returned quantity is `‖A M − I‖` — the **left** residual. `np.linalg.inv` solves `M X = I`, so the classical backward-stability bound (Higham, *ASNA* ch. 14) covers the **right** residual `‖M A − I‖`. The two are not interchangeable for an ill-conditioned `M`, and the certificate needs the one that matches how `A` is used. |
| **H5** | `step_adversary`, lines 148-167 | `kind` is dispatched as `if kind == "single": ... else: <the adversary>`. **Any** unrecognised string — a typo, a stale name — silently returns the adversary rather than the control. The naive probe is the module's designated control (docstring: *"kept as the CONTROL"*), and a control reachable only by exact string match is a control that can be silently switched off. Rider: both `step_adversary` and `holder_ratio` divide by a sup (`np.max(np.abs(de_v))`, line 167; `nrm`, line 198) that nothing gates — **lesson 67**. |

Two further input axes are in scope because the gate names them: degenerate `a` (the guard in
`ReducedProfile.__init__` is `if not 0.0 < float(a)`, which **admits `a = inf`** — `0.0 < inf`
is true — giving `p = 1/a = 0.0` and a nonlinearity `e^0`), and poisoned `b` fed directly to
the five public functions that take it.

## 5. External prior art

The hazard class is textbook and no originality is claimed for it: a status string decoupled

# FINDINGS (appended after the run; the sections above are as committed BEFORE construction)

**Gate answer: YES — three silent-corruption sites, of the five hazards declared above.
ESCALATED, NOT PATCHED.** `solver/reduced_certificate.py` is byte-identical to
`origin/main` (0 lines changed). Full narrative in `experiments/journal/leg_109.md`, every

**The finding in one sentence.** `rehearsal` builds its `verdict` as a hardcoded string
literal that asserts "Y_0 is at machine level and Z_0 is roundoff" without ever reading
either field — so the same sentence ships over a `Y_0` of `1.23e-12` and a `Y_0` of
`1.55e-2`, and two of the three rows already banked in
`writeup/data/p2_route_d_v16_rehearsal.json` carry it over numbers that contradict it.

**The scope boundary, which is the second result.** Everything the module *computes* is
sound: poison propagates in 24 of 24 probes, degenerate `a` is caught by `converged` in 4
of 4, `z0_defect` reports the conservative side of the inversion residual by up to 278×,
and the default `Y_0` grid is honest to under 5%. **No numeric field in any banked JSON is
wrong.** The gap is confined to what the module *says about* what it computed — and to one
NaN-absorption path (`p == 2` exactly) that no call site reaches. That is why this is
escalated as a latent reporting defect rather than as a contaminated result, and why the
recommended repair (journal, §"Recommended repair") is three changes, none in the Newton
path, none of which moves a number on a clean call.

**Lesson 90 twice over.** The verdict string and the three-cutoff ladder are both quantities
that cannot come out differently: one sentence over 15 cases, and ten identical
`1.000000`s. Both were presented as evidence. Each probe that reports them here carries a
positive control that *does* come out differently (`a = 0.3, K = 96` for the verdict;
`a = 0.3` poisons for the ladder), which is what makes them findings rather than artifacts.

**What was NOT claimed, as pre-committed in §6:** nothing about the Route-D v16 rehearsal's
correctness or sharpness; no new `Y_0`/`Z_0`/`a*`/`sup|N''|` threshold/adversary slope/
Hölder threshold; nothing about `Z_1`; no claim that the certificate closes or fails to
close for any new reason; and no patch. Nothing in this leg touches a link of the L1→L4
chain.

**Where the pre-commit was WRONG, recorded rather than quietly corrected.** §1's H2 row
predicted the failure direction as a *false negative* — "a poisoned case reports
`N2_finite = False`, the same value a genuinely divergent case reports". That prediction is
correct for `p != 2` (measured: all-NaN and all-inf both → `False` at `a = 0.3`) but the
actual corruption runs the **other** way and only at `p == 2` exactly, where `nan ** 0.0`
is `1.0` and the flag reports **`True`** on garbage. The declared hazard found the right
line of code for the wrong reason. Likewise §1 predicted H3 and H4 as live corruption
sites; both came back clean, at 2.9–4.7% and 9.6–277.7× respectively.
