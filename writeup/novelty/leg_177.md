## 3. The two verbatim items from Xu that define the realization

Fetched from [arXiv:2607.19762v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19762v1) this leg (Q3):

**(a) the space, eq. (3.2) —**

> `X = {φ : φ odd, φ, φ″ ∈ L²(0,∞), φ(y) = a₁y + o(y) as y → 0 (a₁ ∈ ℂ)}`,
> with `‖φ‖²_X = ‖φ‖²_{L²} + ‖φ″‖²_{L²}`

Two things follow and both matter. The norm has **no `H¹` term** — it is `L²` plus the second
derivative. And `L₀` is *"a densely defined closed operator on `X`"*: **domain and codomain are
the same space.** So the norm pair is symmetric, exactly as leg 46's weighted-sup pair is. Leg
163's obstruction **O4** independently forbids the asymmetric alternative — Xu §4.6: *"One
cannot use the `H²` metric to empty the strip and the `L²` metric to close the origin
channel."*

**(b) §3.2, on discretizations —**

> "The persistent essential smear that the grids without an origin condition place inside the
> strip is the faithful spectrum of the maximal (weak) realization, while **discretizations
> that enforce origin regularity render the origin-`H²` line**."

This is the sentence that makes a *collocation* origin-`H²` realization a published idea rather
than an invention. Its first half is already banked (`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEJ_V1.md` §7.1); **its
second half — that a discretization CAN render the tight realization — is used here and is
Xu's, not this leg's.**

## 4. The one external constraint that could have killed this leg, engaged before building

Xu §8, banked by leg 171:

> "a **weight is a change of norm, eigenvalues are norm-invariant**, and the truncated `L_a`
> genuinely carries essential-smear eigenvalues inside the strip … so no weighted enclosure can
> exclude them."

Taken naively this reads as: *changing the norm on a fixed grid cannot change anything.* If
that were the whole story this leg would be a tautology and would be **withdrawn, not shipped**.
Two separations are pre-registered here, before any number exists, and the second one is a
falsifiable control:

1. **Xu's invariance statement is about EIGENVALUES. The quantities this leg moves are not
   eigenvalues.** `‖A‖`, `Z₁`, `Z₂`, `Y₀` and the budget are **norm-dependent by
   construction** — an induced operator norm is not a spectral invariant, and leg 46's `‖A‖_w`
   is a weighted **row-sum** (the `ℓ^∞_w`-induced norm), which for a dense `N×N` matrix can
   exceed the `2`-norm by up to `√N`. Nothing in Xu forbids that from moving. The consistency
   budget `τ = budget/‖A‖` is built entirely from norm-dependent constants.
2. **A Sobolev norm is not a weight, and that distinction is the mechanism.** A weight
   `w(y)|φ|²` penalizes *location*; `‖φ″‖²_{L²}` penalizes *curvature*. Xu's singular family
   `u_λ(y) = y^{1−λ}/(y + i/2)²` is in `L²` and fails `u″ ∈ L²` at the origin — location cannot
   separate it from the physical modes, curvature can. **On a grid every function is smooth,
   so the separation must show up as a norm ratio, not as a membership test.** That is
   control **C3** below and it can come out either way.

**Construction (new, in the declared runner — no `solver/` module is added or edited).** A
discrete surrogate `X_h` for Xu's `X`:

```
‖z‖²_{X_h} = Σ_i q_i (Ω_i² + (D²Ω)_i²)  +  Σ_i q_i (V_i² + (D²V)_i²)  +  scalar block
```

with `q` the trapezoid weights of the non-uniform grid and `D² = D·D`; plus an **origin
projector** onto the enforceable half of `φ = a₁y + o(y)` on a grid that has a node at `X = 0`.

**Four arms, one of which must reproduce and three of which must be able to disagree:**

| arm | norm | origin condition | role |

**Measured per arm per rung:** `‖A‖`, `B`, `Z₁`, `Y₀`, `budget = (1−Z₁)²/(2Z₂)`,
`τ = budget/‖A‖`, `‖defect_H‖`, `‖defect_D‖`, the ratio `defect/τ`, its measured order across

- **P6** the diagnostic `defect/τ` in `X_h` improves on the sup realization's `1.8537e+07` by
