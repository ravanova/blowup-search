# Leg 393 — `T4` novelty record

**No novelty pass is owed for this unit.** The dispatch brief waived it explicitly. This file exists
so that the waiver is on the record rather than being an omission, and so that the external-source
evidence this leg *did* gather is banked in the place a novelty pass would have put it.

## Why no novelty pass is owed

The unit was a **reproduction**, not a construction. Its entire object was somebody else's published
theorem:

> J.B. van den Berg, M. Breden, J.-P. Lessard, L. van Veen,
> *Spontaneous periodic orbits in the Navier–Stokes flow*, `arXiv:1902.00384` (2019-02-01).

Reproducing a 2019 result is by construction **not** new mathematics (§3h rule 2; pre-committed
reading (a), discharged in `experiments/journal/leg_393.md` §13). There is no claim here whose
priority could be contested, so there is nothing for a novelty pass to search against.

The leg's two *findings* are likewise not mathematical novelty. They are **internal corrections**:

1. **`arXiv:1902.00384` is certified by the banned apparatus**, so ruling C1's scope does not reach
   it and leg 348's abstract-level classification is wrong at full text. This is a statement about
   *this programme's own ruling*, not about the literature. The paper says what its apparatus is, in
   its own abstract, and has said so since 2019.
2. **Both certified rows are 2D lifts** (`N_{x₃} = 0`). The paper states this itself, in Theorem
   1.1's own preamble. Nothing is discovered; something is *read correctly for the first time here*.

Neither is a candidate for a priority claim. Both are corrections to internal documents, and acting
on them is the Conductor's call.

## Prior art actually consulted, and how it was used

* **`arXiv:1902.00384`** — the target. Read at full text (the read surface was authorised). Its
  Theorem 2.15 / 4.23, eqns (2.17)–(2.21) and (4.28)–(4.34), Table 1, Theorems 5.1/5.2/1.1 and
  Lemma 6.2 are the source of every constant reproduced.
* **The authors' package** (`determineradii.m`, `readme.m`, `saveddata/{data,extra}orbit{1,2}.mat`)
  — the source of the `Y₀,Z₀,Z₁,Z₂` bounds, the `solshape.Nrec` truncations, the success flags, the
  `significantceil` rounding convention, and the vorticity/velocity arrays measured in §11 of the
  journal.
* **Leg 316** (Dähne–Figueras) — *methodological* precedent, not mathematical: it established that
  reproducing published constants by documentary extraction plus arithmetic is a C1-clear activity,
  because it builds no operator. Leg 393's apparatus is the same shape and cites it in its
  pre-registration (§2.1).
* **Leg 348** — the abstract-level classification this leg corrects.

## Standing negative

`capabilities.py` was grepped before anything was built (`galerkin`, `tail`, `zgliczy`,
`periodic orbit`, `navier`, `torus`, `interval`). **Nothing in this repository decodes a MATLAB
`.mat` Fourier-coefficient array or evaluates a weighted-`ℓ¹` norm of published Navier–Stokes
vorticity data.** The two files this leg added do so. That is a small **capability**, not a result,
and it is not offered as one.
