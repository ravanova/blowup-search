## 1. The load-bearing prior art is already IN this repository

Leg 255's novelty pass (`writeup/novelty/leg_255.md:64-69`), quoting Breden–Chu (`arXiv:2404.04054`
Remark 40, line 1687, md5 `ff7a34b776bfe5edf97397e5eabdbb7a`, located by leg 245) verbatim on what
the reach does **not** cover:

> * nonlocal operators in the nonlinearity — Hilbert transform, Biot–Savart, **Leray projection**
>   (the pressure), Fourier restriction, averaging;
> * nonlocal dissipation — `(-Δ)^α`, `Λ^γ` for non-even `γ`;

**This is the whole leg, stated before construction.** All three of this leg's target models
carry at least one item off that list: `u_x = H(omega)` (Hilbert transform) in
`fractional_gclm.py` / `critical_dissipation.py`, `u = grad^perp (-Delta)^{-1} omega`
(Biot–Savart-type inversion) in `fractional_boussinesq.py`, and `nu Lambda^{2s} omega` /
`nu (-Delta)^s omega` (fractional dissipation) in all three whenever `s != 1`. Leg 261 measured
the incompressible-fluid instance of this same clause (Leray projection, 18/18 fluid rows); this
leg measures whether the *same clause*, for its *other* named exclusions, bites equally on models
that are not incompressible at all — i.e., whether leg 261's finding was about incompressibility
specifically or about nonlocality in general.

   self-similar blowup profiles for the Okamoto–Sakajo–Wunsch (`a`) family, `a ≤ 1`, and a

   not a computer-assisted enclosure** — no `H²(μ)`, no Newton–Kantorovich, no interval
   arithmetic. Consistent with leg 255/261's finding that Breden–Chu's own machinery has never
   been ported past its stated local-polynomial reach.

   fractionally-dissipative-Boussinesq self-similar profile in Breden–Chu's or any adjacent
   framework. **Ground reads vacated, as the calibration predicted** — not because nobody is
   working the models (Route-F/G/H's own solver files measure them numerically), but because
   nobody has tried to CERTIFY one this way.
