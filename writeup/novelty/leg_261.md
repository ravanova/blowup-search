## §3 — The decisive prior art, quoted, with an md5-pinned extraction

**Source.** Thierry Gallay, *Existence and stability of viscous vortices*, Handbook of Mathematical
Analysis in Mechanics of Viscous Fluids.
URL `https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~gallay/Handbook.pdf`.
**PDF md5 `da65cc05fbae665fbfe3a47837c94b27`**, extraction command
`pdftotext -layout`, **1760 lines**. All line numbers below are into that extraction.

**Line 325–338, verbatim** — the weight, and the space:

> Given `m ∈ [0, ∞]`, let `ρ_m : [0, ∞) → [1, ∞)` be the weight function defined by
> `ρ_m(r) = 1 if m = 0`; `(1 + r/4)^m if 0 < m < ∞`; **`e^{r/4} if m = ∞`** … (2.8)
> Perturbations will be taken in the weighted `L²` space
> `L²(m) = { w ∈ L²(ℝ²) : ‖w‖_{L²(m)}² = ∫_{ℝ²} ρ_m(|ξ|²) |w(ξ)|² dξ < ∞ }` (2.9)

`ρ_∞(|ξ|²) = e^{|ξ|²/4}`. **That is Breden–Chu's Gaussian weight, exactly** — the same `e^{|x|²/4}`
that leg 257 measured `P[(U·∇)U]` failing to be square-integrable against.

**Line 346, verbatim:** "Elements of `L²(m)` are square integrable functions with algebraic decay at
infinity if `0 < m < ∞`, and **Gaussian decay if `m = ∞`**."

**And the object in that space is the VORTICITY, with the velocity recovered separately.**
Line 1232, verbatim: "It is not difficult to show that `L_λ` generates a `C₀`-semigroup in the
polynomially weighted space `L²(m)` for `m < ∞`, and an analytic semigroup in the **Gaussian
weighted space `L²(∞; λ)`**." The system it belongs to, line ~1220, is
`∂_t w − (L_λ − Λ^{ω_{λ,α}})w + (v,∇)w = 0`, **`v = K_{2D} ∗ w`** — the velocity is the
Biot–Savart convolution of the unknown, and is **never asked to lie in `L²(∞)`**.

Line 37–41, verbatim, for the 3D law this leg's witnesses use:

> The Biot-Savart law is a reconstruction formula that expresses `u` in terms of `ω` … In the whole
> space `ℝ³`, if the vorticity distribution is sufficiently localized, the Biot-Savart formula reads
> `u(x,t) = −(1/4π) ∫_{ℝ³} ((x−y) ∧ ω(y,t)) / |x−y|³ dy`.

### What this does to this leg's claim — stated so it cannot be over-read

**It removes any novelty claim from the mathematics and replaces it with something more useful: an
authoritative external witness that the Gaussian-weighted space is the STANDARD space for the
vorticity and has never been the space for the velocity.** Gallay and Wayne did not stumble into
the vorticity formulation; **it is the formulation the Gaussian weight admits.** Leg 257 measured
one half of that (velocity-pressure form fails); the literature has been living on the other half
for two decades.

**This is prior art AGAINST leg 257's third stated repair-failure, and this leg must say so.**
Leg 257's `why_the_two_obvious_repairs_fail[2]` reads: *"VORTICITY formulation, to remove the
pressure — Biot-Savart (u = curl Laplacian^{-1} omega) is nonlocal with the same algebraic tail, so
the obstruction moves rather than lifts."* That inference is **not supported by the decay
arithmetic**, and this leg's Part B measures it rather than arguing it: the velocity's algebraic
tail is real, but in the vorticity equation the velocity appears **only inside products with a
Gaussian factor** (`(u·∇)ω`, `(ω·∇)u`), so the *product* is Gaussian. Whether the obstruction
moves or lifts is a computation, and leg 257 did not run it on this formulation. **This does not
touch leg 257's actual measurement, which stands unchallenged; it touches one sentence of
extrapolation in its repair list.** Leg 255's and leg 257's kills of `KS-NS-LI-ZHOU` are **not**
reopened by this and this leg does not reopen them.
