## Q2 — forward citations of 2505.03091 (external, citation graph)

`https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/arXiv:2505.03091/citations` — **2 citing

* **arXiv:2509.16693** — *Existence and orbital stability proofs of traveling wave
  solutions on an infinite strip for the suspension bridge equation* (2025), DOI
  10.1016/j.physd.2026.135240.

Both were grepped for `vanish`, `lmin`, `l(ξ)`, `Assumption 1`. **Neither relaxes the
non-vanishing-symbol hypothesis.** 2509.16693 carries a symbol
`l(ξ) = |2πξ|⁴ − c²(2πξ₁)² + 1` and works with `sup_ξ 1/l(ξ)`, i.e. it *uses* invertibility
of the symbol rather than weakening it. The only occurrences of the word "vanish" in either
paper are about the *solution* vanishing at infinity or at the domain edge, never the
symbol. Recorded as **restating, not relaxing** — the same classification leg 62 reached
for its three.

Leg 62's three forward citations (`BCF`, `VDAC`, `BH`) and these two are disjoint sets; the
citation index is evidently partial in both directions. Reported as a limitation, not
papered over.
