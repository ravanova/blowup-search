## 1. Reading the theory (fetched live, not from memory)

Fetched `www.netlib.org/lapack/lug/node91.html` through `node93.html` directly (the LAPACK Users'
Guide's "Error Bounds for the Nonsymmetric Eigenproblem" chapter) rather than relying on a
remembered paraphrase, and extracted the embedded equation images' ALT text verbatim:

- Backward stability: `||E|| <= p(n) eps ||A||` for the QR-algorithm family (Table numbers not
  quoted from memory — read directly from the fetched page).
- Table 4.5 (asymptotic, simple eigenvalue): `|lambda_hat_i - lambda_i| <~ ||E||_2 / s_i`,
  explicitly qualified "true for sufficiently small `||E||`, which is why it is called
  asymptotic."
- Table 4.6 (global/guaranteed, eigenvalue cluster — a simple eigenvalue is the size-1 case):
  `|lambda_hat_I - lambda_I| <= 2 ||E||_2 / s_I`, but *only* under the stated precondition
  `||E||_F < s_i * sep_i / 4`.
- An always-true disk theorem (no smallness precondition): every computed eigenvalue lies in a
  disk of radius `n*||E||_2/s_i` around the corresponding true eigenvalue.

`s_i` is LAPACK's `RCONDE`, the reciprocal Wilkinson eigenvalue condition number
(`s_i = |y_i^H x_i|` for unit-normalized left/right eigenvectors). None of these bounds are usable
without `s_i`, and this solver's call site (`solver/rescaled_spectrum.py:333`,
`np.linalg.eigvals`) never computes it.

## 2. Measurement (this leg's own, read-only, against the actual matrix)

Read-only import of `solver.rescaled_spectrum` at `a=0`, `K in {48,96,144,192}` (same four
resolutions leg 367 used, so the comparison is apples-to-apples). Computed `s_i` directly via a
global least-distance greedy pairing of `eig(A)`'s and `eig(A^T)`'s eigenvectors (not a per-index
nearest-value match, which is fragile under near-degenerate clusters) — full method and numbers in
`writeup/data/p2_route_bse_v1.json`. Result: the specific non-structural eigenvalue with the
largest `|Re(lambda)|` at each `K` has `s_i ~ 1.0e-15` to `1.6e-15` (Wilkinson condition number
`1/s_i ~ 6.2e14` to `9.5e14`) — severely ill-conditioned as an individual eigenvalue, even though
its nearest-neighbor spectral gap (`sep` proxy) is `O(1)`, i.e. not itself near-degenerate in the
elementary sense; the ill-conditioning is a genuine non-normality effect, not a trivial spectral-
collision artifact.

## 3. Classification: GAP-NAMED

Using a generous backward-error estimate `||E||_2 ~ 10*K*eps*||A||_2` (the loosest common
rule-of-thumb constant, chosen to be maximally favorable to the theory fitting): Table 4.6's own
applicability precondition `||E||_F < s_i*sep_i/4` is violated by `1.8e4` to `7.4e4`
(four to five orders of magnitude) at every one of leg 367's four `K` values, so the tight,
asymptotic-style bound is not licensed to apply at all here. The bound that *is* unconditionally
licensed — the disk theorem — evaluates to `1.4e5` (K=48) through `1.4e7` (K=192): true, but
`11`-`13` orders of magnitude above leg 356's `1e-6` threshold and `18.8`-`19.7` orders of
magnitude above leg 367's measured floor. Standard non-normal dense-eigensolver backward-error
theory, honestly evaluated against this solver's own matrix with LAPACK's own quoted theorems,
neither derives nor is even informatively consistent with the tightness of the observed
`K*machine_epsilon` floor — it is either inapplicable or vacuous. Full numeric table and the exact
resisting-step statement are in `writeup/data/p2_route_bse_v1.json`.
