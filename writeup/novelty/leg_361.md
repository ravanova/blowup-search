## 2. Method: how the sibling audit avoided over-claiming

Rather than pattern-matching plausible-looking token names by inspection alone, every candidate
was checked against the ACTUAL leaf names of all ten CENSUS families (flattened banked JSON,
`is_volatile()` tested per leaf, then hand-verified against the real semantic meaning of the
field before deciding to add a token). This caught and rejected several regex false positives
(`novelty_pass_commit`, `gate_answer.pre_committed_wording`, `degenerate_ball` matching "gener"
as a coincidental substring of "degenerate") that would have been wrongly "fixed" by
pattern-matching alone. Only `"seconds"` survived as a genuine, measured gap.
