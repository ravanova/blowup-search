## Is leg 235's prescribed predicate adequate?

Leg 235 named the defect precisely — the headline is built from
`newton_weighted_defect_min` while `newton_weighted_defect_max` is never
compared. Adopting *only* "swap min for max" would be adequate for the number
but not for the record, for one reason measured here: the corrected margin is
`0.016`, i.e. **below 1**, and a bare scalar below 1 does not say *which* row
misses, *by how much*, or that the other 10 rows hold. So the repair adopts the
min→max swap **plus** an enumeration (`rows_over_budget`, `worst_violation_x`,
`margin_over_rows_within_budget`, `budget_holds_uniformly`). Stated as an
extension of leg 235's predicate, not as a correction of it.

**Why not a min/max combination.** `v5_budget`'s claim is universally quantified
over the declared `a`-range (`a_range_used`, and the block's own prose "for
a < a*"). A universal budget is certified by its worst case; any average of the
two ends — mean, geometric mean, median — still admits a single over-budget row
and so certifies nothing. The worst case is the only defensible selection, and
the min is retained under an explicit name (`margin_at_best_row`) rather than
deleted, because it is a real and interesting diagnostic once it is not
masquerading as the headline.
