### 3d. Located prior art — LINKS, not counts (leg 174's rule)

1. **arXiv:2305.08221** — J.B. van den Berg, M. Breden, R. Sheombarsing, *Validated integration of
   semilinear parabolic PDEs* (2023). <https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08221>
   **The most important hit.** Computer-assisted rigorous **time** integration for scalar
   semilinear **parabolic** PDEs: Fourier-space variation-of-constants, Chebyshev in time, domain
   decomposition, **Newton–Kantorovich**. Applied to Fisher, Swift–Hohenberg, Ohta–Kawasaki,
   Kuramoto–Sivashinsky. **Breden is one of the four author groups this repo already censused.**
   *Load-bearing for honesty:* this proves NK **can** do time-dependent enclosures, so the naive
   claim "NK cannot reach time-dependent objects" is **FALSE and is not made in this leg**. Its
   reach is bounded by *semilinear parabolic* — a sectorial linear part with a smoothing semigroup —
   which BCG's quasilinear hyperbolic compressible system does not have.
2. **arXiv:2208.09445** — Buckmaster, Cao-Labora, Gómez-Serrano, *Smooth imploding solutions for 3D
   compressible fluids*, Forum of Math Pi **13** (2025) e6. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09445>
   The leg-251 target itself. Abstract confirms: smooth self-similar implosions for 3D isentropic
   Euler at every `γ>1`, **a sequence of solutions in the diatomic case `γ=7/5`**, plus "streamlined
   linear and nonlinear stability arguments extending to Navier–Stokes with density-independent
   viscosity". Listing records **"16500 lines of code"** — computer-assisted, and consistent with
   leg 285's measured `Taylor model: 0`, `interval ODE: 0`.
3. **math/0005247** — Zgliczyński & Mischaikow, *Rigorous Numerics for PDEs: the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky
   equation* (2000). <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0005247> The **self-consistent a-priori bounds**
   framework that rigorously justifies Galerkin truncation for **dissipative** PDEs. Old prior art,
   exactly as the calibration predicted. Directly bounds the cost class in §4: this is the standard
