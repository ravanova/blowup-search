### 2a. The two salvage branches, read in full and assessed

Both were fetched and read before this log was written. `origin/verify/249-h2cv2-v1-wip`
carries a 703-line runner; `origin/verify/192-h2cv-v1-wip` carries an 872-line runner plus a
journal, a test module and a 592-line JSON. The two runners differ by 832 insertions /
664 deletions — the 192 version is the later and fuller one, and the 249 version is a
**partial re-typing of it**, not a successor.

The 192 runner's approach is sound in outline: exact-rational re-derivation of the discrete
realization (V1), an exact partial-fraction evaluation of Xu (4.23) (V2/V3), a four-route
`σ_min` (V4), a prose audit (V5), and one suspicion raised and refuted (V6). **Three defects
make it an unsound basis to inherit, and all three are load-bearing for THIS leg's gate:**

1. **It never re-derives `‖T⁻¹‖_X = 4.026`.** Grep for `4.026` in it returns exactly one hit,
   inside `v5_prose_audit`, which compares leg 176's *prose* against leg 176's *own JSON*.
   That is an internal-consistency check of one artifact against another artifact of the same
   leg — it is not a re-derivation, and it cannot come out differently if leg 176's runner is
   wrong. **My gate names this number explicitly.** It is unverified by the salvage.
2. **It never verifies that the Gram is the `X` norm.** V1 checks
   `G_mn = ∫₀^∞ (1+ξ⁴) ℓ_m ℓ_n dξ` against the module's `I + J⁴` — the *Fourier-side* claim.
   The step that makes that quantity the norm of Xu's Definition 4.1,
   `‖φ‖²_X = ‖φ‖²_{L²} + ‖φ″‖²_{L²}`, is the Plancherel/`ξ⁴ ↔ φ″` correspondence, and it is
   never checked. If the weight were misidentified, every `σ_min` in the leg would be wrong
   and all four "independent" routes would be wrong identically.
3. **Its `σ_min` routes import leg 176's matrices.** `build_section` calls the module's
   `l0_plus`, `symmetry_modes`, `border_row`, `x_gram`, `bordered_gram`. V1's exact check
   mitigates this but does not remove it: the object being verified supplies the data.

Also unexamined there: the `X ⊕ C` Gram puts **unit weight** on the border amplitude while the
`X` block carries the Laguerre-coefficient normalization (no `2π`, no half-line `½`). `σ_min`
is *not* invariant under that convention, and no artifact in the repository measures the
sensitivity.

**Decision, stated plainly: neither salvage branch is inherited.** Nothing is cherry-picked.
The 192 runner is retained as a *read-only cross-reference* — where my independently written
code and its code agree, that is recorded as agreement between two implementations; where they
differ, mine is re-derived from the primary source and the difference is reported. Its three
claimed corrections to leg 176 (6-vs-7 significant figures at `N = 512`; the `N = 1024` rise
being a property of the `eigh` whitening rather than of the Gram; leg 176's residual metric
understating its own accuracy ~10×) are treated as **unverified hypotheses to be re-tested**,
not as findings. Its 592-line JSON is **not** copied forward.

## 3. Prior art OUTSIDE this repository — is either number available externally?

Primary source pulled **fresh this leg**: Xu, arXiv:2607.19762,
`md5 709dac668a1ff508f00d765916db492c`, 3228 lines of extracted text, read at full text for
Definition 4.1, eq. (4.21), (4.22), (4.23), (4.24), (4.25), Lemma 4.5, Proposition 4.6,
Proposition 2 (the realization dichotomy) and §4.6.

What the paper does and does not supply, checked against the text rather than against leg 176's
summary of it:

* **Definition 4.1 confirmed verbatim**: `X = {φ odd : φ, φ″ ∈ L²(0,∞), φ = a₁y + o(y)}`,
  `‖φ‖²_X = ‖φ‖²_{L²} + ‖φ″‖²_{L²}`. So the norm leg 176 discretizes is the paper's norm.
* **(4.21) confirmed**: `L₀⁺ = −1 − y∂_y + i/(y + i/2)` on `H₊²`, via the single-simple-pole
  identity `HΩ − iΩ = i/(y+i/2)` at the exact CLM profile — i.e. **`a = 0` exactness**, the
  ceiling, in the paper's own words.
* **(4.23) confirmed**, including that at `z = 0` the substitution `t = ys` turns
  `I(y) = ∫₀¹ s^{z−2}G₂(ys)ds` into `y∫₀^y G₂(t)/t² dt`, that `c₀ = G₀/(z−1) = −G₀`, and that
  `c₁ = G₁/z` is the only singular object at `z = 0`. Xu records his own check of this:
  *"an exact identity in sympy; floating-point substitution residual 3 × 10⁻¹⁶."*
* **Point spectrum confirmed**: on the odd realization it is *exactly* `{0, 1}` — the scaling
  and time-shift symmetry modes. This is the external warrant for the bordered formulation's
  kernel direction `m` and for `b⁻²`.
* **Realization dichotomy (Proposition 2) confirmed**: on the maximal `L²` realization the
  whole strip is spectrum. This is the external warrant for leg 176's loose-`L²` control.
* **Lemma 4.5 confirmed**: the Hardy operator `T_z` has *exact* norm `1/(Re z + ½) = 1/α`;
  at `z = 0`, `α = ½`, so `‖T₀‖ = 2`.
* **NEITHER `0.0908` NOR `4.026` APPEARS IN XU, in any form.** Proposition 4.6 supplies only a
  qualitative majorant `‖R₀(z)‖_X ≤ C(R,d) α^{−3/2}` with `C` unevaluated. Searched the full
  text for both digit strings and for every numerical constant in §4: the paper contains no
  evaluated resolvent norm at `z = 0`.

**Consequence, and it is the reason this leg is shaped the way it is.** There is no external
number to check against. Independence therefore cannot mean "compare to the literature"; it can
only mean **re-deriving the quantities from the definitions by machinery that leg 176 does not
use**. Lesson 90 applies with full force: a re-run of leg 176's own runner is a control that
cannot come out differently, and the salvage's own V0 says as much.

## 4. What this leg will do that is independent, declared in advance

Declared before any of it is written, so that a route that later agrees cannot be re-described
as having been chosen because it agreed.

* **W1** — the discrete realization (`L₀⁺`, `ℓ`, `m`, `b⁻²`, `G`) re-derived in **exact rational
  arithmetic** from the definitions, *integrating and differentiating the Laguerre polynomials
  term by term*. Neither of the two classical identities leg 176 invokes
  (`∫₀^x L_n = L_n − L_{n+1}`; the `ξL_n`/`ξL_n′` recurrences) is used — they come out as
  by-products or they fail.
* **W2** — the **missing link**: that the Gram is the `X` norm of Definition 4.1. Verified in
  `y`-space by an independent quadrature of `∫|φ|² + ∫|φ″|²` against `2π∫(1+ξ⁴)|φ̂|²`,
  including the `2π`, on data for which the two could disagree.
* **W3** — `σ_min` of the bordered section by **multiple whitenings** (`eigh`, banded Cholesky,
  diagonally rescaled Cholesky, exact-rational `LDLᵀ`) *and* by a **high-precision `mpmath`
  Rayleigh-quotient iteration from exact rational inputs**, which settles how many significant
  figures the banked value actually carries rather than asserting a count.
* **W4** — `‖T⁻¹‖_X` of the tail block, re-derived by the same ladder and the same
  high-precision route. **This is the number the salvage never touched.**
* **W5** — the convention sensitivity: `σ_min` as a function of the border weight and of the
  `X`-block normalization, reported as a magnitude.
* **W6** — Xu (4.23) at `z = 0` evaluated by **exact partial fractions** (`G = b²f` is rational
  for finite Laguerre data, so `∫G₂/t²` has an exact antiderivative), so that none of leg 176's
  Taylor/Gauss-panel/grading machinery enters the comparison.
* **W7** — leg 176's prose audited against the JSON it cites, and PUB2's five quoting sites
  audited against both.

Every one of W1-W7 can report a disagreement. If `σ_min` or `‖T⁻¹‖_X` fails to reproduce at
the precision leg 176 claims, the finding is an **escalation** — banked, submission-track —
and this leg pushes its branch only.
