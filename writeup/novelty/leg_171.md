## 4. What the pass found — the spaces, pre-registered here as the verdict the runner must relocate

Recorded now, in the paper's own words, so the runner cannot drift from the reading.

### 4a. The paper's own enumeration — Xu §3.1, p. 8, heading **"Map of realizations."**

> *"One realization is the one we work in and the others are named alternatives. The chosen
> realization is X (origin-H²): Theorem 1 and Proposition 1 live there, and its essential
> spectrum for a ∈ (0, 1/2) contains two lines. The maximal L² realization (Section 4.6) is
> the weaker alternative, in which the whole strip is spectrum. A third-derivative realization
> is a stronger alternative in which the included origin line moves left and a single far-field
> line is expected to survive for all a; we do not pursue it here. Each statement names the
> realization it lives in."*

### 4b. Maximal L² — verdict **DEAD, proved** (Prop. 2, §3.1 eq (3.5); §4.6). *Already banked by leg 64 — cited, not claimed.*

> *"σess(L0) ⊇ {λ : −1/2 ≤ Re λ ≤ 3/2}"* … *"Hence on the maximal L² realization the whole
> open strip consists of eigenvalues; no point of it is isolated … **In particular there is no
> L² spectral gap.**"* (§4.6)

Hypotheses: `a = 0`, exact CLM profile; the strip is filled by the **explicit** family
`u_λ(y) = y^{1−λ}/(y + i/2)²`, `−1/2 < Re λ < 3/2`, whose odd two-branch combinations
`φ_λ := B₊ − S B₊` are genuine eigenfunctions at *every* strip point.

### 4c. Merely-`C¹` origin realization — verdict **DEAD, proved** (§5.7, "The smoothness hypothesis is sharp") — **NOT previously banked anywhere in this repository**

> *"Dropping condition (D3) admits a continuum of eigenfunctions. The Case-3 functions are
> genuine odd, L², C¹(ℝ) distributional eigenfunctions of L0 for every real λ ∈ (−1/2, 0) (and
> for complex λ with Re λ ∈ (−1/2, 0)) … **On a merely C¹ domain the open strip would therefore
> fill with a continuum of point spectrum.**"*

This is strictly sharper than 4b: it is not "no origin condition" but "one derivative too few",
and it says the invertibility is destroyed by a *one-derivative* relaxation. Witness
computation given at `λ = −1/4, µ = 5/4`.

### 4d. Third-derivative (`H³`) realization — verdict **EXPECTED ALIVE, NOT PROVED; two open questions attached** (§3.1 map, §4.5, §4.6)

> *"Whether σess equals this union for a > 0, and whether a stronger third-derivative
> realization (φ‴ ∈ L² near the origin) leaves the single far-field line uniformly in a, are
> exactness questions (Section 4.5)."*
> *"A stronger H³ realization shifts the included origin line off (whether a single far-field
> line is then exact is the same exactness question), so for a > 0 the two-line picture is
> relative to the choice of realization."* (§4.6)

Hypotheses: this is a **named alternative Xu explicitly does not pursue** — "we do not pursue
it here". It is a conjecture with a stated mechanism, not a theorem. Any downstream leg
quoting it must carry that word.

### 4e. The weighted conjugated family `Y_θ` — verdict **ALIVE, EXACT, for every θ > 0** (Appendix A, Def. A.2, Prop. A.3) — **the largest unmined result, and the one with certificate-shaped content**

Definition A.2 (`v = b²u`, `b = y + i/2`):
`‖v‖²_{Y_θ} = ∫₀^∞ |v|² y^{−2θ−1} dy = ∫₀^∞ |u|² (y² + 1/4)² y^{−2θ−1} dy`.

> *"In particular A_θ is normal on Y_θ, equal to (1 − θ) plus a skew-adjoint part, with
> **spectrum the single vertical line {Re = 1 − θ}** and e^{Aτ} equal to e^{(1−θ)τ} times a
> unitary group; this normality is the structural reason the operator norm is exact and
> function-independent."*

Prop. A.3: **`‖e^{Aτ}‖_{Y_θ→Y_θ} = e^{(1−θ)τ}` (constant 1, every function)**, and at `θ = 3/2`,
`‖S_Y(τ)‖_{Y_{3/2}→Y_{3/2}} = e^{−τ/2}` **exact, constant 1**.

Why this is the certificate-relevant one, in Xu's own words:

> *"In Y_{3/2} (core weight y^{−4}, plain L² at infinity) **both essential lines of L0 coincide
> at Re = −1/2**: the far-field line stays at −1/2, and the origin line, which in plain L² sits
> at the pathological Re = +3/2 (Proposition 2 and Section 4.6), is pushed to −1/2 by the
> y^{−4} core weight."*

Hypotheses, and they are sharp and restrictive — recorded because they are what a downstream
leg would trip on:
* The two symmetry modes are **not in** `Y_{3/2}`: *"The two symmetry modes are excluded from
  Y_{3/2} by that weight … So the pure semigroup already decays at the essential rate with no
  projection inside the space and constant 1."*
* `Y_θ` carries **no pointwise origin traces**, so the modulation projection *cannot act inside
  it*; it acts on `X` and is mapped in by the bounded transfer `J_X : X → 𝒴_{3/2}`,
  `‖J_X φ‖ ≤ C_Q‖φ‖_X`, giving `‖S_Y(τ) J_X φ‖_{𝒴_{3/2}} ≤ C_Q e^{−τ/2}‖φ‖_X`.
  **The exact constant 1 belongs to `S_Y` on `Y_{3/2}`; the composite carries `C_Q`, not 1.**
* θ is **pinned** for physical `X` data: *"For physical X-data the transfer J_X is bounded at
  θ = 3/2 only: the tail forces θ ≥ 3/2 … while the core forces θ ≤ 3/2 (the double Hardy
  inequality is sharp there). So the physical-data statement is the single space Y_{3/2}, with
  rate exactly 1/2."* For origin-analytic forcing the whole range `θ ∈ (1,2)` opens up.
* `a = 0` only, and it is the **conjugated** variable, not the physical one:
  *"on a weighted space of the conjugated variable reached from X by a bounded transfer map;
  we keep the two separate, since L0 is non-normal and a spectral gap does not by itself give
  a decay rate in the X norm"* (abstract).

### 4f. The Hardy block space `X₊ = {u ∈ H²₊ : u″ ∈ L²}` — verdict **ALIVE, and it is where the explicit inverse actually lives** (§4.4, Prop. 4.6, Lemma 4.5)

`‖φ‖²_X = 2‖P₊φ‖²_{X₊}`; the resolvent is built blockwise `R₀(z) = R₀⁺(z) ⊕ R₀⁻(z)` with
`‖R₀⁻(z)‖ = ‖R₀⁺(z̄)‖`, from the scalar Hardy reduction `L₀⁺ = −1 − y∂_y + i/(y + i/2)`.
Lemma 4.5 gives the **exact** Hardy–Mellin norm `‖T_z‖ = 1/(Re z + 1/2) = 1/α`, and the
assembled majorant is `M_an(z) ∼ α^{−3/2}`. This is the constructive object a certificate
would actually consume, and it is *not* origin-H² — it is the Hardy summand of it.

### 4g. Full `L²(ℝ)` **with the even sector included** — verdict **the gap is strictly worse, and for `a > 0` it is destroyed** (§3.2)

> *"The third produces the even mode φ = Ω′, which is an exact eigenfunction of La for every
> admissible profile, at eigenvalue c̃ = c_l + a HΩ(0) = (c_l + a)/(1 − a)"* … *"It reduces to
> c_l only at a = 0, where one checks L0 Ω′ = Ω′ exactly, with Ω′ = (y² − 1/4)/(y² + 1/4)²
> even and O(y^{−2}), **so it lies in L²(ℝ) but not in X.** Centering the collapse at the
> origin, which is what the odd restriction does, removes this mode. At a = 0 the eigenvalue 0
> likewise has the even partner y²/(y² + 1/4)². **So on the full L²(ℝ) the eigenvalues 0 and 1
> each carry an even partner**, while on the odd realization X each eigenspace is
> one-dimensional."*

So **oddness is load-bearing**, separately from the origin condition: at `a > 0` the
translation eigenvalue sits at `c̃ = (c_l + a)/(1 − a) > 0`, an extra unstable direction that
the odd realization removes by construction.

### 4h. The EGM singular-weighted space — verdict **ALIVE, gap certified** (§8 parenthesis). *Already banked by leg 141 — cited, not claimed.*

> *"(The small-a coercivity estimate of Elgindi, Ghoul, and Masmoudi [15, Prop. 2.1], for odd f
> with f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0 in a singular-weighted space, is not a counterexample but an instance
> of the realization mechanism of Section 3.1: **the singular weight and origin constraints
> define their own realization, in which a gap is certified**; it neither contradicts nor
> supplies the X-gap here.)"*

### 4i. The Evans-determinant weighted space — verdict **used as an instrument, no invertibility theorem** (§3.2)

> *"Splitting La against its far field and forming the associated determinant on **a weighted
> space in which the essential line is displaced left of the strip**, the number of eigenvalues
> inside the strip window becomes an integer winding count n_disc(a) along a rectangular
> contour."*

Named, unnamed as a space, exploratory, run outputs not retained. Recorded for completeness;
it carries no verdict.

## 5. The second thing the leg was asked to watch: what Xu's certification TECHNIQUE actually is, and how mature

The prompt asks for this beyond the strict gate. Xu is explicit, and the answer is **less
mature than the origin-H² theorem makes it look, and the two obvious import routes into this
repository's machinery are both closed by Xu himself.**

**What the technique is, in three layers:**
1. **The proved layer is not computer-assisted at all.** Theorems 1–3 are Mellin
   diagonalization + log-widening Weyl sequences + an *explicit closed-form resolvent kernel*
   (§4.4 eq (4.23)) with an exact Hardy–Mellin operator norm (Lemma 4.5) and an assembled
   analytic majorant `M_an ∼ α^{−3/2}`. Machine involvement is a *twelve-check verification
   suite* (six exact in sympy, six numerical) confirming identities, not a certificate.
2. **The certification-shaped layer is the Evans/argument-principle strand (§3.2, §7), and it
   is explicitly incomplete.** Xu names **three gaps** verbatim: *"a uniform large-imaginary-
   part bound, trace-ideal membership of the kernel, and quadrature-error bounds in the trace
   norm"* — *"which the code computes as diagnostics rather than enforcing as interval bounds"*
   and *"its run outputs are not retained"*. *"Upgrading any of these would move this strand
   toward a computer-assisted proof."* **It is not one today.** These are precisely the three
   items the prompt asked to watch for, and they are open, by the author's own statement.
3. **The architecture Xu says it must imitate** is Hou–Wang–Yang's viscous-3D-NS
   nonuniqueness proof: *"the computer-assisted step certifies invertibility only on a
   finite-rank image, after the continuous spectrum has been carried by a coercive part and the
   remainder rendered compact; our spectrally correct essential-line rendering feeding an
   argument-principle count is the same requirement in the gCLM-collapse setting."*

**Two no-gos Xu states in §8, both of which bear directly on this repository's lanes:**

> *"A weighted-energy/coercivity estimate cannot certify the gap: the far field is already
> coercive in plain L² with the sharp edge, but a local positive multiplier +φ HΩ near the
> origin (with HΩ(0) > 0) survives every L²-equivalent weight, **so no coercivity certificate
> in an L²-equivalent norm reaches the spectral gap.**"* (already banked, leg 141)

> *"Likewise **a naive interval-arithmetic resolvent enclosure is unavailable**: a weight is a
> change of norm, eigenvalues are norm-invariant, and the truncated La genuinely carries
> essential-smear eigenvalues inside the strip (the compactified grid mis-renders the
> continuous line at the wrong real part), **so no weighted enclosure can exclude them**. The
> consequence is that the rigorous discrete exclusion must be analytical (the a = 0 anchor plus
> continuation) rather than a black-box computer-assisted enclosure; where a computer-assisted
> proof does enter, it must first be given a spectrally correct rendering of the essential
> spectrum (a non-periodic log-Mellin operator at high resolution feeding an argument-principle
> count), not applied to the raw grid."*

**This second quotation is NOT banked anywhere in this repository** (leg 141 banked only the
first, the coercivity half) and it is the maturity answer: Xu's own paper says the naive
interval-arithmetic route into his object does not work, and names the prerequisite — a
spectrally correct essential-line rendering — before any CAP may enter. Anyone treating "Xu's
certification method" as a mature lane to be ported is treating a program as a method. Also
note §7's honest instrument caveat: the log-Mellin grid's rightmost mode at `a = 0.5` sits at
`Re = +2.4931` against the plain-L² indicial line `+5/2` — it **corroborates Prop. 2 rather
than certifying the far-field edge**.
