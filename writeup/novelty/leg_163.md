## (C) Xu's own stated limitation — the binding find of this pass

**[arXiv:2607.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19762)** — Jie Xu, *The spectral picture of
self-similar collapse in the Constantin–Lax–Majda equation*, 22 Jul 2026 (HTML mirror:
<https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19762v1>).

The abstract itself carries the caveat, in the author's own words:

> "The linear semigroup and its exact decay rate `e^{−τ/2}` are computed in closed form, **but
> on a weighted space of the conjugated variable reached from `X` by a bounded transfer map**;
> we keep the two separate, **since `L₀` is non-normal and a spectral gap does not by itself
> give a decay rate in the `X` norm**."

This is a limitation *of the gap*, stated by the paper that proves the gap. It is the single
most important input to this leg's gate and it was found in the source, not inferred. §4.6
("Remark (realization dependence)") and the abstract's "realization dichotomy" sentence are the
other two places the paper bounds its own reach: the spectrum it computes is a property of the
*origin-`H²` realisation*, and the maximal-`L²` realisation has a genuinely different (smeared)
spectrum which discretisations without an origin condition reproduce faithfully.

**Consequence for novelty.** Nothing this leg says about origin-`H²` may be phrased as "the
operator is invertible, so a certificate exists". Xu explicitly declines to convert his gap
into a norm-level bound on `X`, and a certificate needs exactly the norm-level bound.

## (A) Prior art: has a CAP certificate already been built for this class in a Hilbert realisation?

- **[arXiv:2302.12877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12877)** — Cadiot, Lessard, Nakao (?),
  *Rigorous computation of solutions of semi-linear PDEs on unbounded domains via spectral
  methods*; journal version *SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst.*
  <https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/23M1607507>. **This is the closest existing machinery to
  what an origin-`H²` attempt would need**: fully spectral, works in a Hilbert space `H^l`,
  approximates *bounded linear operators from `L²` to `H^l`* by Fourier series, builds
  approximate inverses of the differential operator that way, and closes with
  Newton–Kantorovich. It also states the obstruction that matters here in general terms:
  on an unbounded domain the inverse of a differential operator **is not compact**, and
  compactness is the ingredient CAPs normally lean on. Already listed in this repository's
  `Papers/MANIFEST.md` Tier 2 as **fetched, text-extracted, NOT READ**. It is prior art for the
  *method*, not for this operator.
- **[arXiv:2605.03920](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03920)** — *Linear instability of a
  Burgers–Hilbert traveling wave*. Nearest neighbour in object: a rigorous spectral statement
  about a Hilbert-transform-nonlocal 1D model's linearisation. Not the CLM collapse profile and
  not a certificate of invertibility.
- **[Nakao et al., *Efficient Approaches for Verifying the Existence and Bound of Inverse of
  Linear Operators in Hilbert Spaces*, J. Sci. Comput. (2023)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10915-023-02097-6)**
  — the general Hilbert-space analogue of the `Z₁` bound this repository computes in `ℓ¹_w`.
  If an origin-`H²` attempt is ever run, this is the source that says what quantity replaces
  `Z₁` and under what hypotheses it is verifiable.
- **[Chen–Hou, semilinear-heat `L²`-based stability with log correction](https://users.cms.caltech.edu/~hou/papers/Semilinear-heat-2024.pdf)**
  and the framework described in **[arXiv:2308.01528](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.01528)** /
  **[arXiv:2605.19716](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19716)**: the standard route in this
  literature realises the linearised operator as a **compact perturbation of a maximal
  dissipative operator in a global weighted Sobolev space of supercritical regularity**, and
  rules out unstable directions by an explicit finite-dimensional argument. That is a
  **coercivity/energy** route, *not* an approximate-inverse route. Recorded here because it is
  the structurally different third option an origin-`H²` scoping must mention.

**Verdict (A).** No published CAP certificate exists for the bordered `a = 0` CLM
linearisation in any Hilbert realisation. The *method* pieces exist (2302.12877, Nakao 2023);
the *object* has only a spectral description (Xu), never a certificate. So the scoping question
this leg asks is not pre-empted — but nor is the answer free: the reason nobody has done it is
one of the things this leg has to determine, not assume.
