# Leg 363 — Route-CKNQ: novelty / prior-art log

**Gate:** does a literature search for explicit-constant CKN and quantitative-Harnack results
locate published values reaching Pineau-Vicol's actual usage for either of leg 360's two
non-constructive `delta_0` ingredients (`epsilon_*`, `K_H`)? **Answer: YES — both adjudicated,
both NO-EXPLICIT-VALUE-LOCATED.** Full record with quoted sources is in
`experiments/journal/leg_363.md` and `writeup/data/p2_route_cknq_v1.json`. This file records
only what is new relative to the existing record (leg 360's), per MF2 (links/quotes, not
counts).

---

## 1. What is genuinely new to this repository

1. **A targeted search of the explicit-constant CKN literature**, disambiguated from the (much
   larger, unrelated) CKN *interpolation-inequality* literature that shares the same author-name
   citation. Located and read in full: Zhen Lei, Xiao Ren, "Quantitative partial regularity of
   the Navier-Stokes equations and applications", `arXiv:2210.01783v1`. This paper had not been
   read by any prior leg.

2. **Identification that PV's own citation list `[CKN82, Lin98, LadySeregin99, Kukavica09]`
   excludes the one existing "quantitative-derivation" route (Vasseur's De Giorgi-iteration
   proof) entirely** — two of PV's four citations (`Lin98`, `LadySeregin99`) are explicitly
   classified as *non-quantitative* by Lei-Ren's own reading of them. New to this repository:
   no prior leg had checked which side of the quantitative/non-quantitative line PV's specific
   citations fall on.

3. **Confirmation that "quantitative" in this literature means derivation technique, not a
   supplied number.** Lei-Ren 2022's own headline theorem (their Theorem 6, built on the
   quantitative route) still only asserts existence of "a universal positive constant
   `epsilon**`" — no number, no formula. This closes a possibility leg 360 left open
   ("effective versions... exist in the literature in *some* contexts, but PV neither cite nor
   derive one here") by actually checking the most current representative of that literature and
   finding it, too, is non-numeric.

4. **A targeted search of the Krylov-Safonov / non-divergence-form explicit-Harnack-constant
   literature**, using the operator form traced this leg (`L = -a^{ij}partial_{ij} + b^i
   partial_i + c`, non-divergence, matching Gilbarg-Trudinger's Chapter 9 rather than Chapter 8).
   Four of eight targeted queries returned zero results (banked individually as refusals); the
   closest hit (`arXiv:2512.21025`) was checked clause-by-clause and rejected on two independent
   grounds (wrong target quantity — Holder exponent, not `K_H`; restricted ellipticity regime not
   established for PV's operator). No prior leg had searched this literature at all.

5. **Confirmation that Vasseur's 2007 NoDEA paper is not on arXiv**, established by a direct
   title search (zero hits) and a full scan of Vasseur's arXiv-listed papers finding no match —
   a fact used to determine that this leg's search could not verify Vasseur's own text directly
   and had to rely on Lei-Ren's characterization of it instead (disclosed as such, not concealed).

**No outreach. No count is load-bearing** — the deciding facts are quoted passages from Lei-Ren
2022 and from `Liouvillev2.tex` itself, plus an honest list of which of thirteen search queries
came back empty. **No ban was touched, lifted, or argued.** **No file belonging to leg 360's
banked record or to B7's screen (`solver/dssp_screen.py`) was edited** — the update is delivered
as a flag in this leg's journal/JSON for the DM to act on.

## 2. Instrument notes (search discipline, MF1/MF2/MF3-style)

- **Refusals banked, not silently omitted:** 5 of 13 arXiv API queries returned zero results
  (`"explicit Harnack constant"`, `"Krylov-Safonov" AND "explicit"`, `"Harnack constant" AND
  "non-divergence" AND "explicit"`, `"Harnack inequality" AND "explicit dependence"`, and the
  title search for Vasseur's paper). Each is listed individually in
  `writeup/data/p2_route_cknq_v1.json` under `refusals_to_measure_banked`, not folded into a
  bare count.
- **Links, not counts:** every source discussed (load-bearing, near-miss, and absent) is given
  its arXiv id/URL in the data JSON; the near-miss (`arXiv:2512.21025`) is explicitly recorded as
  *rejected on reach*, not as supporting evidence, so a reader skimming only counts could not
  mistake "13 queries run" for "13 supporting hits".
- **Name-collision hazard flagged:** "Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg" is shared between the 1982
  Navier-Stokes partial-regularity paper (PV's actual citation target) and an unrelated
  interpolation-inequality literature. All CKN-labelled search hits were individually screened by
  title/abstract before being counted as reaching or not reaching.
