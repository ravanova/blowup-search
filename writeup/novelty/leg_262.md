## §3 — The genuinely relevant prior art *inside* this repository

This is the part that matters, and a naive grep on "rotated" would have missed all of it.

**(a) Leg 255 (Route-P1A) + `writeup/novelty/verify_255.md` — the Gaussian-weighted space.**
Leg 255's screen (iv) is Breden–Chu's reach: *"a local polynomial nonlinearity in `u` and its
first derivatives, over a Gaussian-weighted `H²(μ)`, with a Laplacian / Ornstein–Uhlenbeck
principal part on `ℝ^d`, `d ≤ 3`."* Thirteen of its 21 rows die on that screen, `NSE-3D`
among them, and the reason is always the same: **the Leray projection is nonlocal and is not
in the reach.** `verify_255.md` §1 sharpens this into a prediction handed forward to leg 257:

> *"the Leray projector is built from Riesz transforms (Calderón–Zygmund operators), and CZ
> operators are bounded on a weighted `L²(w)` essentially only when `w` is a Muckenhoupt `A_2`
> weight. Breden–Chu's weight is `µ = e^{|x|²/4}` … emphatically not `A_2` … the expectation
> is that the Leray projector is **genuinely unbounded on `L²(µ)`** … if it holds, the 'argue
> Leray into the reach' lane is dead on arrival."*

**Pineau–Vicol's Lemma 6.4 (`.tex` l.1018–1120) is a direct, published-as-preprint engagement
with exactly that question**, and the authors state the obstruction in their own words at
l.1074: *"we are seeking a bound in the Gaussian weighted `L²_μ` norm, and so we do not have
access to usual Calderon–Zygmund bounds."* This is prior art on the repository's own open
question, from a source the repository has never read. Its resolution — and its price — is
this leg's §3 finding and is developed in `experiments/journal/leg_262.md`.

**(b) Leg 158 + leg 57 — the zero-diagonal / Hermite-differentiation-matrix lesson.**
`writeup/novelty/leg_158.md` l.89–94 records that *"the Hermite function differentiation
matrix … has zero diagonal entries"*, and leg 57 established (from Breden–Desvillettes–Lessard
`arXiv:1503.06315` assumptions (4)–(5), read at full PDF) that the radii-polynomial machinery
**requires a diagonal bounded away from zero**, which the repository's actual target violated.
Any claim this leg makes about a new space being structurally different **must** be measured
against that diagonal, not asserted. It is, in `experiments/p2_route_pvrw_v1_read.py`.

**(c) Stage T / TC and leg 54 (MM) — the bordered-certificate precedent.** The repository has
already built a bordered Newton–Kantorovich certificate with a far-field column
(`test_bordered_hl.py`, stage TC), and leg 54 measured that **no shape of the approximate
inverse `A` closes the block coupling** (best 1.167×, >8× needed; `Z₁[Γ←tail] = 546.57` for all
four border directions). Any bordered construction proposed here inherits the obligation to
re-measure that coupling rather than assume it. Stated as such.

**(d) Leg 253 (parked, branch-only).** Its §5 records that every threshold in every exclusion
in this literature is non-explicit and asks (its point 6) whether PV's thresholds inherit that
problem. That is this leg's dispatched question and is answered with magnitudes.
