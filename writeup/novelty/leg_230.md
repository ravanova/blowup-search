## R2 — the independence requirement, and how it is met

The failure mode a verification leg exists to catch is **leg 220's runner agreeing with
itself**. Re-running `experiments/p2_route_tnr_v1_repair.py` would prove nothing. So:

1. **The flagging count is checked against a CLOSED FORM derived on paper here**, not
   against a numpy re-implementation of the module's own loop. The staggered grid is
   `theta_j = -pi + 2*pi*(j + 1/2)/M`, and a sample is outside the data interval iff
   `theta_j < 2*arctan(X_lo)` or `theta_j > 2*arctan(X_hi)`. Solving for `j` gives an
   integer count in terms of `ceil`, with **no array ever built**. If the module and the
   closed form agree, they agree for a reason that is not shared code.
2. **The asymmetric rungs are this leg's own**, chosen to differ in kind from leg 220's
   five (which all held `X_hi` fixed and walked `X_lo`): this leg includes a
   right-truncated rung, a two-sided offset rung, a rung whose data interval **does not
   contain zero at all**, and a near-symmetric rung with a small count.
3. **Leg 55's margins are compared with `==` against the banked JSON**, and separately
   against the identity `margin = p - s - 1` evaluated in plain Python here.

## R3 — external check (one, and it is narrow)

The only external question a windowing guard could collide with is whether the literature
on **rational / compactified spectral methods** prescribes some different admissible window
for `X = tan(theta/2)` sampling of grid-borne data. It does not bear on this leg, and the
reason is structural rather than bibliographic: the compactification map is a change of
variable on the **whole** line, whereas the interval `[X.min(), X.max()]` here is not a
property of the map at all — it is the extent of a *numerical solve on a truncated `rho`
grid* produced inside this repository (`solver/bordered_hl.py`). Which samples fall outside
that extent is therefore a fact about this pipeline's own data, not a modelling choice any
external author could have made differently.

**Stated as a limitation, not as a clearance:** this leg ran **no fresh external search**,
and `LITERATURE_CHECK.md` contains no entry on compactified-basis windowing to lean on
either — checked, not assumed. So R3 is an argument that the question is internal, not
evidence that no one has written on it. That is the appropriate weight for a verification
leg making no mathematical claim. **No external source is cited by this leg, and
`literature_gates.py` is untouched.**
