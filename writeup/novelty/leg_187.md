## What is being checked for prior art

The object is **Object A** — Chen arXiv:1908.09385 eq (2.2) p.4, the **inviscid** `a = 1/2`
pole-dynamics profile `Ω(x) = −2bx/(x²+b²)²`, `b = √(3/8)`, `c_l = 1/3`, `c_ω = −1`. Leg 125
transcribed it and measured `Y₀` under the radii-polynomial budget at 9 rows. Four questions:

* **(N1)** Does a **CAP / validated-numerics certificate of this specific profile** exist?
* **(N2)** Is the profile already known **in closed form** in the published literature — i.e.
  would a certificate of it be certifying a known exact solution rather than establishing
  existence? (This decides what the YES branch would even be worth, so it must be on the
  record *before* the construction.)
* **(N3)** Is the obstruction this leg expects to hit — the **exact continuous symmetry**
  (dilation) of the steady equation, which puts a zero eigenvalue in the linearisation about
  the exact profile and so breaks the radii-polynomial theorem's **uniqueness/isolation**
  conclusion — already stated in the literature for this equation? (If yes, this leg must
  **cite** it, not claim it.)
* **(N4)** Is there published treatment of the **far-field/tail** hypothesis for a
  whole-line self-similar gCLM profile — the decay rate of the profile against the transport
  operator's far-field symbol?

## Verdict, pre-committed before construction

* **(N1) NOT FOUND.** No CAP / validated-numerics certificate of Object A, or of any exact
  gCLM self-similar profile at `a = 1/2`, appears in the searched literature. The narrow
  novelty question the route brief asks is **clean**.
* **(N2) PRE-EMPTED.** Object A is a **published closed-form exact solution** (Chen eq (2.2);
  HQWW arXiv:2305.05895 for the whole branch `a ≤ 1`). So the YES branch, if it is reached,
  certifies a **known** object; the existence content is nil and the leg must say so in the
  same breath as the certificate. **This is written down here, before the numbers.**
* **(N3) PRE-EMPTED IN ITS GENERAL FORM.** The scaling-symmetry zero mode of the linearisation
  about an exact CLM self-similar profile is published (Xu arXiv:2607.19762, `a = 0`), and the
  standard remedy (phase condition / symmetry reduction) is published in the validated-numerics
  literature. This leg **cites** both and claims neither. What remains unclaimed is the
  certificate-clause audit at `a = 1/2` with magnitudes.
* **(N4) PARTIALLY COVERED.** Far-field algebraic decay rates of gCLM profiles are rigorously
  estimated in HTW26 arXiv:2603.25104. The pairing of that decay rate against the
  **far-field symbol of the self-similar transport operator** — the quantity that decides
  whether the tail of the approximate inverse is bounded — was not found stated anywhere and
  is the one genuinely open sub-question this leg can measure.

**Consequence for the construction, pre-committed here.** The leg builds a **hypothesis
battery**, not a budget re-check: space membership, exactness of the defect, injectivity /
isolation, the bordered repair, the far-field symbol/tail clause, the quadratic constant's
boundedness, and only then the radii polynomial. Every clause reports a **magnitude**. The
gate is answered on the battery, not on `Y₀`.
