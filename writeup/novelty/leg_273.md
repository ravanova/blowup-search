### Q2 answered — and this is the pass's decisive negative

Across the **2,060 lines** of Breden–Chu's full text, the string `blow` occurs **exactly once**,
at line 1925, and it is a **bibliography entry** — reference [16], Chen & Hou, *Stable Nearly
Self-Similar Blowup of the 2D Boussinesq and 3D Euler Equations*. Occurrences of `backward`: **0**.

**The body of `arXiv:2404.04054` never treats a blow-up profile.** Its abstract states the setting:

> *"Such equations appear naturally in several contexts, and in particular when looking for
> **self-similar solutions of parabolic PDEs**."*

and its governing equation, numbered (2) at line 84, is

> `Lu := −∆u − (x/2)·∇u = f(x, u, ∇u),  x ∈ ℝ^d`

This is the **forward** self-similar operator. The Keller–Segel blow-up profile equation carries
the **opposite drift sign** (§4). No prior work applies this machinery to a backward self-similar
profile, and the paper does not claim to.

*(Recorded honestly: at line 191 the same paper writes `L := −∆ + (x/2)·∇` in a prose remark. The
numbered equation (2) at line 84 and the abstract at line 20 both give `−∆ − (x/2)·∇`, twice
against once, so line 191 is read as a sign slip in the prose or the extraction and equation (2)
is taken as authoritative. Flagged rather than silently resolved.)*

### Q3 answered — the explicit profile, in closed form

`arXiv:2209.11206`, equation (1.5), verbatim:

> `u_T(t, x) := (1/(T−t)) U(x/√(T−t))`, where `U(x) = 4(d−2)(2d + |x|²) / (2(d−2) + |x|²)²`

attributed to reference [8] (Brenner et al.). At d = 3: `U(x) = 4(6 + |x|²)/(2 + |x|²)²`, with
`U(0) = 6` and far field `U(x) ~ 4/|x|²`. **Algebraic decay, not Gaussian.** The non-explicit
family carries the same tail: `arXiv:2503.02263` states its profiles approach `2(d−2)/|x|²` at
spatial infinity, and its reduced-mass variables have explicit solutions
`Φ̄₂ = 1/|y|²`, `Φ̄₃ = 2/(2(d−2) + |y|²)` (equation (2.3)).

## §4 — the novelty claim, stated so it cannot be over-read

**What is new to this repository, and as far as this pass can determine to the literature:**

> Both of leg 255's survivors are **excluded from Breden–Chu's own space** by Breden–Chu's own
> Corollary 17(i), because every Keller–Segel backward self-similar profile decays **algebraically**
> (`~ C/|x|²`) while every element of `H²(e^{|x|²/4}, ℝ^d)` must decay **super-Gaussianly**.

Corollary 17(i), `arXiv:2404.04054` line 601, verbatim:

> *"(i) If d ∈ {1, 2, 3}, u ∈ H²(µ) ⟹ e^{|x|²/8} u ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d)."*

with Lemma 19 giving the explicit constant `‖e^{|x|²/8} u‖_∞ ≤ C(d)‖u‖_{H²(µ)}`,
`C₀ = 0.52319, C₁ = 1.0228, C₂ = 0.37467` at d = 3. And Remark 18 states the intended scope in the
authors' own words:

> *"these embeddings are sufficient to cover the range of exponent p for the semilinear heat
> equation for which **rapidly decaying** self-similar solutions are known to exist"*

**What is NOT claimed.** No claim that Breden–Chu erred — Remark 40 is a correct statement about
which **nonlinearities** the space absorbs, and it is silent about which **profiles** lie in the
space. No claim that certifying a KS profile is impossible in some other space. No claim that this
is Clay movement of any kind (§5).
