## 7. PRE-COMMITTED PASS PREDICATE, and the controls that let the gate say NO

The gate asks for a gap that is *"positive and grid-stable across two refinements"*. Leg 111
declared a **relative** tolerance because an absolute one is passed trivially by a sequence
collapsing to zero — which was precisely its `A3`'s behaviour. The same tolerance is adopted
here, unchanged, plus leg 111's two independent disqualifiers, so that this leg's pass bar is
**not looser than the bar its own predecessor failed**:

A `(trial space, weight)` pair **PASSES** iff **all five** hold:

1. **positive** — `gap > 0` at every `n` in the ladder `n ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256}`;
2. **grid-stable** — relative change `≤ 5 %` across each of the last two refinements
   (`128 → 256` and `64 → 128`), i.e. across two refinements, as the gate words it;
3. **quadrature-stable** — at fixed `n = 128`, sweeping the grading depth
   `n_grade ∈ {12, 24, 48, 96}` gives relative spread `≤ 1e−3`. *(Leg 111's `A3` scored
   `2.665e−01` here and its admissible `A2` scored `6.678e−16`; this is the disqualifier that
   caught a number that was finite only because the innermost panel was.)*
4. **admissible** — the membership norm ratio over a doubled grading depth is within `1e−6` of
   `1`, on the **constrained** space, and the exponent margin `2p + 1 − γ` is positive;
5. **under the published ceiling** — `gap ≤ 0.5 + 1e−9` (Xu). A pass above the ceiling is
   reported as an instrument failure and the leg is withdrawn.

**Controls, fixed now:**

- **C1 (reproduction).** `T0` must reproduce leg 111's four banked gaps to `≤ 1e−5` (P1).
  Append-only means the unconstrained path is byte-identical; if the numbers move, the append
  was not append-only in effect and the run is void.
- **C2 (the predicate must be able to say NO).** `T3_hilbert_only` at `γ = 4` must **fail**,
  and on the admissibility clause specifically (P4). If a constrained-but-`p = 1` space passes,
  the predicate is measuring dimension reduction and the result is **withdrawn, not shipped**.
- **C3 (the positive control that can report the other answer, lesson 90).** Leg 111's
