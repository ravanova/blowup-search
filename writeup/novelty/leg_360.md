## 1. What is genuinely new to this repository

Three things, none of them new mathematics in the literature sense — all three are readings of
a section of an existing preprint that no prior leg had read, plus one short computation
combining two of its own displayed results.

1. **§9 of v2 (l.1718-2080, the full proof of Theorem "Approximately self-similar Type I
   solutions are regular") had not been read by any prior leg.** Leg 330 read only the
   *statement* (l.379-419) and flagged two conditions as open without tracing either into the
   proof. This leg reads the proof in full and traces both.

2. **δ₀'s explicit formula, `δ₀ = K_H^{-1}K_2^{-1}·θ_*²/8` (l.2072-2075), located and its full
   dependency chain traced** to two classically non-constructive ingredients: Caffarelli-Kohn-
   Nirenberg's `ε_*` (l.1994-1995, citing `[CKN82,Lin98,LadySeregin99,Kukavica09]`) and a
   Harnack constant `K_H` cited generically to `[Corollary 9.25]{GilbargTrudinger98}`
   (l.2063-2065). No prior leg had located this formula or identified its two bottlenecks.

3. **The pressure-decay divergence computation.** Leg 330 recorded the pressure condition as
   "not verified from the paper's own decay estimates" — a negative/absence finding. This leg
   substitutes the paper's own uniform-in-time DSS/RDSS pressure-decay estimate
   (`eq:decay:pressure:k`, l.1416-1420, extended over the full period at l.1389-1392) into T4's
   own annulus-boundedness hypothesis (`eq:local:pbound`, l.396-399) via the paper's own change
   of variables (l.1730-1733), and obtains `|p(x,t)| ≲ C_{P,0}(σ)·(-t)^{-σ/2}` — a **divergent**
   bound as `t → 0⁻` for every `σ > 0` the paper's estimate ever attains. This two-line
   substitution is new to the repository; both ingredients it combines were already banked
   (one by leg 330, one newly located in this leg's §9 read), but no prior leg had carried the
   substitution through to a computed rate.

**No outreach. No count is load-bearing** — the deciding facts in this leg are two quoted
