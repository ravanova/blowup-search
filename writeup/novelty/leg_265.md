## 4. Q1 = 1, and the single hit is decisive prior art — READ AT FULL TEXT

> **Nonlinear stability of the Larson–Penston collapse** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12435 —
> 2025-09-15.

Downloaded from `arxiv.org/e-print` **by this leg**, e-print tarball md5
`a0a136ed5e961276e135e86fa43fb01a`, `LP-stability-Arxiv-final.tex`, **7675 lines**, read at
LaTeX source. This is **not** an abstract-depth read, because it is load-bearing (leg 174's
§2 caution: abstract-depth reading has been caught wrong four times in this repository).

**What it does, in its own words:**

* l.282 — *"the proof of mode-stability for the non self-adjoint spectral problem which arises
  when linearising the dynamics around the Larson-Penston collapsing solution."*
* l.378 — *"We then employ **interval arithmetic, a rigorous form of computer-assisted proof,
  to exclude potential unstable modes** in this remaining compact set."*
* l.473 — *"we develop a strategy that combines an ad-hoc high-order energy method and
  **rigorous computer-assisted proofs (by means of interval arithmetic)**."*
* Abstract — *"the **first full nonlinear stability result for radially imploding compressible
  flows**"*, and *"the **maximal dissipativity** of the linearised operator is proven on
  arbitrary large backward light cones emanating from the singular point."*
* l.506 cites `BuCaGo2025` — **BCG itself**. The authors place their work in the same lineage.
* Appendix `APP:IA`, l.5812–5818 — the tool is **VNODE-LP**, an interval ODE solver; code
  public at `https://github.com/mrischrecker/Larson-Penston-Stability`; **run on a MacBook Air
  2013**.
* l.498 / l.2194 — the compact box the IA closes is `Re λ ∈ [0,1]`, `|Im λ| ≤ 8`, split at
  `b₀ = 1/5` and `b₁ = 8`, with energy methods at low and high frequency and IA in between.

**Consequence for this leg's claims, stated plainly.** The architecturally-hardest component
of BCG §7 — *rigorous computer-assisted mode-stability of a non-self-adjoint linearised
operator around a radially imploding compressible self-similar profile, with maximal
dissipativity on backward cones* — **has been done, published, and released as code.**
This repository may **not** claim that shape as novel, and this dossier does not. It is
carried as a **template and a feasibility datum**, and it is the single largest downward
revision to the cost this leg reports.

**What it does not do, and this is the whole remaining gap.** Larson–Penston is the
**isothermal Euler–Poisson** system. It is **inviscid**. There is no `F_dis`, no `δ_dis`, no
`r`-restriction, and no dissipative term to enclose. Its CAP closes a **spectral** question
about the linearised operator; leg 266's obligation 1 is about retaining a **non-autonomous
viscous forcing** through the nonlinear stability argument at an exponent where that forcing
**grows**. The two are adjacent, not the same, and this leg states the distinction wherever it
uses the template.
