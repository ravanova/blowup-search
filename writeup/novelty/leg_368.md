## 3. The finding, in one sentence, and why full-text depth (not abstract depth) was required

**Theorem 1.2's hypothesis (`U ∈ Ṁq,1(R³)`, `3/2<q<6`) is strictly broader than both the screen's
`L³` test (T1) and its single-generic-ray decay test (T2), by the paper's OWN cited embedding chain
`L^q ↪ L^{q,∞} ↪ Ṁq,l ↪ Ṁq,1` (eq 1.6) and its own citation of Sawano's non-density result — and
this reach is invisible at abstract depth because the abstract states only which SPACES the paper
covers ("Morrey spaces `Ṁq,1(R3)` ... `Ṁq,l(R3)`"), not the embedding-chain relationship of those
spaces to `L^q`/Lorentz spaces, nor Theorem 1.2's Section-4 proof mechanism (which turns out to
terminate by reconstructing and invoking Tsai's Theorem 2's own local-energy finishing move — a
detail found only by reading the proof, not the abstract or the theorem statement).** Leg 365's
abstract-depth ruling ("Theorem 1's route") is not WRONG about the authors' stated genealogy
(Remarks 1.1/1.2 do cite the Theorem-1/L^q/Lorentz family, not Theorem 2), but abstract depth alone
cannot see that the underlying Morrey HYPOTHESIS strictly widens past what either of the screen's
two encoded tests can reach.
