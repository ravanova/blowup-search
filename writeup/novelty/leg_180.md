## 1. The literature the correction is against (links, not counts)

* **HTW26 — Huang, Tong, Wang, arXiv:2603.25104** (v2, 16 Jun 2026),
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25104>, *Self-similar finite-time blowups with singular profiles
  of the generalized Constantin–Lax–Majda model*. Local copy `Papers/2603.25104.pdf`.
  Read at full text by **leg 112** (`experiments/journal/leg_112.md`), not by search summary.
  The two load-bearing sentences, as leg 112 quotes them from p.1 and p.3:

  > For 𝑎 > 0, we observe one-scale self-similar blowups with regular profiles … In contrast,
  > for 𝑎 ≤ 0, we discover a novel two-scale self-similar blowup scenario …

  and the qualifier leg 112 added, which the repository's own §24 summary had dropped:

  > we call an initial datum **degenerate** if 𝜔 has zero first derivative at the eventual
  > blowup point. The main purpose of this paper is to find out whether and how self-similar
  > blowups can develop from degenerate initial data …

  So the `a ≤ 0` / `a > 0` dichotomy is a statement about **which self-similar blowup
  scenario degenerate initial data produce**, not about which objects exist at which `a`.

* **Same paper, Theorem 2.7 (p.15, proved as Thm 7.9 p.44)** — *"For any 𝑎 ∈ (−∞, 1), the
  generalized Constantin–Lax–Majda model admits a traveling wave solution of the form
  (2.16)"* — and **Theorem 7.10(3) (p.45)**, *"0 < 𝑎 < 1: 𝜔 is compactly supported"*. The
  traveling wave this repository measures at `a > 0` **exists**, published. Its existence is
  not what the `a ≤ 0` sentence is about.

* **HQW25 — Huang, Qin, Wang, arXiv:2401.14615**, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14615> — the
  *exact* `a = 0` (CLM) two-scale traveling wave `Ω₂ = −1/(1+X²)`, which is this repository's
  anchor and the origin of the word "two-scale" in every passage listed below.

* **Okamoto–Sakajo–Wunsch, DCDS 34 (2014) 3155–3170**,
  <https://doi.org/10.3934/dcds.2014.34.3155> — gCLM steady states and traveling waves,
  existence argued numerically **for every `a > 0`**. Already banked in
  `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_P2_LITERATURE_SCOPE.md` §2(c). Independent of HTW26, and it
  points the same way: `a > 0` traveling waves are a real, populated object.

**Consequence for the correction, stated once and reused below.** The `a*` finding is not
withdrawn and no number moves. What is wrong is one word of framing: passages that say *the
two-scale traveling wave survives to `a ≈ 0.5`* read as though the published **two-scale
scenario** were being continued into `a > 0`. It is not. What is continued into `a > 0` is
HQW25's exact `a = 0` two-scale traveling-wave **profile**, under the two-scale
(traveling-wave) **residual** `R₂ = Ω H(Ω) − c_tw Ω_X − a U Ω_X` — an ansatz named after its
`a = 0` anchor. The measured domain is `a ∈ (0, 1]`, sampled at
`a ∈ {0, 0.3, 0.4, 0.45, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.65, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0}`.
