## 4. The three planted/regression controls, and why each was constructed the way it was

**(a) Planted WIDENS-then-close control.** Needed a synthetic exact-SS profile that (i) evades T1
(the global L³ shell integral, over the FULL solid angle, must stay genuinely log-divergent) and
(ii) evades T2 (the specific fitted-ray's magnitude must NOT satisfy
`decays_to_zero_at_infinity()`'s literal check, `mags[-1] < mags[0]` on the one sampled ray) while
(iii) genuinely lying inside Ṁq,1 for some q in (3/2,6). The first attempt (a radially-symmetric
oscillating/bump perturbation of a 1/|x| base) evaded T2 easily but a large enough bump to break
monotonicity ALSO inflated the full-solid-angle L³ shell integral enough to make T1's own
`converged` flag flip to True — an artefact of `l3_norm_ladder()`'s relative-change-of-last-step
convergence check being swamped by one large early rung (documented in this leg's own construction
notes, `experiments/p2_route_b7m_v1.py`'s docstring). The working construction instead makes the
bump ANGULARLY NARROW (concentrated in solid angle on the exact ray
`fitted_far_field_decay_exponent()` samples by default) and radially narrow (centred at r~1000, the
last sampled radius on that ray): large on that one ray (evades T2), negligible as a fraction of the
full-sphere shell integral at each radius (T1's own log-divergence signature survives, evades T1),
and a bounded/localized perturbation of a genuinely decaying base field so the ball-averaged L¹ mass
growth rate is unchanged asymptotically (lands inside Ṁq,1). Measured: T1/T2 verdict `NOT EXCLUDED`,
Morrey verdict `EXCLUDED-BY-MORREY`.

**(b) Real-object control.** The repo's actual DSS large-λ object (leg 351's `field_uB` combined
