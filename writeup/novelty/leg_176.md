DIRECTION.md §176 pre-commits this leg's ceiling, and nothing this pass found moves it.
Everything usable in the origin-`H²` lane is a consequence of `a = 0` **exactness**; leg 163's

no Clay movement.** The novelty question below is therefore only "is the *construction*
new?", never "is the *result* new?" — the result is Xu's.

## Primary source, re-read this leg

**[Xu, arXiv:2607.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19762)** — *The spectral picture of
self-similar collapse in the Constantin–Lax–Majda equation*. Fetched and read at
[the HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19762). Four things were checked, and two of
them changed how this leg is framed:

1. **The space and the origin condition, verbatim**:
   `X = {φ : φ odd, φ, φ″ ∈ L²(0,∞), φ(y) = a₁y + o(y) as y→0}`. This is leg 163's "sharpest
   in-repo finding" restated by the source: the origin condition is the whole difference
   between the realization with a gap and the one without.
2. **Lemma 4.5, verbatim**: `T_z g(y) = ∫₀¹ s^z g(ys) ds` is bounded on `L²(0,∞)` with **exact**
   norm `1/(Re z + ½) = 1/α`. At the certificate point `z = 0` this is `1/α = 2`.
3. **Eq. 4.23**, the resolvent kernel: `u = −b⁻²Φ`, `Φ = I + c₀ + c₁y`,
   `I(y) = ∫₀¹ s^{z−2} G₂(ys) ds`. This is leg 163's G2 gate and this leg's cross-check target.
4. **NEW THIS PASS, and it decides the construction's novelty claim.** Xu's discretizations are
   **compactified-grid Newton continuation** (§2), **log-Mellin discretization** (§3.2), and
   **FFT grids** for the Hilbert transform. He uses **no Laguerre basis, no matrix
   representation of `L₀⁺`, and no finite-section/Galerkin truncation** anywhere. So the
   discrete realization this leg builds is not a re-implementation of Xu's numerics — it is a
   different realization of the same operator. That is the *only* thing this leg can claim as
   construction-novel, and it is a claim about the *method*, not the *theorem*.

**NEW THIS PASS, and it is a pre-emption this leg must respect.** Xu §3.2 records **three
explicit gaps** toward a computer-assisted proof, in his Evans-determinant strand: **(a)** a
uniform large-imaginary-part bound, **(b)** trace-ideal membership of the kernel, **(c)**
quadrature-error bounds in the trace norm. He labels the strand *exploratory* and declines to
enforce them as hard gates. **This leg closes none of the three.** It builds a static
invertibility certificate at `z = 0` on the bordered operator — a different object from the
Evans determinant, in a different strand of the same paper. Any artifact of this leg that reads
as "progress on Xu's recorded gaps" would be false, and this sentence exists so that reading is
foreclosed before the first number is computed.

## Nearest existing machinery, and why it is not this

**[Cadiot–Lessard, arXiv:2302.12877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12877)** — *Rigorous computation
of solutions of semi-linear PDEs on unbounded domains via spectral methods*
([SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst.](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/23M1607507)). Leg 163 named this
as the nearest existing CAP machinery to what a construction leg would need, and flagged it
`fetched, text-extracted, NOT READ` in this repository's `Papers/MANIFEST.md`. Read this pass at
the abstract/method level: fully spectral, Fourier-series **approximate inverses of differential
operators**, closed by **Newton–Kantorovich**, applied to the Kawahara soliton and to eigenpairs
of the linearization about it.

**Why it does not pre-empt this leg, and the reason is lesson 87.** Cadiot–Lessard's approximate
inverse is built for a **multiplier**-shaped operator — a differential operator whose Fourier
symbol is diagonal and grows, so the tail is inverted by dividing by the symbol. The object here
is **shift**-shaped: `L₀⁺ = −1 − y∂_y + i/b` has a *dilation*-generated essential spectrum (the
line `Re λ = −½`), no growing diagonal symbol, and its tail is not invertible by division. That
is exactly the dichotomy leg 57 banked (`2.191 → 11.528`) and lesson 87 names. Their machinery
is the right *shape of argument* and the wrong *shape of operator*. This is a reason to cite
them, not a reason this leg is redundant — and equally, **not** a licence to claim their
constants.

**Related but not this**: [arXiv:2404.04054](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.04054) (constructive
proofs on `H²(e^{|x|²/4})`) and [arXiv:2603.27198](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.27198)
(Gegenbauer-basis stationary-solution proofs) are the same Newton–Kantorovich family in other
weighted-Hilbert realizations. Neither treats a dilation-generated essential spectrum.

## What this leg does NOT claim to have discovered

The construction rests on two facts of classical Laguerre analysis, and this leg claims neither
the discovery nor the naming of either:

* `∫₀^x L_n(t) dt = L_n(x) − L_{n+1}(x)`, which makes the Volterra operator with kernel
  `e^{−(ξ−η)/2}` exactly **lower-bidiagonal** in the Laguerre basis `ℓ_n = L_n(ξ)e^{−ξ/2}`. This
  is a standard consequence of `L_{n+1}′ = L_n′ − L_n`; see the
  [MathWorld Laguerre entry](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/LaguerrePolynomial.html) for the
  identity family. Searching for a bidiagonal-matrix statement of it turned up
  [the Laguerre fractional integro-differentiation paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.05335)
  (Volterra-of-the-second-kind with Laguerre fractional integrals) as the nearest published
  framing, which is a different operator.
* the three-term recurrence `ξL_n = (2n+1)L_n − (n+1)L_{n+1} − nL_{n−1}` and
  `ξL_n′ = n(L_n − L_{n−1})`, which together make the Mellin generator `ξ∂_ξ` **tridiagonal**.
  Also classical.

**What is left as this leg's own**: putting them together, so that `L₀⁺` for *this* operator is
an exactly tridiagonal matrix with rational entries `(−n/2, ½, (n−1)/2)`; observing that Xu's
two symmetry modes then occupy **exactly** `span{ℓ₀, ℓ₁}` (`b⁻² ↔ −(ℓ₀−ℓ₁)`,
`m = yb⁻² ↔ −(i/2)(ℓ₀+ℓ₁)`), so that "modulating out the symmetry modes" is literally truncating
two coefficients; and measuring a certificate diagnostic on it. A search for the combination
(["Laguerre basis Hardy space Mellin generator tridiagonal
Volterra"](https://www.google.com/search?q=Laguerre+basis+Hardy+space+Mellin+generator+tridiagonal+Volterra))
returned nothing on the combination — the hits are Laguerre-expansion Hardy spaces
([J. Fourier Anal. Appl.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00041-008-9020-6)), a
different subject.

**And this is worth exactly what the ceiling says it is worth.** A new discrete realization of an
operator whose inverse is already known in closed form is infrastructure, not a theorem.
