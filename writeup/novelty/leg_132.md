## 2. The structural argument, stated BEFORE the run, so the run can refute it

The repair's own account says every guard can only move the reported number **DOWN**. Taking that
at face value, three things could still be wrong, and they are the three this leg is built to see:

1. **A shared-defect blind spot.** The exact reference (`ref_l1_linf`) and the repaired evaluator
   were tuned against each other across two commits on the same branch. If the reference's own
   restriction convention (column 0 deleted) or its exact-rational conversion is wrong in the same
   direction as the fix, the battery reports 0 violations that are not 0. **Prediction: an
   independently derived reference agrees to the last bit on every case**, because both are exact
   rational arithmetic on the same floats — so a *disagreement here would be the sharpest possible
   finding*, and an agreement is a real (if quiet) control.
2. **An overshoot the repair's own second clause cannot see.** The clause is `≤ 1e-9` relative,
   downward only, at J = 200/300/400, α = 1.5, γ = 0.5. The banked line also runs at
   α = 1.4, γ = 0.15 and J = 800/1600, where `max|A|`, the cancellation ratio and the certified
   `rel` bound are all different. **Prediction: the seven rows move down by ~1e-12 and no more**,
   because `NORM_SLACK = 1e-12` dominates the deflation on a benign operator; a row that moved by
   ≫1e-12, or UP, or whose `argmax` changed, is a regression.
3. **Deflation is admissible; losing a bound is not obviously so.** DIRECTION.md's own words: "a
   deflation that moves the bound down is admissible, one that moves any validated magnitude is
   not". The repair reports `sound_but_zero` rising 41 → 48 (7 cases lost their bound to guard 3)
   while `sound, nonzero` rose 118 → 161. Those are *adversarial* cases, not validated magnitudes,
   so they do not trip the gate — but the count is a magnitude this leg re-measures rather than
   inherits, and **`rejected` must be 0 on every production row**, which is the same question
   asked where it does count.

A fourth possibility is recorded because it is the one that would be embarrassing to discover
later: the repair changed `_Quotient`'s *ordering* semantics (search compares `r_scaled`,
certification happens once at the end). The claim is that the search sees a bit-identical
ordering to the pre-repair code. **Prediction: `family_argmax` is identical on all seven rows and
on every adversarial case where both modules produce one.** This is checkable only with the
pre-repair module in hand, which is why §3 reconstructs it.

## 3. Pre-committed decision rule (fixed BEFORE any number was re-measured)

DIRECTION.md's gate, quoted verbatim, both branches:

> **Gate.** Post-repair, does solver/op_lower.py (a) return a true lower bound (L <= N_true)
> on every one of leg 101's 209 original adversarial cases in an independent re-run, and
> (b) reproduce the 307.878-decade headroom and every other validated magnitude to the digit?
>   yes -> Repair confirmed sound and non-regressive by an independent run. Bank the 209-case
>          battery as a permanent regression suite.
>   no  -> An incomplete fix or a repair regression. Report the exact case precisely; escalate
>          as a priority finding, do not patch under this leg's own authority.

Operationalised, and no other rule decides it:

**Clause (a) — SOUNDNESS.** Over the 209 gate-deciding cases, the count of records with
`L > N_true` at **any** number of ULP must be **0**, where `N_true` is the exact induced operator
norm over the searched subspace `{g : g_0 = 0}`, in exact rationals, computed by **this leg's own
route** (§4). Grading is at 0 ULP — stricter than leg 101's own 1-ULP concession, and matching
the repair's own pre-committed rule. `L = 0` against a large `N_true` is a **PASS** (weak, true);
a raised exception is a **PASS**; a case whose reference is itself non-computable is **OUT OF
SCOPE** by the gate's own words and is counted so the excluded fraction stays visible.

**Clause (b) — NON-REGRESSION.** A **validated magnitude** is, exhaustively and fixed now:

| # | magnitude | banked value | admissible move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | headroom decades, J = 200/300/400 | 307.878177 / 307.878142 / 307.878127 | **0 exactly** (bit-identical; nothing about candidate construction changed) |
| 2 | seven banked v10 `lower` rows | `p2_route_d_v10_lower.json` `w1_ladder` J = 200/400/800/1600, `w2_operating` J = 200/400/800 | **down only, ≤ 1e-9 relative** (the repair's own budget; observed 1.648e-12) |
| 3 | seven banked v10 `sign_patterns` rows | same file | **down only, ≤ 1e-9 relative** |
| 4 | seven banked v10 `argmax` | `bump3.12/0.50` ×6, `bump3.01/0.50` ×1 | **unchanged, exactly** |
| 5 | `rejected` / `saturated` on every production row | 0 / 0 | **0 / 0** |
| 6 | leg 101's headline battery counts | 209 gate-deciding of 408, 199 excluded | **exact** (a different count means a different battery, and the re-run is not the re-run) |

**REGRESSION** := any magnitude moving UP, or moving DOWN by more than its admissible budget, or
an `argmax`/count changing. **ADMISSIBLE DEFLATION** := `L_post ≤ L_pre` **and** `L_post ≤ N_true`,
within the budget. The asymmetry is the point and it is DIRECTION.md's, not this leg's.

**Discipline: magnitudes, never booleans.** Every case reports `L_post`, `L_pre`, `N_true`, the
ratio `L/N_true` in full precision and the excess in ULP; the headlines are the **maximum ratio**
and the **worst relative move**, not "sound / not sound".

## 4. Battery design, fixed before the run

**Everything is written fresh in `experiments/p2_route_olb_v1_postrepair.py`. Leg 101's runner is
not imported, not subclassed and not copied** — it is read as a *specification* and re-derived,
because a re-run that imports the artifact under test is not an independent re-run. Four strands:

**S1 — the 209-case battery, independently reconstructed.** The operator zoo (19 operators),
weight zoo (9), grid zoo (3) and pairing rule are rebuilt from leg 101's documented specification,
plus its 8-point denormal dimension sweep. **Case counts are a gate magnitude (§3 row 6):** if
this reconstruction does not land on 408 total / 209 gate-deciding / 199 excluded, the
reconstruction is wrong or the battery is not what it says it is, and that is reported before
anything else is read.

**S2 — an independently derived exact reference.** Leg 101 used the *closed form*
`N = max_{i,j} t_i|A_ij|/u_j`. This leg instead **enumerates the extreme points** `±e_j/u_j` of
the weighted-`l1` ball, forms `A g` and takes the exact weighted sup, all in `Fraction`. Same
theorem, different derivation — and it validates the closed form itself rather than assuming it.
Agreement between the two routes is reported as a magnitude (expected: exact, every case). For the
`l2→l2` pair, `sigma_max` is taken from `eigvalsh(MᵀM)` rather than `svd`, a different LAPACK
path; per leg 101's own rule that reference is float and may decide only the overflow class.

**S3 — the pre-repair module, reconstructed and measured, not quoted.** `ef0ea2e:solver/op_lower.py`
(the last commit before `147e8c2`) is written to the scratchpad and imported under a distinct name.
This is what makes A/B possible: every adversarial case and every production row is run through
**both** modules in the same process, so 47 → 0, the `argmax` invariance, and the `PRE` literals in
`p2_route_ola_v1_bench_check.py` become **measurements** instead of assertions. Nothing is written
into the repository tree by this step and `solver/op_lower.py` is untouched.

**S4 — the known-answer line, all seven rows.** `w1_ladder` (α=1.5, γ=0.5, J=200/400/800/1600) and
`w2_operating` (α=1.4, γ=0.15, J=200/400/800) are re-run at the banked call
(`n_centre=30, n_step=40`) through both modules and compared against
`writeup/data/p2_route_d_v10_lower.json`, plus the 307.878-decade headroom at J=200/300/400.

**The positive control, which can report the other answer** (lesson 90 — a control that cannot
come out differently is not a control): the pre-repair module is run through the *same* S1 grader.
It must report **47 violations**. If both modules report 0, the grader is broken, not the repair
vindicated — and the run says so instead of claiming a pass. Symmetrically, S2's two reference
routes disagreeing would indict the reference, not the module.

## 7. FINDINGS (appended after construction, 2026-08-06)

§§0–6 were committed before any number was re-measured (`8a029a5`). What the run then produced,
over **408 cases in ~80 s** (209 gate-deciding, 199 excluded), through **both** modules:

**Gate answer: YES on both clauses.** Max finite `L/N_true` = **0.999999999998994804** over the
209 decided cases, **0** violations at 0 ULP; the seven banked v10 rows down only, worst
**1.648e-12** relative against a git-reconstructed pre-repair module, all seven `argmax`
unchanged, headroom bit-identical between the two modules at J = 200/300/400. Full narrative:
`experiments/journal/leg_132.md`.

### What §2's four predictions did

1. **Prediction 1 held, and it was the precondition for everything else.** The independently
   derived reference (extreme-point enumeration) agrees with leg 101's closed form on **208/208**
   exact-reference cases, in exact rationals. No shared-defect blind spot.
2. **Prediction 2 held.** Seven rows, both (α,γ), J up to 1600: every move is
   **1.08e-12 – 1.67e-12**, i.e. the `NORM_SLACK = 1e-12` deflation, three orders inside the
   `1e-9` budget, down on all seven, `argmax` unchanged on all seven, `rejected`/`saturated` 0.
3. **Prediction 3 held, and the count came out on the nose** — `sound_but_zero` 41 → 48,
   `sound, nonzero` 118 → 161, `rejected` 0 on every production row.
4. **Prediction 4 held.** `family_argmax` identical on all seven banked rows and across the
   battery: the repair did not change which direction the search picks, only the value reported
   for it. This was checkable *only* because §4's S3 reconstructed the pre-repair module.

### What the pass did NOT anticipate, and it is this leg's one new number

**Clause (b) has a noise floor, and it had never been measured.** The reconstructed pre-repair
module reproduces the banked literals on **0 of 7** rows bitwise, landing within **2.2588e-14**
relative of them, **two-sided** — `np.linalg.inv`'s cross-build last-bit reproducibility, not
anything in `solver/op_lower.py`. So the repair's 1.648e-12 deflation is **72.9×** the floor
beneath the literals it was graded against, not the ~600× of margin that "1.6e-12 against a 1e-9
budget" reads as. The conclusion survives; the ratio, not the raw move, is the honest magnitude.
`p2_route_ola_v1_bench_check.py` could not have seen this — it grades against those literals.

### The two instruments repaired mid-run, both reported

- **This leg's own `l2→l2` reference excluded 16 cases.** `eigvalsh(MᵀM)` was chosen for LAPACK
  independence from leg 101's `svd(M)`, but the Gram **squares** `M`, which overflows on 7
  operators at the `decay_huge_entry` weight where `svd` is fine. First run: 193 gate-deciding
  instead of 209, control 37 instead of 47. **The pre-committed case-count magnitude (§3 row 6)
  is what caught it** — that clause existed for exactly this reason. Fixed by one more exact
  power-of-two rescale of `M`, exponent carried through.
- **The first grader was stricter than §3.** It required 0 adversarial headlines to have risen,
  and 15 had. But §3 enumerates six validated magnitudes and §2.3 says in advance that
  adversarial headlines are not among them — grading on it would have decided the gate on a rule
  invented after the data. Corrected to the pre-committed rule; the 15 are reported as a
  magnitude, and every one of them is still under the true norm.

### The ban, re-checked against what was actually built

**"Another Route-D bound-sharpening leg"** is not tripped. No solver module was edited under
either branch, no candidate direction, shape family, ascent or operator was added, and the
decision rule treated an *upward* move of any banked bracket row as a **failure**. The 15
adversarial headlines that rose did so through the repair recovering bounds previously lost to
overflow — the evaluation, not the search — and all 15 remain under the true norm.
