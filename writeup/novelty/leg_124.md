# Leg 124 — Route-FSA novelty pass (run and committed BEFORE construction)

**Date:** 2026-08-06. **Agent:** LEG-124. **Branch:** `leg/124-fsa-v1`.
**Verdict: `PROCEED_AS_ROBUSTNESS_AUDIT`.**

This leg builds no new mathematics, sharpens no bound, and claims no novel mechanism. It
audits one *software* property of `solver/finite_support.py`: whether the module can return a
**finite, plausible-looking wrong number** under adversarial, degenerate or poisoned inputs
instead of flagging them. `solver/finite_support.py` is **READ-ONLY** under this leg, under
either branch of the gate — the gate's yes-branch escalates and does not repair.

## 0. The explicit non-claim, stated first

`solver/finite_support.py` is **SUPERSEDED** by `solver/first_integral.py` (Route-D v14) and
its own docstring, lines 1 and 22-34, says *"DO NOT USE"*, *"DOES NOT CONVERGE YET"*, *"no
number it produces should be quoted"*. Nothing in this leg contradicts that, revises it, or
tries to make the module converge. In particular this leg does **not** claim any value of
`X_c`, of `||A||` (the module's "kill switch"), of the edge exponent `p = 1/a`, or of anything
about the two-scale profile. Every number produced here is a **discrepancy magnitude, a case
count, or a test outcome**. The audit question is arithmetic and domain hygiene of one module.

Why audit a superseded module at all, honestly stated: it is the last solver module in the
repository with **no dedicated test file of any kind** (below), the `capabilities.py` entry
records its validation as literally *"nothing"* (`capabilities.py:535-538`), and leg 107's
bench-repair branch explicitly recorded its `np.clip` at line 115 as *"a known second
instance ... left alone rather than silently widened into"* because it was out of that
branch's territory. This leg's territory is exactly that instance and its siblings. The
deliverable on either branch is a **permanent executable regression battery** where there is
currently none — lesson 68, *a check that is not executable decays at the rate of memory*.
