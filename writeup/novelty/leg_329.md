### 7a. Precision protocol, fixed now

`prec = 200` decimal working digits; nodes with `min(θ, π−θ) < τ` are recomputed in MP,
`τ = 1e-1` for the reported run. `φ` and the quadrature weights are **not** patched: both are
cancellation-free by inspection (`φ_B = (2 sin θ/2)^(−γ)(2 cos θ/2)^(−2)`,
`φ_E = (1+X²)³/(2X⁴)` with `X = tan(θ/2)`; the geometric panel edges are exact binary scalings
of `h`), and patching only where the cancellation is, is leg 312's own protocol.
