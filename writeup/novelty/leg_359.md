**Content query, both directions controlled:**

Query `abs:"local energy estimates" AND abs:"self-similar" AND abs:"Navier-Stokes"` →
`totalResults=2`:

1. **Bradshaw & Tsai, `arXiv:1801.08060`**, *"Discretely self-similar solutions to the Navier-Stokes
   equations with data in `L²_loc` satisfying the local energy inequality"* (2018). Abstract,
   verbatim: *"Chae and Wolf recently constructed discretely self-similar solutions to the
   Navier-Stokes equations for any discretely self similar data in `L²_loc`... In this note...
   we construct discretely self-similar suitable weak solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations
   that satisfy the classical local energy inequality of Scheffer and Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg."*
   **This is a CONSTRUCTION (existence) paper, by Tsai's own later coauthor, of NONTRIVIAL DSS
   solutions satisfying local energy estimates** — for general `L²_loc` DSS initial data, a
   forward-evolution existence question, not a backward self-similar blow-up-profile Liouville
   question. It does not conflict with this leg's finding (Theorem 2's Liouville mechanism
   requires the exact SS ansatz (1.2), not DSS); if anything it is consistent supporting context:
   nontrivial DSS objects satisfying local energy estimates are known to exist in a different
   (initial-value, not self-similar-profile) setting, so "DSS + local energy estimates" is not a
   combination the literature has shown forces triviality in general — reinforcing that the
   ansatz gate (SS vs DSS), not the decay/L³ gate, is where this leg's adjudication should and
   does land. **Located, read at abstract depth, flagged as adjacent — not adjudicated further;
   out of this leg's gate**, which is about NRS/Tsai's Liouville theorems specifically.
2. Second hit, unrelated: a 2D forward self-similar NS paper — checked, off-topic, not pursued
   further.

**No result in this query duplicates this leg's adjudication.** Nothing in the two hits states or
adjudicates whether Tsai 1998's Theorem 2 reaches a log-divergent (not-in-L³) backward
self-similar profile, or whether that reach extends to DSS. This leg's finding — that Theorem 2's
own worked example (eq. 1.5, `U(y) ~ A(y/|y|)/|y|`) is essentially this repository's own measured
decay exponent (`-0.9971` vs Tsai's exact `-1`), and that the ansatz gate rather than the decay
gate is what protects the DSS object — is not present in the searched literature and is not
present anywhere in this repository prior to this leg.
