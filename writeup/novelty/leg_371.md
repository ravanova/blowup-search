# Leg 371 — Route-SFX — novelty note

**Question.** Leg 367 measured the E5 family's `max|Re(lambda)|` noise floor over
non-structural (continuum) eigenvalues of `RescaledFlow.generator` tracking
`K * machine_epsilon` (1.8e-14 to 3.5e-13 across K=48/96/144/192). Leg 369 showed
that GENERIC non-normal eigensolver backward-stability theory (LAPACK Users' Guide
Ch.4, Tables 4.5/4.6, the always-true disk bound) cannot license that tightness at
this matrix's condition numbers (~1e15) -- either the theorem's precondition is
violated by 4-5 orders of magnitude, or the unconditional bound is true but ~19
orders too loose to be informative. This leg (the thread's TERMINAL leg, closing it
either way per its own gate) asks whether the matrix's SPECIFIC structure -- its
realness, and in particular how a real matrix's genuinely complex-conjugate
eigenvalue pairs are computed -- explains the gap.

**What is new here, and what is not.** Leg 369 already established, correctly, that
the generic per-eigenvalue condition-number channel (kappa_i = 1/s_i, computed via
nearly-parallel left/right eigenvectors) does not explain the floor. This leg does
not dispute that. What it adds is that the generic channel is not the only one: for
a REAL matrix, LAPACK's dgeev (called by `numpy.linalg.eigvals`, per leg 369's own
read of numpy's source) does not compute a genuinely complex-conjugate eigenvalue
pair's real part through that channel at all. It computes it through the REAL
SCHUR FORM: a real, orthogonal similarity T = Q^T A Q, block upper-triangular with
1x1 (real) and 2x2 (complex-conjugate-pair) diagonal blocks. LAPACK's own source
(`dlanv2.f`) shows the pair's real part is literally the SAME floating-point scalar
(the standardized block's shared diagonal entry `AA = DD`) handed to both
eigenvalues of the pair -- not two independently perturbed numbers. And the
deflation logic that finalizes each block (`dlahqr.f` for the small-matrix path at
K=48, `dlaqr3.f`'s aggressive-early-deflation window within `dlaqr0` for K=96/144/
192, per the documented K>75 crossover) accepts a block once its residual coupling
is small relative to machine epsilon TIMES a LOCAL norm (the block's own diagonal
magnitudes), not relative to the eigenvalue's global condition number. Both facts
are quoted directly from LAPACK's primary source (fetched live from
Reference-LAPACK's GitHub mirror), including the deflation criterion's own
attribution to Ahues & Tisseur, "A New Deflation Criterion for the QR Algorithm"
(LAPACK Working Note 122, 14 Jan 1997 -- title page fetched and read directly).

**Verification, not assertion.** The candidate mechanism was checked, not assumed:
an independent computation of the real Schur decomposition (`scipy.linalg.schur`,
LAPACK's `dgees` driver -- a different LAPACK entry point than `dgeev`/`eigvals`,
sharing the same core Hessenberg-QR machinery) of the SAME generator matrix at all
four of leg 367's resolutions, extracting each 2x2 block's half-trace directly
(the exact quantity `dlanv2.f` identifies as the shared real part), reproduces leg
367's measured `max|Re(lambda)|` to within a factor 0.93-1.68 at every single point
-- versus leg 369's generic route, whose backward-error/threshold ratio was 4-5
orders too loose and whose disk bound missed by roughly 19 orders of magnitude.
The structural mismatch check (trace(A) is an exact structural invariant, measured
at exactly -1.0 for all four K; the float64 discrepancy between trace(A) and the
computed trace(T) grows with K in the same family as the block-level mismatch) is
an independent cross-check of the same mechanism, not a second free parameter fit
to the same four points.

**What is honestly not claimed.** This is a mechanism-derived, numerically-verified
explanation at checkable precision, not a closed-form theorem with a rigorously
proven universal constant (Ahues & Tisseur's paper is about the deflation
criterion's design and convergence properties, not a stated-and-proven bound of the
form `max|Re(lambda)| <= C * K * eps * ||A||_2` for an arbitrary real matrix). What
is established is that the SPECIFIC, verifiable structural property (realness, via
the shared-real-part Schur-block mechanism and its local-norm deflation gate) is
the right quantity to check -- and that it tracks the measured floor tightly, where
the generic non-normal theory leg 369 exhausted does not.
