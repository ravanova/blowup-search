## Channel 3 — what leg 150 already measured, and where an independent run can differ

Read in full: `experiments/journal/leg_150.md` (200 lines), `writeup/novelty/leg_150.md`,
`experiments/p2_route_cnr_v1_repair.py` (464 lines), `writeup/data/p2_route_cnr_v1_repair.json`.

Leg 150's self-report claims: 10/10 gate-scoped failing cases fixed, 7/7 pins inverted, **40/40**
clean cases bitwise identical over **3570** float64 `==` comparisons, `critical_radius` 13/13

Four places an independent re-run is a different measurement, not a repeat:

1. **Case provenance.** Leg 150's arm A rebuilds the failing cases from its own reading of the
   mechanisms. This leg reconstructs them from **leg 114's banked JSON**
   (`writeup/data/p2_route_cna_v1_adversarial.json`), where the 8 `SILENT_WRONG` entries carry
   their own `magnitudes` blocks — `om0_zeros`, `om0_ones`, `om0_1e-8_times_anchor`,

   `pure_sign_noise`, `chain_bogus_radius_into_zero_order`. The adversary is read from the
   record, not re-derived from the repair's understanding of it.
2. **Differential breadth.** Leg 150 compared 40 clean cases. An independent check should widen
   the grid rather than replay it, and should compare on cases leg 150 did **not** pick.
3. **Consumer-level reproduction.** Leg 150 asserted a blast-radius argument for the consumers.
   `solver/turning_point.py` and `experiments/p2_route_d_v12_defect.py` import this module and
   can be exercised end-to-end pre- vs post-repair rather than argued about.
4. **The pins themselves.** Re-running `test_collocation_newton_adversarial.py` and
   `test_collocation_newton.py` is a check leg 150 could not independently provide, having
   authored the inversions.
