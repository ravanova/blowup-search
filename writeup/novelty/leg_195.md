## What "independent" means here, and what it does not

`experiments/p2_route_pqver_v1_verification.py` does **not** import, exec, or copy anything
from `experiments/p2_route_port_v1_bordered_evidence.py` or
`experiments/p2_route_port_v2_reach_evidence.py`. It reads only the two curated JSON files
and re-implements the shared machinery from scratch:

* the **"half a unit in its own last quoted digit"** rule is rebuilt on `decimal`, reading
  the literal's exponent via `Decimal.as_tuple()` rather than parsing the string by hand;
* the **v2 slopes are re-fitted** with `numpy.polyfit` from the five rungs, not read from
  the stored `verdict` fields (the stored field is then checked against the re-fit as its
  own separate row, agreeing to `3.6e-11`);
* the **`-0.437` reach power law was re-identified**, not assumed: it is the log-log fit of
  `|ratio - chl_ratio|` against `X_max` over all six reach rungs, which reproduces the
  stored `-0.43697400622689114` to `2e-16`.

It is **not** independent of the underlying computation. The curated JSON is legs 46/47's
and is taken as given, as it must be for a reproduction check — this pass verifies that the
*prose matches its data*, which is exactly the claim leg 60 made, not that the data is right.

**`capabilities.py` was grepped before anything was built.** It is an object registry
(`find`/`modules`); it holds no ledger, rounding or reproduction machinery, so there was
nothing to reuse and nothing was rebuilt that already existed.

## The two pre-committed clauses

Fixed after reading `e0eba8e` in full, before the runner was written, and recorded verbatim
in the runner's docstring.

> **V1 (the correction itself).** Does an independent re-derivation straight from the
> committed curated JSON confirm all three of leg 60's landed corrections
> (`28x -> 63x`, `-2.541222 -> -2.541024`, `1.168% -> 1.169%`) **and** both ban-bearing
> numbers (`distance/r_max = 1.55e+08`, slope `+0.4703`), each to the precision the
> corrected prose states?

> **V2 (completeness).** Does any **uncorrected** copy of a corrected literal survive in the
> tracked tree, and is any such copy **consumed as a number** by live code?

`V1` was kept separate from `V2` deliberately, so that a pass on the correction's arithmetic
could not be used to wave away an incomplete propagation, nor the reverse.
