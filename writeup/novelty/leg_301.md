## 2. External channel — the queries, verbatim, with the links returned

All queries run 2026-08-11 via web search.

### Q1 — `Chebyshev series computer-assisted proof radii polynomial nonlocal operator Hilbert transform`

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00596 — Slevinsky & Olver, *A fast and well-conditioned spectral method for singular integral equations*
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.02073 — *Truncated Hilbert Transform: Uniqueness and a Chebyshev series Expansion Approach*
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.07412 — *Continuation of homoclinic orbits in the suspension bridge equation: a computer-assisted proof*
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.01491 — *Computer-assisted proof of shear-induced chaos in stochastically perturbed Hopf systems*

**What it establishes.** Chebyshev-coefficient CAPs are standard and live in the *same* `ℓ¹_ν`
weighted Banach-algebra framing as the Fourier ones (`|a₀| + 2Σ|a_k|ν^k`). And the Hilbert/Cauchy
transform of a weighted Chebyshev polynomial has a closed form that makes it **banded between two
different bases** (Chebyshev in, ultraspherical `C^{(1)}` out) — i.e. a **shift**, not a
multiplier, in any single basis.

### Q2 — `Cadiot arXiv 2505.03091 computer-assisted proof dominant part operator`

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03091 — Cadiot, *Stability analysis for localized solutions in PDEs and nonlocal equations on ℝ^m* (6 May 2025, 30pp, math.AP)
- https://arxiv.org/html/2505.03091 — full text
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00745 — Gómez-Serrano, *Computer-assisted proofs in PDE: a survey*
- https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16693 — suspension-bridge travelling waves (same dominant-part philosophy)

**What it establishes.** The recorded (leg 57 / VER-D) dominance-hypothesis observation is
confirmed as this paper's structural pattern: the Jacobian is split into a **dominant linear part
whose Fourier symbol is explicitly invertible / asymptotically controllable**, plus a
compact/decaying remainder, and the spectral control runs through a pseudo-diagonalization plus a
**generalized Gershgorin disk theorem**. Gershgorin is the operative word: a Gershgorin argument
is *definitionally* a statement that the diagonal dominates the off-diagonal row mass. An operator
whose dominant part has an identically zero diagonal has no Gershgorin argument at all.

### Q3 — `ultraspherical spectral method singular integral equations banded Hilbert transform Olver Townsend well-conditioned`

- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021999116306507 — Slevinsky & Olver, JCP
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.00304 — ultraspherical method for Fredholm/Volterra integro-differential equations of convolution type
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021999120301571 — symmetrizing the ultraspherical method for self-adjoint problems
- https://www2.cms.math.ca/Events/winter20/abs/pdf/sms.pdf — *Spectral Methods and Singular Integral Equations*

### Q4 — `wavelet basis validated numerics rigorous computer-assisted proof PDE multiresolution`

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.18361 — Cadiot, Jaquette, Lessard, Takayasu, *Validated matrix multiplication transform for orthogonal polynomials with applications to computer-assisted proofs for PDEs*
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07628 — Harnish–Matouš, adaptive multiresolution wavelet method (error control, **not** validated existence)
- https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-28884-8_1 — Dahmen-school wavelet multiresolution for stationary PDEs

**What it establishes.** **No interval-arithmetic CAP in a wavelet basis was found.** Rigorous PDE
CAPs are overwhelmingly Fourier / Chebyshev / orthogonal-polynomial; wavelet work supplies *a
posteriori* error control, not validated existence. A wavelet candidate would be a build from
zero with no existing machinery.

### Q5 — `Malmquist-Takenaka basis Hilbert transform diagonal Hardy space real line rational functions spectral method`

- http://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/na/NA_papers/NA2019_03.pdf — Iserles & Webb, *A family of orthogonal rational functions … with a skew-Hermitian differentiation matrix*
- https://arxiv.org/html/2407.04013v1 — *Approximation of rogue waves using Malmquist–Takenaka functions*
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1007570420304019 — *Analysis of Malmquist–Takenaka–Christov rational approximations with applications to the nonlinear Benjamin equation*
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.08652 — efficient spectral method for the fractional Schrödinger equation on the real line
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022123622000817 — a.e. convergence of Malmquist–Takenaka series
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.00413 — semiclassical Schrödinger on the real line

**What it establishes — the load-bearing external fact of this leg.** The MT system
`φₙ(x) = iⁿ√(2/π)(1+2ix)ⁿ/(1−2ix)^{n+1}`, `n ∈ ℤ`, is a complete orthonormal basis of `L²(ℝ)`
whose **non-negative indices span the Hardy space `H²` of the upper half-plane and whose negative
indices span its conjugate**. Consequently the Riesz/Szegő projection — and therefore the
**Hilbert transform — is diagonal in this basis, acting as multiplication by `∓i`**. Separately,
the **differentiation matrix is tridiagonal and skew-Hermitian** (Iserles–Webb), and coefficients
are computable by FFT. The MTC/Benjamin paper states the exact fit: the basis suits "Fourier
multipliers whose symbols are not smooth at the origin — precisely the Hilbert-transform-type
symbol `sgn(ξ)`", and MT is designed for solutions with **algebraically slow decay**, the case
where Fourier methods are notoriously bad.

### Q6 — `Fourier-Gevrey space computer-assisted proof analytic norm blow-up De Gregorio Cordoba`

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.05422 — Chen–Hou–Huang, asymptotically self-similar blowup of the Hou–Luo model
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.06387 — Chen–Hou–Huang, finite-time blowup of De Gregorio
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40324-019-00186-x — Gómez-Serrano CAP survey
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.04194 — blow-up criterion for 3D NSE in Sobolev–Gevrey spaces (analytic, not computer-assisted)

**What it establishes.** No CAP for a Córdoba/De Gregorio-type model runs in a Fourier–Gevrey
space; Gevrey appears only in analytic blow-up *criteria*. The certified 1D blow-up results
(Chen–Hou–Huang) use weighted `H^k`/energy methods with interval arithmetic, not a Gevrey norm.

### Q7 — `Nakao Plum Watanabe computer-assisted proof finite element H^1 eigenvalue bound Newton-Kantorovich nonlinear PDE`

- https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-981-13-7669-6 — Nakao, Plum, Watanabe, *Numerical Verification Methods and Computer-Assisted Proofs for PDEs*, Springer SSCM 53 (2019)
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1007570421004883 — CAP for stationary Navier–Stokes over 3D domains
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.13896 — CAPs for the many steady states of a chemotaxis model

**What it establishes.** A genuinely *different technology*, not a different basis in the same
technology: Part I (Nakao/Watanabe) is FEM projection with constructive a-priori error estimates;
Part II (Plum) replaces the approximate-inverse bound entirely with **explicit eigenvalue bounds
for the linearization**. Neither requires a diagonally dominant approximate inverse.

### Q8 — `rigorous numerics validated interval arithmetic Hermite functions real line unbounded domain computer-assisted proof`

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.12877 — Cadiot–Lessard–Nave, *Rigorous computation of solutions of semi-linear PDEs on unbounded domains via spectral methods*
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10450 — non-radial localized patterns in planar Swift–Hohenberg
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.08044 — scaling-optimized Hermite approximation

**What it establishes.** **No validated interval-arithmetic CAP directly in a Hermite basis was
found.** The unbounded-domain CAP state of the art (Cadiot–Lessard–Nave) is *still Fourier*, with
compactly supported trace constructions, inside `H^s(ℝ^m)`.

### Q9 — `"rigorous numerics" OR "computer-assisted proof" rational orthogonal basis Hardy space Hilbert transform diagonal SQG Cordoba`

- https://sites.brown.edu/jgs/papers/ — Gómez-Serrano's papers (SQG CAPs; Castro–Córdoba–Gómez-Serrano)
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03920 — *Linear instability of a Burgers–Hilbert travelling wave* (partly computer-assisted, Hilbert-transform structure)
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.00157 — adaptive Fourier decomposition of slice regular functions (rational orthogonal system in a Hardy space, unrelated to fluids)

**What it establishes — the novelty claim itself.** **No paper was found that uses a
Malmquist–Takenaka / Christov rational Hardy basis inside a rigorous-numerics framework, for a
fluid transport model or anything else.** The two literatures exist and do not touch: MT is used
in (non-rigorous) spectral methods for exactly the operators in question, and rigorous numerics
for Hilbert-transform problems is done in Fourier or Chebyshev. This is a gap in the external
literature, not only in this repository.

## 3. Novelty verdict

- The **question** as posed — *enumerate* candidate spaces/bases and screen **each** against
  **all three** measured death mechanisms — has never been asked here. `grep -ril "fourth
  space\|fourth basis\|FOURTH space/basis"` over `*.md`/`*.py` returns **8 files**:
  `plan_of_record.py` (the ban wording), `experiments/journal/leg_262.md`,
  `experiments/journal/leg_285.md`, `experiments/journal/leg_260.md`,
  `experiments/p2_route_h2cv_v1_postconstruction.py`, `experiments/p2_route_nfs_v1_scoping.py`,
  and `writeup/novelty/leg_{249,256,273}.md`. Every one of them is either a **ban-walk
  non-contact line** ("this is not that leg") or leg 262's single named candidate. **No leg has
  ever run the enumeration.** The honest statement is therefore *not* "no leg has ever named a
  candidate" — leg 262 did — but **"no leg has ever produced a candidate table, and no candidate
  has ever been screened against mechanisms 2 and 3."**
- Of the five candidate classes enumerated, **four are untried here and one (weighted `ℓ¹`
  Fourier) is realization 1** — so the candidate table contains no accidental re-proposal of a
  dead realization.
- The MT/Hardy candidate is novel **externally** as well (Q9), which is a cost as well as a
  virtue: there is no existing validated MT transform to inherit, and §2's Q4/Q8 links
  (arXiv:2411.18361, arXiv:2302.12877) are the nearest transferable machinery.

**Distinctness from Route-D v3 (`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTED_SPACES.md`).** That leg asked *which pair of
diagonally weighted `ℓ¹` spaces works* and answered **none**, for one operator, in one basis. This
leg asks *which basis at all*, and takes v3's no-go as an **input constraint** on the candidate
table (§1). The two do not overlap: v3 varies the weight at fixed basis; this leg varies the
basis.

**No ban is touched.** This leg builds nothing in `ℓ¹`-Fourier or collocation machinery, on any
model. It proposes; only the lift condition lifts.
