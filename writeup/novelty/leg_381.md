# Leg 381 novelty pass — Route-CLOC: verifying CLAY_OBLIGATIONS §4

**Committed before the runner existed.** This is the pass required by ORCHESTRATION §6 before any
construction on this leg.

## 0. What kind of pass this is

This leg makes **no new mathematical claim**. It is a *verification* leg: it re-derives an energy
scaling that is textbook, and it reads a primary source (the Clay problem statement) to check a
reviewer's paraphrase of it. Disclosed up front, because the honest novelty verdict on the
mathematics is **nil, and deliberately so**:

* The similarity-variable energy identity `∫|u|²dx = (T*−t)^{1/2}∫|U|²dy` for backward
  self-similar 3D NS is standard and dates to the Leray-ansatz literature; it is used explicitly in
  Nečas–Růžička–Šverák (Acta Math. 176, 1996) and in Tsai (ARMA 143, 1998) as the reason a
  self-similar profile in `L²` would give a finite-energy solution. Nothing in step (i) below is
  new to the world.
* The DSS variant (profile periodic in `s = −log(T*−t)` with period `2 log λ`) is likewise standard
  in the DSS literature the repository has already read in full: Chae–Wolf `arXiv:1610.09464`
  (leg 253, leg 330) states the Type-I bound for `λ`-DSS solutions directly.
* The Clay statement is a published primary document (Fefferman, *Existence and smoothness of the
  Navier–Stokes equation*, Clay Mathematics Institute official problem description). Reading it is
  reading, not discovery.

**What is new is repository-local only:** this repository had never (a) read the official statement's
own text, (b) written the energy arithmetic down, or (c) put magnitudes on the cutoff. That is the
whole claim, and it is a Tier-2-ceiling bookkeeping claim, not a mathematical one. **No link of the
`L1 → L4` chain moves. Clay stays ~0.05%.**

## 2. Capability grep (the standing pre-build requirement)

`grep -n "interval\|enclosure\|decay" capabilities.py` — the relevant existing capabilities are

is leg 382's instrument in slot C; this leg does not build it and does not depend on its landing —

substituted when it exists.

## 3. Instrument disclosure

The numerical part uses a **synthetic** exactly-divergence-free, exactly-`λ`-DSS field with a
prescribed algebraic far-field exponent and a prescribed log-periodic modulation. It is not the
route-4 candidate profile and is not claimed to be. Its only job is to catch an algebra error in the
derivation — if the closed-form scaling law and the quadrature disagree, the derivation is wrong.
A synthetic field can confirm an identity and can refute one; it cannot certify the real object's
`α`, and nothing here claims it does.
