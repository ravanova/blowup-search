## 3. Prior art OUTSIDE — what is and is not novel here

**Not novel, and not claimed:** the orthogonality of the Blaschke/Cayley basis
`φ_n(y) = i aⁿ/b^{n+1}` (`a = y − i/2`, `b = y + i/2`) with constant `2π` on the line. This is
the classical Malmquist–Takenaka / Laguerre–Fourier pair — `φ_n` is the Fourier image of the
Laguerre function `L_n(ξ)e^{−ξ/2}` on `ξ > 0`, and Plancherel supplies the `2π`. Leg 249's W2
already measured it. Nothing below re-claims it.

**What is new, if it holds:** (i) the discrimination between Xu's two stated norms *as applied
to this certificate*, with the half-line Gram measured rather than assumed to be half the
full-line one — it need not be, since the half-line Gram of a non-odd basis is Hermitian with a
possibly nonzero imaginary part, and only its real part is pinned by the full-line result;
(ii) a **per-quantity conversion table** with each entry's predicted homogeneity stated in
advance and then tested; (iii) a located answer to whether Xu publishes any comparand at all
at `z = 0`.

## 4. Pre-registered predictions (falsifiable, recorded before the first number)

Write `κ` for the constant multiplying the coefficient Gram `G = I + J⁴` to make it Xu's `X`
norm (`κ = 2π` full-line, `κ = π` half-line **if** the half-line Gram turns out to be
`½ ×` the full-line one). The `X ⊕ ℂ` Gram carries `κG` on the `X` block and weight `1` on the
border amplitude.

* **P1 (equivalence of the two routes).** Scaling both Grams' `X` blocks by `κ` with unit
  border weight is identical to leaving the `X` blocks alone and setting the border weight to
  `1/κ`, because an overall positive scaling of *both* Grams leaves `σ_min` fixed. **Predicted:
  agreement to float64 round-off.** This is what makes leg 249's `bw = 1/(2π)` call the
  full-line conversion, and it is tested, not asserted.
* **P2 (the unbordered quantities are exactly invariant).** `‖T⁻¹‖_X` and the tail-block
  `σ_min` (`K = 2, 4, 8, 16`) never touch the border coordinate, so `κ` cancels between range
  and domain. **Predicted: unchanged to round-off, for every `κ`.** If any of them moves, that
  is a discrepancy finding.
* **P3 (the bordered quantities are NOT a fixed power of `κ`).** `σ_min` of the bordered
  section mixes an `X`-weighted block with an unweighted `ℂ`, so it is **not** homogeneous;
  leg 249's own sweep is non-monotone (it peaks near `w = 10` at `0.13580` and falls back to
  `0.09268` at `w = 100`), which no pure power can do. **Predicted: no closed-form conversion
  factor exists; the number must be recomputed.** The small-`w` asymptote is predicted to
  approach `σ ∝ √w`, and the deviation from that law **at** the Xu point is to be reported as a
  magnitude.
* **P4 (the dual norm carries a clean factor).** `‖ℓ‖_{X*}` maps `X → ℂ` with `ℂ` at weight
  `1`, so it is homogeneous of degree `−½` in `κ`. **Predicted: exactly `0.8873620/√κ`.**
* **P5 (gate (b) has no comparand at `z = 0`).** By **L4**, Prop. 4.6's majorant is stated on
  `{Re z > −½}\{0,1}` and `z = 0` is excluded — which is precisely the point the bordered
  formulation exists to handle. **Predicted: Xu publishes no value directly comparable to
  `σ_min` or `‖T⁻¹‖_X`, and the reason is structural, not an omission.** The one exactly
  published operator norm, **L3**'s `‖T₀‖ = 2`, is a *different* operator (the Hardy operator on
  `L²(0,∞)`, not the inverse of the tail block of `L₀⁺`), and is used here as an **external
  control on the half-line quadrature** rather than as a comparand.

**And the question §1's L1+L2 posed is answered.** The half-line Gram is
`H = π(I + J⁴) + iS`, `S` real antisymmetric with **exact integer entries** (max `245890` at
`n = 8`, identical to 10 digits across four quadrature rules): the two norms Definition 4.1
names are **not proportional as Hermitian forms**. On the **real** coefficient subspace — where
the bordered section lives after leg 249 §8's realification — `S` contributes exactly zero and
`H = πG` to `2.20e−14`, while the same ratio on complex data deviates by `0.676`.

**So the constant for the displayed definition (4.2) is `π`, and `σ_min = 0.057643`
(`‖R‖_X = 17.348`) — not the `0.0420` that leg 249's W5 and leg 270's E1 carry, which is
Definition 4.1's *equivalent* full-line norm. A factor of `1.3715`.** The prediction that this
discrimination had never been made is confirmed: it is a discrepancy in the bridge itself, and
it is **not** an escalation, because leg 176's gate property is invariant under every positive
constant.
