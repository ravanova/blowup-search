local-energy-estimates Liouville route, as opposed to Theorem 1's `L^q`-membership route) —
i.e. a theorem proving Liouville-type rigidity for genuinely discretely self-similar weak
solutions via a local-energy-estimates mechanism analogous to Tsai's, or explicitly extending
Theorem 2's proof to the DSS ansatz?

## 3. The finding, in one sentence, and why it required a primary-text read to find

**Chae-Wolf's proof of their Theorem 1.3 (the DSS Liouville theorem PV restate as their own
"Main result for DSS solutions") contains the literal sentence (l.~669–676 of the pdftotext
extraction): "u [is] backward self-similar ... In particular, u satisfies local energy estimate
(3.4). Thus, [we are] in [a] position to apply Tsai's result [15, Theorem 2], u [is] identical[ly]
zero."** No abstract, title, or MSC classification anywhere in this literature signals this
connection — Chae-Wolf's own abstract says only "We prove that possible blow-up ... has only one
point of singularity. In case the scaling parameter λ is near 1 we remove the singularity" — the
word "Tsai" appears nowhere in it, "local energy" appears nowhere in it, "Liouville" appears
nowhere in it. **This is exactly why the arXiv abstract-field searches below (§4) never surfaced
Chae-Wolf under any of this leg's query shapes, and why finding the connection required reading
the paper's Section 3 in full, not searching for it.**
