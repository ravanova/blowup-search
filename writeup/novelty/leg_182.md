## Pass 3 — external: is the interpolation move already known to work, or known to fail?

### 3a. What space do computer-assisted proofs actually use, and why?

* [arXiv:2302.12877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12877) — Cadiot–Lessard–Nave, rigorous
  computation of semi-linear PDEs on unbounded domains via spectral methods. Works in a
  **Hilbert `H^l ⊂ H^s(ℝ^m)`**. Already in this repository's `Papers/MANIFEST.md` Tier 2,
  marked *fetched, text-extracted, NOT READ*; leg 163 flagged it as the nearest existing
  machinery. It is one of the two endpoints' worth of prior art, not an interpolant.
* [arXiv:1503.06315](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06315) (Breden–Desvillettes–Lessard),
  [arXiv:2505.03091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03091) (Cadiot),
  [arXiv:1706.10107](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.10107),
  [arXiv:2405.12446](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.12446),
  [arXiv:1704.03827](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.03827),
  [arXiv:1910.12472](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.12472) — all the weighted-`ℓ¹_ν` lane.

**The load-bearing fact this pass surfaced, and it is a real constraint on the yes-branch:**
the reason the CAP literature is bimodal (`ℓ¹_ν` **or** Hilbert `H^l`, nothing between) is that
`ℓ¹_ν` is a **Banach algebra under convolution** — `‖a*b‖ ≤ ‖a‖‖b‖`, which is what `Z₂` needs —
and **`ℓ^p` for `p > 1` is not an algebra**. A Hilbert `H^s` recovers the algebra property by a
different route (`s > d/2` Sobolev embedding). So an interpolant at `1 < p < 2` has the algebra
property only in a *weighted* window, and that window is an extra hypothesis a construction leg
would have to carry. Recorded here **before** the analysis so it cannot be discovered
conveniently later.

### 3b. Is the specific interpolation move in the literature?

Searched: radii-polynomial / CAP in Fourier–Lebesgue or Besov scales; weighted Besov complex
interpolation ([arXiv:1212.1614](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.1614),
[Hiroshima Math. J. 12 (1982) 581–605](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/hiroshima-mathematical-journal/volume-12/issue-3/Weighted-Besov-and-Triebel-spaces--interpolation-by-the-real/10.32917/hmj/1206133649.pdf),
[arXiv:1406.0542](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.0542)); Fredholm kernel/cokernel decay in weighted
sequence spaces ([arXiv:2207.09006](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.09006),
[Wendl's Fredholm notes](https://www.mathematik.hu-berlin.de/~wendl/Winter2020/FunkAna/notes_Fredholm2.pdf)).

**No hit performs a radii-polynomial / Newton–Kantorovich certificate in an interpolation space
between a weighted `ℓ¹` Fourier space and a Sobolev space.** The interpolation theory
(1212.1614, HMJ 1982) is standard and gives exactly the scale this leg needs to *test*; it does
not contain the test. So the question is open in the literature as well as in this repo —
which means a NO here is a genuine closure, and a YES would have been genuinely new.
