## R2. The dispatch said "the same fix shape leg 226 applies". That premise needed checking.

It was checked, and it is **half true** — recorded because an assertion is not a check.

Leg 226's branch `leg/226-pnr-v1` carries exactly one file, its own novelty pass, and that
pass **measured its own dispatch's premise FALSE**: the `weighted_defect` it was sent to
incorporate is *anti-correlated* with leg 202's off-branch cases (3.6e-12 … 5.6e-08 on the
spurious roots vs 5.9e-03 on the genuine a=0.30 profile), so 226 had to go find a different
discriminator (decay class vs. the anchor, its D3).

**No such search was needed here, and this leg does not claim 226's.** On
`ACollocation.newton` the non-scale-invariant companion is not something to be hunted: the
two gauge rows `F[J]`, `F[J+1]` are *already assembled by the method* and then discarded,
they are absolute and **affine** in Omega (a degree-1 leading part plus a nonzero constant,
so they move as `|lam-1|` times the gauge value under `(Omega,c) -> (lam Omega, lam c)`,
where `rel` -- a ratio of two degree-2 quantities -- is degree 0 exactly), and leg 237's own
report named the exact expression to add. So the shared shape with 226 is at the level of *class* — incorporate an
available, non-scale-invariant diagnostic into the verdict — and the concrete fix is
**leg 150's**, one method away in this same file, not leg 226's D3. Anyone reading these two
legs as one result would be wrong about both.
