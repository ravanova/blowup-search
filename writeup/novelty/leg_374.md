# Leg 374 novelty pass — Route-SBIV: spectral bases for unbounded R³ with algebraic weight

**Scoping/literature-inventory leg. Nothing built, nothing tested, no basis constructed, no
figure.** This pass differs from the repository's standing arXiv-API novelty-pass instrument
(MF1/MF4-controlled, positive/negative-control battery, `export.arxiv.org/api/query`) — it uses a
general web-search-and-fetch instrument instead, because the target material (classical spectral-
methods numerical analysis literature: Boyd, Guo, Christov, Coulaud-Funaro-Kavian) predates or sits
outside arXiv's own coverage for several of the located items, and titles/journal names/page ranges
were the search target rather than arXiv IDs. This difference is disclosed here and again in the
JSON's `honest_ceiling`, not papered over. No positive/negative control battery was run against this
instrument; this is recorded as a real limitation, not corrected within this leg's scope.

## 1. Search instrument and what it found

General web search/fetch (not the arXiv API), targeting each of the three named candidate families
plus leg 350's own composite. Every citation below is recorded at the read depth actually achieved
(snippet/abstract, or citation-only where even that was unavailable) — nothing is upgraded to "read
in full" that was not.

### 1a. Hermite functions (plain, Gaussian weight)

* Boyd, "The Rate of Convergence of Hermite Function Series", *Math. Comp.* 35(152), 1980,
  1309–1316. Snippet: convergence rate depends jointly on the target's analyticity (order/nearest
  singularity) **and** on `a`, "the exponent of real-axis decay" — i.e. decay rate matters
  independently of analyticity for this basis family. This is the load-bearing fragment behind the
  cross-row concern raised for the algebraic-weight Hermite variant below.
* Completeness of plain Hermite functions in `L^2(R)` (and by tensor product, `L^2(R^3)`) is
  standard textbook material, not independently re-derived — but the Gaussian weight is exactly the
  weight leg 260/341 excluded from route 4's space, so this row is a baseline for contrast, not a
  candidate.

### 1b. Generalized/weighted Hermite functions, algebraic weight `(1+x²)^{-γ}`

Located four papers, all snippet/abstract level:

* Xiang, Wang, "Generalized Hermite Spectral Method and its Applications to Problems in Unbounded
  Domains", *SIAM J. Numer. Anal.* 48(4), 2010, 1231–1253.
* Zhang, Guo, "Generalized Hermite spectral method matching asymptotic behaviors", *J. Comput.
  Appl. Math.* 255, 2014, 616–634 — constructs Hermite-type functions orthogonal w.r.t. the
  **algebraic** weight `(1+x²)^{-γ}`, γ real; this is the closest published weight-match to route
  4's own `ρ = (1+|y|)^{-s}` located this leg.
  `sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377042713003166` returned HTTP 403 — full text not
  reachable this leg; citation/paraphrase only, sourced via search-snippet, not the publisher page.
* Guo, Zhang, "Generalized Hermite Spectral Method Matching Different Algebraic Decay at
  Infinities", *J. Sci. Comput.* 65(2), 2015, 648–671.
* Guo, "Error estimation of Hermite spectral method for nonlinear partial differential equations",
  *Math. Comp.* 68(227), 1999, 1067–1078.

All four are 1D scalar-equation papers (Burgers-type or generic nonlinear PDE on the real line);
none was found in R³ or vector/Leray-projected form.

### 1c. Rational Chebyshev functions (Boyd's `TL_n`/`TB_n` maps)

* Boyd, "Orthogonal Rational Functions on a Semi-infinite Interval", *J. Comput. Phys.* 70, 1987,
  63–88. Abstract snippet truncated mid-clause: "...retains orthogonality, completeness, exponential
  o[r...]" — the completeness claim is legible, the convergence-rate clause is **not**, and is
  recorded as unconfirmed rather than completed by inference.
  `sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0021999187900024` returned HTTP 403 — full abstract
  not independently confirmable this leg.
* Boyd, "The Optimization of Convergence for Chebyshev Polynomial Methods in an Unbounded Domain",
  *J. Comput. Phys.* 45, 1982, 43–79. Located by citation search only (title/author/journal/pages);
  content not read at all this leg. Flagged as the weakest-sourced individual citation carried in
  the table, included only because it is the standard citation for this convergence-optimization
  result and omitting it would understate this row's literature base.
* Christov, "A Complete Orthonormal System of Functions in `L^2(-∞,∞)` Space", *SIAM J. Appl.
  Math.* 42(6), 1982, 1337–1344 — genuine published completeness theorem, unweighted `L^2(R)` case.

### 1d. Mapped Laguerre functions

* Coulaud, Funaro, Kavian, "Laguerre Spectral Approximation of Elliptic Problems in Exterior
  Domains", *Comput. Methods Appl. Mech. Engrg.* 80(1–3), 1990, 451–458 (DOI
  10.1016/0045-7825(90)90050-V). This is the **only** paper located for this family. The retrieved
  snippet gave one contrastive sentence (this line applies Laguerre bases directly on the unbounded
  domain, vs. Guo's alternative of mapping to a bounded domain first) and no citable theorem
  statement — completeness, weight, and convergence rate all UNCONFIRMED by this leg's own search.
  Recorded per the gate's own instruction as itself decision-relevant: a basis family this leg
  could not source past one citation is a real gap in the located literature (or in this leg's
  search instrument specifically — the two are not distinguished further here), not a null result
  to discard.
