**Structural reading of the set, which is the reason for enumerating it rather than counting it:**

- **Twenty of the twenty-three are FORWARD-DSS or weak-solution-existence papers** — "Forward
  discretely self-similar…", "Existence of… global weak solutions…", "…with rough data", "…with

  (build a global solution from scale-invariant data); the screened object is a **backward**
  blow-up profile. These papers are not adverse and are not near-misses; they are a different
  literature that shares a phrase.
- **1610.09464 (Chae–Wolf) is already in the repository's net** — it is the source of the object's

- **1610.05680 (Rotationally corrected scaling invariant solutions)** is the RSS-class precursor —
  banked here as the one item in the set that is upstream of Pineau–Vicol's rotated ansatz. Not
  adjudicated by this leg (it is a *forward*/existence-side paper by title, and the gate did not
  require it); flagged for whoever next works the RSS sub-locus corner.
- **Exactly one backward-DSS rigidity paper appears in the whole set: 2607.09619.**

Three things here are new to this repository. None is new mathematics in the literature sense; all
three are readings of an existing preprint that had not been made before.

1. **The adjudication itself.** Leg 262 read v1 and mapped its constant chain; leg 326 located v2
   and explicitly declined to adjudicate it, routing it to the DM. No leg had checked the
   hypotheses clause-by-clause against the screened object's specification. That is now done: 6

   themselves at `Liouvillev2.tex` l.293 and l.295 ("a slight modification of the proof of Theorem
   1.4 immediately recovers the main result in [ChaeWolf17], which we re-state here for
   convenience"). Consequence, which no repo file previously recorded: the packet cannot *gain* an
   obstruction from this theorem, because the object was already screened against exactly it via
   leg 253's `λ_*`.

3. **The v2-only local theorem and the conditional screen derived from it.** v2 adds a theorem
   absent from v1 (its label occurs 0 times in v1, 8 times in v2). It does not reach the object
   either. But its contrapositive, applied at every phase of a periodic orbit, yields a structural
   check on any future numerical candidate — **conditional** on closing a strictly-positive
   pressure-decay loss the paper leaves open, and **not numerically usable** as it stands because
   the constant is non-explicit. Recorded with both conditions attached in the journal and JSON;
   flagged here as this leg's only derived object, and flagged as *derived*, not as something
   Pineau–Vicol assert.
