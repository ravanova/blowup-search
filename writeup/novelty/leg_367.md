# Leg 367 — ROUTE-E5P — novelty / prior-art pass

**Role: read-and-report verification/downgrade leg on one named ground of leg 356's repair.** No
solver touched, no repair, no gate answer for the E5 family changed. Date 2026-08-12.

## 0. What is already known (prior art inside this repo)

Leg 356 (`experiments/journal/leg_356.md`, `writeup/CORRECTIONS.md` Sec.25) repaired
`p2_route_e_v1_spectrum`'s eigenvalue-ordering nondeterminism by asserting, as its stated
mechanism, that the discretized essential/continuum spectrum of the a=0 gCLM linearization "sits
on the imaginary axis in exact arithmetic (`Re lambda = 0`)". That sentence is quoted, but not
sourced or derived, in both leg 356's journal and CORRECTIONS.md Sec.25 — it is read off
`solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s own module docstring, which itself states the claim without a
citation and with a derivation that (see below) proves a narrower statement than the one being
leaned on.

A repo-wide grep for `Re lambda = 0`, `pinned to the imaginary axis`, and `essential spectrum`
(case-insensitive, excluding leg 356's own files) turns up exactly one prior leg with an adjacent
but DIFFERENT result: leg 343 (`experiments/journal/leg_343.md`), which measured a genuinely
different operator (`L f = -Delta f + (1/2)(y . grad) f + f`, `ell=0` radial channel of the
backward-rescaled Navier-Stokes/heat operator on a compactified `[0,1]` domain — not gCLM's
dynamically-rescaled flow on the whole line). Leg 343's continuous-spectrum band edge sits at
`Re(lambda) = 1.0`, and its discretized eigenvalue count GROWS without saturating under
refinement (6, 9, 13, 18, 23, 30 across six resolution pairs) — the textbook signature of a
discretized continuum that never converges, which is a materially different numerical signature
from leg 356's report of a STABLE 16-entry multiset that merely reorders under noise. Conflating
the two would be a citation error; this leg keeps them separate throughout.

No prior leg has attempted to locate a theorem or construct a derivation for leg 356's specific
claim about the E5 operator. This is the first leg to do so.

## 1. Method

Three lines of attack, run in parallel rather than sequentially, since none was expected to be
decisive alone:

1. **Theorem search.** Read `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s own closed-form section clause by
   clause against its stated hypotheses (admissibility class: vanishing at `X=infinity`, bounded
   at `X=0`, versus the STRONGER analyticity-at-`X=0` class the sine discretization actually
   lives in). Checked leg 70's realization audit (`experiments/journal/leg_70.md`) and the
   Xu paper (`arXiv:2607.19762`) as read by legs 111/127/141/163/173, since leg 70 cites Xu's
   Prop 2 for the same discretization's "maximal L^2 realization" fact.
2. **Derivation attempt.** Tried to reduce the empirical pinning to a checkable linear-algebra
   identity: is the discretized generator matrix skew-symmetric (or skew-adjoint under the
   basis's natural Gram matrix) up to a low-rank structural correction? Five diagonal weightings
   plus the Gram-weighted test, computed directly against `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s own
   `RescaledFlow` class (read-only import, no edits).
3. **Empirical corroboration, independent of leg 356's own run.** Measured `max|Re(lambda)|`
   over the non-structural eigenvalues at four resolutions (`K=48,96,144,192`), a superset of
   leg 356's own `K=96,144` pair.

## 2. What was found

**(1) fails to qualify as the needed theorem.** The module's own closed-form section proves that
the EXACT (infinite-dimensional) operator's spectrum, restricted to eigenfunctions analytic at
`X=0` (the class compatible with the sine trial space), collapses to exactly `{0, -1}` — nothing
else survives that admissibility test even in principle. That is a statement about the true
operator's analytic-at-origin subspace. It says nothing about what a FINITE `K`-dimensional
matrix truncation does with the `K-2` remaining numerical eigenvalues that a real
`np.linalg.eigvals` call actually returns — those are artifacts of representing an operator whose
genuine continuum eigenfunctions have a branch point at `X=0` (fractional powers of `w-1`) using a
basis (`sin(k theta)`, entire in `theta`) that cannot represent them at all. The theorem and the
claim are about different objects; leg 356's ground does not follow from it by clause-matching.
Leg 70's `K-3`-scaling finding (a different diagnostic, on a related but distinct subclass,
`CriticalDissipativeFlow`) corroborates the qualitative phenomenon (a discretized-continuum
signature) but likewise stops short of proving `Re(lambda)=0` exactly rather than merely small.
Xu's own paper, as read by leg 163, explicitly declines to extend spectral-location results to
norm-level statements because the underlying linearization is NON-NORMAL — the opposite of what a
clean derivation would want, and no proposition anywhere in this repo's Xu-reading legs is quoted
proving the finite discretization's real part is exactly zero.

**(2) fails to produce a derivation.** Tested whether the generator matrix `A = S^{-1} J`
(`K=48`, `a=0`) is skew-symmetric or skew-adjoint under any of six natural weightings: plain,
Gram-weighted (`S^T S`, confirmed discretely diagonal to `5.5e-15` relative — the basis genuinely
is orthogonal), and five diagonal reweightings (`1`, `k`, `k^2`, `1/k`, `1/k^2`, the natural
mode-scalings for a derivative-type operator). Every one leaves a FULL-RANK symmetric part
between 13.5% and 89% of the matrix's Frobenius norm — none isolates a low-rank symmetric residual
attributable to just the two structural directions. `A` is genuinely non-normal and not close to
skew-adjoint under any tested weight, yet its non-structural eigenvalues still cluster at
noise-floor real parts. That is a striking, reproducible fact, but this leg could not reduce it to
a checkable identity in the time available.

**(3) strongly corroborates the claim empirically, without proving it.** `max|Re(lambda)|` over
the non-structural eigenvalues: `1.81e-14` (`K=48`), `8.60e-14` (`K=96`), `1.88e-13` (`K=144`),
`3.48e-13` (`K=192`) — growing roughly linearly in `K`, staying within single-digit multiples of
`K` times machine epsilon at every resolution, i.e. tracking accumulated float64 roundoff rather
than any `O(1)`-scale genuine real part. This is consistent with exact `Re(lambda)=0` at every
finite `K` tested (not merely a `K->infinity` asymptotic statement) but "consistent with" is not
"proven." Independently reproduces leg 356's own `K=96/K=144` multiset (`{0, 0.271, 0.840, 7.156,
8.135, 14.828, 23.008, 45.174}`, each conjugate pair) bit-for-bit.

## 3. Classification and consequence

**(iii) UNVERIFIED-ASSUMPTION, empirically corroborated.** No theorem located whose hypotheses
match the finite discretization; no derivation constructed despite a genuine attempt that ruled
out the most obvious mechanism (skew-symmetry under six natural weightings). The claim is,
however, robustly reproduced by three independent measurements (leg 356's own run, leg 70's
unrelated `K-3` diagnostic, this leg's fresh four-resolution run) and by nothing that contradicts
it.

**Consequence for leg 356's canonical ordering scheme: HOLDS, with a caveat, not undermined.**
`canonical_order()`'s `round_dp=6` rounding threshold (`1e-6`) needs only the strictly weaker fact
that the noise floor sits far below `1e-6` and far below the smallest genuine isolated real part
this module has ever reported (`>1e-3`, per its own docstring) — a fact now independently
re-measured with roughly `6.5` orders of magnitude of margin (`1e-6 / 3.48e-13 ~ 2.9e6`) at every
resolution tested, `K=48` through `K=192`. The repair does not depend on the "exactly 0" claim
being a theorem; it depends on a practical separation that is, if anything, over-confirmed. The
caveat is textual only: leg 356's own prose states the ground as settled fact ("in exact
arithmetic") where it should read as a strongly-corroborated but unproven empirical regularity.
Per this leg's territory, that caveat is recorded here and in
`writeup/data/p2_route_e5p_v1.json` only — leg 356's own files
(`experiments/p2_route_e_v1_spectrum.py`, `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`,
`experiments/journal/leg_356.md`, `writeup/CORRECTIONS.md` Sec.25) are read-only for this leg and
were not edited.
