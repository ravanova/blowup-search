## §3 abstracts fetched in full (not snippet-only), with the screen-relevant properties read directly from source, not re-derived from leg 313's prose

- **`arXiv:2405.10916`** (Hou, "Nearly self-similar blowup of generalized axisymmetric
  Navier-Stokes equations"). Independently re-confirms leg 313's three screen failures by
  fetching the abstract myself: axisymmetric with swirl; solution-dependent viscosity,
  effective dimension ≈3.188 (Navier-Stokes but *generalized*, not the target); "nearly
  self-similar"/"one-scale self-similar" blowup — not time-periodic. This is **re-confirmation,
  not re-derivation**: leg 313's screen result stands and gains an independent second read.

- **`arXiv:1310.0497`** (Luo-Hou, "Potentially Singular Solutions of the 3D Incompressible
  Euler Equations"). Axisymmetric; **Euler, not Navier-Stokes** (no viscosity term); self-similar
  in the meridian plane, not time-periodic. Candidate seed source #2 in the journal.

- **`arXiv:2605.15149`** (Chen, "Asymptotically Self-Similar Blowup for 3D Incompressible Euler
  with C^{1,1/3-} Velocity I"). Axisymmetric, no swirl; **Euler, not Navier-Stokes**; self-similar,
  not time-periodic; method is "a fixed-point argument around a numerically constructed
  approximate profile" — read past the search snippet to confirm this is a Newton-Kantorovich/
  contraction-mapping fixed point (certification machinery), **not** a dynamical bifurcation off
  a fixed point of a rescaled flow — the two "fixed point" senses are checked apart deliberately
  because the cheap-entrance ban's wording turns on which sense applies (§4 of the journal).

- **`arXiv:2506.19243`** (Hou et al, "High precision PINNs in unbounded domains: application to
  singularity formulation in PDEs", 23 June 2025). The **load-bearing PINN find**. Studies 1D
  Burgers and 2D Boussinesq (the latter "directly related to the singularity formulation in 3D
  Euler and Navier-Stokes equations" — a proxy model, not NS itself), achieving 4 digits higher
  precision than a prior result on the same Boussinesq self-similar profile with fewer training
  steps. This is the *same profile family* as `arXiv:2210.07191`/`arXiv:2305.05660` (Chen-Hou),
  already among this repo's own grounding papers (`scripts/fetch_papers.sh`). Not fetched to
  full text: whether the profile is axisymmetric is not addressed in the abstract, and 2D
  Boussinesq is not axisymmetric in the 3D-Euler sense regardless.

- **`arXiv:2011.02800`** (Kwon-Tsai, "On bifurcation of self-similar solutions of the stationary
  Navier-Stokes equations", 5 Nov 2020). **Adverse finding, flagged prominently.** Searches for
  axisymmetric discretely-self-similar solutions of the *stationary* NS equations via
  bifurcation from Landau solutions — Landau solutions are exact stationary (fixed-point)
  solutions of the rescaled/similarity system — and reports numerically **no bifurcation
  found**. This is read as a structural example of the exact shape the cheap-entrance ban
  forbids (bifurcation off a fixed point of a rescaled flow), see the journal §4 ban check.

- **`arXiv:2604.16842`** (Y. Wang thesis, "Singularity Formation: Synergy in Theoretical,
  Numerical and Machine Learning Approaches", 18 Apr 2026). PINN/Neural-Operator/KAN framework
  applied to Nonlinear Heat, Complex Ginzburg-Landau, 3D Keller-Segel. Navier-Stokes named only
  as motivating context ("famously challenging... one of the seven Clay Prize problems");
  **Euler is not mentioned at all**; no DSS/time-periodic terminology appears. Located, not a
  candidate — recorded so a future leg does not re-locate it and misread the abstract as a
  direct hit.
