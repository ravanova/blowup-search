first. Recording the correction because a repair leg that silently adopts a wrong label
propagates it into every downstream reader.

**What leg 203 scopes as this repair.** Leg 203 names eight mechanisms and does **not** bundle

## 3. THE CALL-SITE CENSUS — GREPPED, NOT ASSUMED

This is the step the dispatch specifically demanded ("grep, don't assume — the same care leg
221 is applying to leg 205's finding"), and it changes what the re-confirmation has to run.

**There is exactly ONE literal `converged_spectrum(` call site in the repository:
`test_rescaled_spectrum.py:132`.** Neither Route-E nor Route-G calls it:

- **Route-E's `e5_sweep`** (`experiments/p2_route_e_v1_spectrum.py:164–171`) calls
  `spectrum()` twice and `match_filter()` directly. It never enters `converged_spectrum`.
- **Route-G's `g4_cross_model`** (`experiments/p2_route_g_v1_collapse.py:383`) calls
  `continuation()` only.
- **Route-H's `h5`** (`experiments/p2_route_h_v1_critical.py:224`) calls
  `converged_dissipative_spectrum`, the *separate* re-implementation in
  `solver/critical_dissipation.py:576`.

Leg 203's JSON lists these under `S6_exposure.s3_call_sites`; read carefully, that list is of
`converged_spectrum`-**shaped** sites (sites carrying a `K_coarse`/`K_fine` pair through the
same filter), **not** of literal calls. Nothing in leg 203 claims otherwise, but a reader
skimming the field name would get it wrong.

**Consequence for the gate, stated before the run:** the repair is *structurally* incapable of
moving a Route-E or Route-G banked number, because those rows never execute the repaired
function. **That argument is not what this leg banks.** The gate demands an explicit fresh
re-run, and leg 152's standing formulation — "the yes-branch is taken on the measurement and
not on an argument" — is the reason all 14 rows are re-run through both module versions anyway
rather than reasoned away. The structural argument is recorded here only so that a *surprise*
in the measurement would be legible as the priority finding it would be.

## 4. THE STANDARD THE REPAIR IS HELD TO

Same normative anchor leg 203 used: **the module's own written contract**, since it implements
no published algorithm. `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py:114` — *"the discrete eigenvalues are the
ones that stop moving under refinement, and the continuum is the part that never does."* The
guard is that sentence made executable: without refinement there is no such thing as "stopped
moving", so the function refuses rather than answers.

**Why `K_fine < K_coarse` is rejected too, even though leg 203 measured it returning the
correct answer (2).** The guard rejects on the *specification*, not on the outcome: a coarser
second grid is not a refinement, so the quantity the filter reports is not the quantity its
contract names, and its being accidentally right at `a = 0` is not a licence. This is a
deliberate widening past the strict equality case and is flagged as such rather than buried —
it is the one place this repair does more than the minimum, it converts a silent
accidentally-right answer into a loud refusal, and no caller in the repository passes it.

## 5. THE ESCAPE HATCH — PRECEDENT, NOT INVENTION

`on_no_refinement="allow"` follows the repair family's established shape:

repair lands, and battery B4 asserts they come back **bit-for-bit**.
