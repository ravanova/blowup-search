## 1. The defect, in one sentence

`writeup/PUB_0C_CENSUS_SPINE.md` §1 defines the census over *"any **published** work"*, and the
paper that now occupies the Grade-A × fluid cell — **`arXiv:2509.25116` (Hou–Wang–Yang)** — **carries
no journal-ref on its abstract page.** On the ordinary reading of "published" it is an **unrefereed
preprint**, and the criterion does not say whether that counts.

## 2. Why this is the user's to rule and not the Conductor's

`ORCHESTRATION.md` §3h rule 1: **a ban or criterion is superseded by a MEASUREMENT, never by a
decision.** The measurement here is unambiguous — there is no journal-ref, and `V-W4` checked it at
the abs page. What is ambiguous is the **wording**, and a defective ban or criterion **wording is a
user escalation**. An entity that both raises and rules an escalation has defeated the mechanism.

Note that this is the **second** time the same census sentence has produced a wording escalation
this week; the first was ruled on 2026-08-18 as `RULING_W3_WORDING_2026-08-18.md`. That ruling
settled *what W3 asserts*. It did not touch *what the census admits*.

## 3. The question, stated so it can be answered yes or no

**Q. For leg 174's Grade-A × fluid census, is an arXiv preprint with no journal-ref a "published
work"?**

Three readings, all defensible, with what each does:

- **(i) YES — arXiv posting is publication.** Nothing changes. The cell is OCCUPIED, as recorded.
  This is the reading the record has used *de facto* throughout: much of the census is preprints.
- **(ii) NO — "published" means refereed.** The cell-occupancy **fact** changes status to
  *occupied pending refereeing*, and the census needs a **third column** (refereed / not) rather than
  a re-grading. **It does not change W3**, which the user ruled stands on the prose test regardless.
- **(iii) The criterion is the wrong shape** — replace "published" with a stated evidentiary
  standard (e.g. *"any work whose certificate is publicly checkable"*), which is what the census was
  always actually testing. This is the largest change and the only one that touches other rows.
