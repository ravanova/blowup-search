## Why it is DIFFERENT — the one line, and the arithmetic behind it

Leg 111's construction is a plain weighted `L²` **of the function**, whose damping is a
**pointwise multiplier** `D_φ` made negative **at the origin** by pushing `γ` past `3` — which
the space forbids, and that is the zero-width window.

The located functional acts on the **derivative**, at `γ = 2` — *below* leg 111's damping
threshold and on the **admissible** side of leg 111's own admissibility test — and its damping
is not a pointwise multiplier at all. Chen's Remark 2.5, verbatim:

> "Formally, the above identity can be interpreted as that `sin^{−2}(x/2)` is an eigenfunction
> of the adjoint of `sin x ∂_x` with eigenvalue `1`. … It seems that the mysterious inner
> product `⟨·,·⟩_H` and singular weight `sin^{−2}(x/2)` constructed in [19] arise naturally
> from the viewpoint of energy estimates."

`sin x ∂_x` is *character-for-character* this repository's own transport term: leg 111 records
the `a = 0` CLM linearisation as `(L h)(θ) = cos θ · h − sin θ · (H h) − sin θ · h_θ`. So the
weight is not *chosen singular enough* — it is **the exact adjoint eigenfunction of this
repository's own transport field**, and it is unique for that job. **There is no threshold to
push past, so there is nothing for the admissibility threshold to coincide with.**

**The load-bearing arithmetic, on leg 111's OWN weights** (`m_γ = ` leg 111's `damping_factor`
minus its `cos θ` stretching term; predicted in the novelty log §8 **before** the runner

## What did NOT escape, and it is most of the literature

Recorded because a deep-mine that reports only its hit is not auditable. `window_ladder` in the
JSON is the pure-arithmetic table of leg 111's window `(3, 2p+1)` in the weight exponent as a
function of the vanishing order `p`: width `0` at `p = 1`, `2` at `p = 2`, `4` at `p = 3`,
`6` at `p = 4`.

* **EGM [arXiv:1906.05811] Prop. 2.1** — leg 141's answer, and it is **leg 111's own
  functional**: a plain weighted `L²` at `γ = 4`, admissible only from the `p ≥ 2` row, bought
  with **two** origin hypotheses (`f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0`).
* **Jia–Stewart–Šverák [arXiv:1710.02737] (3.82)–(3.83)**, `‖ |sin(θ/2)|^{−γ} f ‖_{L²}` with
  `γ ∈ (3/2, 2)` — that is leg 111's family at exponent `2γ ∈ (3, 4)`, again the far side of
  the damping threshold. Their Remark (3.132) gives the **published ladder in the vanishing
  order**, `γ ∈ (m − 1/2, m)` with `Y^{(m)} = Y_0^{(m)} ⊕ P_{m−1}`; the runner places it in leg
  111's window and it lands **inside** for every `p = m ≥ 2` and **outside (below)** at `m = 1`.
  Same family, same coincidence, same escape.
* **Chen–Hou's weighted `L∞` + weighted `C^{1/2}` combination** ([arXiv:2210.07191],
  [arXiv:2305.05660]) — a genuinely different *norm*, but damping is still extracted by making
  a pointwise multiplier negative, and their own §2 carries the same squeeze verbatim (*"we
  need to choose `β < 1` so that the energy is well-defined … This forces one to choose a very
  singular weight … e.g. `α ≥ 14` if `β = 0`, a new difficulty"*). The exponent moves; the
  mechanism does not.
* **A log-corrected critical weight** `φ = θ^{−3}(log 1/θ)^{−β}` — the obvious device for
  separating two thresholds that meet at a point. Not in this literature, and the reason is
  visible without computing: at `γ = 3` leg 111's `D_φ(0)` is `0` and a log correction moves it
  by `O(1/log(1/θ))`, which degenerates to zero at the origin — no uniform gap. Searched,
  rejected, reason recorded.

So the pattern the deep-mine actually found is sharper than "there is another functional":
**every published escape that keeps the pointwise-multiplier mechanism buys its window by
constraining the origin, and the only located functional that has no `γ`-threshold at all is
the one that abandons that mechanism.**

## A correction to a banked reading

Leg 141's novelty log Q2 records arXiv:1811.09754 as *"concerns global regularity/blowup for
the convective model, carries **no** weighted-energy coercivity estimate"*, and its journal
records both arXiv:1811.09754 and arXiv:2010.12700 as *"read and found not to carry the
coincidence"*.

The **second** statement is right and answers leg 141's own gate — neither paper states the
coincidence. The **first** is not: §3.2 of arXiv:1811.09754 proves the linear decay estimate
in a weighted `Ḣ¹` "using a direct energy method", and arXiv:2010.12700 quotes it as
Lemma 2.2(c) in the form `⟨L₁f, f⟩_Y ≤ −(3/8)‖f‖²_Y`. That is a weighted-energy coercivity
estimate, and it is exactly what leg 159 was sent to find. **Recorded as a correction to a
banked reading, not as a criticism of a gate that answered its own question correctly** — and
it is the concrete case for the standing discipline that a paper dismissed under one gate has
not been read under another.

## ESCALATION, and the ceiling

**Escalation:** a **leg-111-v2 construction leg** that tests the `H_DW` / Chen
weighted-derivative functional on the `a = 0` CLM linearisation this repository already
carries, with its own pre-committed gate. **Not this leg's job and not done here.** Two things
it has to survive, named now so the escalation is honest:

1. **the nonlocal terms the derivative form generates** — `−cos θ · Hh − sin θ · H(h_θ)` —
   which no weight controls; leg 111's own docstring already says *"the nonlocal Hilbert term
   is what the weight cannot touch"*;
2. **the `−sin θ · h` term**, which couples the `Ḣ¹` form back to the `L²` one.

Either could be fatal, and the published rate `3/8` is at **`a = 1`** (De Gregorio), not at
`a = 0`: the Jacobi coefficients differ (leg 111's matrix has column `k` with row `k+1` entry
`1 − k/2`, row `k−1` entry `k/2`, row `1` entry `−(−1)^k`) and the advection LLR's damping
partly rides on is absent.

**Ceiling.** Nothing here lifts a ban, promotes a route, claims a coercivity gap, or moves a
link of the `L1 → L4` chain. `L1` stays measured-dead in all three realizations. Leg 111's
measurements stand untouched and remain correct for the construction leg 111 built; what this
leg adds is that leg 111's construction was **not** the only weighted-energy functional on
offer, and the one it did not try has no `γ`-threshold to collide with its space. Clause S7
unchanged; a wall measured on `a = 0` still bounds `HL_S2_nonsymmetric` from below only. Clay
odds unchanged at **~0.05%**.
