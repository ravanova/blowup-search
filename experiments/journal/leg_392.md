### 0.5 The adjudication rule, fixed before any result was seen

A located record answers the gate **YES** only if its own statement, read verbatim, carries **all
four** of:

* **K1 — DOMAIN**: `T³` / periodic. Not `ℝ³`, not a bounded domain with boundary, not 2D, not a
  slab.
* **K2 — EQUATION**: 3D incompressible **Navier–Stokes**, viscous term inside the theorem. Not
  Euler, not a 1D model, not an averaged equation.
* **K3 — CONCLUSION**: an **exclusion** — no finite-time singularity, or triviality/nonexistence of
  the singular object. Not an existence theorem, not a numerical study.
* **K4 — CLASS**: the hypothesis class is one a Lane T ansatz would have to sit in.

Then, and only then, the branch is read off:

* hypothesis restricted to (asymptotically) self-similar / DSS objects → **branch (a)**,
  *narrowing, not killing*;
* hypothesis a bounded-energy / scaling-invariant smallness or regularity condition on a general
  solution → **branch (b)**, *bites directly*, including when the exclusion is **conditional** (a
  regularity criterion), in which case the hypothesis a Lane T ansatz must violate is named;
* nothing satisfying K1–K4 → **branch (c)**, a controlled null, reported with coverage and
  **never as "clear"**.

**§3d binds:** a null from an under-covered search returns **`UNDER-RESOURCED`** with the coverage
stated and a cost for the compliant search — **never `NO`**. If throttling cuts coverage, the leg
states what fraction of the planned query set actually executed.

## 5. Which pre-committed branch fired

**Branch (b) fired**, in its literal wording. Published torus/periodic global-regularity theorems
**do** exist under scaling-invariant smallness or LPS-type regularity hypotheses — they are
broader than the self-similar class, so (b) governs, and the lane must state what class survives
before anything is built. The located items, with hypotheses verbatim in
`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTETRIG_V1.md` §5: `1909.09125` (Nonlinearity **33** (2020),
DOI `10.1088/1361-6544/ab9246`, *"the initial data is sufficiently close to being two
dimensional"*, with an explicit torus clause on arbitrarily large `Ḃ^{-1}_{∞,∞}` data);
`math/9811161` (EJDE **1999** no. 11, thin 3D periodic domain, *"as long as there is some control
on the size of the initial data and the forcing term"*); `2009.07631` (LPS periodic case —
**preprint, no journal_ref, and the gate's word is *published***); `0710.1604`
(Dynamics of PDE **4** (2007) 293–302 — not an exclusion, but the sharpest statement of the target:
a Lane T ansatz **is** a refutation of Tao's `F`).

**What survives, stated as (b) requires:** on `T³`, nothing located excludes an ansatz that at its
initial time is (i) not small and not almost-2D in `Ḣ^{1/2}`/`Ḃ^{-1}_{∞,∞}`, (ii) not on a thin
torus below `math/9811161`'s control, (iii) not in an LPS class up to the singular time — automatic
for a genuine singularity, but it must be *stated*, not assumed — and (iv) a counterexample to
Tao's `F`. **Every clause of that is a region a blow-up ansatz would be outside of anyway: the bite
is real in shape and thin in substance, and this leg reports the magnitude rather than the shape
alone.**

**(a) did not fire** — no `T³` theorem restricted to (asymptotically) self-similar objects exists
to narrow with, because none was located. `1306.0305` is the near miss and it is `ℝ³`: its
"periodic" is a **TIME-periodic profile** in `C^1(R; L^3(R^3) ∩ C^2(R^3))`, not a periodic domain.
**(c)** governs the reporting of the null throughout. **(d)** is honoured — see §6. **No fifth
branch was constructed.**
