**Verification performed, in order:**
1. Confirmed leg 338's precedent: `USC.cite` (arXiv:2509.14185) was fixed at commit `b396c64`
   from `"Wang, Lai, Leger, Buckmaster"` to `"Wang, Lai, Gomez-Serrano, Buckmaster"`, verified
   by leg 338 directly against that paper's own 22-author list. Re-fetched arXiv:2509.14185's

2. **Applied the same verification step to `USC2.cite`'s own paper, arXiv:2511.22819 — the
   step the dispatch explicitly required before applying anything.** Fetched
   arXiv:2511.22819's live page. Its byline is: **Yongji Wang, Tristan Léger, Ching-Yao Lai,
   Tristan Buckmaster** — four authors, no Gómez-Serrano at all.

**Result: the dispatched premise is false.** `"Leger"` in `USC2.cite` is not a misspelling —
it is the ASCII rendering of **Tristan Léger**, a genuine coauthor of arXiv:2511.22819, a
different person from Javier Gómez-Serrano (the coauthor of the sibling paper,
arXiv:2509.14185, whom leg 338 correctly restored). The two papers, despite sharing three of
four author surnames (Wang, Lai, Buckmaster), do NOT share the fourth: one paper's fourth
author is Gómez-Serrano, the other's is Léger. Leg 338's fix to `USC.cite` does not generalize
to `USC2.cite` — applying "Gomez-Serrano" here would replace a correct citation with an
incorrect one.

**Disposition: NOT applied. `sources.USC2.cite` is left byte-identical to the merge base.**
