## 1. What was built (novelty pass first, per calibration)

`writeup/novelty/leg_312.md` (committed before any construction, per the brief):
arbitrary-precision interval arithmetic is old, well-populated ground (INTLAB,
MPFI, mpmath's `iv`, Arb/FLINT, kv, Boost.Interval, Sollya, Julia's
`IntervalArithmetic.jl` — seven items, all either non-Python, a third-party
dependency this repo's `requirements.txt` bans, or fixed at double precision).
None importable here; this leg builds the two specific patches leg 178/176 need
from `decimal.Decimal` (the stdlib precedent `spectral_certificate.py` already
names via `fractions.Fraction`), not a general library.

`solver/interval_mp.py`: `MPInterval` (directed-rounding `+ - * /`, `isum`/`dot`)
plus Taylor-series `dsin`/`dcos`/`dtan_ratio`/`mp_pi` with proved remainder
bounds (Section 1, for leg 178), and flagged-non-rigorous banded Cholesky /
banded triangular inverse / banded matmul (Section 2, for leg 176), exploiting
`x_gram`'s exact bandwidth-4 structure.

`test_interval_mp.py`: 13-test adversarial battery against `fractions.Fraction`
ground truth. Caught THREE real bugs before either re-measurement ran (see §4
of `TECHNICAL_P2_APIA_V1.md` for the full account): a missing ratio factor in
`_atan_small`'s recurrence, bare `abs()`/unary `-` silently rounding to the
AMBIENT 28-digit decimal default context instead of the module's own `prec`
digits, and `Decimal(1)/Decimal(239)` computed outside any explicit context in
`mp_pi` (1/239 doesn't terminate in decimal, so it also silently truncated to
28 digits and was then treated as exact). All three fixed; 13/13 pass.

## 2. Leg 178's re-measurement: gap flips from −230.71 to +0.5

Config (read off `experiments/p2_route_wes_v1_space.py` verbatim, not chosen
here): `wes_coercivity_gap_exact(128, "T2_egm", "E", 4.0, n_grade=96)`.

Mechanism: `F_m = sum_k V[k,m] sin(k*theta)`, formed pointwise, cancels to order
`theta^(2p+1)` by construction of the exactly-constrained integer basis `V`. At
`theta ~ 3e-31` (the innermost `n_grade=96` node), the pre-cancellation terms are
`O(theta)`, float64's roundoff floor is `O(eps*theta) ~ 1e-47`, the TRUE signal
is `O(theta^3) ~ 1e-92` — swamped before it's even that small.

Fix: patch only the 1,640 (of 7,824) quadrature nodes with `min(theta, pi-theta)
< 1e-3`, using `interval_mp.py`'s rigorous `MPInterval` sin/cos at 120 working
digits (via a locally-adaptive Taylor term count — spot-checked against the
rigorous `dsin`/`dcos` on 6 nodes, all contained). Everything else (quadrature
nodes/weights, the final eigenvalue solve) left exactly as leg 178 computed it.

**Before (banked, float64): gap `−230.7108027866136`, contamination `3066.38`.**
**After (MP-patched): gap `+0.4999999874556027`.** Matches the `+0.5` ceiling
every other clean depth reads, and the clean `n_grade=48` reading
(`+0.49999975`), to 5+ significant figures. The `−230.71` reading was a float64
catastrophic-cancellation artifact — the math was never in the negative region.
(Caveat: the `contamination` diagnostic itself, re-applied to the patched `F`,
reads UP not down — an artifact of that specific formula being float64-roundoff-
specific, not a sign the MP-patched value is untrustworthy; see TECHNICAL doc
§2.4 for why the gap comparison, not the reused diagnostic, is the honest check.)

Float64 re-derivation (this leg's own mirror of `wes_form_matrices_constrained`,
unpatched) reproduces the banked reading bit-for-bit — confirms the mirror is
faithful before trusting the patch.

## 3. Leg 176's re-measurement: N=1024 monotonicity restored

Config: `rect_sigma(1024, bordered=True, gram=True, lo_mode=0)`, cross-checked
at `N=512` (the banked "reliable window" top rung).

Mechanism: `Gch @ A @ Gdih` whitening via `origin_h2_certificate._sym_sqrt`, a
dense `eigh`-based symmetric square root of `x_gram`'s Gram matrix, condition
number `~1e13` at `N=1024`.

Fix: since ANY valid factorisation `G = R^T R` gives the same singular values
of the whitened operator (Cholesky and symmetric-square-root factors differ
only by a unitary), replace `_sym_sqrt` with `banded_cholesky` +
`banded_triangular_inverse` (`O(N^2*bw)`, not `O(N^3)` `eigh`) at 60 working
digits, exploiting `x_gram`'s exact bandwidth-4 structure and the fact that
`bordered_gram` is exactly block-diagonal (so its Cholesky/inverse are too).
`A = A_re + i*A_im` splits into purely-real blocks (`l0_plus`'s tridiagonal
part real, the symmetry-mode column and border row purely imaginary), so every
Decimal operation is on real matrices — no complex Decimal arithmetic.

**Before (banked, float64): `sigma_min(N=1024) = 0.09093626075500859`**, which
is HIGHER than the banked `N=512` value `0.09080465147034879` — a direct
violation of `rect_sigma`'s own documented monotone-non-increasing shape.

**After (MP-patched): `sigma_min(N=1024) = 0.09079112560266907`** — below the
`N=512` value, restoring monotonicity. `0.16%` relative change from the banked
value — real, but far smaller in magnitude than leg 178's sign flip. `N=512`
cross-check between float64 and MP agrees to `5e-7` absolute, confirming the
substitution is faithful in the region already trusted before extending it to
`N=1024`.

## 4. Performance (assessed before the long run, per CONTINUATION_PROMPT.md)

Leg 178: initial profiling of `interval_mp.py`'s own fixed-term-count
`dsin`/`dcos` showed `~0.1s`/node (`~3` min for 1,640 nodes) — improved to
`~0.007s`/node via a local adaptive-term series exploiting that `theta~3e-31`
needs only ONE Taylor term at 120 digits. Measured: `25.9s` for the patch,
`36.5s` leg-178 total.

Leg 176: profiled at `N=64/128/256` (`0.14s/1.2s/6.5s`) before committing to
`N=1024`, to extrapolate cost. Measured: `117s` at `N=1024`, `48s` at `N=512`
cross-check, `309s` leg-176 total.

**Combined measured wall time: `346s` (5.8 minutes)**, under the 10-minute
budget — no long run was needed to answer the gate; the estimate and the
achieved runtime are both recorded here per the binding performance rule.

## 5. Adversarial battery extension

`test_interval_mp.py` extends `test_interval.py`'s pattern
(`fractions.Fraction` as exact ground truth) to the arbitrary-precision module:
13 tests, including the exact leg-178-regime theta (`3e-31`), zero-crossing
guards failing closed (not silently returning garbage), and
`banded_cholesky`/`banded_triangular_inverse`/`banded_matmul` checked against
independent `Fraction` arithmetic on random banded SPD matrices. It caught the
three bugs in §1 above BEFORE either re-measurement ran on top of them — an
external ground-truth comparison (pi's own digits), not an internal
self-consistency check, is what caught all three.
