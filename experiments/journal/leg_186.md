the weaker true number is what the gate actually asks for. **This is the only place any bundled
number was altered, and it was altered downward.**

  space axis as an axis, with leg 127 as one endpoint, leg 163 as the other, leg 182 as the

- The **only** shared material is leg 127's `σ_min` ladder, which PUB1 uses as *the proof of a
  theorem* and PUB2 uses as *the measurement of one endpoint*. Merging the two would require a
  deliberate editorial decision and both drafts say so.
