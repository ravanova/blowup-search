## §6. THE FINDING THAT CHANGED THE PAPER — and it was found by writing, not by measuring

**`FINDINGS.md` F1.** Every per-term field in `p2_route_l5_finite_energy_v1.json` is an `L³` field
(`T1_L3` … `T123_L3`). **There is no per-term field in the load-bearing pressure-free
`‖curl F‖_{L¹_t L^{3/2}_x}` norm anywhere in the artefact** — that norm is banked only as the total,
`curl_L32`. So the two ratios the paper was built on,

    T12_over_T1_at_largest_rho   = 2.5780635399678998e-08   =  T12_L3 / T1_L3
    total_over_T3_at_largest_rho = 0.9999978617027289       =  L3     / T3_L3

**are secondary-norm ratios**, and `gate.the_obstruction_named` quotes both inside a sentence whose
subject is the load-bearing norm, without saying so.

**How I found it, recorded because the method is the transferable part.** Not by suspecting anything.
By writing a table for a referee with a **units column** and discovering I could not fill it in from
the field names. **The `L3` in `T3_L3` is not decoration; it is the whole norm, and it had been read
as a variable name for eleven legs and through a `120/120` verification.**

**What I did about it.** Narrowed the paper's own sentence, in the abstract, in a box before §4's
first number, and in the conclusion, to *"in the secondary norm"* — **narrower than the contribution
ceiling, which is permitted; at the ceiling would not have been supported.** Escalated it as
`ESCALATE NOW` because the fix is a change to *what is written to the artefact*, not a new
calculation, and because one of its two outcomes means the paper as drafted is wrong.

**What I did NOT do.** It does **not** reopen `W4` clause (b), and I did not write it as if it
might: the `NO` is read off `curl_L32` directly and needs no decomposition.
