## 2. The three recorded reasons, each with its realization (lesson 91)

All three are **spectral statements about the linearization at a FIXED POINT** of the gCLM
rescaled flow, in the compactified basis `X = tan(theta/2)` with odd sines, under the gauge
`c_omega = 1 + (a-1) H(Omega)(0)`. Conclusions are carried at exactly two parameter values,
`a = 0` (closed form) and `a = 1/2` (`alpha = 3` exactly, residual `1.4e-14` at `K = 192`),
because the free error bar (the computed value of the exactly-zero dilation eigenvalue) is
**0.35 at a = 0.2** and **8.9e-5 at a = 1/2** — the sweep's other rows were not allowed to
carry a conclusion.

**Reason 1 — Route-E v1, `cd43893` (2026-08-02), §26,
`solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`, `writeup/data/p2_route_e_v1_spectrum.json`, fig34.**
The only grid-converged isolated eigenvalues are `0` and `-1`, the two exact symmetry modes
(defects `3.0e-15 / 3.3e-16` at `a = 0`; `1.3e-10 / 9.1e-15` at `a = 1/2`; 2x2 block exact
to `6.1e-16`). No complex pair, nothing near the imaginary axis, **no Hopf**. Planted
positive control returns `+1.083` (V=6) and `+4.578` (V=12), isolated and in the right half
plane. Mechanism, in closed form at `a = 0`: the linearization's spectrum is a **continuum**
`s_lambda = (w-1)^{1-lambda}(w+1)^{1+lambda}` on `-1 < Re lambda < 1`, whose imaginary
members `X^{1-iy} e^{iy tau}` **are** the log-periodic structure a DSS solution is made of.
*A continuum has no eigenvalue to move.*

**Reason 2 — Route-H v1, `9dba93f` (2026-08-03), §29,
`solver/critical_dissipation.py`, `writeup/data/p2_route_h_v1_critical.json`, fig37.**
Promoting `mu = nu/(A L^{2s})` to an autonomous dynamical variable, dissipation **does**
discretize Route-E's continuum — converged eigenvalues go **2 -> 8**, condensing onto the
negative integers — and the lane stays shut anyway because everything that condenses lands
on the **negative real axis, max Re = 3e-13**, and nothing goes complex. Positive control:
**6 -> 9** converged, one at `Re = +1.58`.

**Reason 3 — Route-I v1, `35929a4` (2026-08-03), §30,
`solver/marginal_flow.py`, `writeup/data/p2_route_i_v1_driven.json`, fig38.**
At `a = 1/2` the inviscid rescaled fixed point has **45/48, 93/96, 141/144** unstable
directions (`K = 48/96/144`), max `Re +4.52 / +4.55 / +4.56`; leading eigenvalue
**`+4.5455 + 430.35i`** at `K = 96`, with `Re` **increasing** with `|Im|`
(`+0.313 / +0.718 / +1.004 / +2.252 / +3.365 / +4.546` restricted to
`|Im| <= 2/5/10/30/100/all`) and `max|Im|` growing with `K` (430 at `K = 96`, 661 at
`K = 144`). These are exactly Route-E's continuum modes `exp(i y (tau - log X))`. **Any
`mu > 0` removes all of it**: at `mu = 0.05` the whole band is gone, **max Re = -1e-13**,
and the spectrum collapses to a discrete negative ladder (`0, ~-1, ~-4.7`) whose gap is flat
in `mu` across four decades.

**What all three have in common, and it is the finding.** Each is a reason that *no
eigenvalue crosses the imaginary axis at a fixed point*. That is precisely and only the

introduces as "the cheapest way a periodic orbit could exist near this project's objects".
**3 of 3 reasons are local-linear statements at a fixed point. 0 of 3 mention a global
search, an unseeded orbit, or a function space in which one would be sought.**

## 6. The substantive residue, reported because it is real and does not change the gate

carry it: **§26 §4.1 says where such an orbit would have to live** — the log-periodic
directions, which are (i) continuous spectrum and (ii) **of limited regularity at the

  says a periodic orbit need not be near one (§6.4, quoted above).

  conclusions at exactly `a = 0` and `a = 1/2`. Route-E's own "not claimed": *"Nothing at
  generic `a` — the instrument cannot resolve even the modes it is known to have there."*

  scaling structure is not NS's; the only reason DSS is interesting for NS is a theorem
  about NS."* Phase 0 (leg 251) selects a target **under the Clay goal**, i.e. NS. The three
  reasons' realization does not contain the new target at all.
