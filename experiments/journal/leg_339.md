## 3. Clause (b), part one — Enumeration A adjudicated

### A1 — screen (iv_a) / Remark 40's stated reach → **MISATTRIBUTES**

Leg 331's deciding sentence, `experiments/journal/leg_331.md:176-181`:

> *"Those 21 kills now have measured footing rather than inferred: the machinery does fail on a
> nonlocal operator, and the reason it fails is **not** the reason Remark 40 gives. Remark 40
> talks about what the method is for; what actually breaks is that `Lam^{2a}` leaves `L^2(mu)`,
> i.e. it is the **weight** `e^{|x|^2/4}` versus an algebraic tail, not nonlocality as such."*

Both halves of the verdict word are satisfied. **The kill survives** — with magnitudes, not
adjectives: the truncated weighted norm of `Lam^{2a}psi_0` runs `0.9655 → 8.713 → 1196 →
2.651e6 → 5.872e10 → 1.147e16 → **1.869e22**` across `R`, against a local control `d_x psi_0`
that **saturates at 1.000000000** on the same code path (`leg_331.md:82-83`); at Breden–Chu's own
published resolution `n = 1500` the coefficient norm reaches log₁₀ = **1546.389**
(`leg_331.md:99`); the algebraic tails fit **1.507674 / 2.012245 / 2.517908** against predicted
1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 (`leg_331.md:73-75`). Nothing there re-opens the row. **The mechanism named for
it does not survive.** (iv_a) is banked at `leg_261.md:79` as *"incompressibility is a nonlocal
constraint, so no fluid row can pass it, ever"* — a statement whose force is *incompressibility*.
Leg 311 already moved it once (`experiments/journal/leg_311.md:31`, verdict code
`SCREEN_IV_A_BITES_OFF_AXIS_MECHANISM_NONLOCALITY_NOT_INCOMPRESSIBILITY`: *"the operative reach
failure is NONLOCALITY, not incompressibility specifically"*), and leg 331 moves it again, to
*weight versus algebraic tail*. That is a two-step drift in the stated reason under a fixed
verdict — the signature of a ground carried further than it was measured.

Consequence, stated so the verdict is not read as bigger than it is: the reason is now a
property of the **weight**, so it is refutable by changing the weight — which is precisely why
leg 331 routes *"which weight tolerates an algebraic tail"* to the DM. Under the old reason
("incompressibility, ever") no such question existed. That is what makes this MISATTRIBUTES and
not a stylistic quibble: the misattribution was **hiding a live question**.

**Honest limit on this verdict (lesson 91 applied to my own verdict):** leg 331's realization is
`Lam^{2a} = (−Δ)^a` in `d = 1`, not Biot–Savart in `d = 3`. It is a *nonlocal operator*, not
*the* nonlocal operator that (iv_a) is invoked against. The verdict is therefore that the
**stated reason is refuted in a measured realization of the same claim class**; it is not that
Biot–Savart has been costed. Leg 261's own text already says the Biot–Savart cost *"remains
uncosted"*, and it still does.

### A2 — the NRS/Tsai composition → **STANDS AT MEASURED WIDTH**

Leg 332 does not weaken this way; it independently **re-derives it and puts a number on it**.
`experiments/journal/leg_332.md:255-259`:

> *"`u ∈ L³(ℝ³)` is *exactly* the hypothesis of the Nečas–Růžička–Šverák / Tsai theorem, which
> forces `u ≡ 0` for backward-self-similar 3D Navier-Stokes. So the vorticity formulation buys
> admissibility into the space and hands the target straight to the ansatz constraint that
> already binds the velocity form. Leg 261 recorded this composition; here it carries a number."*

Magnitudes: `‖u_B‖_{L³(ℝ³)}` = **0.7307683991070311**, converged (integrand integrable to
`R = 60`), with the decay exponent fitted at **−3.0000000000000027** against the predicted −3
(`leg_332.md:246-253`). Leg 261 claimed `u ~ |x|^-3` by class C3; leg 332 measured it to 16
digits on an independently constructed witness. The way is claimed at exactly the width it is
now measured at — a backward self-similar 3D NS profile, the ansatz Clay's object carries — and
neither measuring leg touches it. **STANDS.**

### A3 — Gallay–Wayne prior art as evidence about the target → **OVERSTATES**, narrowly

The way as banked reads *"Gallay and Wayne have used this exact weight on exactly the vorticity
for two decades without a self-similar blow-up profile appearing in it."* Leg 332 measured the
one thing that qualifies **"this exact weight"**, at `leg_332.md:270-287`:

> *"Breden-Chu's `L = −Δ − (x/2)·∇` and Gallay's rescaled vorticity operator are the **forward**
> self-similar generators, for which the *growing* Gaussian weight `e^{+|x|²/4}` is the natural
> one. A **backward** self-similar profile — Clay's object — carries the opposite drift,
> `+(y/2)·∇`."*

with `‖L_forward ω_A‖_{L²(μ)}` = **5.716528960333894** against `‖L_backward ω_A‖_{L²(μ)}` =
**6.041350443296389**, and Rayleigh quotients **7.2727** forward vs **6.4545** backward. So the
two decades of Gallay–Wayne use are use of the weight **in its forward-drift role**, whereas the
absence the way treats as evidence is an absence of a **backward** blow-up profile. The evidence
is real but its reach is narrower than the sentence: "nobody found X while using this tool for a
different job" is weaker evidence about X than "nobody found X while looking for it."

This is the *narrowest* of the six verdicts and it is flagged as such. The way is not refuted —
leg 332 is explicit that the drift sign *"touches the obstruction **not at all**: both signs are
still maps `H²(μ) → L²(μ)`"* — and it never claimed to be more than *"evidence about the target,
not the method."* But it is quoted at a consuming site as one third of a **closure**, and
absence-of-sighting evidence gathered under a different drift sign is not a closure. OVERSTATES
is the correct word, and it belongs to the sites, not to leg 261's own careful hedge.

### B2 = A1 — screen (iv_a) → **MISATTRIBUTES** (§3, A1; not re-argued)

### B3 — the Leray obstruction (legs 257/261, class C1) → **OVERSTATES**

Leg 332's deciding sentence, `experiments/journal/leg_332.md:31-34`:

> *"**The step that fails is S4.** It fails because the entire non-vanishing tail of leg 257's
> obstruction lives inside `grad v` — the Leray pressure gradient — and `curl(grad v) ≡ 0`. The
> obstruction's engine is precisely the one term that the vorticity formulation deletes."*

The obstruction is real where it was measured — leg 332 reproduced leg 257's divergence
independently to 12 digits, max relative difference **4.088e−12**, no shared code (FT1) — and it
is **formulation-specific**: the killing step is exhibited as an exact identity, `curl grad ≡ 0`,
field identity to **1.652e−10** (FT2), with a two-arm control whose arms separate by ~11 000
decades on one code path (FT5). The finite mapping bound on the surviving lane is **0.731194238**
at ratio 0.13863 (leg_332.md §6). So a ground stated at the width "the Leray obstruction closes
the Grade-A/fluid cell" claims a formulation-independent kill that has now been measured to be
velocity-formulation-specific. OVERSTATES.

**Whose overstatement this is, precisely.** Not leg 261's. Leg 261 already recorded C4 as
convergent and headed its own section *"What kills the vorticity route instead"* — its text is
careful that the Leray tail is **not** what closes the vorticity lane (*"Three named obstructions
remain, and none is leg 257's tail"*). The overstatement is manufactured at the **consuming
sites**, by compressing a per-formulation result into an unqualified count. That is exactly the
failure mode of over-read closures #1–#5, and it is why this is closure #6 rather than a
correction to leg 261.
