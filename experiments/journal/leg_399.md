### R1 — `arXiv:2509.25116`, Hou–Wang–Yang. **THE YES. REPORTED FIRST, UNSOFTENED.**

*Never graded before this leg.* Leg 242's control net surfaced it
(`experiments/journal/leg_242.md:73`) and, in `WALLS.md`'s own words, "graded none against leg
174's Grade-A criterion, because that was not its gate". It is **absent from leg 174's 11-row
ledger** although v1 (2025-09-29) predates leg 174. The gap `WALLS.md`'s 2026-08-18 coverage
correction flagged was real, and it was **not empty**.

**Clause 1 — Grade A. PASSES.** Proposition 1 (Exact self-similar profile), eq. (1.15), p.5,
verbatim: *"there exists a `U_bar` satisfying the following equation in the distributional sense:
`−½U_bar − ½ξ·∇U_bar − ΔU_bar + Π(U·∇U_bar + U_bar·∇U + U_bar·∇U_bar) = −ΠE_U`, `div U_bar = 0`
… so that `Ũ = U + U_bar` solves the first and third equations of (1.12). Moreover, the
perturbation `U_bar ∈ H¹` satisfies `‖U_bar‖_{L²} ≲ ε_U`."* **The Laplacian is a term of the very
equation the enclosure solves.** The enclosure mechanism is interval arithmetic by the paper's own
statement — §7.3, p.55: *"All pointwise evaluations `f_{i,j}` are computed with interval arithmetic
to enclose round-off"*; §7.4: *"with interval arithmetic capturing floating-point uncertainty."*

**Clause 2 — fluid. PASSES.** §1, eq. (1.1), p.1: *"`∂_t u + u·∇u − Δu + ∇p = 0`, `div u = 0`"* —
*"the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equation on the whole space without
forcing"*. Transport nonlinearity **and** incompressibility, both carried into the certified
profile system as `Π(Ũ·∇Ũ)` and `div Ũ = 0`.

**Both clauses pass. Verdict `GRADE_A_FLUID`.**

**The qualification that must travel with the YES, and does not withdraw it.** The certified
object is **not a finite-time singularity**. The paper says so itself, §1.2, p.2, verbatim:

> *"The readers should not confuse this self-similar setting, which starts from singular initial
> data, with the backward self-similar setting related to finite-time singularities from smooth
> initial data."*

Theorem 1's solutions are *"smooth for positive times"*; the theorem's conclusion is
**nonuniqueness of Leray–Hopf solutions**, not blow-up. Leg 174's criterion — quoted at source in
§0, handed to this leg with an explicit instruction not to tighten it — has **exactly two
clauses** and contains no finite-time-singularity clause. Adding one would be tightening. So the
YES stands as graded and the qualification is recorded beside it. See §5 for what this does to
W3's wording, which this leg **flags and does not rule**.

Independent corroboration that this row belongs in the fluid-CAP family at all: Jiajie Chen's own
literature survey in R3 (`2605.15149`, §1.4, p.7) lists it as *"nonuniqueness of Leray–Hopf weak
solutions for 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes without forcing [34]"* alongside the SQG, 3D Euler,
compressible-Euler and CGL computer-assisted proofs.
