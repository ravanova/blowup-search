## 2. What the thesis got right, adjudicated rather than carried

The thesis claims **315's blocker does not transfer** — that Zgliczyński's dissipative/parabolic
hypotheses, which blocked leg 315's `O1` (BCG's quasilinear hyperbolic compressible-Euler system),
do not block route 4's object. **I adjudicate this TRUE**, not merely transcribe it. Leg 334's own
JSON already carries this exact claim but explicitly labels it a transcription, never
independently checked (`writeup/data/p2_route_dssp_v1.json`, `carried_measurements.leg_315`).

The check: route 4's object is the self-similar/dynamically-rescaled 3D Navier-Stokes system —
leg 253's pinning of Nečas–Růžička–Šverák (1996) / Tsai (1998)'s own hypotheses is for exactly this
system (leg 260/341 both cite it). Under the standard rescaling `u(x,t) = (2(T-t))^{-1/2} V(y,s)`,
the rescaled equation retains the viscous term as `ν Δ_y V` — its principal part is elliptic and
dissipative — plus a linear drift `-(y·∇V)/2 - V` and the usual quadratic nonlinearity. BCG's
compressible-Euler imploding profile, by contrast, **has no diffusive term at all**; dissipation
enters only as an exponentially-decaying forcing prefactor (`e^{-δ_dis s_0}`, leg 315's own words,
`experiments/journal/leg_315.md:73`). The PDE-type mismatch that gave leg 315's `O1` cost class C
is a fact about BCG's *hyperbolic* system specifically, and does not restate itself for route 4's
genuinely parabolic one. **Full reasoning and both quotes are in the JSON**
(`thesis_check_315_blocker`).

**The finding that decides the classification**: every located instance of the self-consistent-
a-priori-bounds / Galerkin-plus-tail / NK-in-Fourier-space technology — Zgliczyński's own KS paper
(`math/0005247`), the validated-parabolic-integration paper already banked in this repo
(`arXiv:2305.08221`, *explicitly* periodic BC), the one paper found that is a genuine periodic
orbit of 3D NS with the viscous term inside the certified equation (`arXiv:1902.00384`, on the
**three-torus**), a Taylor-Couette UPO paper (`arXiv:2409.09234`, *"minimal periodic domain"*),
and both Arioli-Koch-line papers located (`arXiv:2105.04148`, Dirichlet BC on a bounded domain;
`arXiv:2009.12762`, Navier BC on a bounded square) — closes its tail-domination (neglected-mode)
estimate against a **COMPACT domain** with a discrete, geometrically/exponentially decaying
spectral basis (Fourier or Chebyshev). Route 4's object needs the opposite: an **unbounded** `R³`
domain in an **algebraically** weighted space (leg 260's `L^p(p>3)` / weighted `L²(ρ)`, `s>1`),
which explicitly excludes the one weight family (Gaussian) that would supply an analogous discrete
spectral basis (leg 260 §1, leg 341 §S1). The seventh link (`arXiv:2308.01528`, Hou-Luo on the
real line) is the one instance combining an unbounded domain with algebraic far-field decay and a
rigorous existence result — but it certifies a **stationary** profile via a different (analytic
fixed-point, not Galerkin-tail) method, in 1D, with no Leray projector and no axisymmetry
question, so it does not carry over as a positive instance of *this* technology on route 4's
domain shape.

## 4. Why (ii) and not (i) or (iii)

**Not (i) REACHES.** REACHES requires the Galerkin-plus-tail bridge to actually **close** for
route 4's object. No located instance closes it in the unbounded/algebraic-weight setting; §2's
adjudication clears one obstruction (PDE type) but does not supply the other (domain shape/basis).
A positive instance would have to exist and be named for (i); none does.

**Not (iii) DOES-NOT-REACH.** DOES-NOT-REACH requires a **failing hypothesis**, established as a
fact — a theorem or a definite structural argument that the bridge *cannot* close, the way leg 315
had one (a proven PDE-type mismatch). I do not have one. The nearest candidate — leg 334's own
measurement (§3.3 there) that the linearization in the algebraically-weighted space has
**continuous** spectrum, vs. discrete spectrum in the excluded Gaussian weight — is flagged in the
JSON as a **risk factor, not a proof**, because it is a statement about a *different* specific
operator (the linearized stability operator around a candidate profile in Route-E's model), not
about whether a Galerkin-truncation basis for the *state space itself* can be built in an algebraic
weight. Conflating the two would be exactly the kind of over-reading this repository's discipline
(lesson 87: a certification method's shape is a property of the operator) warns against, and leg
341's own three-realization death (`S1_A/B/C`) is offered as a second, *suggestive but not direct*
risk factor for the same reason — different apparatus (NK fixed-point contraction vs. Galerkin-tail
dynamical closure). **Absence of a positive instance is not a proof of impossibility**, and I am
not banking it as one.

**(ii) OPEN-AND-REACHABLE fits**: a real, positively-identified obstruction — the domain-shape/
spectral-basis mismatch — is named with seven pinned links as evidence, and cost-classed at **C**
(research-level; no located instance is a package nobody installed, it is a construction nobody
has attempted in this exact combination), by the same A/B/C reasoning leg 315 used for its own
`O1`. Full statement and both risk factors: `writeup/data/p2_route_pocp_v1.json`,
`obstruction_named` and `cost_class`.
