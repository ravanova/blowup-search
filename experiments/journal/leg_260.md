## 0. The answer in one paragraph

## 1. (a) THE FUNCTION SPACE — answered

**An algebraically weighted space on `R³`, graded to the Type-I rate.** Concretely either
`L^p(R³)` for a fixed `p > 3`, or a weighted `L²(ρ)` with `ρ = (1+|y|)^{-s}`, `s > 1`.

Both thresholds are **computed here and both are sharp**, by shell integrals `∫_R^{2R}`
started at `R = 1e4` (deep in the asymptotic regime — see §6 on why that matters):

| `p` | 2.0 | 2.5 | 2.9 | **3.0** | **3.1** | 3.5 | 4.0 | 6.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shell ratio | 2.000035 | 1.414245 | 1.071801 | **1.000027** | **0.933059** | 0.707130 | 0.500019 | 0.125008 |
| finite? | no | no | no | **no** | **yes** | yes | yes | yes |

The ratio crosses 1 **exactly at `p = 3`** (measured `1.000027`), agreeing with the
closed-form `2^{3-p}` to between `1.7e-5` and `6.0e-5` relative across all eight rows. The
weight table is the same computation and crosses **exactly at `s = 1`**.

**Where the object's decay comes from.** Chae–Wolf `arXiv:1610.09464` Thm 1.1, carried from
leg 253's full-text read: every `λ`-DSS solution satisfies `|u| ≤ C/(√(−t)+|x|)` — DSS
blow-up is **Type I**. In the similarity variable that is `|U(y)| ≤ C/(1+|y|)`: bounded at
the origin, decaying exactly like `|y|^{-1}`.

**Three exclusions, each load-bearing:**

* **NOT `L³`.** Chae–Wolf Remark 1.2: `u ∈ C((−∞,0);L³)` gives **full regularity**. An
  `L³` orbit is not a blow-up at all. The candidate survives the screen *because* it sits
  outside `L³` — and the table shows `p = 3` is exactly the divergent side of the boundary,
  logarithmically. **The property that makes the object survive is the same property that
  makes it hard to represent.**
* **NOT `L²`.** The `p = 2` row diverges *linearly*. **A DSS profile has infinite energy in
  the similarity variable.** (The physical solution has finite energy; the rescaling is
  what spends it.) This is the row that decides §3.
* **NOT Breden–Chu's Gaussian-weighted `H²(µ)`** — leg 257's "fourth space". It fails
  **twice, independently**. Leg 257 measured the Leray projector leaving `L²(µ)` (algebraic
  `|x|^{-4}` tail, fitted exponent **−3.000000**, coefficient `= ∫|U|² > 0`). This leg adds
  a second reason that needs none of that: `µ` **grows** like `e^{|y|²/4}` while the profile
  decays only algebraically, so the shells grow super-geometrically —
  **26.6 → 1.0e5 → 3.9e20 → 1.3e83**. The Type-I profile is not in `L²(µ)` at all. Leg 257's
  §3 anticipated exactly this shape for forward-self-similar solutions ("*fail the space for
  a different reason — algebraic decay*"); the same reason applies here, and it means the
  repository's most-developed Grade-A space is not merely unavailable but **anti-aligned**:
  it demands Gaussian decay where the target has algebraic growth-of-mass.

### 1.1 Clause (a)'s recorded difficulty, carried and resolved at mechanism level

Entry B's clause (a) requires carrying §26 §4.1's difficulty: the building blocks are of
**limited regularity at the origin** (`X^{1-iy}`, a fractional power at `X = 0`), and in the
**viscous** gCLM problem that band is **absent entirely** (`max Re = -1e-13` at `mu = 0.05`,
Route-I `35929a4`).

**It is real, and it does not transfer — it relocates.** Both objects carry a first-order
rescaling **drift** and a **dissipation** term, and both carry a log-periodic block. Compute
the ratio on the block itself:

* **NS**, similarity variables, far-field block `f(r) = r^{-1+iκ}`, `d = 3`:
  `|dissip|/|drift| = 2ν|κ| / r²`, crossover `r_* = √(2ν|κ|)`.
* **gCLM**, Route-E §4.1's origin block `f(X) = X^{1-iy}`:
  `|dissip|/|drift| = µ|y| / X²`, crossover `X_* = √(µ|y|)`.

**Identical functional form. Opposite limits — because the bands sit at opposite ends of the
domain.**

| | band lives at | ratio there | measured here |
|---|---|---|---|
| gCLM | `X → 0` (**inside** the crossover) | `→ ∞` | `1.0000` at `X_*`, **`1.0e+4`** at `X_*/100` |
| NS | `r → ∞` (**outside** the crossover) | `→ 0` | `1.0000` at `r_*`, **`1.0e−4`** at `100 r_*` |

The NS ratio matches its closed form to `< 1e-9` relative at every sample.

**So dissipation deletes the gCLM band because that band sits where dissipation wins, and
cannot delete the NS band because that band sits where the rescaling drift wins by `r²`.**
This *explains* Route-I's measured deletion rather than restating it, and it explains why
the deletion is a gCLM fact. As a consistency check on a banked measurement — not a
re-derivation, and no Route-I eigenvalue is recomputed here — at `µ = 0.05` and Route-I's
leading `|Im| = 430.35` the crossover is `X_* = 4.6387`, so dissipation dominates across
essentially the whole resolved region, matching Route-I's *total* deletion.

**This is Route-E's own warning, made quantitative.** Route-E wrote *"Nothing about NS.
gCLM's scaling structure is not NS's"*; the crossover formula is what that sentence costs in
arithmetic. **The difficulty is not removed — it moves**, from limited regularity at the
origin to a **non-decaying oscillation at infinity**, and that move is precisely what forces
the space answer to be algebraic rather than Gaussian.

## 2. (b) THE OBJECT — answered, and its customer named

**Primary: `NS3D-DSS-NONAXI-LAMBDA-LARGE`** — leg 251's first CONDITIONAL-tier candidate.
3D incompressible NS, **non-axisymmetric** backward DSS, `λ` significantly larger than 1,
profile **not** in `L^∞_t L³`.

As a search target it is a **periodic orbit of the dynamically rescaled flow with period
`T = 2 log λ`**, with `λ` an **output** of the search, not an input. That is the whole
translation of Entry B's abstraction into a concrete object, and leg 251 states the
equivalence in as many words: *"A DSS blow-up **is** a periodic orbit of the dynamically
rescaled flow."*

Every structural condition comes from leg 253 and every one is **structural rather than
numerical**, because leg 253 measured that **`λ_*`, `λ̄` and `ᾱ` are all non-explicit**,
each depending on an unpublished Type-I constant. A candidate cannot buy safety by picking a
number; there is no number to clear. It can only clear the structural conditions —
non-axisymmetric, outside `L^∞_t L³`, `λ` not near 1 — which is exactly what this candidate
is defined to do.

**Non-axisymmetric is forced, and it is expensive.** Leg 253's composition (Chae–Wolf
Thm 1.1 with the axisymmetric Type-I exclusion, labelled there as that leg's own inference)
kills axisymmetric DSS outright. So **no symmetry reduction is available** — which is Wall 2
in its own words, and it is what makes §3's DOF count what it is.

**Secondary, named and not recommended: `NS3D-RDSS`** — leg 251's second candidate (rotated
DSS, outside Pineau–Vicol Thm 1.7's two windows). It is the **same search with a
relative-periodic target** (periodic modulo a rotation), so it adds a continuous symmetry
parameter and a group-orbit phase condition. A **strict superset** of the primary in both
machinery and cost. Named because leg 251 named it; not proposed as the entry point.

**The customer is concrete.** Leg 251 (parked, PR #20) reports both of these in a
CONDITIONAL tier and says they are *"blocked on Entry B's own scoping leg (function space,
object, price)"* — this leg. **Its second, independent blocker is leg 257's Leray-projector
obstruction**, and §1 above sharpens rather than relieves that: the Gaussian space fails for
a second reason too. **Neither blocker implies the other, and this leg lifts neither.**

## 3. (c) THE PRICE — answered in part, and the rest resists

### 3.1 What exists — the grep the standing ban exists to force

`capabilities.py` indexes **47 solver modules** (`solver/` holds 48 `.py` files, one being
`__init__.py`, so the index is complete). Read in full:

* **modules holding a periodic-orbit search of any kind, at any dimension: 0**
* **modules holding a 3D velocity field: 0**
* **modules holding a Leray projection or 3D Biot–Savart: 0**
* every fluid object in the tree is **1D gCLM/Hou–Luo** or **2D Boussinesq (polar)**

Six modules carry a **pattern** and nothing more — `marginal_flow.py` (time-integrating a
rescaled flow as an IVP, 1D); `gclm_rescaled.py`/`hl_rescaled.py` (dynamic rescaling **with
a gauge** pinning the scaling DOF — a periodic-orbit search needs the same phase condition,
1D); `decay_grading.py`/`decay_collocation.py`/`holder_norms.py` (**algebraic**-decay
gradings on an unbounded domain — the only existing machinery aligned with §1's space
answer, 1D); `port_certification.py` (GMRES + Krylov ladders + preconditioners — the
Newton–Krylov pattern, 2D); `spectral_utils.py` (FFT plumbing, 1D); and `ga_search.py`, a
dimension-agnostic real-coded GA that is **banned on an unvalidated fitness** — and a global
orbit search has none.

**Five things must be built from nothing:** 3D incompressible NS in similarity variables;
a 3D Leray projection/Biot–Savart; a 3D unbounded-domain discretisation carrying an
**algebraic** `|y|^{-1}` far field with log-periodic oscillation in `log|y|`; a
periodic-orbit search of any kind; and a phase/gauge condition fixing `τ`-translation and
the DSS scaling.

### 3.4 What resists, and why it is not a price

The **search step** has no stateable upper bound, for four reasons. The first three are
structural; the fourth is the discipline consequence.

1. **The only demonstrated unseeded method's substrate is absent.** Recurrent-flow
   extraction (Lucas–Kerswell `arXiv:1406.1820`, read at abstract this leg) extracts orbits
   *"directly from chaotic/turbulent flows"* — its substrate is an **ergodically visited**
   trajectory. The rescaled NS flow supplies none: generic finite-energy rescaled
   trajectories decay to the trivial state, and a DSS orbit is by hypothesis not among them.
2. **The target is not in the trawl's state space.** §1 measured `∫|U|² dy` divergent —
   **infinite energy in the similarity variable**. A finite-box spectral method represents
   finite-energy states. **A trawl cannot recur near an object outside its own space.**
3. **Representing it requires the unknown.** The far-field *exponent* is theorem-given
   (Chae–Wolf Thm 1.1, Type I) and so is legitimate structure, not a guess. But **closing**
   the far field needs `λ` — and `λ` is the search's own unknown, with **no published value
   to clear** (leg 253: `λ_*`, `λ̄`, `ᾱ` all non-explicit). Impose it and the search is
   seeded; do not impose it and the target is unrepresentable.
4. **A time-boxed negative would not be reportable.** A cold Newton or loop-space
   variational search over ~4.2e6 dimensions with no prior has **no coverage metric**, so no
   number of leg-hours converts into a magnitude. Under this repository's own discipline —
   report magnitudes, never booleans; name a negative in its realization — a search with no
   measurable coverage cannot produce a reportable negative. **The price buys nothing
   statable.**

**Reasons 2 and 3 are the finding.** They are consequences of the answer to question (a),
not of any budget. Entry B is defined by two words — **global** and **unseeded** — and the
function space its object requires is incompatible with the second.
