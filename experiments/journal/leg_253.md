## §1 — The screen, pinned. What "under the relevant decay" actually says.

The repository carried this screen at 19 sites as the single sentence *"NRS and Tsai exclude
nontrivial exactly-backward-self-similar 3D NS blow-up under the relevant decay."* Read at full
text, the hypothesis set is a **ladder**, and it is far stronger than the paraphrase implies.

| result | hypothesis | conclusion | locator |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRS 1996 | `U ∈ L³(R³)`, weak solution of Leray's system | `U ≡ 0` | *restated by* `tsai-leray.txt` l.79–80 |
| Tsai 1998 **Thm 1** | `U ∈ L^q(R³)`, `q ∈ (3, ∞]` | `U` constant, `≡ 0` if `q < ∞` | `tsai-leray.txt` l.131–133 |
| Tsai 1998 **Thm 2** | `u` satisfies the **local** energy estimates (1.4) in `Q₁(0,T)` — explicitly **not** required to be Leray–Hopf, and **no** boundary condition | `u ≡ 0` | l.134–136; the "only (i) and (ii)" clarification at l.236–238 |
| Tsai 1998 **Remark 5.3** | `\|U(y)\| ≤ b\|y\|` for some `b < a`, `\|y\| > r₀`, with `P = O(\|y\|^N)` | triviality (via Lemma 5.1) | l.1121–1126 |

**Remark 5.3 is the headline and the repository never had it.** The sharp hypothesis is not a
*decay* condition at all — it is **sub-linear growth relative to the self-similar rate `a`**. A
profile may grow almost linearly and still be killed. One caveat is recorded rather than dropped:
Lemma 5.1 yields "`Π` constant", and the printed last step from there to `U ≡ 0` (l.1104–1118)
still uses `U ∈ L^q` or `U → 0`. Tsai's own necessity example (Remark 5.4, l.1127–1135) shows some
growth hypothesis is unavoidable: for **any** harmonic `Φ`, `U = ∇Φ`, `P = −½|U|² − ay·U` solves
Leray's system with `Π = 0`.

**The erratum was checked because a hypothesis change there would have been decisive.** ARMA 147
(1999) 363 inserts a factor `s^{-2}` inside one integral on p.47 and says *"Everything else
remains the same"* (`tsai-leray-erratum.txt` l.13–19). **No hypothesis moves.**

**NRS 1996 itself was not obtained.** Four routes returned HTML landing pages or 404s (intlpress
fulltext path; `link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF02551584.pdf`; Project Euclid `.full`;
Project Euclid `journalArticle/Download`), matching `LITERATURE_CHECK.md` l.197's standing record.
Its row above is tagged `restated_by`, never `read_at_source` — the restatement is by Šverák's own
student, at full text, acknowledged as such at `tsai-leray.txt` l.1145–1146, and is corroborated
independently at `survey.txt` l.74–75 and `rotated.txt` l.1657–1658. **This is a real gap and it is
labelled, not laundered.**

## §2 — The forward sweep

Seed: Tsai 1998, Semantic Scholar `paperId dd415ee4db27b93e0d72a2e95f8f0207ada443c4`
(`DOI 10.1007/S002050050099`), **233 citations retrieved**, all of them. Title-filtered on

**92 of 233 on-topic**. The bulk of the 92 are *forward*-in-time constructions (existence, not

Justification, and it is a justification not a proof: every one of the five full-text sources

backward-case question. Recorded as a gap.

## §3 — What has been published since, and what it does to the survivor space

**(a) The `α = 0` exclusion is now much stronger than NRS/Tsai.** Chae–Wolf, ARMA **225** (2017)
549–572 (`arXiv:1609.06962`) Thm 1.2 / Cor 1.4: `U ∈ L^{p,∞}(R³)` for **some `p > 3/2`** ⇒ `U`
constant (`cw-liouville.txt` l.62–68, l.86–89). The exponent floor drops `3 → 3/2` and the space
weakens from `L^p` to **weak**-`L^p`. Their Thm 1.5 additionally removes *asymptotically*
self-similar blow-up. Guevara–Phuc are named alongside them as the other Lorentz/Morrey-space
generalization (`survey.txt` l.83–85).

**(b) The DSS class — a survivor 251 named — is cut in three places.** Chae–Wolf, Comm. PDE
**42**(9) (2017) 1359–1374 (`arXiv:1610.09464`):
* Thm 1.1 (`cw-dss.txt` l.79–86): every `λ`-DSS solution in `C((−∞,0);L^p) ∩ C^∞(Q)`, `3 ≤ p < ∞`,
  is regular off the origin **and satisfies `|u| ≤ C/(√(−t)+|x|)` — i.e. DSS blow-up is Type I.**
* Thm 1.3 (l.92–99): `λ ∈ (1, λ_*(C_*))` under that bound ⇒ `u ≡ 0`.
* Remark 1.2 (l.87): `u ∈ C((−∞,0);L³)` ⇒ **full regularity**, via Escauriaza–Seregin–Šverák.
* Remark 1.4 (l.100–101): small `C_*` alone suffices.

**(c) A second, orthogonal screen: symmetry, not decay.** Chen–Strain–Yau–Tsai (IMRN 2008) and
Seregin–Šverák (Comm. PDE **34**(2) 2009) rule out Type I blow-up under axisymmetry; mechanism read
at source in Koch–Nadirashvili–Seregin–Šverák, Acta Math. **203** (2009), Thm 6.1/6.2
(`knss.txt` l.896–906, l.938–951). Stated as a class fact by Pineau–Vicol (`rotated.txt`
l.160–162): *"In the axisymmetric setting, Type I blowup for 3D Navier-Stokes has been ruled out
[14, 51]; consequently, any putative singularity must be of Type II."*

**Composing (b) Thm 1.1 with (c) kills axisymmetric DSS outright.** `|x| ≥ √(x₁²+x₂²)`, so
`|u| ≤ C/(√(−t)+|x|)` implies KNSS Thm 6.1's hypothesis `|u| ≤ C/√(x₁²+x₂²)`. **This composition is
this leg's inference, not a published statement**, and is labelled as such in the runner. It is the
single most consequential line here for a repository whose entire heritage is
Hou–Luo-adjacent and axisymmetric.

**(d) A class 251 never named, and it is the one that is genuinely open.** Pineau & Vicol,
`arXiv:2607.09619v1`, **10 July 2026 — twenty-eight days old, a preprint, not yet published**:
* Thm 1.4 (`rotated.txt` l.189–195): **rotated** backward self-similar (RSS, rotation at angular
  speed `α` in self-similar time) under the Type I bound is trivial for `|α| < α_lo` or
  `|α| > α_hi`, *"but leaves open the case `α ≈ 1`"*.
* Thm 1.6 (l.286–290) recovers Chae–Wolf's DSS result quantitatively; Thm 1.7 (l.337–344) extends
  to rotated-DSS.
* The hierarchy is `SS ⊆ RSS ⊆ DSS ⊆ RDSS` (l.317).
* **Why it was open** (l.173–188): the rotation terms `α(JU − (Jy·∇)U)` destroy the maximum
  principle for the Bernoulli head pressure `Π` on which NRS's and Tsai's whole method rests.
  Pineau–Vicol's weighted-`L²` framework is explicitly *"not sensitive to whether the Bernoulli
  head pressure satisfies a maximum principle"*.
* The conjecture is Perelman's, via Tsai's book Conjecture 8.9 and Bradshaw–Tsai Open Problem 5.2
  (l.135–144).

**(e) Independent corroboration that this survivor map is the field's own.** Bradshaw–Tsai's survey
(`arXiv:1802.00038`, `survey.txt` l.72–104) says in as many words that rotated backward SS are
*"not known"* to be excluded under `|u| ≤ C/(|x|+√(−t))* "even though this assumption does exclude
backward self-similar solutions"*, and that *"backward DSS solutions haven't been ruled out under
any condition when `λ` is significantly larger than one."*

## §4 — Adjudication of leg 251's three survivor classes

| class 251 named | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| discretely self-similar | **NARROWED** | dead for `λ ∈ (1, λ̄(C))`, dead for `u ∈ C_t L³`, and **dead entirely under axisymmetry**. Survives only as: non-axisymmetric, `λ` significantly `> 1`, profile not in `L^∞_t L³`. |
| unstable-self-similar with finite unstable spectrum | **EXCLUDED** | the exclusion is **stability-blind**. NRS/Tsai/Chae–Wolf constrain `U` through integrability alone and say nothing about the linearised spectrum. Under merely the Type I bound, `U ∈ L^p` for every `p > 3` (`rotated.txt` l.176–180), so Tsai Thm 1 applies and `U ≡ 0` — however unstable that `U` would have been. |
| non-self-similar | **INTACT**, and now *forced* | for axisymmetric candidates the Type-II route is not merely permitted, it is the only one left (`rotated.txt` l.160–162). |
| *(rotated SS/RDSS — **not** named by 251)* | **OPEN WINDOW** | `α ≈ 1` for RSS; RDSS outside Thm 1.7's corners. Expert-flagged (Perelman), not an unexamined gap. |

**The "unstable-self-similar" row is the escalation's core.** That class does not survive the screen
as worded; it survives only by **leaving the hypothesis set** — forced NS (Albritton–Brué–Colombo's
route), forward-in-time, a non-Type-I/growing profile, or a *rotated* ansatz. Those are four
different classes and leg 251 must name which one it means, because they have four different
literatures and four different Phase-1 costs.

## §5 — Magnitudes, not booleans

Every threshold in every exclusion found here is **non-explicit**:

| threshold | source | published value |
|---|---|---|
| `λ_*` (Chae–Wolf DSS) | compactness/contradiction, depends on `C_*` | **none stated** |
| `λ̄`, `ᾱ` (Pineau–Vicol Thms 1.6/1.7) | depend on `C_{U,0}` | **none stated** |
| `α_lo`, `α_hi` (Pineau–Vicol Thm 1.4) | depend on `C_{U,0}` | **none stated**; window described only as `α ≈ 1` |
| smallness of `C_*` (Chae–Wolf Rem 1.4) | ε-regularity | **none stated** |

**Operational consequence for Phase 0:** a candidate **cannot** buy safety by choosing a numerical
`λ` or `α` and citing these papers. There is no number to clear. What a candidate *can* do is clear
the **structural** conditions — non-axisymmetric, profile outside `L^∞_t L³`, `λ` not near 1 — and
say so explicitly.

Sweep sizes: 233 citations retrieved, 92 title-filtered on-topic, 5 read at full text, 1
abstract-only, 1 primary source unobtainable after 4 routes, 4 of 5 screen hypotheses read at
source, 7 strengthenings recorded, 2 of the 7 are unpublished preprints.

## §6 — What this is not

**Clay odds are unchanged at ~0.05%.** No link of the `L1→L4` chain moved. This leg removed a
folklore dependency from a screen and narrowed a candidate space; that is hygiene, not progress
toward Clay, and the distinction is the point of the rule.

Two dependencies are recorded as risks rather than assets: **the Pineau–Vicol results are a
28-day-old preprint** (v1, unrefereed), and the axisymmetric-DSS kill is **a composition of two
published theorems, made here**. If either is wrong, the DSS row softens — it does not restore the
`unstable-self-similar` row, which rests only on Tsai 1998 Thm 1, published and errata-checked.

## §7 — For leg 251 (or its successor), the four things to carry

1. **Drop or re-word "unstable-self-similar with finite unstable spectrum."** Stability is not a
   hypothesis in any of these theorems. Name the escape hatch instead (forced / forward /
   non-Type-I / rotated).
2. **If the candidate is axisymmetric, it cannot be self-similar or DSS.** Type I is ruled out
   there; a Type-II ansatz is mandatory.
3. **The rotated class `α ≈ 1` is the narrowest genuinely open window in this literature**, is
   expert-flagged (Perelman/Tsai/Bradshaw–Tsai), and was invisible to the screen as 251 held it.
   It is offered as an observation, **not** as a recommendation — this leg has no authority to
   select a target and does not attempt to.
4. **The DSS ban is untouched.** This leg re-asked nothing about entering that lane; it read what
   the literature published about the class. The finding makes the lane *worse*, not better.
