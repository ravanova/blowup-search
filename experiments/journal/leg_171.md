## The three things a downstream leg must not lose

**1. `Y_{3/2}`'s exactness is not free, and the constant is not 1 where you want it.** The
exact constant 1 belongs to `S_Y` **on `Y_{3/2}`**; physical `X`-data enters through the
bounded transfer `J_X : X → 𝒴_{3/2}` and **the composite carries `C_Q`, not 1**. Xu keeps
them separate deliberately, and says why in the abstract: *"L₀ is non-normal and a spectral
gap does not by itself give a decay rate in the X norm."* Further, the two symmetry modes are
**not in** `Y_{3/2}` at all (the weight ejects them), and `Y_θ` carries **no pointwise origin
traces**, so modulation cannot act inside it.

**2. θ is pinned to 3/2 for physical data — a ZERO-WIDTH window.** Tail forces `θ ≥ 3/2`,
the sharp double Hardy inequality forces `θ ≤ 3/2`. This repository has met that shape
before (leg 111's weighted-energy window; leg 141 re-read it and found the zero width was an
artifact of the trial space). **Here it is not an artifact** — Xu derives it from a two-sided
squeeze and states the widened window (`θ ∈ (1,2)`) that origin-analytic forcing buys.
Recorded explicitly as a caution against repeating leg 141's correction in the wrong
direction.

**3. X's own gap is not uniform in `a`.** For `a ∈ (0, 1/2)` the origin-H² realization
acquires a **second** essential line protruding into the strip by up to 0.037 — a defect *of
the H² realization itself*, which Xu says an H³ realization is **expected** (not proved) to
remove, and whose exactness is open either way.

## The second ask: what Xu's certification TECHNIQUE is, and how mature

Asked for beyond the strict gate. The answer is **less mature than the origin-H² citation
makes it look**, and Xu says so himself.

* **The proved layer is not computer-assisted at all** — Mellin diagonalization, log-widening
  Weyl sequences, an explicit closed-form resolvent kernel, exact Hardy–Mellin norm, analytic
  majorant `α^{−3/2}`. Machine involvement is a **twelve-check identity suite** (six exact in
  sympy, six numerical). It confirms identities; it is not a certificate.
* **The certification-shaped strand (Evans determinant / argument principle, §3.2, §7) is
  explicitly incomplete**, with **three gaps Xu names verbatim** — and they are *exactly* the
  three the prompt asked to watch for: *"a uniform large-imaginary-part bound, trace-ideal
  membership of the kernel, and quadrature-error bounds in the trace norm"* — *"which the code
  computes as diagnostics rather than enforcing as interval bounds"*, *"its run outputs are
  not retained"*, *"Upgrading any of these would move this strand toward a computer-assisted
  proof."* **All three open, by the author's own statement.**
* **Two §8 no-gos.** The coercivity one (*"no coercivity certificate in an L²-equivalent norm
  reaches the spectral gap"*) is **already banked by leg 141** and is cited, not re-claimed.
  The second is **NOT banked anywhere in this repository**: *"a naive interval-arithmetic
  resolvent enclosure is unavailable ... so no weighted enclosure can exclude them ... the
  rigorous discrete exclusion must be analytical ... where a computer-assisted proof does
  enter, it must first be given a spectrally correct rendering of the essential spectrum ...
  not applied to the raw grid."*
* **It does not scale off `a = 0`.** The Hardy collapse rests on the single-simple-pole
  identity `HΩ − iΩ = i/(y + i/2)`, which Xu says is *"exact only at the single-pole a = 0
  profile"*. At `a > 0` everything discrete is numerical evidence at sampled advections and
  the essential spectrum is an **inclusion** under `Adm(a)`, not an equality.

**Reading, for the user's "Xu lane vs ℓ¹-Fourier" interest:** Xu's lane is **not yet a method
to port** — it is a one-point analytic anchor plus a program, and its author states the
black-box interval-arithmetic entrance is closed for this object. What *is* mature and
transferable is the **diagnosis**: name the realization before reading any spectrum, because
the same operator is alive on some spaces and dead on others.
