## 2. `2509.14185`, fetched to full HTML text this leg

`https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14185v1` fetched and read for architecture, per-system precision,
and the exact list of discovered objects (not the abstract, which truncates under the tool's
quoting limit — same finding leg 344 already recorded; re-confirmed, not re-derived, this leg
by an independent fetch). `v2` was not re-checked this leg (leg 344 already confirmed HTTP 404,
no update; this leg does not re-spend an instrument call to re-confirm an already-banked
environment fact).

**Architecture** (re-confirming leg 344's correction to leg 342's "PINN/KAN" label, not
re-deriving it): MLPs with tanh activations, compactified `(q, β)` coordinates, hand-structured
"solution envelopes" enforcing symmetry/decay/origin behaviour, trained with a full-matrix
Gauss-Newton optimizer via `kfac-jax`; multi-stage refinement adds a Fourier-feature network for
residual correction. No KAN component anywhere in this paper.

**Twelve discovered objects**, read directly from the paper's own enumeration:
- **CCF** (1D whole-space, authors' own words: "a simplified model for the 3D Euler equations
  and the Navier-Stokes equation"): stable, 1st unstable (`λ₁≈0.6057`), 2nd unstable
  (`λ₂=0.4703`).
- **IPM** (2D incompressible porous media, with boundary — a distinct PDE, not a stated NS/Euler
  proxy the way Boussinesq is): stable, 1st/2nd/3rd unstable.
- **Boussinesq** (2D, with boundary, authors' own words: "mathematically analogous (up to
  exponentially small terms) to the incompressible 3D Euler equations under the assumption of
  axial symmetry"): stable, 1st/2nd/3rd unstable, and a 4th unstable candidate the authors
  themselves flag as unresolved ("not resolved to the same level of accuracy as the others").

**Symmetry class**: the paper's own stability analysis is explicitly "restricted to Ψ that lie
within the same symmetry class as the original solution" — every object lives inside an imposed
symmetry class, and Boussinesq's is the axisymmetric-3D-Euler stand-in by the authors' own
stated correspondence.

**Temporal structure, all twelve objects**: stationary self-similar — "the original dynamical
equations are now converted into the steady equations." No time-periodicity or discrete
self-similarity anywhere in the paper (re-confirmed, not re-derived, from leg 344's identical
finding).

**Precision, per family** (exact quotes): CCF stable/1st-unstable "residuals of order O(10⁻¹³)
for both solutions... limited only by double-floating point round-off"; CCF 2nd unstable
"residuals of order O(10⁻⁷)"; IPM "residuals whose orders of magnitude range from O(10⁻¹¹) to
O(10⁻⁸)" (stable/1st-unstable via multi-stage refine to O(10⁻¹¹)/O(10⁻¹⁰)); Boussinesq
"residuals whose orders of magnitude range from O(10⁻⁸) to O(10⁻⁷)"; the 4th Boussinesq
candidate has no reliable precision figure at all, by the authors' own statement.

**Boundary-free 3D Euler/NS as future work, exact quotes**: *"A key challenge is to discover
self-similar solutions to the incompressible 3D Euler equations (or similar equations such as
IPM) in the absence of boundary."* *"It is hypothesized that singularities in foundational,
boundary-free problems like the 3D Euler and Navier-Stokes equations must be unstable."* Named
as the authors' own next step, not attempted or claimed solved anywhere in this paper.
