## 0. The gate, pre-committed

**Gate.** Does the (D)-variant ledger re-derive with every §1–§5 obligation either
**named-as-vacated** or **priced-as-transferred**, **AND** does the §2 enumeration close with each
row's **ℝ³-only / carries-to-T³** verdict tied to its **landed record**?

* **yes** → The ledger has a priced (D)-variant. Report whether §4's deletion **is or is not
  repurchased by periodization, as a magnitude**; and which §2 rows re-open. **CEILING TIER 2**;
  §6's open items unchanged by a scoping answer.
* **no** → A step cannot be priced (for instance, no coherent periodization of the DSS ansatz
  exists): **the incoherence is the deliverable**, banked verbatim, and (D) is recorded
  **CLOSED-AS-INCOHERENT** rather than named a third time.

**THE CONSTRAINT THAT DEFINES THIS LEG, IN MY OWN WORDS: THE RETARGET DECISION IS THE USER'S AND I
MAKE NONE.** This leg prices an option; it does not exercise it. (D) looks like a free lunch — it
deletes the hardest priced obligation in the document — and free lunches in this repository have a
history of being repurchased elsewhere at a worse rate. My job is to find the price and state it,
in whichever direction it comes out, and to note explicitly that (D) **re-opens §2's rigidity
screen**, which is the known cost side. **No recommendation is written, and nothing here should be
read as one.** A reader must finish this document knowing the magnitudes and still facing the
choice.

**Pre-committed decision rules (fixed before any number was computed):**

1. **§1–§5 disposition vocabulary is closed, three values only:** `VACATED` (the obligation's
   premise is absent from (D)'s own text), `UNCHANGED` (the obligation's content is
   domain-independent), `TRANSFERRED` (the obligation survives in a different section or a different
   form, and then it must carry a *number*). A section left in prose without one of these three
   words, or a `TRANSFERRED` without a magnitude, is a **NO** on the gate's first clause.
2. **§2 row verdicts are decided by a pre-registered domain-marker rule, applied by machine to the
   landed record's own strings**, not by my reading: a row is **ℝ³-ONLY** if the hypothesis text in
   its landed record invokes whole-space structure that `T³` does not carry (a global
   `L^p(ℝ³)`/Morrey/decay-at-infinity condition, or the dilation action itself); it
   **CARRIES-TO-T³** otherwise. Every verdict must name the record file and the field read.
3. **The periodization price is reported as an exponent threshold with its measured scaling**, in
   the same currency as leg 381's cutoff bill (`α` required vs `α` available; deficit; ratio), so
   the two bills are directly comparable. If the required exponent is *lower* than §4's, (D) is
   cheaper and I say so; if higher, (D) is dearer and I say so.
4. **No green without a demonstrated red path.** Every check that can report agreement must be shown
   firing on a planted violation, in the same artifact.
5. **Ceiling TIER 2 in both branches.** §6's two no-method obligations stay OPEN in both branches.
   No `L1 → L4` link moves. Clay stays ~0.05%.
