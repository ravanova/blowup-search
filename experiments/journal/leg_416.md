## §4 — ITEM 3. THE `×130` SEPARATION OF §52 FROM §51. **FALSE AS ARITHMETIC. THE CONCLUSION SURVIVES.**

§53 writes: divergence coefficient `+6.22e-5` **per decade**, `L6`'s last ladder step `−8.064e-3`,
**"ratio ×130"**, therefore *"two orders of magnitude too small to account for the ladder's
flatness."* **`6.22e-5` and `8.06e-3` are not commensurable, and the ×130 is not a computed
quantity — it is a per-DECADE rate divided by a per-RUNG total.** Two independent errors:

**(a) The rungs are not one decade apart. They are 0.157 decades apart.** `[P]` `J3` is `nq_r = 60`
(`Nr = 16`, `3·Nr+12`), `J4` is `nq_r = 72`; recomputed reaches `5062.081055` and `7269.860638`, so
`log10(7269.86/5062.08) = 0.157197` decades. On §53's **own** choice of coefficient the comparison
should be `8.064e-3 / (0.157197 × 6.22e-5)` = **×825**, not ×130.

**(b) The far-field coefficient is the wrong coefficient at that reach, and it is wrong by 150×.**
`6.22e-5`/decade is measured at `r_max ∈ [1e6, 1e14]`. `L6`'s rungs truncate at `5.06e3` and
`7.27e3`, inside the band where `L-JVER`'s own sweep reads **`+9.573e-3` per decade** — `154×`
larger. §53 priced a truncation difference occurring at `r ≈ 5–7×10³` with the asymptote at `10⁶⁺`.

**The two errors run in opposite directions, which is why `×130` looked plausible.** `[P]` Doing it
properly: fit the tail density from `L-JVER`'s own two bands (`∫_3^4 = 9.573375e-3`,
`∫_4^5 = 3.315863e-4` ⟹ `j(x) ∝ 10^{−1.4605x}`, i.e. `×28.9` decay per decade — the fit reproduces
both bands exactly by construction) and integrate over `[log10 5062.08, log10 7269.86]`:

> **truncation term across the `J3 → J4` reach change = `+3.81e-4`.**
> **`|8.064e-3| / 3.81e-4` = ×21.2, not ×130.**

**So the honest statement is ONE order of magnitude, not two.** §53's *"two orders of magnitude too
small"* is **FALSE**; *"§52 does not subsume §51"* is **UPHELD**, on a margin about six times
thinner than the one recorded.

**And the direction favours §51.** The truncation term is `+`, the ladder step is `−`, so at matched
truncation `J4` would read `≈ 3.8e-4` LOWER and the step would be `−8.44e-3` rather than `−8.06e-3`.
Carrying that through the whole ladder (`nq_r 48 → 72`, `+1.224e-3`): §51's `−4.994561%` becomes
`−5.066616%` and the ratio `×1.3518` becomes `×1.3326`. **§51's headline is robust to §52 at the
0.02-order level. It just is not robust by ×130.**

### The defect, stated exactly

```python
def orch_live_block(text):
    i = text.find("## LIVE")          # unanchored, FIRST occurrence only
    if i < 0: return None
    j = text.find("\n## ", i + 4)
    return text[i:] if j < 0 else text[i:j]
```

`reports/ORCH_STATE.md` **already contains two lines matching `## LIVE`** — line 13 (the real one)
and **line 113**, a demoted wave-7/8 block whose own heading still literally begins `## LIVE — ` and
which sits under a `## Superseded LIVE block …` wrapper. `find` is a substring search, not a
line-anchored one, and it takes the **first** hit.

**Consequences, all demonstrated above, not argued:**
1. **MD/MD2:** rename the live heading and the check silently measures the **stale wave-8 block**

3. **Ordering luck.** The check assumes the live block is the first `## LIVE` in the file. Today it
