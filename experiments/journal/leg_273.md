### The measurement

Every element of `H²(µ)`, `µ = e^{|x|²/4}`, must decay **super-Gaussianly**.
`arXiv:2404.04054` **Corollary 17(i)**, line 601, verbatim:

> *"(i) If d ∈ {1, 2, 3}, u ∈ H²(µ) ⟹ e^{|x|²/8} u ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d)."*

(with Lemma 19's explicit constant `‖e^{|x|²/8}u‖_∞ ≤ C(d)‖u‖_{H²(µ)}`,
`C₀ = 0.52319, C₁ = 1.0228, C₂ = 0.37467` at d = 3). Its contrapositive is a **theorem-level
exclusion**, not a heuristic.

Every Keller–Segel **backward** self-similar profile decays **algebraically**:

* **`KS3D-EXPLICIT`** — Brenner et al., `arXiv:2209.11206` eq (1.5), line 107, verbatim:
  `U(x) = 4(d−2)(2d + |x|²)/(2(d−2) + |x|²)²`. At d = 3: `U(0) = 6`, far field `U ~ 4/|x|²`.
* **`KS3D-NONEXPLICIT`** — `arXiv:2503.02263` proves the whole family approaches `2(d−2)/|x|²`
  at spatial infinity. Its reduced-mass explicit solutions, eq (2.3) line 544, are
  `Φ̄₂ = 1/|y|²`, `Φ̄₃ = 2/(2(d−2)+|y|²)` — the same `|y|^{-2}` tail.

Measured in log space so nothing overflows (`log10`, radii `r ∈ [1,32]`, truncations `R ∈ [2,32]`):
