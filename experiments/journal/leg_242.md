## The thing a later leg would otherwise get wrong

A future reader seeing "**0** of 7 fluid-adjacent" could conclude this author line has never
touched a fluid model. **It has.** Joel Dahne certified profiles for the **Burgers–Hilbert
equation** (`arXiv:2205.00802`, with Gómez-Serrano, ARMA 247, 5) and the **fractional KdV
equations** (`arXiv:2308.16579`, JDE 401, 550–670). Both are fluid-adjacent and both are
computer-assisted.

They are on the wrong side of the gate in **two** independent ways:

1. **Timing** — both **pre-date** the parent (2022 and 2023 vs 2024-10-07), so neither is
   "subsequent work".
2. **Category** — the certified object is a **highest cusped travelling wave**, a *stationary*
   profile with a corner. **Not a finite-time singularity.** A cusped wave is a spatial
   singularity of a steady solution; blow-up is a temporal singularity of an evolving one.

The same caution applies to `arXiv:2605.03920` (Castro, Gómez-Serrano, Pascual-Caballo, *Linear
instability of a Burgers–Hilbert traveling wave*, 2026-05-05) from the coauthor net: fluid,
new to this repository, and still a **linear instability of a travelling wave** — not a blow-up,
not a certificate on a viscous object.
