## §3. RESOURCING, DECIDED BEFORE THE RUN AND NOT CHANGED DURING IT

- **6 shards**, fixed at launch and **never raised mid-run** — a unit with a changing
  shard count cannot report a clean per-attempt cost.
- Box: 12 cores. At dispatch `L6-b`'s three processes held ≈5.2 cores (173% CPU each)
  and `R-prof` had banked `MACHINE_WAS_NOT_QUIET = true` with a positive control failing
  at 7.15× against an expected ~4×.
- Briefed budget **~91 core-h / ~15.2 h wall**. Measured figures, load averages at start
  and end, and per-attempt cost go in `cost_and_shortfall` — and if the run is more than
  30% slower than 15.2 h that is **said and attributed**, not buried in a total.
- Commit cadence: a committer thread commits the tracked partials every **55 min**, with
  a message that says the commit is **NOT a landing and NOT a verdict**.

## §4. TEMPTATIONS RECORDED, KNOBS LEFT ALONE

Recorded as the brief requires, so that the temptation is in the record rather than in
the parameters:

1. **`E`'s attempt 2 (UPO35 S) stalled at `ΔT = 0.036`, `Δ|s| = 0.085`** — *just* outside
   the `0.05` matching tolerance. Widening `MATCH_S_TOL` to `0.10` would have converted a
   near-miss into a "recovery". **Not touched.** `MATCH_T_TOL`/`MATCH_S_TOL` are U3's, and
   `e_hhard_diagnostic._assert_table_iv()` asserts them against `u3_g1_attempts` at import.
2. **14 of `E`'s 16 exited `stalled`, not `max_newton_hit`.** Relaxing `STALL_K`/`STALL_W`
   would let them run longer. **Not touched** — the stall rule is U5's and `E` re-validated
   it against both banked ledgers (kills 0 of 23 converged attempts, margin 6.9× / 8.2×).
3. **Neither `TOL` (1e-8), `MAX_NEWTON` (52), `MAX_GMRES` (140) nor `GMRES_RTOL` (1e-3)
   is touched.** They are imported from `u5_stratified_attempts`, not redefined.
