## 1. The construction

A single closed-form witness vorticity, chosen to sit on the Type-I
self-similar envelope and to already lie inside brick B1's unweighted
`L²(R³)` with room to spare:

    Ω_B(x) = 2 S(r) (-x₂, x₁, 0),   S(r) = (1+r²)^(-3/2)

— algebraic, not Gaussian (leg 332's own witness B used a Gaussian source
and needed the weighted space `L²(μ)`; the whole point of B3 is that the
*unweighted* space already suffices here, per B1's own margin). `u_B = curl
A` is recovered by solving `-ΔA = Ω_B` via the same axisymmetric swirl
ansatz `A = a(r)(x₂,-x₁,0)` leg 332 used, reducing to the radial ODE
`a'' + 4a'/r = 2S(r)`.

The mathematically load-bearing finding this leg made beyond a straight
port of leg 332's template: **`a(r)` has an exact elementary closed form**,
not merely `a'(r)` (which leg 332 also had closed-form). Multiplying the ODE
by `r⁴` gives `a'(r) = 2G4(r)/r⁴`, `G4(r) = ∫₀ʳ s⁴S(s)ds`, itself elementary
(`-1.5·arcsinh(r) + 0.5r√(1+r²) + r/√(1+r²)`, with a Taylor-series branch
below `r=0.6` for the same cancellation reason leg 302/318 hit and leg 332's
own `G4` already worked around). Integrating `a'(r)` a second time, by parts
(`u=G4(s)`, `dv=2s⁻⁴ds`, using `G4'(s)=s⁴S(s)` and `∫sS(s)ds=-(1+s²)^(-1/2)`),
gives the antiderivative `F(s) = -2G4(s)/(3s³) - (2/3)(1+s²)^(-1/2)`, which
→0 as `s→∞`, so with the decay normalisation `a(∞)=0`:

    a(r) = -2G4(r)/(3r³) - (2/3)/√(1+r²)

— **no quadrature, no interpolation table, anywhere in the module.** This
matters directly to the leg's own runtime and correctness culture: three
earlier prototype iterations built (1) a v-compactified single semi-infinite
quadrature for the normalisation constant, (2) a direct log-spaced-panel
quadrature cross-check, and (3) a Chebyshev-Lobatto-node interpolation table
(explicitly tied to leg 350's `dssp_basis` v-grid, to make the "proceeds on
350's enriched basis" language literal) — all three were superseded once the
closed form above was found and verified to agree with all three numerically
to their own respective precision (the table-building attempt had also hit
and required fixing a real performance bug: a sub-panel-count heuristic that
scaled linearly with the outermost table interval's width, which spanned
~1e9 and produced ~5 million sub-panels for a single interval before the fix
capped it). None of that intermediate machinery is in the final module;
`a_of_r` is three lines.

`a(0) = -2/3` exactly (so `u_B(0) = (0,0,4/3)`, confirmed to be the field's
global sup by a dedicated 3000×48×24 grid search independent of the norm
quadrature's own nodes), and `a(r)·r → -1` as `r→∞` (checked out to `r=1e10`
in `test_dssp_biot_savart.py`) — i.e. `u_B ~ 1/r`, the Type-I envelope this
brick's gate names, arrived at by **genuinely solving** the vector Poisson
equation for an algebraic source, not assumed or fitted.

`grad_uB`/`grad_omegaB` are analytic Jacobians depending only on the
closed-form `a, a', a''` (never on a quadrature-derived quantity), so
`div u_B = 0` and `curl u_B = Ω_B` are checked to `<1e-10` with **no finite
difference anywhere** — this was the honest fix to a real testing-instrument
artefact hit mid-leg: an earlier finite-difference cross-check of `grad_uB`
against `field_uB` (back when `a(r)` was still quadrature-based) looked like
a genuine ~2e-3 discrepancy, traced to the FD estimate itself subtracting
two nearly-equal ~0.1-sized quadrature sums to extract a ~1e-5 derivative,
amplified by `1/(2h)` — a testing-methodology artefact, not a bug in
`grad_uB`, confirmed by checking the algebraic identities directly instead.
A planted-wrong-vorticity control (`Ω_B·1.01`) is included specifically so
the `curl u_B = Ω_B` check is not vacuous: it fails by `~7.6e-3`, well above
the `<1e-10` pass threshold, so the check could have come out otherwise.

## 2. The mapping-bound measurement

Unweighted `L²(R³)` norms need a radial quadrature domain running to
`R~1e5` (not leg 332's `R=12`), because this witness's tail is **algebraic**
(`|Ω_B|² ~ r⁻⁴`), not Gaussian — a single-interval Gauss-Legendre rule
cannot resolve five decades of decay, so the radial rule here is composite
and log-spaced (60-90 panels, 40-node Gauss-Legendre each), with azimuth
integrated by its own rule (no axisymmetry assumed at the quadrature level,
matching leg 332's own leg-261-caught-defect precedent).

Independent closed-form check on the quadrature itself:
`∫|Ω_B|²dx = 2π²` exactly (Beta-function integral of `r⁴(1+r²)⁻³`, angular
factor `8π/3`), so `||Ω_B||_L² = π√2 = 4.442883`; the live quadrature at the
finest ladder point gives `4.442845`, relative difference `8.5e-6` — an
independent second instrument on the L² norm itself, not just a
resolution-convergence check.

Resolution ladder (fixed domain `R_hi=1e5`, increasing panels/angular
resolution) gives ratio `0.195230 → 0.195210 → 0.195204`; domain ladder
(fixed resolution, `R_hi` from 50 to 1e5) gives `0.196298 → 0.195479 →
0.195264 → 0.195216 → 0.195210` — both converge to the same value, last-step
relative change `<1e-4`.

Final numbers (finest ladder point, all four factors positive and finite):

    sup_u        = 1.333333   (= 4/3 exactly, at the origin)
    sup_grad_u   = 0.616321
    ||Ω||_L2     = 4.442845   (closed form: 4.442883)
    ||∇Ω||_L2    = 4.301803
    actual (‖N‖) = 1.654147
    bound        = 8.473954
    ratio        = 0.195204

`ratio ∈ (0,1)` strictly — the bound is non-vacuous, in the same range as
leg 332's own `0.10094` and `0.13863`. **Gate answer: YES.**
