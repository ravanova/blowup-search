## The two reasons, and they are different reasons

**(a) The spectral machinery produces neither constant — it *consumes* the profile branch.**
Xu's two branch-wide spectral-side quantities are the far-field essential-spectrum line and
the §6 exponent. Both are **exact algebraic images of the single profile quantity `c_l`**:
`1 − c_l/2` and `1/c_l` reproduce **8/8 of Table 1's rows with zero deviation at the printed
precision**, while two decoy maps (`1 − c_l`, `2/c_l`) run through the identical comparison
miss by **0.500** and **12.906** (X1 — the lesson-90 control that can, and does, come out
differently). Xu says so himself in §1: he recomputes the branch *"to supply the operator
coefficients (Ω, c_l) that Proposition 1 and the exponent s∗ below need at a > 0."*

The one spectral computation he runs *across* the advection branch is the §3.2
Evans-determinant continuation. It returns **`n_disc(a) = 0`, a single constant integer, on
`[0, 0.65]`** — declared *"exploratory evidence only"*, with three recorded gaps and *"its
run outputs are not retained"* — and it **stops 0.0391 short of `a_c`**, 5.7% of the way to
it. A constant carries no location information (X6).

**(b) The number Xu *does* produce is non-spectral, self-declared a cross-check, and
under-resolved.** §2's Newton continuation on a compactified real-line grid is genuinely
independent of LSS — it starts at the exact `a=0` CLM anchor and steps up in `Δa = 0.04`,
using no LSS data. Reproducing it from Xu's own two printed branch values
(`c_l(0.68)=0.0185`, `c_l(0.72)=−0.0659`) gives **`a* = 0.6887678`, 0.043% from LSS**, which
rounds to his printed `0.6888` and `0.04%` (X2 — the third source's arithmetic checks out).
But:

* Xu frames it himself: *"we take theirs as the reference; the recomputation here is an
  independent lower-order **cross-check**."*
* It is **bracket-dependent** (X3): three admissible brackets built from his own published
  values, through one code path, put the crossing at `0.688768`, `0.687831` and `0.686349` —
  a spread of **9.37e-04 in `a` = 0.136% of `a_c`** between the first two, i.e. 3.1× the
  0.043% headline; scored against LSS, the `a=0.65` bracket alone gives **4.13×** the
  headline error.
* His own two-grid self-consistency (`≲5e-4` on `c_l`, and explicitly *"a self-consistency
  measure rather than a certified continuum error bound"* which *"can understate"*), pushed
  through his local slope `dc_l/da = −2.110`, is **δa = 2.37e-04** on `a*`. The observed
  disagreement with LSS is only **1.26×** that (X4). **The cross-check is consistent with
  LSS but does not resolve `a_c`** — it confirms the two-to-three significant figures he
  claims for it, no more.

**On `alpha(1/2) = 3` Xu cites rather than computes.** Table 1's caption: *"c_l(1/2) = 1/3 is
exact ([20]; also [4, Thm. 2])"*; §6 repeats it and then says the decisive thing: *"The only
quantitative validation of the branch itself is the a_c cross-check of Section 2."* His
`a = 0.5` row is a degree-6 interpolation printing `0.3333`, which sits `3.3e-05` from the
exact `1/3` — **15× below his own `5e-4` resolution**, so the row cannot distinguish a
computation from a transcription of the cited value (X5). And §6.1 calls `s*` *"a formal
scaling (dissipation-relevance) threshold"*, explicitly *"not the sharp critical dissipation
curve."*

The provenance ledger (X7) is four cells — two constants × two machineries — and **exactly
one produces a number: the non-spectral one. Zero spectral cells produce either constant.**

* **J-8's "worst of three sources" survives on the NUMBER, and is narrowed on the

  worst of the three sources, the mirror image of J-8.**
* **The triangulation question is closed with a null result.** There is no third independent
