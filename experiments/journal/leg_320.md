MT-Galerkin-transported real operator collapsed `2.6e-2 → 7.0e-16 → 4.3e-17 → 1.1e-17` — which
reads, on its face, as M1 recurring worse than in the incumbent. **Did not report this.** Ran a
control first (lesson 90): the analysis/synthesis Gram matrix of the MT basis against the same
grid's quadrature, with no operator at all, should be the identity. It showed the identical
collapse (`σ_min(G)`: `0.987 → 1.1e-16 → 2.6e-17 → 1.0e-17`). That is a grid-resolution failure,
not a finding (lesson 86). Swept `n = 201…3201` against `M = 32, 64, 128` to find the
requirement: roughly `n ≥ 6×(2M+1)`. Chose `GRID_N = 801`, capped `M_LADDER` at `64`
(`M_PROJECT_NL = 64` for the nonlinearity probe too) — verified `σ_min(G) ≥ 0.945` there.

**Performance note (assessed per the standing rule).** A single Newton solve at `n = 801` timed
at **78.57s**. Full corrected run: **144.43s total**, well under the 10-minute threshold that
would require further cutting — no additional optimisation was needed, and none was done at the
