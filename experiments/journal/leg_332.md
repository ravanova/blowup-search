## GATE — answered in its pre-committed wording

> **Gate.** Re-derived in the vorticity formulation on the Gaussian-weight space
> (velocity reconstructed by Biot-Savart, outside the space), does the Leray
> obstruction still force the target out of the space — magnitudes, not booleans,
> realization named per lesson 91?

## **NO.**

> no → *The obstruction is an artifact of the velocity formulation's specific
> space choice; bank this as a genuine escape route candidate and report exactly
> which step fails to carry over and why.*

**The step that fails is S4.** It fails because the entire non-vanishing tail of
leg 257's obstruction lives inside `grad v` — the Leray pressure gradient — and
`curl(grad v) ≡ 0`. The obstruction's engine is precisely the one term that the
vorticity formulation deletes.

**REALIZATION (lesson 91).** Two exactly-divergence-free Gaussian witnesses on
ℝ³:

* **Witness A** — leg 257's own field `U = curl(e^{−|x|²} e_3)`, in its original
  role as a *velocity*, so that the audit is run against leg 257's own object and
  not a convenient substitute;
* **Witness B** — the *same* field reinterpreted as a *vorticity*, `ω_B := U`,
  with the velocity **reconstructed** by Biot-Savart in closed form from the
  `l = 1` radial vector-potential ODE `a'' + 4a'/r = 2 e^{−r²}`, so that `u_B`
  is algebraic (`|x|^{−3}`) and genuinely **outside** the Gaussian-weight space
  — which is the configuration the gate actually names.

The Leray potential is obtained **exactly** from the `l = 0` and `l = 2` radial
Green's-function integrals, not by 3D convolution. All weighted norms are
**converged integrals over all of ℝ³** (Gauss-Legendre in `r × cos θ × φ`, no
axisymmetry assumed anywhere), not ray densities — deliberately, because leg 261
caught itself measuring its class C4 on the `e_3` axis, where the witness `ω`
vanishes identically.

## 2. FT1 — leg 257's divergence reproduced independently

Pre-registered bar: reproduce to ≥ 6 digits by a method sharing no code.
Leg 257 used a 216 000-node tensor quadrature; this leg uses the exact
`l = 0, 2` radial Green's function.

| `r` | `v` on axis, this leg | `v` on axis, leg 257 | relative difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 3.1332853432887914e−04 | 3.1332853433e−04 | 3.577e−12 |
| 20 | 3.9166066791115055e−05 | 3.9166066791e−05 | 2.938e−12 |
| 40 | 4.895758348891509e−06 | 4.8957583489e−06 | 1.734e−12 |
| 80 | 6.11969793612502e−07 | 6.1196979361e−07 | 4.088e−12 |

Fitted exponent of `v`: **−2.999999999998944** (predicted −3).

**The coefficient identity, in closed form.** The multipole expansion gives
`v ~ −(1/8π r³)(3Q₃₃ − tr Q)` on the axis, with `Q_kl = ∫ y_k y_l div F`. The
identity `Q_kl = 2∫U_k U_l` holds to **3.342e−14**, and `Q₃₃ = 0` because
`U₃ ≡ 0`, so `3Q₃₃ − tr Q = −2T` exactly and the coefficient is `T/4π`:

* measured `v(80)·80³` = **0.313328534329601**
* `T/4π` = **0.3133285343288751**
* relative residual **2.317e−12**

with `T = ∫|U|² = π√(π/2) = ` **3.9374024864306048** (closed form), matching
leg 257 to 1.168e−13 and leg 261 to 1.470e−13. The monopole of the source
vanishes to **−1.134e−16** — which is *why* the tail is `r^{−4}` and not
`r^{−2}`, and it is checked rather than assumed.

**So leg 257 is right, and this leg confirms it at 12 digits.** The divergence is
real. The question is only whether it survives passing to vorticity.

## 3. FT2 — the killing step exhibited as an identity, not asserted

`P F = F − grad v`, therefore

```
curl P[F] = curl F − curl grad v = curl F      (residual identically 0)
```

This is not a numerical near-miss; `curl ∘ grad ≡ 0` is exact, so leg 257's
entire algebraic tail lies in `ker(curl)`. The vorticity formulation never
constructs the Leray projector at all: taking the curl of the momentum equation
removes the pressure **before** any projection is needed.

Independently confirmed as a *field* identity, at generic off-axis points, via
`curl[(u·∇)u] = (u·∇)ω − (ω·∇)u` for divergence-free `u`:

* `G_A := curl[(U·∇)U]` vs the vorticity nonlinearity built from `ω_A` and `U`:
  max residual **1.652e−10** (8th-order finite differences).

**A defect this check caught.** The first version of the runner had this sign
backwards. The residual came out **2.895** — *twice the term*, not a small
error — which is exactly the signature of a sign flip and is why the check
exists. A second check (`GB_unit_vs_direct`) caught a missing
Gaussian-derivative term in the stripped gradient at residual **3.089e−1**.
Neither was found by reading the code.

One function, `weighted_sq_norm` / `log10_weighted_density`, applied to five
fields. Nothing in it knows which arm it is on; the field is the only thing that
varies, and it is wired all the way through. Lesson 90: **a control that cannot

| B `(u·∇)ω − (ω·∇)u` — vorticity, reconstructed velocity | −19.38 | −76.38 | −304.38 | −1216.40 | **−4864.48** | convergent |

C1 at r=80 reproduces leg 261's banked `+683.4` to within the difference in
angular rule (leg 261 measured on a ray; this leg integrates the sphere).

**An instrument note, not a cosmetic one.** At `r = 80` a Gaussian field is

look symmetric when they are not. Each Gaussian-carrying field is therefore also
written as `unit(x)·exp(logpref(r))` with `unit` polynomial, the density is
assembled in logs, and each pair is checked against direct evaluation where the
direct one is still valid (residuals 0.000e+00, 0.000e+00, 7.077e−17).

## 6. The positive content — an explicit finite mapping bound

The gate's "no" branch is not merely the absence of a divergence. The vorticity
nonlinearity **is** a map `H²(μ) → L²(μ)`, with a bound that is exhibited:

```
‖(u·∇)ω − (ω·∇)u‖_{L²(μ)}  ≤  ‖∇u‖_∞ ‖ω‖_{L²(μ)}  +  ‖u‖_∞ ‖∇ω‖_{L²(μ)}
```

| | `‖u‖_∞` | `‖∇u‖_∞` | `‖ω‖_{L²(μ)}` | `‖∇ω‖_{L²(μ)}` | bound | actual | actual/bound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| witness A | 0.857463504 | 2.828427091 | 0.718071784 | 1.936495591 | **3.691487980** | **0.372629937** | 0.10094 |
| witness B (reconstructed `u`) | 0.666666662 | 0.688598396 | 0.330719583 | 0.755191901 | **0.731194238** | **0.101364697** | 0.13863 |

Both ratios are strictly inside `(0, 1)`: the bound holds and is not vacuous.

**Why this has no velocity-formulation analogue.** Every term of the vorticity
nonlinearity carries a factor of `ω` or `∇ω`, which is Gaussian. `u` and `∇u`
are *bounded* even though neither is in `L²(μ)` — bounded is all that is needed,
because they are always multiplied by something that decays like a Gaussian. In
the velocity formulation the projector's output `grad v` **stands alone**, with
nothing to multiply it down, and `e^{r²/4} r^{−8} r²` diverges. This is exactly
leg 261's discriminator; here it is the derivation rather than the observation.

## 7. The audit — leg 257's S1–S6, step by step

| step | statement | carries over? | magnitude |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | `U` divergence-free and Gaussian, so `U ∈ H²(μ)` | **YES** | `div U` residual 3.886e−11; `ω = curl U` Gaussian too, `‖ω_A‖_{L²(μ)} = 0.718072` |
| **S2** | `P[F] = F − grad v`, `Δv = div F` | **VACUOUS** | no Leray projector exists in the vorticity formulation; curl removes the pressure first |
| **S3** | multipole tail set by lowest non-vanishing moment; monopole and dipole vanish, quadrupole does not | **YES, still true** | monopole −1.134e−16; quadrupole identity 3.342e−14; `Q₃₃ = 0` since `U₃ ≡ 0` |
| **S4** | `\|grad v\| ~ C r^{−4}`, `C ∝ T = ∫\|U\|² > 0`, cannot vanish | **NO — THIS IS THE STEP THAT FAILS** | exponent −2.999999999999, coefficient 0.313328534 = `T/4π` to 2.317e−12 — **and curl of that entire term is exactly 0** |
| **S5** | `∫_{\|x\|>R} r^{−8} e^{r²/4} r² dr = +∞` | YES for velocity, **VACUOUS** for vorticity | velocity density 3.72 → 682.32 over r=10→80; vorticity density −155.93 → −10410.72, same code path |
| **S6** | hence not a map `H²(μ) → L²(μ)`; target forced out | **NO** | it *is* such a map, bound 0.731194 attained at ratio 0.1386 |

The failure is at S4, and it is not a technicality: **the coefficient is
non-vanishing precisely because it equals the energy, and it is carried
precisely by the pressure gradient.** The very feature that makes leg 257's
obstruction unavoidable in the velocity formulation is the feature that makes it
invisible in the vorticity formulation.

## 8. Where the escape route actually lands — the replacement wall

Banking this as an escape route candidate without naming its price would be
dishonest, so the price is measured.

The reconstructed velocity decays **algebraically**, fitted exponent
**−3.0000000000000027** (predicted −3, matching leg 261's class C3), because
`∫ω = 0` for any decaying divergence-free field
(`∫ω₃ = ∫ω·∇x₃ = −∫x₃ div ω = 0`), which kills the `|x|^{−2}` Biot-Savart term
and leaves `|x|^{−3}`. Therefore

* `‖u_B‖_{L³(ℝ³)}` = **0.7307683991070311**, converged (integrand integrable to
  `R = 60`; `r^{−9}·r²` converges).

`u ∈ L³(ℝ³)` is *exactly* the hypothesis of the Nečas–Růžička–Šverák / Tsai
theorem, which forces `u ≡ 0` for backward-self-similar 3D Navier-Stokes. So the
vorticity formulation buys admissibility into the space and hands the target
straight to the ansatz constraint that already binds the velocity form. Leg 261
recorded this composition; here it carries a number.

**This does not un-answer the gate.** The gate asks about the *Leray
obstruction* — a mapping property of the nonlinearity on this space, which leg
257 itself stated ansatz-free ("d=2 and d=3, self-similar or not"). That
obstruction does not carry over. The NRS/Tsai wall is a *different* obstruction,
with a different hypothesis, and it is named here so that nobody reads "escape
route" as "open road".

## 9. Rider, measured not asserted — the weight is adapted to the FORWARD drift

Assembling early (lesson 89) turned up a term worth writing down. Breden-Chu's
`L = −Δ − (x/2)·∇` and Gallay's rescaled vorticity operator are the **forward**
self-similar generators, for which the *growing* Gaussian weight `e^{+|x|²/4}` is
the natural one. A **backward** self-similar profile — Clay's object — carries
the opposite drift, `+(y/2)·∇`.

This touches the obstruction **not at all**: both signs are still maps
`H²(μ) → L²(μ)`, and the magnitudes say so —

* `‖L_forward ω_A‖_{L²(μ)}` = **5.716528960333894**
* `‖L_backward ω_A‖_{L²(μ)}` = **6.041350443296389**

— but the Rayleigh quotients differ (**7.2727** forward vs **6.4545** backward),
i.e. the drift sign flip moves the *spectrum*, not the mapping property. Recorded
here rather than buried, because a later leg reaching for this space with a
backward ansatz will meet it.

## 11. Honest limits

* Two witnesses, not a theorem. The mapping bound of §6 is *derived* in general
  (`|G| ≤ |ω||∇u| + |u||∇ω|`, with `u, ∇u` bounded by Biot-Savart on a Gaussian
  `ω`), but it is *evaluated* on two fields. A general statement needs the
  Biot-Savart `L^∞` bound proved on all of `H²(μ)`, not measured on witnesses.
* The gate concerns the **obstruction**, not the route. §8 names the wall the
  route runs into next, and it is a hard one.
* `H²(μ)` membership of `ω` is used; the leg does not check that the *full*
  `H²(μ)` norm (not just `L²(μ)` of `ω` and `∇ω`) is what a fixed-point argument
  would need. That is a scoping question for whoever picks the route up.
* No claim is made that the vorticity formulation is *usable* for a
  computer-assisted proof on this space. That is a separate leg with a separate
  gate, and stage V's `ℓ¹`-Fourier/radii-polynomial ban still stands over it.
