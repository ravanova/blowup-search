## The read: `arXiv:2006.15776` at primary text

**Morrey norm** (Sec 2.1): `||f||_{Ṁp,l(Ω)} = sup_{R>0} sup_{x∈Ω} [ R^{3(1/p−1/l)} ∫_{Bx(R)∩Ω}
|f(y)|^l dy ]^{1/l}`. This is a purely averaged, ball-integral (Lebesgue-measure) scaling condition
— it has NO pointwise-decay content at all.

**Embedding chain (eq 1.6), quoted verbatim**: "we hold the following embedding relation `L^q(R³)
↪ L^{q,∞}(R³) ↪ Ṁq,l(R³) ↪ Ṁq,1(R³)`, `1 ≤ l < q`. This fact can be found in [5]." `Ṁq,1` is the
LARGEST (weakest-hypothesis) space in the entire chain for fixed `q`, strictly containing `L^q`.

**Density fact, quoted**: "It is worth pointing out that Sawano [21] showed that `Ṁq,l2(Rⁿ)` is
not dense in `Ṁq,l1(Rⁿ)` if `1 < l1 < l2 ≤ q`." — the authors' own justification that different
`l` give genuinely, strictly distinct populated classes, not merely nested technicalities.

**Theorem 1.1**: "Let `U ∈ W^{1,2}_loc(R³)` be a weak solution of (1.3). If `U ∈ Ṁq,l(R³)` with
`2 < l ≤ q < ∞`, (1.7), then `U ≡ 0`."

**Theorem 1.2**: "Let `U ∈ W^{1,2}_loc(R³)` be a weak solution of (1.3). If `U ∈ Ṁq,1(R³)` with
`3/2 < q < 6`, (1.10), then `U ≡ 0`."

**Remarks 1.1/1.2, quoted**: "This theorem extends all the known results involving non-existence
of Leray's backward self-similar non-trivial solutions of 3D Navier-Stokes equations in [3, 11, 19,
25]" — Chae-Wolf (Lorentz `L^{q,∞}`), Guevara-Phuc (narrower Morrey), NRS (`L³`), and **Tsai's
FIRST result** (`L^p`, `p∈(3,∞)`). Both remarks cite exactly this family — never Tsai's Theorem 2 —
as what is being generalized. This CONFIRMS leg 365's abstract-depth genealogy claim.

**But the proof mechanism of Theorem 1.2 (Sec 4, read in full) is not a pure Theorem-1-style
argument.** From the `Ṁq,1` hypothesis and a new Caccioppoli-type inequality (1.11), the proof
derives local decay bounds `||∇U||_{L²(B_{y0}(1))} = o(|y0|^{1/2})` and `||U||_{L³(B_{y0}(1))} =
o(|y0|^{2/3})` as `|y0| → ∞`. Quoted verbatim: "However, in light of (1.5), this is not enough to
show that `U ≡ 0`." (This paper's own eq (1.5) is Chae-Wolf's local-norm decay condition, distinct
from Tsai's own numbering — checked explicitly so as not to conflate the two "(1.5)"s across
papers.) The proof then closes the gap by constructing the pressure via `A_p`-weighted
singular-integral inequalities: "a similar argument has been used by Tsai in [25, 26] for the proof
of his second result (1.4)" — i.e. the proof TERMINATES by reconstructing and invoking exactly the
local-energy-estimates mechanism of **Tsai's Theorem 2**. This is the same finishing move Chae-Wolf's
DSS paper (leg 365's read of `arXiv:1610.09464v2` Theorem 1.3) also terminates in.

**Ansatz check**: grep of the full extracted text for "discretely"/"DSS" — the only hit is a
reference-list title (`[4] D. Chae and J. Wolf, "Removing discretely self-similar singularities for
3D Navier-Stokes equations"`), never cited or discussed in the body. Every theorem and every proof
step is stated "If `u` is of the form (1.2)" — Leray's exact backward self-similar ansatz, the same
gate as Tsai/NRS/Chae-Wolf's exact-SS work. **No DSS content anywhere in the primary text.**

## Classification

**WIDENS**, scoped strictly to the exact-SS ansatz class (the same class both T1 and T2 already
require).

The `Ṁq,1(R³)`, `3/2<q<6` hypothesis of Theorem 1.2 is a strictly broader condition than both T1's
`L³`-convergence test and T2's `decays_to_zero_at_infinity()` proxy, individually and jointly:

- Broader than T1 by the embedding chain (1.6): `Ṁq,1` strictly contains `L^q` for its range,
  so it certainly reaches profiles that fail `L³` convergence.
- Not implied by, and does not require, T2's pointwise single-generic-ray decay test: the Morrey
  norm is purely a ball-averaged (Lebesgue-integral) quantity — it places no requirement on
  pointwise behavior along any particular ray, does not need monotonic decrease, and tolerates
  local concentration/oscillation on small-measure sets that a single sampled ray could catch as
  non-monotonic or non-decaying.

**Evading profile class**: an exact-SS weak solution `U` that is (a) NOT in `L³(R³)` (T1 reads
NOT-EXCLUDED) AND (b) fails the screen's single-generic-ray fitted-exponent-and-monotonic-decrease
test (T2 reads NOT-EXCLUDED — e.g. a transient bump, anisotropic decay concentrated off the sampled
ray, or local oscillation) BUT whose ball-averaged mass on growing balls is nonetheless controlled
at the critical `Ṁq,1` scaling rate. Such a profile is excluded by Theorem 1.2 and evades both of
the screen's current encoded tests. This class is non-empty in principle precisely because Morrey
membership is the weakest (largest) space in the paper's own cited embedding chain and, per the
authors' own citation of Sawano's non-density result, is a genuinely distinct population from the
narrower classes (L^q, Lorentz, narrower-Morrey) that the screen's proxies were built to detect.

## Leg 365's abstract-depth ruling — confirmed, and corrected by pointer

CONFIRMED: exact-SS ansatz only (no DSS content anywhere in the body); and the authors' own stated
genealogy (Remarks 1.1/1.2) does cite exactly the Theorem-1/L^q/Lorentz/narrower-Morrey family, not
Tsai's Theorem 2, as what is being generalized.

CORRECTED BY POINTER: full-text reading of Theorem 1.2's proof (Sec 4) shows the proof mechanism is
a hybrid — it derives decay from the Morrey hypothesis and then explicitly reconstructs and invokes
Tsai's Theorem 2 finishing move to close the argument. And, independent of proof mechanism, the
`Ṁq,1` HYPOTHESIS itself (not just the proof route) is strictly broader than either T1's or T2's
encoded tests as separately implemented in `solver/dssp_screen.py`. Leg 365's "not Theorem 2"
framing was correct about genealogy but incomplete about reach — reading only the abstract could
not have caught the Section-4 proof mechanism or the embedding-chain argument that drives the WIDENS
finding here.
