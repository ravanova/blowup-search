## Method-by-method read of all four reports

**Leg 125.** Measures `a*` as the zero of `Δ(a) := 2c_l/|c_ω| − 1` at `ν = 0` — a
scaling-consistency condition on the *steady* profile equation's own coefficients, computed by
sweeping `a ∈ [0.30, 0.70]` with every point converging to machine precision. `a* =
0.3864963972206034`, "determined two independent ways that agree," residual `1.933e-15`. This
computation never solves the full nonlinear `Ω` system at the crossing — it is closed-form
algebra in `c_l, c_ω`, both read off the profile equation.

**Leg 210.** Verifies leg 185's `ν(a)` sign-crossing by an independently discretized
re-derivation (6th-order differences, chain-rule second derivative, degree-5 Lagrange
quadrature, odd-subspace LU — all four operators pre-validated against closed forms to
`≤1.3e-08`). Locates its own crossing with a **scan** (a bisection was tried first, discarded
per lesson 67 because it silently stepped through non-convergent points and printed a
precise-looking wrong answer), reporting a bracket only between *converged* scan points:
`[0.36, 0.37]`. This excludes leg 125's `a* = 0.3865`. Critically, leg 210's own report states
`a = 0.3865` **fails to converge** in its scheme (relative residual `6.0e-03`), and separately
that its solution amplitude **collapses toward the trivial null** as `a` increases past its
converged range (`a = 0.45`: 160× collapse; Chen's `a = 1/2`: further collapse, consistent with
leg 185's own D1 finding of Newton converging to `Ω ≡ 0`). Both of these facts are in leg 210's
report independently of anything leg 284 later found.

**Leg 283.** Carries leg 210's finding into the record with no new measurement: `a* = 0.3865`
marked UNPINNED, citing (1) leg 185's own two starts straddling zero at that `a`
(`+0.00000035` / `−0.00425080`) and (2) leg 210's independent non-reproduction. Leg 283
explicitly and correctly distinguishes this from leg 125's own `a*` value, which it states "leg
210 neither measured nor refuted" and leaves untouched everywhere. Its sweep of leg 174's
catalog itself returned **0 hits** on any `a*` value — the catalog carries no number to
downgrade.

**Leg 284.** Uses pseudo-arclength continuation in `(Ω_odd, ν, a)` from the independently
converged `a = 0.30` root — the decisive methodological change from both 185 and 210, which
restarted Newton from the Chen profile at *each* `(n, a)` and so had no way to detect a fold
(a branch turning around in `a`) as anything other than convergence failure or basin drift.
Leg 284's `a`-unknown formulation makes the fold traversable. On all four tested grids
(`n = 401, 801, 1601, 3201`) the branch **turns around before Chen's `a = 1/2`**, with the fold
location converging monotonically: `0.39259 → 0.38626 → 0.38592 → 0.38568`, spread `0.008719`
against a pre-registered pass threshold of `0.02` (P5 PASSES), and amplitude at the fold stable
at `~1.506` (a real turning point, not a numerical collapse). Leg 284's own Finding 2
(independently, at Chen's `a`, well past the fold) measures the augmented Jacobian's
`σ_min/σ_max` collapsing to `3.454e-19` by `n = 1601` — seven orders below the control's drift —
confirming that fixed-`a` Newton solves in this scheme's family become severely ill-conditioned
in the same neighborhood where the branch folds.

## The reconciliation

Three of the four legs' measurements — 125, 284, and (weakly) leg 210's own sign-law
confirmation — place `a*` near `0.386`. The fourth (leg 210's bracket) is not a fourth
independent measurement of the *same* quantity: it is a scan using a method-family (fixed-`a`
Newton, restarted from Chen's profile) that leg 284 subsequently showed cannot track the branch
through the exact region where it folds and its Jacobian goes singular. Leg 210's own data
already shows the expected symptom of this — non-convergence exactly at `a = 0.3865` and
amplitude collapse toward the trivial null immediately beyond it — recorded before leg 284
existed, which rules out the explanation being retrofitted to fit a preferred answer. This is
therefore a defensible, mechanistically grounded resolution rather than a vote-count: **state
(a)**, not (b).

**What stays unresolved and is not claimed resolved.** Leg 185's specific claim — that its own
`ν(a)` zero-crossing lands on leg 125's value — remains refuted: its two starts straddled zero
at `a = 0.3865`, an internal inconsistency independent of the fold mechanism. This leg does not
restore that claim. What is newly supported is the separate, narrower claim that `a*` itself
(as a feature of Object B, located by 125's algebraic route and 284's continuation route) is
real and pinned near `0.386`.
