## What was wrong, and what the repair is

Leg 119's mechanism, restated in the module's own terms: the payer rule
`take_T = (cT <= cS)` charges each increment to the cheaper account. As the pair separation
`dφ → 0` the **sup** account does not vanish (`cS → 2cos^α(inner/2) ≠ 0`) and the near kernel
has `|cot(t/2)| ~ 2/|t|`, so the sup-account integrand in `d(log s)` tends to a **nonzero
constant** — logarithmically divergent at the endpoint. Only `cT ~ s^γ → 0` tames it, and that
needs `γ > 0`. At `γ ≤ 0` the returned pair is finite **only** because the log-graded mesh
starts at `s = ε·lo`: it is the ε-truncation of a divergent integral, not a bound, and leg 119's
adversary exceeds it by up to **1.2311×**.

The repair is a **domain guard**, not a change to the majorant. The majorant is correct wherever
the payer rule selects the seminorm account near the pole; nothing about it was touched.

**The predicate is exact and threshold-free, and simpler than the sibling's.** The charged
envelope is `min(cS, cT) ≤ cT` *always*, folding is 1-Lipschitz so `dφ ≤ s`, and
`|cot(t/2)| ≤ 2/|t|`, so the head the `E_N` quadrature discards over `(0, s0]`, `s0 = ε·lo`, is
bounded in closed form by `Σ_pieces 2·cmax^(α−γ)·s0^γ/γ` — which **vanishes as `ε → 0` iff
`γ > 0`**. So the guard's predicate reduces to: every argument finite, `ε ∈ (0,1)`, and `γ > 0`.

**Three policies**, on leg 130's template: `"raise"` (default — a bound that does not dominate
must not reach a consumer silently), `"inflate"` (the head added to **both** coefficients, valid
since `h_S S + h_T T ≤ head·(S+T)`; at `γ ≤ 0` the head is `+∞`, so the honest dominating pair is
`(∞, ∞)` — correct and useless, which is the content of V1/V2), and `"extrapolate"` (the
pre-repair number with a warning).

## What the guard does NOT contain, and why that is deliberate

Leg 130's predicate carries a **payer-crossover** clause — *"`s* = (2cos^γ(θ/2)/ρ)^{1/γ}` is a
representable positive float"* — which is what catches its `ρ = 1e300` violator. **This module
has no `ρ`.** Its crossover is `s* = 2^{1/γ}·cos(inner/2) ≥ cos(inner/2) > 0`, measured
representable over `γ ∈ {1e−3 … 10}` (`2^{1/γ} = 1.072e301` at `γ = 1e−3`). The clause can never
fire, so it is **absent rather than transplanted dead** — lesson 90's tell, caught in the
novelty pass before it was written rather than after.

## The correction to leg 119 — its DIAGNOSTIC, not its verdict

Leg 119 reported its ε-drift diagnostic separating the regimes with *"a clear gap (max `1.34×`
when sound — `gamma = 0.05` is the closest approach — vs. min `1.98×` when not)"*. Its γ ladder
stopped at `0.05`. This leg reproduced leg 119's `γ = 0` column exactly (`16.7098 → 43.8938`,
its own tabulated `16.71/43.89`) and extended the ladder to `1e−6`:

| γ | −1.0 | −0.5 | −0.1 | 0.0 | 1e−6 | 1e−3 | 0.01 | 0.05 | 0.15 | 0.5 | 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| drift `u(1e−16)/u(1e−6)` | 2.633 | 2.634 | 2.776 | 2.627 | **2.382** | **2.359** | **2.168** | 1.606 | 1.129 | 1.001 | 1.000 |

The bolded columns are **sound**. Two magnitudes, both banked:

* Against leg 119's **banked** `1.98` unsound floor, **three** sound configurations misclassify,
  and its quoted max-when-sound of `1.34` is wrong once the ladder goes below `0.05` — the true
  value is **`2.3816`**.
* A threshold **re-fitted** to this ladder does still separate (`2.6268` over `2.3816`), but the
  margin collapses from `1.478×` to **`1.103×`**, a `1.34×` collapse, and must be re-fitted for
  every new γ probed, because the drift is continuous through `γ = 0⁺` (`s0^γ/γ → ∞`).

So the honest statement is not "the diagnostic fails to separate" but **"no fixed threshold on
it is safe"**. Leg 119's **verdict is unaffected** — it rests on the exact predicate `γ > 0`,
which stands and is now enforced. The novelty log carries this as an appended correction to its
own §4b, which had stated the stronger version.

## The band deliberately WARNED, not rejected — and it is the production optimum

The map's own production optimum `(α, γ) ≈ (1.4, 0.15)` has a discarded head reaching
**5.080e−02** of its returned pair at the default `ε`, against the shipped `(1.5, 0.5)`'s
**1.393e−05** — a factor **3648** apart. But `γ = 0.15 > 0`, so its exact majorant is **finite**:
this is **under-refinement, not divergence**, and rejecting it would be a claim about a sound
object (leg 119 separately measured true/bound `0.1081` there, a ~9× margin the head does not
close). It is closed by a `HilbertHolderTruncationWarning` that **changes no returned value** —
which is precisely what keeps clause (b) true. `HEAD_WARN_TOL = 1e-3` is this leg's one free
constant; it governs a warning only, never a rejection and never a number, and sits **72×** above
the worst shipped configuration and **51×** below the worst production one.

**Reported honestly rather than tuned away: production warns on 481/481 of its pairs.** At
`γ = 0.15` the default `ε` does not resolve the head *anywhere* on the grid — `head ~ s0^γ/γ` is
`O(0.1)` throughout. That is the finding, not a mis-set constant; a tolerance chosen to fire on
fewer pairs would have to sit near `5e−2` and would have no margin at all.

**The control that could report the other answer** (lesson 90). "Under-refinement, not
divergence" is a claim that *refining fixes it*. Driving `ε` from `1e−10` to `1e−40` at the
worst production pair:

| | `γ = 0.5` (shipped) | `γ = 0.15` (production) | `γ = 0.0` | `γ = −0.5` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| head shrink factor | **1e15×** | **3.162e04×** | stays `+∞` | stays `+∞` |
| resolved by refinement | yes | yes | **no** | **no** |

The two sound configurations resolve; the two unsound ones do not, at any `ε`. The control
reports the other answer where the other answer is true.

## Residuals — named, not hidden

* **`_raw`'s own arithmetic returns `NaN` below `ε ≈ 1e−20` at wide pairs** (`d = 1.19`). This
  is **pre-existing** and untouched: repairing it would move previously-passing numbers and
  fail clause (b). The refinement control above therefore reads the *head*, which is closed-form,
  and records the `NaN` onset separately in the JSON.
* **`γ ≥ 1` is accepted.** It is not a soundness gap (the far-region integral stays finite), it
  is merely a Hölder class containing only constants. Not guarded, so that no previously-passing
  value moves.
* **`α ≤ 0` is accepted**, for the same reason. `α = inf` is now rejected as non-finite; leg 119
  pre-declared its `(0, 0)` return *vacuously* true rather than a corruption, so this is a
  tightening with no violated case behind it.
* **`experiments/p2_route_d_v8_quadratic.py` gains warnings, not changes.** Its γ sweep bottoms
  out at `0.05`, all `> 0`, so nothing is rejected; small-γ rows will emit truncation warnings
  with identical returned numbers.
