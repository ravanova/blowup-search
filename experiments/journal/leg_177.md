The first half is already banked (`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEJ_V1.md` §7.1). **The second half — that a

### And it does not close at either end of a 6.86-decade bracket on `B`

`B`'s lower end is an alternating-ascent witness; its upper end is the analytic transfer
`B_X ≤ C_eq · B_{L²}`. At `n = 801`:

| | lower witness | upper bound | width |
|---|---|---|---|
| `B_X` | `85.585` | `6.1774e+08` | **6.86 decades** |
| resulting `worst/τ` (`X_h`) | **`9714.54`** | **`7.0119e+10`** | — |

**Neither end is below 1.** The gate's answer therefore does not depend on the one constant
this leg cannot pin, which is the only reason a 6.86-decade bracket is publishable at all.

**The bracket is honestly diagnosed rather than left as a shrug.** The upper end is known-loose
and provably so: `C_eq` runs `1715.69 → 7167.06 → 29320.42`, i.e. ratios `4.18` and `4.09`, so
it grows like `h^{−2}`, while both `B` witnesses grow like `h^{−1}` (`X`: `20.71 → 42.33 →
85.58`; `L²`: `21.06 → 42.75 → 86.92`). **The bracket WIDENS under refinement, so the transfer
bound cannot be the right way to price the `X` realization's budget.** Naming that is this
leg's clearest statement of what it does not know.

**The witness's looseness is measured, not guessed.** In `L2_h` an analytic upper bound exists
(leg 46's own decomposition, whose single norm-specific step `‖s·u‖ ≤ ‖s‖_∞‖u‖` is *exactly*
true in `L²` and false in `X` — which is precisely why the `X` arm keeps a witness). The ratio
`B_upper/B_witness` there is **242.39**, and it is stable across rungs (`241.0`, `243.3`,

## C2 — lesson 90, asked twice, and the second answer is a finding

- **The curvature term is wired**: `‖A‖_{X}/‖A‖_{L²} = 0.0602` and the defect ratio is
  `103.99`. Two very different numbers; nothing is a tautology of the code.
- **The origin restriction is wired but nearly inert**: `‖A‖_{X_origin}/‖A‖_{X} = 1.000379`,
  and the `Θ` gains differ by `0.08%` (`3.7009` vs `3.6981`).

**Why inert, and this is the load-bearing observation.** Leg 46's bordered system already
carries three border rows — `Ω(0)`, `Ω_X(0)`, `V(0)`. So the **enforceable-on-a-grid half of
Xu's origin condition (`φ(0) = 0` on the perturbation) was already imposed by the certificate,
before this leg existed.** What the collocation realization has never had is the other half,
`φ'' ∈ L²` — and on a grid *that is not a constraint at all, it is the norm*. Leg 163's list of
what this repository lacks says "a discretization that enforces the origin condition"; the
sharper statement this leg can now make is that **half of it was already there as a border row,
and the missing half was never a constraint to add.**

## C3 — the mechanism, and it is the leg's cleanest positive result

Xu's explicit singular family `u_λ(y) = y^{1−λ}/(y + i/2)²` against a physical mode, both
sampled on this grid, both amplitude-normalised. Separation = `‖singular‖/‖physical‖`:

| λ | `weighted_sup` | `L2_h` | `X_h` | `X_h` growth 201→801 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | 0.9819 | 0.8151 | **6.015** | **2.35×** |
| 0.50 | 0.9698 | 0.7004 | **24.842** | **3.80×** |
| 0.75 | 0.9610 | 0.6129 | **74.165** | **5.53×** |

The weighted-sup and `L²` separations are **flat in `n`** (`1.0008`, `1.0004` at λ=0.50) and
**below 1** — those norms do not merely fail to penalise Xu's singular modes, they mildly
*prefer* them. The `X_h` separation is above 1 at every λ and **grows with refinement**.

**So a Sobolev norm on a grid does what a weight cannot: it enforces origin regularity, and it
does so more sharply as the grid refines.** That is Xu's §3.2 sentence, rendered as a number on
this repository's own object for the first time, and it is the half of the leg that came out
the way the novelty pass predicted. **It is also exactly why the gate still answers NO: getting
the realization right is not the same as getting the budget right.**

## P4 — leg 56's retracted mechanism claim is TRUE in the space it was retracted from

Leg 56 first claimed both defects were "one interpolation error through two operators"; VER-C
refuted it and leg 56 shipped the correction. **In an `L²`-scale realization the original claim
is correct**, because `H` is an `L²` isometry commuting with `d/dx`:

| `n` | `‖H(Π f) − H_M f‖_{L²} / ‖Π f − f‖_{L²}` | same, with the endpoint-ZEROED operator |
|---|---|---|
| 201 | **0.9215** | 694.41 |
| 401 | **0.9184** | 2843.43 |
| 801 | **0.9243** | **11060.92** |

Left column: flat at `0.92`, i.e. the identity holds to **8%**, the residue being that `H_M` is
truncated while the isometry is on the whole line. Right column: **diverges like `n`**.

**So VER-C's correction was right about the operator leg 46 actually runs, and leg 56's
original intuition was right about the operator an `L²` realization would want. The
disagreement was a statement about the norm.** This is the one place where this leg genuinely
sharpens a banked result rather than confirming it.
