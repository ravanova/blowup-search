## §2. THE CONSTRUCTION — the ansatz, written down before it is measured

### §2.1 Similarity variables with the modulation carried

`λ(t) > 0` is the modulation scale, `y = x/λ(t)`, `ds/dt = λ^{−2}`, `u(x,t) = λ^{−1} V(y,s)`, and
`a(s) := −λ λ_t` is the **modulation parameter** (`a ≡ 1/2` ⟺ `λ = √(T*−t)`, the (D)SS normalisation).
Then, identically,

    R[V] := V_s + a(s)(V + y·∇V) − ΔV + V·∇V + ∇P = 0     ⟺     u solves NS,     div V = 0.

### §2.2 The ansatz `A(ρ₀, κ, α, λ)` — cutoff and modulation INSIDE the ansatz

The profile is poloidal, `U(y,s) = m₁(s) 𝒫[ψ_α](y;c) + m₂(s) 𝒫[ψ_α](y;d)` with
`𝒫[f](y;e) = curl curl (f(|y|) e) = (f″ − f′/r)(ŷ·e)ŷ − (f″ + f′/r) e`, `ψ_α(r) = (1+r²)^{(2−α)/2}`,
`c ⟂ d` (non-axisymmetric), and `m₁, m₂` `2 log λ`-periodic in `s` — leg 381's banked realization.

**The ansatz cuts off the POTENTIAL, not the field**, so that it is **exactly divergence-free by
construction and needs no Bogovskiĭ corrector at all**:

    V(y,s) := m₁(s) 𝒫[g(·,s)](y;c) + m₂(s) 𝒫[g(·,s)](y;d),
    g(r,s) := χ(r/ρ(s)) ψ_α(r),        ρ(s) = ρ₀ e^{κ s},

with `χ ≡ 1` on `[0,1]`, `χ ≡ 0` on `[2,∞)`, `χ` a degree-9 (`C⁴`) smoothstep on `[1,2]`. The
**cutoff radius `ρ(s)` and its rate `κ` are part of the ansatz**, as is the modulation `a(s)`. In
physical space the cutoff radius is `R(t) = λ(s) ρ(s) = R₀ e^{(κ−a)s}`.

**The branches, fixed here:**
- `κ = 0` — cutoff frozen in **similarity** variables. Then `V(y, s+2logλ) = V(y,s)`: the ansatz is
  **exactly DSS**. Reading (c)'s branch.
- `κ = a` — cutoff frozen in **physical** space, `R(t) ≡ R₀`. The natively finite-energy branch.
- `0 < κ < a` — intermediate.
- `κ > a` — `R(t) → ∞`; energy unbounded in `t` (checked in §3.5), so **Clay condition (7) forbids
  it**. Recorded, not pursued.

### §2.3 The profile system of the ansatz — EVERY extra term the modulation and the cutoff generate

With `w := V − χU` (the potential-cutoff commutator, `w ≠ 0` only on the annulus `ρ ≤ r ≤ 2ρ`), the
**total localisation-plus-modulation error** is the commutator

    R_loc := R[V] − χ R[U]
           = T1 + T2 + T3 + T4 + T5 + T6 + T7,

    T1  (cutoff drift)          =  the `∂_sχ` part of `V_s − χU_s`,  `∝ κ`
    T2  (modulation transport)  =  a[(V + y·∇V) − χ(U + y·∇U)],      `∝ a`
    T3  (MODULATION COMMUTATOR) =  the `ṁ` part of `V_s − χU_s`,     `∝ ṁ`, ZERO iff the profile is
                                                                     exactly self-similar
    T4  (viscous commutator)    =  −[ΔV − χΔU]
    T5  (nonlinear commutator)  =  V·∇V − χ(U·∇U)
    T6  (pressure)              =  ∇P_V − χ∇P_U   — removed exactly by taking `curl`
    T7  (divergence corrector)  =  ≡ 0 by construction here — the potential cutoff is div-free; the
                                   field-cutoff variant needs a Bogovskiĭ corrector, which leg 381
                                   measured GROWING at `ρ^{+0.5004}` in `L²`

**Predicted magnitudes on the annulus (`|U| ~ ρ^{−α}`, `|∇χ| ~ ρ^{−1}`), written before measuring:**
`T1, T2 ~ (κ − a)·ρ^{−α}` (so they **cancel identically at `κ = a`**), `T3 ~ |ṁ|·ρ^{−α}`,
`T4 ~ ρ^{−α−2}`, `T5 ~ ρ^{−2α−1}`. In the scale-invariant norms of §3.1 that is
`‖T1+T2‖ ~ |κ−a| ρ^{1−α}`, `‖T3‖ ~ |ṁ| ρ^{1−α}`, `‖T4‖ ~ ρ^{−α−1}`, `‖T5‖ ~ ρ^{−2α}`.
**The modulation term dominates the cutoff terms by a factor `ρ²`.**

## §3. CRITERIA — fixed here, applied mechanically

### §3.1 The named norms (lesson 91: realization, trial space, basis)

- **Velocity form.** `‖F‖_{L¹_t L³_x}`, the scale-invariant forcing norm dual to the critical space
  `L^∞_t L³_x` under Duhamel (§0.3). In similarity variables it is **exactly**
  `∫ ‖R(·,s)‖_{L³_y} ds`, because `‖F(t)‖_{L³_x} = λ^{−2}‖R(·,s)‖_{L³_y}` and `dt = λ² ds`.
- **Vorticity form (LOAD-BEARING, because it is PRESSURE-FREE).**
  `‖curl F‖_{L¹_t L^{3/2}_x} = ∫ ‖curl_y R(·,s)‖_{L^{3/2}_y} ds`. `L^{3/2}` is the critical Lebesgue
  space for 3-D vorticity (`ω ∈ L^{3/2} ⟺ u ∈ L³`), and taking `curl` annihilates `T6` identically,
  so **no pressure term, no Leray projector, and no operator-norm constant enters the load-bearing
  number**.
- **Realization:** leg 381's synthetic poloidal exactly-DSS field, `α ∈ {1.0, 1.25, 1.6}`, `λ = 1.7`,
  `m₁ = 1 + 0.35 cos ωs`, `m₂ = 0.6 + 0.25 sin(ωs + 0.7)`, `ω = π/log λ`, `c = e_z`, `d = e_x`.
  **Trial space / basis:** product Gauss–Legendre quadrature on geometric radial shells × Gauss in
  `cos θ` × uniform in `φ`; the cutoff basis is the degree-9 `C⁴` smoothstep on
  `[ρ, 2ρ]` (control `C6` repeats with leg 381's quintic `C²` smoothstep). Derivatives of `g` are
  analytic; `curl R_loc` is 4th-order central finite differences with a refinement control.

### §3.2 What is measured

For each `(α, κ, mode ∈ {DSS, SS}, ρ₀)` and for `s` on a uniform grid over **one whole DSS period**:

### §3.3 THE CLOSURE THRESHOLD, and what counts as YES / NO — fixed before any number exists

A backward-DSS blow-up requires the construction to run over **`s ∈ [s₀, ∞)`, i.e. infinitely many
DSS periods**. The **closure threshold** is therefore, for any positive `ε_close`:

    Σ(S) := ∫_{s₀}^{s₀+S} ‖curl_y R_loc(·,s)‖_{L^{3/2}_y} ds   must satisfy   Σ(∞) < ε_close.

- **`YES`** — there exist `ρ₀` and one of the named norms with `Σ(∞) < ε_close` for some fixed
  `ε_close > 0`, i.e. the per-period accrual `c_mod` **decays with `ρ₀`** and is **summable in `s`**.
  A `YES` is reported under reading (a) as a **LEAD, NOT A BROKEN WALL**, with the certification cost.
- **`NO`** — `Σ(∞) = ∞` for every `ρ₀`, i.e. the per-period accrual is bounded below uniformly in
  `ρ₀`. Then the number reported is `c_mod` **per unit `s`** and per DSS period, the measured
  ρ-exponent with its residual, and `N(ε) = ε/(c_mod · 2 log λ)` — the number of DSS periods any
  threshold `ε` can afford. **This form of `NO` is threshold-free: it holds for every `ε_close > 0`
  and uses no closure constant.**
- **Reading (c) fires** if the ansatz that achieves a small error is **exactly (D)SS** — checked
  mechanically by testing `V(y, s + 2 log λ) = V(y, s)` to `1e−12` relative, exactly as leg 381
  checks its own field. If the `κ = 0` branch is the only cheap one, the answer is **`NO`** and this
  unit says so and stops.
- **Falsifier that can flip `NO` → `YES` (planted, must be reported either way).** The ansatz is
  *modulated*: the parameters `(a, κ)` are free functions of `s`, and a modulated construction is
  entitled to spend them absorbing the residual. Control `C7` minimises
  `‖R_loc − δa·(V + y·∇V) − δκ·∂_κ V‖` over `(δa, δκ)` **on all of ℝ³, core included**, and reports
  the residual fraction and its ρ-exponent after optimal absorption. **If optimal absorption drives
  the ρ-exponent strictly negative, the gate answer is `YES`.**
