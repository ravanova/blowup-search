## 0.5 THE DECISION RULE — FIXED BEFORE ANY HIT WAS READ

A window is a **REFUSAL** iff **both**:

* **(R1) AN ACT OF NOT-DOING.** The text declines, defers, narrows, abandons, or declares
  un-proposable some *specific* piece of work — not merely mentions that a ban exists.
* **(R2) THE BAN IS THE STATED REASON.** The ℓ¹-Fourier / radii-polynomial ban (or one of its three
  named dead realizations, or Theorem NGX) is given as *why*.

**Mentions are not refusals.** Ban-list recitations, `plan_of_record.py` inventory prints,
ban-walk compliance lines (*"this leg does not touch the ban"*), escalation packets *describing* the
ban, and the ruling documents themselves are **NOT** refusals: they decline nothing. They are
counted separately as `MENTION` so the corpus arithmetic closes.

Each REFUSAL is then classified:

* **APPARATUS-BASED** — the stated reason is that the *machinery is banned per se*. The refusal
  appeals to the rule, to the apparatus's name, or to the ban's existence, **and cites no
  measurement of the candidate at hand**.
* **REALIZATION-BASED** — the stated reason includes a **measurement**: a number, a named space and
  basis in which it was tried and failed, Theorem NGX / `Z₁ ≥ 1` / `σ_min(L_M) = c_s M^{−(1−s)}`,
  legs 54 / 56 / 163 / 176 / 341, or any "we ran it and it failed" clause.
* **BOTH ⇒ REALIZATION-BASED** (reading (d)). **AMBIGUOUS ⇒ REALIZATION-BASED** (reading (d),
  second sentence). **The tie always goes to staying refused.**

**RE-OPENABLE UNDER C1 iff ALL THREE hold** — and the third is the one that does the real work:

1. The window is a **REFUSAL** (R1 ∧ R2); **and**
2. it is **APPARATUS-BASED**; **and**
3. **THE C1 SCOPE TEST.** The *refused work itself* is outside the ban's object: it is **not** a
   `Y₀/Z₀/Z₁/Z₂` contraction in any space, and it does **not** construct **a single bounded
   approximate inverse uniform in `M`**. An apparatus-based refusal of work that *is*
   radii-polynomial machinery is **NOT** re-opened by C1 — C1 is a scope ruling, and that work is
   squarely inside the scope that remains.

**Lesson 91** binds every negative recorded here: a negative names its realization, its trial space,
and its basis.
