### 3.2 The two exterior moment identities — REPORTED AS FAILING

The manuscript requires `∫₀^∞ r²R_θ dr = 0` and `∫₀^∞ rR_z dr = 0`, which is what makes its stress


**The `R_θ` ratio of exactly `1.0000` is the sharp statement:** the residual has **one sign** along
