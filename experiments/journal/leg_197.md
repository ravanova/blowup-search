## What was actually wrong, and it was not a reading error

`arXiv:2208.09445` — Buckmaster, Cao-Labora, Gómez-Serrano, *Smooth imploding solutions for 3D
compressible fluids* (Forum of Math Pi 13 (2025) e6) — has been read at full text **twice** in
this repository: leg 113 (Route-MS) and leg 174 (Route-VBS). It had never reached
`solver/viscous_novelty.py::PRECEDENTS`, the ledger stage V's novelty gate actually reads. Leg
174 measured that hole itself and recorded it as
`arXiv_2208.09445_in_viscous_novelty_PRECEDENTS: false`. **This is the same "found but not
shared-ledgered" gap leg 190 closed for EGM.**

The row is now appended, with leg 174's characterization transcribed:

* **Theorem 1.3** — finite-time singularity for the **3D isentropic compressible
  Navier–Stokes** equations, smooth finite-energy data, density constant at infinity.
* **GRADE B, not A** — the enclosed object is the self-similar profile solving system **(1.5)**,
  the ODE reduction of the **inviscid** Euler system (1.3); Navier–Stokes is reached by the
  analytic stability argument of §§7–8.
* **The viscous term is DOMINATED, not enclosed** — §7: *"in the Navier-Stokes case we need to
  restrict the parameter r to a regime where the self-similar profile dominates the
  dissipation"*, i.e. it becomes an exponentially decaying forcing in self-similar time,
  rather than sitting at the leading scaling order the way `eps` does in DF-CGL.
* **Computer assistance essential and non-removable** — first **10000** Taylor coefficient
  pairs `(W_j, Z_j)` at `r = r*` with rigorous error bounds, **~14 hours on a single CPU**
  (§1.3); Lemmas A.27/A.28; Appendix B *"Implementation details of the computer-assisted
  part"*.
