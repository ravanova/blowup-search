## The located statement, with its hypotheses verbatim

**Elgindi–Ghoul–Masmoudi, arXiv:1906.05811, Prop. 2.1** (Anal. PDE **14** (2021) 891):

> "There exists a universal constant `C > 0` so that if `a` is small enough and if `f` is
> odd, `f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0` and `∫_R |f|² φ(y) dy < +∞`,
> `∫_R f M_a f φ(y) dy ≤ (−1/2 − C|a|) ∫_R f(y)² φ(y) dy`."

with, from the same paper's Main Theorem, **`φ = (1 + y²)²/y⁴`** — `γ = 4` at the origin —
and, from its §1, *"when `a = 2` we get the De Gregorio model and **when `a = 0` we get CLM
model**"*, with `F₀(y) = y/(1 + y²)`, `HF₀(y) = −1/(y² + 1)`.

That is **a published, positive weighted-energy coercivity gap of `1/2` for the `a = 0` CLM
linearization, in a singular-weighted space at exactly the exponent `γ = 4` leg 111 measured
as inadmissible.** The three hypotheses are leg 111's two thresholds, imposed on the
perturbation instead of tested on a fixed basis:

| leg 111's half | EGM's hypothesis |
|---|---|
| "the basis is in `L²_φ` only for `γ < 3`" | `∫_R \|f\|² φ dy < +∞`, plus `f` odd and `f′(0) = 0`, which is what buys it at `γ = 4` |
| "damping at the origin needs `γ > 3`" | the weight is `γ = 4`, and the gap is `−1/2` anyway |

Their §3 states the price openly: *"Since our coercivity estimate from the previous section
relies on `∂_y q(0) = H(q)(0) = 0`, we will use that we have the 'free' parameters `µ` and
`λ` to fix these conditions."* **The window is bought with modulation.**

## The same two halves, paired on this repository's own profile

**Chen–Hou–Huang, arXiv:1905.06387 §2.1** publishes leg 111's damping multiplier and its
derivation: *"we choose a singular weight `x^{-k}` … Using integration by parts, we obtain
`I = ⟨−C(k−1)/2 + (c̄_ω + ū_x), ω² x^{-k}⟩ ≜ ⟨D, ω² x^{-k}⟩`. **We will choose `k` so that the
coefficient `D` is negative** (we choose `k = 4` for `a = 1` and small `|a|`)."*

Their **Prop. 3.1** pairs both halves in one statement, on the CLM profile: weight (3.6)
`φ = (b² + x²)²/(b² x⁴)`, `b = 1/2` — *literally leg 111's `Ω(y) = −y/(y² + 1/4)`*, obtained
in their words *"by using the exact formula of the solution of (1.3) with `a = 0`"* — under
the hypothesis *"`ω₀` is odd, `ω₀ ∈ H²`, **`ω₀,x(0) = 0`**"*. `γ = 4` **and** `ω₀,x(0) = 0`,
together, on this object.

## The obstruction's SHAPE is in print twice, and neither time as a no-go

- **CHH §2.1, at the far field:** *"we have to choose a weight `ϕ` that decays more slowly
  than `x^{-1}` so that the weighted `L²` norm of `ω` is not too weak for large `|x|`. As a
  result, `(c̄_l x + aū)ω_x` does not produce a damping term for large `|x|` … **This is one
  of the subtleties in our analysis.**"* Same integration-by-parts identity, same squeeze,
  threshold `k = 1` instead of `γ = 3`. They escape it by taking the far-field damping from
  the vortex-stretching term instead.
- **Chen–Hou, arXiv:2210.07191 §2, on the 2D Boussinesq operator:** *"Since `ω(x,0) ≠ 0`,
  `η(x,0) ≠ 0`, **we need to choose `β < 1` so that the energy is well-defined** … **This
  forces one to choose a very singular weight to extract a damping term for `η`, e.g.
  `α ≥ 14` if `β = 0`, a new difficulty**"*. Decisively, the mechanism they name is leg 111's:
  it is the **non-vanishing of the perturbation at the singular point** that caps the
  exponent. There the window is non-empty but expensive; leg 111's is empty, and the whole
  difference is the trial space.

## Xu §8: a published weighted-energy no-go on this operator — with a different quantifier, and a carve-out

> "Two natural approaches do not reach the gap. **A weighted-energy/coercivity estimate cannot
> certify the gap**: … a local positive multiplier `+φ HΩ` near the origin (with `HΩ(0) > 0`)
> survives every `L²`-equivalent weight, so **no coercivity certificate in an `L²`-equivalent
> norm reaches the spectral gap.** (The small-`a` coercivity estimate of Elgindi, Ghoul, and
> Masmoudi [15, Prop. 2.1], for odd `f` with `f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0` in a singular-weighted space,
> **is not a counterexample but an instance of the realization mechanism of Section 3.1**: the
> singular weight and origin constraints define their own realization, **in which a gap is
> certified**…)"

This is **not** leg 111's result in print: it quantifies over `L²`-*equivalent* weights (bounded
above and below) — a strictly different class from `φ ~ θ^{-γ}` — and its mechanism is a
surviving positive multiplier, not a threshold coincidence. Its parenthesis hands over the
gate's answer directly.

Xu's **Appendix A** carries the same tension inside his own construction: `‖v‖²_{Y_θ} =
∫₀^∞ |u|²(y² + ¼)² y^{-2θ-1} dy`, `θ = 3/2` giving "core weight `y^{-4}`", and *"**The two
symmetry modes are excluded from `Y_{3/2}` by that weight**: the integrands of `‖u₁‖²` and
`‖u₀‖²` behave like `y^{-4}` and `y^{-2}` at the origin and diverge."* He resolves it, rather
than reporting it: *"the modulation projection is therefore defined on the physical `X` (which
has traces) and mapped into `Y_θ`, not applied within `Y_θ`."*

## What is capped, and what is not

**Leg 111's measurements stand untouched** and are not re-run here: on the odd-sine basis the
gap really does converge to `−(3 − γ)/2` (`−1.499886`, `−1.006201`, `−0.499924` at
`γ = 0, 1, 2`), `A3` really does collapse `+0.042609 → +0.000121`, and `A4_chen_hou` really
is inadmissible for that basis at ratio `1.678e7`. **Nothing in `writeup/` is deleted or
rewritten** (escalation #4 does not apply).

**What does not survive is the reading of them** as "a candidate structural obstruction to
Chen–Hou-style weighted-energy certification on this operator class" (`DIRECTION.md` leg 141's
own framing of the thesis). The window is zero-width **on the unconstrained odd-sine trial
space** — which is not the space the published method is run in, and the published method's
first hypothesis is the one that reopens it. Wherever leg 111's zero-width finding is cited,
it must now be cited as: *the weighted-energy realization is dead for this repository's fixed
odd-sine basis; the coincidence that kills it is the `p = 1` corner of a phenomenon that
Chen–Hou–Huang name a "subtlety", that Chen–Hou meet again at `α ≥ 14`, and that
Elgindi–Ghoul–Masmoudi dissolve with two modulation parameters to obtain a gap of `1/2` on
this very object.*

**And the honest ceiling.** EGM's positive gap is on the `a = 0` CLM object — this
repository's *friendliest* substrate, whose blowup is explicit (Constantin–Lax–Majda 1985)
and whose spectrum Xu has published — **not** on `HL_S2_nonsymmetric`. So this changes
nothing about `L1`, which stays measured-dead in the coefficient-basis, collocation and
weighted-energy realizations alike. **No link of the `L1 → L4` chain moved.**

## Note for the Decision Maker (a scoping note, not a claim, and not an escalation)

The published record shows a *positive* coercivity gap where leg 111 measured only negatives,
and the difference is one hypothesis on the trial space. A leg that re-measured leg 111's form
on the constrained subspace would be well-posed and cheap. **It is not recommended as a
critical-path item and it is not requested here**, because the object on which the gap exists
is the `a = 0` CLM linearization — already-solved, already-published, and explicitly *not*
this repository's target. Such a leg would reproduce EGM, not extend them, and could not move
`L1`. Recorded so the option is visible and priced, not so it is taken.
