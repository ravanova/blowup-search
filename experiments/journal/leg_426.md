   were designed after the ledger pass refuted four of its eight expectations. Their numbers were
   not pre-committed and are labelled so in `dag.json` (`text_pass_is_post_hoc: true`).

   asserts the *banked* ones.
5. `SPINE_M`'s fan-in threshold 20 was calibrated for one pass; on the equation pass it admits 63
   nodes, which is not a spine. `R4` takes the 27-edge chain plus the prereg list, not `SPINE_M`.
