### 0.2 Instrument rules — the leg-392 standard, adopted whole and not weakened

1. **Controls fire BEFORE any substantive verdict is recorded.** A generic positive control; a
   **TOPICAL** positive control in this literature; a nonsense negative that must return
   **exactly 0**; and **AND-operator control pairs** of phrases known to intersect, so that an
   ANDed zero is a measurement rather than an artefact.
2. **`MEASURED` vs `DID-NOT-MEASURE` is a per-query field.** `MEASURED` requires HTTP 200 **and** a
   parsed `totalResults`. A 429 surviving backoff, a timeout, a non-200 or a parse failure is
   `THROTTLED` / `FAILED`, and `total` is `None` **by construction** so that no downstream reader —
   including one who never reads this journal — can render a non-measurement as a zero.
3. The **opensearch namespace actually served** is banked verbatim from the raw feed and the parser
   names no namespace version anywhere. **Checked against the response, never assumed from the
   request.**
4. ≥ 12 s spacing between arXiv calls; exponential backoff on 429; reduced coverage is reported as
   reduced coverage, and a null from an under-covered search is **`UNDER-RESOURCED`, not `NO`**.
5. **A control that does not fire as planted is DISCLOSED in the artefact and the verdicts it
   governs are VOID. It is never quietly re-planted.**
6. Ids are banked, never counts alone.

### 0.5 The adjudication rule, fixed before any result was seen

**Effect (a) is answered YES** only if a located text, read at `FULL TEXT`, states that the
**recovered/converged SET of invariant solutions is systematically skewed** as a consequence of
using the recurrence measure (or a threshold on it) to decide **which candidates are attempted**.
A statement about the **per-guess convergence probability** as a function of the residual is
**NOT** effect (a) — it is about the success rate, not about the composition of the output set —
and it is graded `S0 ADJACENT`, not YES.

**Effect (b) is answered YES** only if a located text, read at `FULL TEXT`, states that continuing
or repeating the search **over the same or a fixed trajectory predominantly returns solutions
already found** — duplication, diminishing returns, or saturation of the distinct-solution count.

**Strength grades**, applied to whichever effect is hit:

| grade | meaning |
|---|---|
| `S3 EXPLICIT-QUANTIFIED` | stated **and** measured — a number, a table or a figure supports it |
| `S2 EXPLICIT-QUALITATIVE` | stated as a claim in the running text, unsupported by a measurement |
| `S1 IN PASSING` | a caveat sentence, no support, not returned to |
| `S0 ADJACENT` | related but does not meet the effect's own wording above — **NOT a YES** |

**The word "silently" in effect (b) is adjudicated separately and is not allowed to rescue a `NO`.**
If a paper reports the duplication openly, the **effect** is in print even though it was not silent
for that paper; the leg reports the effect as `YES` and records the "silently" qualifier as a
separate, weaker sub-claim.

## 2. THE GATE ANSWER

### (a) score-monotone admission bias — **YES**, `S3`, DEPTH `FULL TEXT`

Page, Holey, Brenner & Kerswell, *JFM* **991** (2024) A10, `10.1017/jfm.2024.552`
(arXiv:2309.12754v1), **p.18**: all the UPOs found are relatively low dissipation, the
limitation is attributed to recurrent flow analysis itself, and the more unstable structures
are **`not flagged in this approach at all`**. Reinforced at p.2 and at p.16 §4, where the
two shortcomings are stated as the reason the new method exists.

Chandler & Kerswell 2013 (arXiv:1207.4682v1) **p.13** is the same effect in shift
coordinates and is **twelve years old**: the search was confined along `s`, `m`, so the
recovered set was confined along `s`, `m`, and Series B was commissioned to undo it.

Lucas & Kerswell 2015 (arXiv:1406.1820**v1**) **p.5**: the skew toward low periods, with the
authors' own `R`–`T` shadowing reading of it (`S1`→`S2`).

Redfern, Lazer & Lucas (arXiv:2408.05079v1, **preprint, publication status UNVERIFIED here**)
makes it the paper's whole premise.

**NOT counted, by my own §0.5 rule:** Page & Kerswell, *JFM* **886** (2020) A28
(arXiv:1906.01310v1) p.2 — `S0 ADJACENT`. It is about the *requirement* of shadowing, not
about *which* orbits the requirement selects. The rule that stopped me promoting it was
written before the corpus was read, which is the only reason it can be trusted now.

### (b) re-mining re-finds — **YES**, `S3`, DEPTH `FULL TEXT` — but `silently` **FAILS**

Chandler & Kerswell 2013 **p.14** §4.2 states the `considerable duplication` outright, and
Table 1 quantifies it. Lucas & Kerswell 2015 v1 **p.5** skipped roughly two thirds of the
short-period guesses **because they were known repeats of Chandler & Kerswell** (reference
`[6]`, verified in the bibliography at p.21, not inferred).

**The `silently` qualifier is CONTRADICTED by the sources.** The duplication is reported,
counted in a published table, and budgeted for as a documented methodological decision.
Nothing about it is silent. A `P1` draft asserting otherwise would have carried a novelty
claim its own bibliography refutes on the page. **That is what the check bought.**
