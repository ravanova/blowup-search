## Named mechanism (lesson 91)

**THE COMPACTIFICATION TRANSPOSITION.** The compactification that makes the norm finite
carries the far-field block `r^{-1+iκ}` to `(1-X)^{1-iκ}` — the identical functional form to
clause (a)'s recorded gCLM difficulty `X^{1-iy}` (§26 / Route-E §4.1), at the opposite end of
the domain. **The log-periodic block's regularity defect is an invariant of the entrance, not
a property of the domain's far end: you can move it from infinity to a boundary point, you
cannot delete it.** It is not about the seed, which is why seeding does not touch it.
