## FF1 — the one genuine gap this leg's wider net turned up, and why it stays a NO

`arXiv:2605.19716` (Shao–Wei–Zhang–Zhang), *"Self-similar blow-up solutions of
`d`-dimensional incompressible Euler equations with `C^{1,(1-2/d)-}` velocity"* —
v1 2026-05-19, v2 2026-07-22 — is a genuinely **analytic** (fixed-point, not
computer-assisted) certificate of an inviscid self-similar blow-up profile, complete
with a finite-codimensional stability result. It falls squarely inside gate (a)'s
wording "by any method," and it is a real miss by legs 74/77/82/90: none of their
`"Hou-Luo"`, `"Constantin-Lax-Majda"`, or rank-2/3-specific queries were built to
catch a *different* equation family's own construction (`d`-dimensional axisymmetric
Euler without swirl, not Hou-Luo, not gCLM).

**It still does not answer this leg's gate YES**, for one dispositive reason: it
predates the window by **more than two months even at v2** (v2 2026-07-22, window
open 2026-08-06T00:35:44Z). It was not published *since* the last window closed — it
was sitting in the corpus, unrevised, well before leg 90 or leg 93 ran earlier today.
The gate asks about *new* news since the last close, and this is *old* news the
family's narrower watches happened never to be aimed at. Both facts are recorded
verbatim in `writeup/novelty/leg_123.md` §4 and the JSON's `false_friend_ff1` block,
so the next re-ask does not have to rediscover this from scratch, and so the honest
gap in prior coverage is not smoothed over even though it does not change this leg's
answer.

Its 21-months-older sibling, arXiv:2410.21765 (Ken Abe et al., *"Stationary
self-similar profiles for the two-dimensional inviscid Boussinesq equations"*, v1
2024-10-29), was also found to have just appeared in *Archive for Rational Mechanics
and Analysis* (`doi:10.1007/s00205-026-02195-3`) — a journal typesetting of
21-month-old content, not new mathematics, and also outside the window either way.
