## 4. The two terms that resist — the gate's no-branch, at full strength

### RESIST 1 — **A13, bootstrap / continuity**: its parameter contract is empty at the target

**Specifiable:** its definition (BCG `prop:bootstrap`, l.3033) and its dependency position
(consumes A8/A10/A12, consumed by A14). Both are specified.

**Resists:** its **parameter contract**, at the target's own `δ_dis`.

BCG's chain (l.2994, verbatim) is

```
 1/s0 << delta_0^{3/2} << delta_1 << delta_g*delta_0 << delta_0 << 1/Ebar
      << 1/K << 1/m << eta_w << delta_g << delta_dis = O(1)
```

and it contains the link **`δ_g ≪ δ_dis`**. `δ_g` is not a free label: **A9's decomposition
`L = A_0 − δ_g + K` requires `δ_g > 0`** (`lemma:abstract_result`, l.2764), because that is
exactly what makes `Λ = σ(L) ∩ {Re λ > −δ_g/2}` **finite** — the whole reason the unstable
space `V` is finite-dimensional. The recommended target is `n = 3`,
`δ_dis ∈ (−0.5778, −0.4302)`, i.e. **`δ_dis < 0`**. **There is no `δ_g` with
`0 < δ_g ≪ δ_dis < 0`. The admissible set for the chain's second-from-top element is EMPTY,
not merely small** — checked as arithmetic in the runner, against a **live control** at BCG's
own sign (`δ_dis ≤ 0.1458980`, where 5 of 5 candidate `δ_g` are admissible).

**Why this is not leg 265 restated.** Leg 265 reported that `δ_dis` tops an eleven-element
chain and named two consequences at the level of **estimates** (the `s_0` knob's monotonicity
flips; the Duhamel integral diverges). This leg asks a different question — *can each term be
handed to a builder with a complete contract* — and the answer **localises the damage to one
of the fifteen** and names the exact broken link. The other fourteen, **including A9, A8 and
A12, have `δ_dis`-independent contracts and survive**. That is the specification-level form of
leg 265's measured finding that the sign extraction survives, since `e^{−δ_dis s} > 0` for
every real `δ_dis` and `F_dis` is a **forcing**, not part of `L`.

**Cost consequence, stated as the gate demands.** A13 **cannot be dispatched as a build brief
on day one**. It needs a **prior analytic leg** that re-derives the constant hierarchy without
a positive `δ_dis` at its top — finding a replacement top element, or showing there is none.
**That is one unbudgeted analytic leg standing in front of the build, and leg 265's estimate
does not contain it.** It is also the leg that would decide whether the target is constructible
at all, so it is cheap relative to what it settles.

**Where it sits: layer 5 of 7** — late enough that four layers of build would complete before
it bites, **which is precisely why naming it now is worth more than naming it then.**

### RESIST 2 — **A15b, interval ODE solver**: no BCG/CGSS anchor exists

Leg 265's row A15 reads *"ball arithmetic (Arb) **or** interval ODE solver (VNODE-LP)"* — one
capability with two implementations. **It is two capabilities**, and the gate requires
traceability *to BCG/CGSS*. Measured in the pinned TeX:

| | BCG | CGSS |
|---|---|---|

| `VNODE` | **0** | **0** |
| `interval ODE` | **0** | — |
| `Taylor model` | **0** | — |
| `computer-assisted` | (16 statements, per leg 265) | **0** |

BCG's computer-assisted part **evaluates expressions** — barriers, Taylor coefficients, `r_j`
enclosures. **It never integrates an ODE rigorously.** So A15a (ball arithmetic) is anchored at
BCG `sec:computer` l.5688/5693 and is the **cheapest term in the list** (real substrate:
`solver/interval.py`); **A15b's only anchor is LP `arXiv:2509.12435` — a different paper, and
an unrefereed one.** Nothing in BCG tells a builder what A15b must enclose, because **BCG never
needed it**. If a build wants it, the requirement is imported from LP wholesale, template and
all.

**This is a finding *about* leg 265's row, not against it.** Leg 265 wrote the row as the
tooling family it is, which is the right granularity for a **count**. It is the wrong
granularity for a **bill of materials** — and that difference is exactly what this leg was
dispatched to find. The CGSS column also **independently reconfirms leg 265's
"CGSS computer-assisted content is 0"** on a second instrument.

**Where it sits: layer 1, off the critical path entirely.**
