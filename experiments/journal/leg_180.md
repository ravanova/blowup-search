## The finding, in one paragraph

The strategic review found a **domain mismatch, not a wrong number**. `arXiv:2603.25104`
(Huang–Tong–Wang) scopes the **two-scale self-similar blowup *scenario*** to `a ≤ 0`, reporting
*one-scale* blowups for `a > 0`. This repository's `a* ≈ 0.5–0.55` survival boundary is
measured entirely at `a > 0`. Both statements are true and they are about **different things**,
which is exactly why the prose could drift: the word "two-scale" in this repository names the
**residual/ansatz** `R₂ = Ω H(Ω) − c_tw Ω_X − a U Ω_X` and its `a = 0` anchor (HQW25's exact
CLM traveling wave `Ω₂ = −1/(1+X²)`), while in the literature it names a blowup scenario. The
`a*` finding is **re-framed, not withdrawn**: `a*` is the boundary of the `a > 0` **continuation
of the `a = 0` two-scale traveling wave**, and that continued object is itself published — the
same paper's **Theorem 2.7** gives a traveling wave for every `a ∈ (−∞, 1)` and **Theorem
7.10(3)** its compact support at `0 < a < 1`.

**Leg 112 had already established all of this** (`experiments/journal/leg_112.md`, full text of
both PDFs, plus the numerical re-check of the sign flips). Its own conclusion — *"The a-sign
dichotomy governs which blowup scenario degenerate initial data produce. Whether the traveling
wave exists at `a > 0` is a different question, and its published answer is yes"* — went into a
journal and an executable ledger and **never propagated to the scope lines**. That propagation
is this leg. Nothing here is a new literature claim; every citation is leg 112's.

**The qualifier that matters and was nearly lost twice.** The `a ≤ 0` / `a > 0` dichotomy is
**conditional on degenerate initial data** (the paper's stated purpose, p.3). It is not a
general statement about gCLM at `a > 0`. `PHASE2_P2_NOTES.md` §24 dropped that condition when it
recorded the dichotomy from a search summary, and then drew the inference *"the question is …
whether it is the RIGHT OBJECT"* — an inference leg 112 refuted and which was **still live in
the file** when this leg started. It is now corrected in place, in brackets, without rewriting
the historical record of what §24 actually found.
