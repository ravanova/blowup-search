Leg 313 §2 found Hou `arXiv:2405.10916` on **the wrong side of the screen, three times over**:

## 4. Creation paths — the (ii) branch, named and costed

**P1 — complete Hou's own `ν₀`-continuation plan.** Named in `2405.10916` but not executed
there (drive `c_l(ν₀) → 1/2`, `n(ν₀) → 3`). **Not costed — ruled out, not merely priced.**
Hou's generalized-dimension machinery is axisymmetric by construction; the continuation moves
viscosity/dimension, not symmetry, so completing it clears screen way 2 only (dimension → 3)
and leaves ways 1 and 3 untouched. Its natural endpoint is a stationary axisymmetric fixed
point of the true-viscosity rescaled NS flow — and the obvious next move from there, perturbing
that fixed point to find a nearby DSS orbit, **is** "BIFURCATION OFF A FIXED POINT of a
rescaled flow," the cheap-entrance ban's own literal text. This path terminates at the mouth of
a banned move, not at a seed, so a leg-cost number for it would misstate what it buys.

**P2 — retarget Hou's own PINN/KAN machinery (`2506.19243`) at the actual non-axisymmetric 3D
NS DSS ansatz.** The only creation path this leg finds that is not structurally blocked.
Costed by reusing leg 260/313's five-module floor, at this leg's live rate: `capabilities.py`
reports **49 modules** indexed (grepped live, not hardcoded); this is leg **342**; legs/module
= 342/49 ≈ **6.98**; five-module floor ≈ **35 legs**. Four of the five required modules (3D
non-axisymmetric NS solver in similarity variables; 3D Leray/Biot–Savart, formulation-specific
per leg 332's finding; unbounded-domain discretisation carrying leg 313's measured algebraic
far-field/log-periodic block, 1414.9× radial resolution penalty; phase/gauge bordering
condition) are **unchanged by the seeding choice** — they are needed whether the seed comes
from Hou's late-mesh cutoff, a Newton–Krylov continuation, or a PINN. The fifth module
(periodic-orbit search) is **replaced**, not added: a PINN/KAN training loop enforcing
`V(y,s) = V(y,s+S₀)`, `S₀ = 2 log λ`, `λ` trainable, adapted from `2506.19243`'s demonstrated
unbounded-domain sampling/optimization — genuinely new (no located paper trains against a
time-periodicity constraint for NS or Euler), but built on published, working technique rather
than invented whole. **Reported as a shape, not an endpoint** (lesson 72, same discipline leg
313 applied to its own extrapolations): the ≈35-leg number is the same order as leg 313's own
≈33-leg estimate for the seeded Newton–Krylov route, and the finding worth banking is that
**the floor barely moves with the seeding mechanism** — it is set by the four modules the
seeding choice does not touch.
