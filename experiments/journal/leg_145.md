`arxiv.org`'s `citation_*` metadata on 2026-08-06 and compared field by field: all five

**(b) DOES NOT HOLD, 2 of 12 rows.** Two rows drift between the stored summary and the cited

provenance note*, not in the pre-emption calls. The ledger's conclusions are right; two of
its citations do not say what the rows say they say.

## DRIFT 1 — `2607.19762 Theorem 2` under-supports the note it carries

**The row.** `claim`: *"the CLM linearization's isolated eigenvalues are only the symmetry
modes"*, `source`: **`2607.19762 Theorem 2`**, `verdict`: `CONFIRMED_AND_PRE-EMPTED`.
Its note asserts *"Point spectrum exactly {0,1} on the odd origin-H^2 realization, proved,
**no embedded eigenvalues**"*. The module docstring (L50–52) says the same: *"Theorem 2: the
**full point spectrum** … is exactly {0, 1}"*.

**What XU's Theorem 2 actually says**, verbatim:

> Theorem 2 (CLM discrete exclusion). For the CLM linearization L0 on the realization X …
> σ_disc(L0|X) ∩ {Re > −1/2} = {0, +1}, so the open strip (−1/2, 0) contains no discrete
> eigenvalue.

That is the **discrete** spectrum, and **only in the half-plane Re λ > −1/2**. The two
things the row attributes to it — the *full* point spectrum over all of ℂ, and the *no
embedded eigenvalues* statement — are **Theorem 3**:

> Theorem 3 (full point spectrum over C). Under (D1)-(D4) with λ arbitrary in C, the same
> conclusion holds: λ ∈ {0, 1}. Hence the point spectrum of the physical realization of L0
> is exactly {0, 1}, and the essential line {Re λ = −1/2} of Theorem 1 carries no embedded
> eigenvalues.

**The claim line is fine; the locator is not.** "Isolated eigenvalues are only the symmetry
modes" *is* Theorem 2's scope. It is the row's **note** that reaches past the theorem it
cites. A reader who follows the citation does not find the sentence the row attributes to it.

**Mechanism — and it is a reading-plan artifact, not carelessness.**
`PRIMARY_SOURCES["2607.19762"]["read"]` records *"full text; abstract, sections 2, 3, 6, 7
closely"*. **Theorem 2 is stated in §3.3. Theorem 3 is stated only in §5** — outside that
set. The abstract does point at it (*"to all of C by Theorem 3 of Section 5"*), so the
stronger result was visible while only the weaker theorem's *statement* was read closely, and
the two fused onto the locator that had been read. The module's own `read` field predicts
exactly which citation would drift.

**Verdict unaffected.** XU *does* prove both assertions, in Theorem 3. `CONFIRMED_AND_PRE-EMPTED`
stands. Route-E v1's negative is pre-empted either way.

**Blast radius — 5 sites, of which 4 carry the drift and 1 is correct:**

| site | carries drift? |
|---|---|
| `solver/literature_gates.py:50` (docstring) | **yes** — *"Theorem 2: the full point spectrum"* |
| `solver/literature_gates.py:487` (ledger row) | **yes** — source + note |
| `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEE_V1.md:15` | **yes** — *"arXiv:2607.19762 Theorem 2 (Xu) proves the full point spectrum … no embedded eigenvalues"* |
| `capabilities.py:88` | **yes** — *"point spectrum {0,1} at a=0, which XU Theorem 2 later proved"* |
| `LITERATURE_CHECK.md` verdict-table row | **no** — *"isolated eigenvalues are only symmetry modes \| XU Thm 2"* is **correct as written**, because *that* claim is exactly Theorem 2's scope |

That last row is the useful control: the shared ledger states it right, so the drift is not
systemic — it entered where the note was expanded beyond the claim line.

## Why the module's own test could not have caught either drift

`test_literature_gates.py::test_8_ledger_does_not_rot` asserts: verdict in a closed
vocabulary, `claim`/`leg`/`source`/`survives` non-empty, counts summing, and
`PRIMARY_SOURCES` populated for four hard-coded ids. **Every one of those passes on both
drifted rows.** It gates that the ledger has the *shape* of a citation record. Nothing in
this repository gated that it was *true*. That gap is now closed by
`test_literature_gates_selfconsistency.py` (7/7).

## The honest residual

Verification here is **locator-and-sentence fidelity**, not mathematical re-derivation: this
leg confirms that the cited section says what the row says, not that the paper's theorem is
correct. Route-J already re-derives four of the published *numbers* from the published
equations and this leg does not redo that. Rows whose source is Tier 2/3 (`2302.12877`) are
reported unresolved against `PRIMARY_SOURCES` — a finding about the ledger's structure, not a
verdict on the paper. And the audit read four papers closely enough to check thirteen
sentences; it did not read them end to end, so a drift in a row's *unquoted* clauses would
survive this pass.
