## §0. THE GATE — copied verbatim from the dispatch, NOT re-worded, NOT narrowed

`L5` (`writeup/data/p2_route_l5_finite_energy_v1.json`) swept the cutoff radius on its load-bearing
row `α = 1 | κ = a_physical_frozen | DSS` and banked `c_mod = 869.288` with the reading: *"the error
PER UNIT SIMILARITY TIME saturates ... measured ρ-exponent 0.000109 over ρ0 in [10,1000]: enlarging
the cutoff radius buys NOTHING."*

`CORRECTIONS.md` §53 flags this: **a logarithmic divergence is exactly the exponent-zero case**, and
`L5`'s sweep stops at ρ≈1.26e3 — roughly three decades short of where `L-JVER`'s analogous sweep
flattens to a nonzero floor. The last two increments in `L5`'s own load-bearing row are POSITIVE
(+0.404, +0.052 per decade).

Extend the sweep on that row from ρ≈1.26e3 out to **ρ = 1e8**. Same apparatus, same norm
`‖curl F‖_{L¹_t L^{3/2}_x}`, same ansatz. Report the increment in `c_mod` **per decade of ρ** for
every band.

> **Is the per-decade increment approaching a NONZERO CONSTANT — YES or NO?**

### §0.1 THE PRE-COMMITTED READINGS — fixed before any number exists, not revised

- **YES** ⟹ `c_mod` is logarithmically divergent; `869.288` is a value of `L5`'s cutoff, not of the
  functional; §53's flag is DISCHARGED AS DIVERGENT. **`L5`'s gate answer `NO` is STRENGTHENED** (a
  growing error is further above threshold). Write it that way. **A retraction may not be described
  as progress, and this is not a retraction.**
- **NO**, increments keep decaying toward zero ⟹ `c_mod` genuinely saturates, `L5`'s original
  reading was right, §53's flag is DISCHARGED AS SATURATING. **This is a good result and MUST NOT be
  written as a null result.**
- **Increments neither settle nor decay within reach** ⟹ `UNDER-RESOURCED` with a price.
  **NEVER a `NO`.**

### §0.2 The decision rule, written down before the numbers, so it cannot be fitted to them

The gate asks a limit question about a sequence of band increments
`Δ_k := (c_mod(ρ_{k+1}) − c_mod(ρ_k)) / log10(ρ_{k+1}/ρ_k)`.
I commit, before running, to reading the *last three bands actually reached* by the same test §53
applied to `L-JVER`: **`YES` iff the last three consecutive per-decade increments agree with one
another to within 1 % of their mean AND that mean is nonzero at more than 10× the measured
arithmetic-noise floor of the instrument** (the noise floor being established by the quadrature and
finite-difference self-convergence control, §4c, not asserted). **`NO` iff the increments continue
to fall in magnitude toward zero**, i.e. the last three each shrink and the terminal one is at or
below the noise floor. **Anything else is `UNDER-RESOURCED`.** I fix the 1 % tolerance here because
§53 used exactly that tolerance ("three bands inside 1 %") on the sister object, and I may not
choose a tolerance after seeing the spread.

**The sign is reported whatever it is.** §53's whole structure rests on every increment being
positive. If a negative increment appears at any reach on this row, I report it in the gate answer,
because §53 says that is the most consequential thing that could be found.

## §3. THE ANALYTIC PRIOR, STATED BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT SO IT CANNOT BE RETROFITTED

`psi_α(r) = (1+r²)^{(2−α)/2} = r^{2−α}(1 + r^{−2})^{(2−α)/2}`. If `psi` were the pure power
`r^{2−α}`, the whole construction — profile, cutoff `chi(r/ρ)`, annulus `[ρ,2ρ]`, and every one of
`T1…T5` — would be **exactly scale-invariant in ρ**, and at `α = 1` the pre-registered
scale-invariant size `ρ^{1−α}` is `ρ⁰`, i.e. every norm would be **exactly ρ-independent**. The
only thing that breaks that invariance is the `1 +` in `1+r²`, whose relative size on the annulus is
`O(ρ^{−2})`.

**So the prior is a `NO` with increments decaying like `ρ^{−2}`, i.e. by ×100 per decade**, down to
whatever arithmetic-noise floor the instrument has. I write this here because if the answer comes
back `NO` I must not be able to claim afterwards that I predicted nothing, and if it comes back
`YES` the prior is wrong and that is a stronger result. **The prior is not evidence and does not
substitute for the measurement.** It also does not by itself settle the gate, because a noise floor
that is flat in ρ would *masquerade* as a nonzero constant increment — which is precisely why §4c
is mandatory and not optional.
