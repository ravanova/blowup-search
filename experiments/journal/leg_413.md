## §3. THE ANALYTIC PRIOR, STATED BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT SO IT CANNOT BE RETROFITTED

`psi_α(r) = (1+r²)^{(2−α)/2} = r^{2−α}(1 + r^{−2})^{(2−α)/2}`. If `psi` were the pure power
`r^{2−α}`, the whole construction — profile, cutoff `chi(r/ρ)`, annulus `[ρ,2ρ]`, and every one of
`T1…T5` — would be **exactly scale-invariant in ρ**, and at `α = 1` the pre-registered
scale-invariant size `ρ^{1−α}` is `ρ⁰`, i.e. every norm would be **exactly ρ-independent**. The
only thing that breaks that invariance is the `1 +` in `1+r²`, whose relative size on the annulus is
`O(ρ^{−2})`.

**So the prior is a `NO` with increments decaying like `ρ^{−2}`, i.e. by ×100 per decade**, down to
whatever arithmetic-noise floor the instrument has. I write this here because if the answer comes
back `NO` I must not be able to claim afterwards that I predicted nothing, and if it comes back
`YES` the prior is wrong and that is a stronger result. **The prior is not evidence and does not
substitute for the measurement.** It also does not by itself settle the gate, because a noise floor
that is flat in ρ would *masquerade* as a nonzero constant increment — which is precisely why §4c
is mandatory and not optional.
