## Part B — the measured scope of the Leray obstruction (the DM's mandatory addition)

The brief required that every relaxed-tier candidate be checked against **both** (a) the
Remark-40-silence screen and (b) **leg 257's positive mechanism**, and that I not just re-run 255's
screen unchanged. So I asked what leg 257's mechanism *is*, structurally, and measured its reach.

**It is a multipole statement.** `P[F] = F − ∇ψ`, `Δψ = div F`. The tail of `∇ψ` is set by the
**lowest non-vanishing multipole moment** of `div F`. Leg 257's `|x|^-4` is the statement that the
monopole and dipole vanish and the **quadrupole** is the energy tensor.

### First: leg 257 reproduced independently, then extended

Same witness `U = curl(e^{−|x|²}e₃)` (div-free exactly; 8th-order FD residual `1.4e-13`), but the
source derived in **closed form** — `S = div[(U·∇)U] = 8(2x₁²+2x₂²−1)e^{−2|x|²}` — and checked
against finite differences with clean `h²` truncation scaling (`6.38e-4 → 1.60e-4 → 3.99e-5 →
9.97e-6` as `h` halves). No CAS in this repository, so the symbolic step is checked numerically.

### The six operator classes, measured

Weight `e^{|x|²/4}` — Breden–Chu's `μ`, and (novelty pass §3) **exactly Gallay's `ρ_∞`**. Reported

**Two findings.**

**(1) NEW, and it strengthens 257: the buoyancy coupling is reached, and harder.** For
Boussinesq, `P[ρe₃]` has source `∂₃ρ`, whose monopole vanishes but whose **dipole is `−∫ρ`, the
total mass** — conserved and sign-definite for a transported non-negative density (identity checked
to `1e-10`). Tail `|x|^-3`: **one power slower than 257's `|x|^-4`, i.e. a worse divergence — and
produced by a term that is LINEAR in the unknown.** A Boussinesq-type row leaves `L²(μ)` **before
its quadratic nonlinearity is even considered.**

**(2) The mechanism does NOT reach the vorticity formulation.** `N = (u·∇)ω − (ω·∇)u` measures
`5.74e-64` at `r=10` and underflows beyond, `log₁₀` density `−113.6` and falling — **while its
algebraic factors are large and nonzero on the same rays**: `|u| = 4.4e-4`, `|∇u| = 1.9e-4`,
decaying at exponent −3 and −4. The tails are real (`C3` confirms the velocity itself leaves
`L²(μ)`); **they are multiplied away**, because every term of `N` carries a Gaussian factor `ω` or
`∇ω`. Measured on `e₁`, **not** `e₃`, because the witness `ω` vanishes identically on the `e₃`
axis and measuring there would have silently dropped the `(ω·∇)u` term.

### The discriminator, stated as measured

**It is not "is the model nonlocal".** `C5` **is** nonlocal, and its nonlocal factor `∇c` has a
genuine algebraic `|x|^-2` tail (fitted `−2.0000`) — and its nonlinearity is still Gaussian. The
discriminator is:

> **does the nonlocal operator's output stand alone, or is it multiplied by a localised factor?**

Standing alone → reached (`C1`, `C2`: the projector acts on the whole right-hand side).
Inside a product → not reached (`C4`, `C5`). **That also explains, after the fact, why leg 255's
only two survivors were Keller–Segel**: not because chemotaxis is local, but because its
nonlocality enters multiplicatively.
