## D1 — the residual trend, and why leg 125's one number could not decide it

The two signatures were written into the runner before the traces were read: **non-existence**
= absolute residual plateaus at a positive floor with the amplitude bounded away from zero;
**artifact** = absolute residual reaches the Newton floor with the amplitude falling alongside
it. What the trace shows at `ν = 1.0`:

| quantity | first iterate | last iterate |
|---|---|---|
| absolute residual (RMS) | 7.8133e+00 | **1.2071e-15** |
| amplitude ‖Ω‖∞ | 1.7307 | **1.6056e-15** |
| relative residual | 1.6824e+01 | 5.4857 |

**There is no stall.** Newton *converged*, quadratically (3.8019e-03 → 6.3851e-05 →
1.7971e-08 → 1.2071e-15 over the last four iterates), to the **trivial null Ω ≡ 0**, which
solves the equation exactly. The relative residual leg 125 reported is `‖R‖/‖(c_ω+HΩ)Ω‖` with
**both** numerator and denominator going to zero — a 0/0 ratio, not a residual floor. This is
lesson 67 (gate the quantity the measurement divides by) arriving from the other side: leg
125's scale-invariant residual was the right instrument for catching the collapse — and it
did catch it, the row is flagged `collapsed_to_trivial_null: true` — but it is the wrong
instrument for classifying it, and the absolute residual was never reported alongside.

The `ν = 0.3` control run through the identical code converges to a **non-trivial** solution
(amplitude 1.5056, relative residual 2.4372e-14), so the classifier is not returning one
answer for structural reasons.

## D2 — Jacobian conditioning: no fold, no singularity, and the real degeneracy

Singular values along the whole stalled path. At the point Newton reaches:

* `σ_min(∂R/∂Ω)` = **0.928708** — order one, nowhere near singular;
* `cond(∂R/∂Ω)` = **4.8567e+03** — the *best*-conditioned point on the entire path (it is
  1.159e+06 at iterate 2 and 6.600e+07 at iterate 3, both at bounded amplitude, then falls);
* `‖∂R/∂a‖` = **9.8198e-30**, down from 1.281e+01 at the start.

So the trivial null is a **non-degenerate root** of the residual in `Ω` — which is exactly
*why* Newton converges to it quadratically — and the only thing that becomes singular is the
`a`-column, because `∂R/∂a = −(VHΩ)(DΩ)` is **quadratic in Ω** and therefore vanishes
identically wherever Ω does. That is why `a` freezes at −10.09246141 and stays there for the
last four iterates: it stopped being identifiable, for a reason with no bearing on existence.
**No fold, no singular Jacobian, no obstruction anywhere on the path.**

## D3 — the parametrization degeneracy is real, and it belongs to the solver

Leg 125 wrote the covariance down in `m7_gamma2_branch`'s docstring and used it only as a
*check*. Differentiating it at `μ = 1` turns it into an exact **null relation**:

    F(Ω(·/μ), μ²ν)(X) = F(Ω, ν)(X/μ)   ⟹   ∂F/∂Ω[−X Ω_X] + 2ν ∂F/∂ν = 0   at every solution.

Discrete violation, relative, under refinement: **2.0682e-02 (n=401) → 3.0873e-03 (n=801) →
1.7661e-03 (n=1201)**. It converges to zero, so the identity is exact in the continuum and
what is measured is sinh-grid discretisation error — the only honest way to claim an identity
holds. Consequently `(−XΩ_X, 2ν)` is a null direction of the `(Ω, ν)` Jacobian **along every
solution**: `ν` is a **gauge coordinate** of this profile equation, not an independent
parameter, and leg 125's continuation *in* `ν` with the dilation freedom unfixed was moving
along a gauge orbit rather than along a branch. The corroborating tell sits in the same table:
the `a` that `newton_gamma2` converges to is itself **grid-dependent** (0.38208549 → 0.37499471
→ 0.37140324), which a determinate solution's parameter would not be.

This is the defect the novelty pass pre-committed to reporting at solver altitude if it
appeared: every dynamic-rescaling paper in the Q3 results fixes the profile's scaling freedoms
by explicit normalization conditions, and `newton_gamma2` fixes `c_ω` (amplitude) but leaves
the dilation freedom open. `newton`, the *other* routine in the same module, does impose it
(`scale_gauge`). The two routines disagree about gauge discipline and the stalled one is the
one that skipped it.

| exact dilation of leg 125's own `ν`=1e-3 solution (μ=31.623) | +0.37973896 | 1.347e-14 | 1.5135 |
| exact dilation of its `ν`=1e-2 solution (μ=10.000) | +0.32035523 | 3.452e-14 | 1.7173 |
| exact dilation of its `ν`=0.1 solution (μ=3.162) | +0.28342063 | 4.029e-14 | 1.8859 |
| exact dilation of its `ν`=0.3 solution (μ=1.826) | +0.38243817 | 9.823e-15 | 1.5070 |
| Chen profile × 2 amplitude | +0.20622444 | 5.717e-14 | 2.4107 |
| Chen profile × 0.5 amplitude | +2.48486371 | 1.903e+00 | 0.0000 |
| Chen profile, `a₀` = 0.0 | +6.55954914 | 4.191e+00 | 0.0000 |

**5 of 8 starts reach a non-trivial solution at the target leg 125 could not solve**, worst
relative residual **5.717e-14**, in 2–7 iterations. That is *"recoverable convergence from a
different initial guess at the SAME target"* — the gate's solver-artifact criterion, word for
word. And the three that fail are all basin failures of the same kind (collapse to Ω ≡ 0),
including one produced by nothing more than halving the guess's amplitude.

The second thing this table says is the one that limits the good news: the five recovered
solutions **disagree with each other on `a`**, spread **0.1762** (0.20622 to 0.38244), all at
relative residual ~1e-14 at the *same* `ν`. So at a single `ν` the solution set is at least
one-parameter, and recovering convergence does not by itself deliver a determinate profile.
Leg 125 saw this from the other side ("short by one equation") and was right.

## D5 — is a genuine profile reachable? Partly, and `a` decides

D3 says how to parametrize: `a` is a **model parameter**, so fix it; the dilation gauge must be
imposed; and `ν` is then the **unknown**. That system is square (n+1 equations, n+1 unknowns).
Solved once per `a`, from two different `ν` starts, `c_l = 1/2` and `c_ω = −1` imposed
throughout (i.e. Δ = 0, the γ = 2 steadiness condition):

| `a` | `ν` from `ν₀`=0.3 | `ν` from `ν₀`=0.01 | admissible (`ν`>0)? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.30 | **+0.01799583** | **+0.01799583** | **yes** |
| 0.3865 (leg 125's `a*`) | +0.00000035 | −0.00425080 | at the crossing |
| 0.45 | −0.00606636 | −0.00792936 | no |
| **0.50 (Chen's)** | **−0.00817525** | **−0.00895316** | **no** |
| 0.55 | −0.00874691 | −0.00930286 | no |
| 0.70 | −0.00865086 | −0.00949025 | no |

Every one of these converges quadratically to relative residual ≤ 5.2e-14 — so the stall was a
parametrization problem end to end. The content is the **sign** of the recovered `ν`, which
agrees across both starts at every `a`: **positive — a genuine diffusive profile — below
`a* ≈ 0.3865`, and negative — anti-diffusive, not a viscous profile at all — at and above it,
including Chen's `a = 1/2`.**

The zero crossing lands on **leg 125's own independently measured `a* = 0.3864963972206034`**,
which came from a completely different computation (the Δ(a) sweep at `ν = 0`, with `c_l` as
the output). Two different calculations agreeing on the same number is a cross-check, not a
restatement.

The `a = 0.30` object is refined, because a single converged solve proves nothing:

* **grid**: `ν` = 0.01802863 (n=201) → 0.01799583 (401) → 0.01799376 (801) → **0.01799364**
  (1201) — converged to 6 digits;
* **truncation**: `ν` = 0.01800309 at `X_max`=100.9 → 0.01799376 at 745.2 → **0.01799378** at
  5506.6, i.e. insensitive over a 55× domain extension, with the profile decayed to
  `|Ω(edge)|/‖Ω‖∞` = **1.92e-08** at the widest;
* **symmetry**: odd-symmetry defect **4.22e-14**.

**Honest limits on this half, stated with it and not after it** (lesson 76 — the negative
construction stays in the artifact). These are float solutions of a *discretised* equation on
a *truncated* domain. No existence claim is made and no certificate is computed. Where `ν` < 0
the two starts differ by up to **31%** in magnitude (a=0.45: −0.00607 vs −0.00793), so the
square system still has more than one root there and **only the sign is banked**. And the
negative half is a measurement on this discretisation, **not a non-existence proof** at Chen's
`a`.

## D6 — the classifier can return the other answer (lesson 90)

A classifier whose non-existence arm cannot fire is not a classifier. The same Newton was run
on a **deliberately inconsistent** over-determination — two contradictory normalizations,
`Ω_X(0) = g` and `Ω_X(0) = m·g` with `m ≠ 1`, which provably has no solution:

| `m` | consistent? | residual floor | amplitude | signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | yes | 4.5334e-14 | 3.1992 | CONVERGED |
| 1.5 | no | **1.5338e-01** | 3.6040 | **RESIDUAL PLATEAU at a positive floor, bounded amplitude** |
| 3.0 | no | **6.1354e-01** | 4.6986 | **RESIDUAL PLATEAU at a positive floor, bounded amplitude** |

The plateau is flat to four significant figures over the last five iterates (1.534e-01 ×5),
the amplitude stays bounded, and the floor **scales exactly with the imposed inconsistency**:
measured ratio **4.0000** against the predicted `(3−1)/(1.5−1) = 4`. So the non-existence arm
fires, quantitatively, on a system that deserves it — and it did *not* fire on leg 125's
stall. That is what makes the artifact verdict a measurement rather than a preference.

## The gate, answered in its pre-committed wording

> *"Does the Newton stall on Object B, diagnosed against standard non-existence signatures
> (residual plateau shape, Jacobian singularity structure, parametrization degeneracy) versus
> standard solver-artifact signatures (basin-of-attraction sensitivity, recoverable
> convergence from a different initial guess at the SAME target), classify as one or the
> other?"*

**SOLVER ARTIFACT.** All three non-existence signatures are absent and both artifact
signatures are present:

* **residual plateau shape** — absent. Absolute residual 1.2071e-15 with amplitude 1.6056e-15;
  Newton converged to the trivial null. D6 shows what a real plateau looks like in the same code.
* **Jacobian singularity structure** — absent. `σ_min(∂R/∂Ω)` = 0.928708, `cond` = 4.8567e+03
  at the reached point; the only collapse is the `∂R/∂a` column at 9.8198e-30, which is
  quadratic in Ω by construction.
* **parametrization degeneracy** — **present, and it is the solver's**: `ν` is a gauge
  coordinate (exact null relation, discrete violation 2.07e-02 → 1.77e-03 under refinement),
  so continuing in `ν` with the dilation freedom unfixed was continuing along a gauge orbit.
* **basin sensitivity** — present. Halving the initial amplitude alone flips the outcome.
* **recoverable at the same target** — present. 5 of 8 starts, worst relative residual 5.717e-14.

**What changed the outcome**, exactly, as the branch requires: **the initial guess, and
nothing else.** Leg 125 used one fixed guess (Chen's inviscid profile) at every `ν`, and at
`ν = 1.0` that guess lies in the basin of the trivial null. Seeded instead with the exact
dilation of leg 125's *own* lower-`ν` solutions — which the covariance identity says already
**are** solutions at `ν = 1.0` — it converges in 2–3 iterations.

**Whether a genuine profile becomes reachable:** **partly, and `a`-dependently.** Re-parametrised
as D3 prescribes, an admissible `ν > 0` profile is reachable at `a = 0.30`
(`ν = 0.01799364`, grid-converged, truncation-insensitive over 55× of domain), and **is not
reachable at Chen's `a = 1/2`, where the recovered `ν` is negative (−0.00818 / −0.00895) —
anti-diffusive, not a viscous profile at all.** The sign crossing sits at leg 125's own
`a* = 0.3865`.

**Branch taken:** *solver artifact → report exactly what changed the outcome and whether a
genuine profile becomes reachable; **ESCALATE** as a candidate follow-up construction leg; do
**not** build the corrected continuation here.* No corrected continuation is built in this
module — D5 is a set of single square solves, deliberately not a continuation.

## What this leaves for the DM, stated as a candidate and not a result

A follow-up **construction** leg is now well-posed in a way it was not before: fix `a`, impose
the dilation normalization, solve for `ν`, and continue in **`a`** (the only non-gauge
parameter) rather than in `ν`. Its first job is the thing this leg deliberately did not do —
establish whether the `a < a*` objects are admissible *profiles* (far-field behaviour under a
genuine second-order operator, uniqueness, and the multiplicity D4 and D5 both measured) and
not just converged discrete solves. **The `a`-dependence is what makes it worth a leg**, and it
also sharpens leg 174's (VBS) catalog input, which is the other thing §185 asked for:

> **for leg 174** — the "missing rung" framing **survives at Chen's `a`**, and for a sharper
> reason than a stall. It is not that a viscous γ = 2 gCLM steady state fails to *compute*;
> it is that the steadiness condition Δ = 0 and Chen's advection `a = 1/2` demand
> **opposite signs of `ν`**. But the framing does **not** survive as a blanket statement about
> the model: below `a* ≈ 0.3865` the object exists numerically. Any catalog entry saying "no
> viscous γ = 2 gCLM profile exists" would be wrong as written; "none at Chen's `a`, and the
> sign of `ν` crosses at `a* ≈ 0.3865`" is what was measured.

## Honest ceiling, pre-committed and unchanged

Not movement on `L1→L4`; not Clay. Clay odds stay **~0.05%**. In 185 legs no link of the chain
