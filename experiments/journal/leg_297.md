## Leg 252's three options, quoted verbatim (from `experiments/journal/leg_252.md`)

> 1. **Land the regeneration whole** — artifact becomes self-consistent and
>    current-environment-reproducible; accepts that 202 leaves move at once,
>    and needs the downstream readers (fig29's evidence script, leg 236)
>    re-checked.
> 2. **Keep the stale artifact, land nothing** — leaves the `margin` field
>    wrong by `6.55e+11x`, i.e. leaves residue 1 open. Not recommended: the
>    field is simply incorrect, which is what leg 247 found.
> 3. **Re-bank deliberately, as its own leg**, with the environment pinned
>    and the 202-leaf move stated as a finding in its own right.

## Decision: Option 3

**Reasoning, applying leg 252's own stated framing, not a new criterion:**

- Leg 252 itself ruled out Option 2 in its own words ("Not recommended: the
  field is simply incorrect"). That leaves 1 and 3, both of which land the
  same regenerated numbers — they differ only in *how deliberately and
  documentedly* the landing happens, per leg 252's own phrasing ("as its own
  leg", "environment pinned", "stated as a finding in its own right" vs. "land
  ... whole").
- Leg 252 explicitly deferred this exact choice to "integration" ("**That is
  a decision for integration, not for this leg.**") — this leg (297) *is*
  that dedicated integration leg, dispatched for exactly this purpose. Option
  3's own description ("as its own leg") is a description of this leg's
  dispatch, not a description of leg 252's own dispatch (which was an
  artifact-freshness leg that escalated on discovering the 202-leaf move was
  bigger than a one-field fix). Applying Option 3 is therefore following leg
  252's own reasoning about *which future leg* should do this, not inventing
  a new criterion.
- Leg 252's own residue #1 explicitly names the gap Option 3 closes and
  Option 1 does not: "Nothing in the repository records the environment its
  banked numeric artifacts were produced in." Option 1's own definition
  ("land the regeneration whole") says nothing about recording environment;
  Option 3's definition requires it verbatim. Since leg 252 flagged the
  missing environment record as a residue in its own right, Option 3 is the
  literal execution of fixing that residue, not merely a stylistic
  preference between otherwise-identical options.
- Downstream-reader re-checking, which Option 1's definition calls out as a
  cost still owed, has already happened as an independent consequence of
  other, already-landed legs, not as part of this leg's decision-making:
  leg 294 (Route-V11X, landed) already consolidated the impact trace against
  leg 252's regenerated JSON and re-based leg 236 against it in prose; leg
  295 (landed) already fixed PUB3's one stale citation site flagged by 294.
  fig29's evidence script and the remaining two downstream documents 294
  flagged are out of this leg's territory (a "future light
  pointer-correction leg" per 294's own framing) and are not touched here.

**Conclusion applying leg 252's own reasoning: Option 3.** Not Option 1
(leaves the environment-recording residue open, which leg 252 itself flagged
as a gap), not Option 2 (leg 252 itself said "Not recommended").
