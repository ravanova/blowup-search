# Leg 252 — Route-VBRG: regenerating Route-D v11's banked anchor JSON

**Role: LEG.** Branch `leg/252-vbrg-v1`. Takes up the residue leg 247 declared
and deliberately did not touch: the banked artifact
`writeup/data/p2_route_d_v11_anchor.json` still carried the pre-repair
`v5_budget` margin.

## The gate, in its pre-committed wording

> "Does regenerating the banked anchor JSON from leg 247's repaired
> `experiments/p2_route_d_v11_anchor.py` produce a margin at `a=0.45` matching
> leg 247's own reported 62.02x-budget-miss finding, with every OTHER banked
> value in the JSON unchanged?"

**NO — and, as in leg 247, it is a conjunction whose two clauses fail for
different and separable reasons.** Escalated per the dispatch's NO branch:
**branch pushed, `main` NOT touched.**

- **Clause 1 (margin matches leg 247's reported 62.02x): NO, by 0.82%.** The
  regeneration gives **`62.53156368658738x`**, leg 247's *headline* was
  **`62.02373957411577x`**.
- **Clause 2 (every other banked value unchanged): NO, by a wide margin.**
  **202 of 359** banked leaves moved outside `v5_budget`/`meta`.

Both failures are explained below, and neither turns out to impeach leg 247's
repair. One of them impeaches the *banked artifact* far more broadly than the
single `margin` field this leg was dispatched to refresh.

## First: leg 247's repair was ported, not waited for

Leg 247's repair was on `leg/247-vbr-v1` commit **`10be909`** and was **not** an
ancestor of `origin/main` (checked with `git merge-base --is-ancestor`). Per this
leg's brief I ported it into my own territory rather than blocking. It was
applied as leg 247's own diff and the result is **byte-identical** to
`10be909:experiments/p2_route_d_v11_anchor.py` — verified by `diff`, zero lines
differing. **No line of the repair logic was re-authored or re-tuned here.**
Provenance is recorded in the regenerated data itself
(`meta.repair_ported_from`), not only in this prose.

## R1 — Clause 1: the 0.82% is leg 247's own, already-published number

This is the key measurement of the leg, and it *exonerates* the repair:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| regenerated `violation_x` at `a=0.45` | `62.53156368658738` |
| leg 247's **fresh re-solve** (`r2_resolve.rows[1].violation_x_fresh`) | `62.53156368658738` |
| relative difference | **`0.000e+00` — exact float equality, to the last bit** |
| leg 247's **headline** (`r1_corrected_block`) | `62.02373957411577` |
| relative difference vs headline | `8.188e-03` |

My from-scratch regeneration reproduces leg 247's own independent from-scratch
re-solve **exactly, bit for bit**. The 0.82% gap is *not* a new disagreement: it
is precisely the banked-vs-fresh gap leg 247 measured and published in its own
commit message ("fresh `1.532023e-02` vs banked `1.519582e-02` (0.82%)"). My
regenerated `a=0.45` weighted defect is `0.015320233103213909` — leg 247's
`1.532023e-02`.

**Reading.** Leg 247's headline was computed by applying the corrected selection
to the **banked** `v2`/`v4` rows (correctly so — a selection change cannot be
re-solved away, and that made the repair auditable against the artifact it was
repairing). A *regeneration* re-solves those rows first, so it necessarily
carries the fresh `a=0.45` defect instead. **The repair logic reproduces
perfectly; the two numbers differ only in which input row they were built on,
and leg 247 published both.** So clause 1 fails as written, and the finding
behind it stands undamaged.

The corrected margin is correspondingly `1.5991923775e-02` rather than
`1.6122858874e-02` — still **below 1**, still a budget **miss**, still ~62x, and
still `1` row over budget out of `11` good rows. The correction against the
stale bank is a factor of **`6.55e+11`** (`1.0467862586e+10` -> `1.5992e-02`).

  `grids_reaching_machine_precision` **`1` -> `2`** in both.

### Why — and it is NOT solver drift

Ruled out at source rather than assumed:

- `solver/profile_newton.py` has **one** commit on `origin/main`, `6a17ce6` —
  the *same commit* that banked the JSON. The solver has not changed since.
- The runner is genuinely deterministic *within this environment*: R1 shows two
  independent processes (leg 247's harness and this regeneration) landing on
  `62.53156368658738` bit-for-bit.

So the same code, run on the same inputs, produces materially different numbers
**than the banked artifact** while being bit-reproducible **today**. The banked
artifact was produced on 2026-07-31 in a different numeric environment; this
machine is `Intel i7-10750H`, `python 3.12.3`, `numpy 2.5.1`,
`scipy-openblas 0.3.33.112.0`. The runner's docstring claims "deterministic
Newton, no GA, no seeds", and that is true *within* an environment — but it is
**not** environment-portable, and the rows where it is least portable are exactly
the ill-conditioned ones (near and past the survival boundary, and rows whose
defect sits at the `1e-14` rounding floor) where Newton's basin selection is
decided by the last bits.

## Why this is escalated rather than banked and closed

The dispatch's NO branch is unambiguous and both of its triggers fired. Beyond
the letter of it, the substantive reason:

The leg was dispatched as "a pure artifact-freshness fix" touching one stale
field. It is not. Refreshing `margin` from a fresh run also rewrites **202 other
banked leaves**, including a `converged` flag and two `grid_converged` counts —
values other legs may have read. Landing that silently under the banner of a
one-field freshness fix would be exactly the kind of unannounced movement the
standing discipline exists to prevent. **That is a decision for integration, not
for this leg.**

The regenerated artifact **is** committed on this branch, because it is the
evidence, and because hand-editing a single field into the stale file was
explicitly forbidden ("regenerate, do not hand-edit") and would have manufactured
a JSON that no run ever produced — internally inconsistent, with a `v5_budget`
margin derived from `v2` rows sitting next to it that disagree with it.

**Three options for integration, none of which this leg took:**

1. **Land the regeneration whole** — artifact becomes self-consistent and
   current-environment-reproducible; accepts that 202 leaves move at once, and
   needs the downstream readers (fig29's evidence script, leg 236) re-checked.
2. **Keep the stale artifact, land nothing** — leaves the `margin` field wrong
   by `6.55e+11x`, i.e. leaves residue 1 open. Not recommended: the field is
   simply incorrect, which is what leg 247 found.
3. **Re-bank deliberately, as its own leg**, with the environment pinned and the
   202-leaf move stated as a finding in its own right.

**Sequencing note for leg 236:** 236 was told to read this leg's regenerated
JSON rather than the stale one. Because this escalated, **nothing landed on
`main`** — so 236 will still see the stale artifact. It should be told
explicitly, not left to infer it. The stale `margin` (`1.0467862586e+10`) is
wrong by `6.55e+11x`; if 236 reads that field, its result is invalid.

## Residues

1. **The artifact is not environment-portable.** Same code, same inputs, 46
   leaves differing by >10% across environments, one `converged` flag flipped.
   Nothing in the repository records the environment its banked numeric
   artifacts were produced in. This is a general hazard, not a Route-D v11 one.
2. **A second worktree was running this same runner concurrently.** PID 1202017
   / 1261585 in `.claude/worktrees/agent-a8a4edd4c145c8418`, twice, while this
   leg ran. It writes its own copy so there is no diff collision, but another
   live leg is regenerating an artifact this leg declared as territory. Flagged
   for the orchestrator.
3. **Cost.** The full regeneration is ~7-8 CPU-hours under this run's
   contention; a single `n=801` Newton solve was timed at `335s` and `n=401` at
   `70.4s` with load average ~46 on 12 cores. Two earlier attempts were killed
   mid-run at ~71 and ~50 minutes; the run only completed once launched fully
   detached via `setsid`. Anyone re-running this should budget for it and detach.
