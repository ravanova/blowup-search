## 1. (a) THE MATH IS FORCED, AND IT IS FORCED INDEPENDENT OF ADOPTION STATUS

The point of the corrected framing is that (a) must be answerable *without* the false "already
used" premise. It is: the question is what the rule says, not who has adopted it.

**Six formulations of the 2/3 rule, each evaluated at `n=3`** (`V5`). Wavenumbers retained:

| reading | source | keeps `\|k\|` | admits `n=3` |
|---|---|---|---|
| loose `\|k\| <= n/3` | `spectral_utils.py:36`, `boussinesq.py:153` as `main` ships | `{0, 1}` | yes |
| floor `\|k\| <= floor(n/3)` | the corpus's informal "keep the first 2/3" | `{0, 1}` | yes |
| strict `\|k\| < n/3` | Bowman 2013 p.29; leg 129's repair | `{0}` | **no** |
| Bowman padding `N >= 3K+1` | Bowman 2013, as a padding requirement | `{0}` | **no** |
| zero `\|k\| >= (2/3)k_Nyquist` | arXiv:2603.08892 (2026) | `{0}` | **no** |
| zero the highest `1/3` of components | FourierFlows.jl `dealias!` | `{0, 1}` | **no** (disqualified) |

**Readings admitting `n=3` that are not the cut leg 120 measured defective: 0.** Both admitting
readings *are* the loose cut — the same off-by-one, stated two ways.

**The one loophole is closed by measurement, not by rhetoric.** The novelty pass had argued the
FourierFlows fraction-of-modes rule denies `n=3` because "one third of three modes is one, and
the two non-constant modes `k = ±1` are the highest". Implemented **literally**, that is wrong:
`ceil(3/3) = 1` component is zeroed, and only one of the conjugate pair `k = ±1` goes, so the
literal rule *does* leave a non-constant mode. It is disqualified for a different and stronger
reason this leg measured: the resulting mask is **not conjugate-symmetric**, so it maps a real
field to a complex one. Masking a real test field through it leaves an **imaginary residue of
2.23e-01** against `0.00e+00` for all five symmetric readings. All four consumer modules evolve
real fields. That is not a looser reading of the 2/3 rule; it is a mask no consumer can run.
This corrects the novelty pass's stated reason while confirming its conclusion — recorded rather
than quietly aligned.

**The `n=3` band under the loose cut is genuinely aliased** (`V4`, Bowman's own experiment: put
the field on the top retained mode, square it, read that mode back — the exact continuum
coefficient there is zero). At `n=3`: **2.4999999999999994e-01**, i.e. **0.4999999999999999 of
the field's own amplitude**. Positive control at `n=4` (3 ∤ 4): **7.4988e-33**. **Separation:
31.5 decades.**

**Acceptance differs at exactly one grid in 5000** (`V1`): `n = 3`, loose accepts → strict
refuses. The lesson-90 control — a deliberately wrong rule `cut = n//3 - 1` nobody proposes —
reports **3** differing grids (`n = 3, 4, 5`) through the identical census, so the census can
report a number other than the one it reports.

**Nobody's judgment is exercised at `n=3`.** `solve_boussinesq`'s guard is condition-based
(leg 89, defect 4) and hardcodes no grid size: `if not np.any(mask & (Ksq > 0.0))`. Leg 129
leaves that line **byte-identical** (sha256[:16] `9a43d4c736c18aa0`), and neither `main` nor the
parked branch hardcodes any numeric floor (`V3`). The floor moves because the *mask* moved; the
guard fires on its own pre-existing criterion, written 40 legs earlier. The only choice point is
**upstream of `n=3`**: whether to make the cut strict at all.

**And admitting `n=3` anyway, under strict, is not an option either** (`V8`): a random `n=3`
vorticity of non-constant amplitude **1.069308980730229** comes back from the strict mask with
non-constant amplitude **exactly 0.0, bitwise**. That is leg 89's defect 4 verbatim — the
configuration that "used to report the most reassuring guard numbers in the battery precisely
because nothing happened".

**Scope of the whole question, as a fact rather than a lucky measurement** (`V2`, `V6`): the two
readings differ **iff `3 | n`** (333 of 1000 grids; every differing grid divisible by 3, no
agreeing grid divisible by 3), and `2^m mod 3 ∈ {1, 2}` for all `m = 0..64`, never 0 — because
`2 ≡ -1 (mod 3)`. No power-of-two grid can ever sit at the marginal case. Of the ten grid sizes
this repository's plan and bans name, **three are divisible by 3: 96, 201, 801**.

**The (a) answer remains conditional on adopting strict**, which no shipped module does. What
this leg establishes is that the conditional's *consequent* is forced: given strict, `n=3` is
excluded with no admissible alternative.

## 3. (b) THE INCONSISTENCY DOES NOT ARISE, FOR TWO INDEPENDENT MEASURED REASONS

### 3.1 Three of the five named sites are not masks

Every `n/3` site in `solver/*.py` on `main`, classified by its **consuming line** (each line
located verbatim on `main` as a whole code line, so leg 129's docstrings quoting the old cut in
backticks cannot false-match — that bug bit this runner once and the fix is `has_code_line`):

| site | consuming line | kind |
|---|---|---|
| `spectral_utils.py:36` | `return wavenumbers(n) <= n / 3.0` | **DEALIAS MASK** |
| `boussinesq.py:153` | `return (np.abs(KX) <= cut) & (np.abs(KY) <= cut)` | **DEALIAS MASK** |
| `boussinesq.py:419` | `tail_mask = (KX*KX + KY*KY) > (0.75 * k_cut) ** 2` | diagnostic band |
| `fractional_boussinesq.py:259` | `band_mask = (self.kmag > 0.9*k_cut) & (self.kmag <= k_cut)` | diagnostic band |
| `fractional_gclm.py:265` | `keep = self.k <= k_cut` | diagnostic band |

**2 masks, 3 diagnostic reporting bands.** A diagnostic band gates no grid — it defines the
support of a reported tail-energy ratio. It cannot be "internally inconsistent with a mask on the
same admissibility question" because **it does not answer that question at all**. The framing's
"four other shipped loose masks" is a miscount: three of the four are not masks.

### 3.2 The fourth is a real mask, and leg 129 strictifies it in the same diff

`solver/spectral_utils.py:36` **is** a real mask, and `git diff main...leg/129-sur-v1` replaces
its cut with `(n - 1) // 3` alongside `boussinesq.py:153`. **2 of 2 dealias masks strictified;
0 of 3 diagnostic bands touched.** Files whose cut changes: `solver/boussinesq.py` **and**
`solver/spectral_utils.py`. The premise "boussinesq.py alone" is **false**.

### 3.3 The one real mask/diagnostic mismatch, and its magnitude

There *is* a genuine mismatch, but of a different kind and of measured size zero. At `3 | n` the
repaired mask retains `|k| <= (n-1)//3` while the diagnostic band still keeps `|k| <= n/3.0` —
**exactly one extra wavenumber**, at `n = 96, 99, 201`; **zero** extra at `n = 32, 64, 128, 256`.
The consequence is nil: the statistic is `band.max() / pk.max()`, and the extra mode carries
identically zero power after masking, so a max cannot move. **Largest tail-ratio difference
across all seven tested grids: 0.000e+00** (e.g. `n=96`: 7.423609e-01 either way). The module
authors already anticipated this — `fractional_gclm.py:255` says in its own comment that the
band's top is zeroed "because the 2/3 dealiasing rule has already zeroed that band".

So: the (b) answer is **no**, and *not* for the reason the gate's no-branch anticipated ("their
own `n` never goes below 4"). It is for a structural reason: the sites are not four peer masks.

## 4. THE NEW FACT THE CORRECTION DOES SURFACE — IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION

The corrected framing expected, if anything, that leg 129's repair was **too narrow** in a way
that would create inconsistency. The measurement says the reach is **wider than escalation #4
states**, and that is the finding worth escalating.

**Four modules consume the two repaired masks. Escalation #4 names one.**

| consumer | call | named in escalation #4 |
|---|---|---|
| `solver/boussinesq.py:348` | `mask = dealias_mask2d(n)` | yes |
| `solver/gclm.py:178` | `mask = dealias_mask(n)` | **no** |
| `solver/fractional_gclm.py:195` | `self.mask = dealias_mask(self.n)` | **no** |
| `solver/fractional_boussinesq.py:218` | `self.mask = dealias_mask2d(self.n)` | **no** |

**And leg 129 adds a hard refusal that `main` does not have.** The repaired
`spectral_utils.dealias_mask` raises `ValueError` when `(n-1)//3 < 1`, i.e. at **`n = 1, 2, 3`**.
On `main` it raises at none of them: it returns a mask retaining **1 non-mean mode at `n=3`** and
0 at `n = 1, 2`.

**`solver/gclm.py` has no grid guard of its own** — no hardcoded floor, and not
`solve_boussinesq`'s mean-mode guard. It reaches `dealias_mask` unguarded at line 178. Measured
live on `main`: **`solve_gclm(n=3)` runs today**; after the repair it would raise. That is a
behaviour change in a module leg 129 never edits and escalation #4 never mentions, reached
through the shared 1D mask.

This does **not** make leg 129's repair wrong — the `n=3` gCLM grid is aliased for exactly the
reason §1 measures at 31.5 decades. It makes escalation #4's **stated scope** incomplete: the
ruling the user is being asked for is not "one Boussinesq floor and one banked verdict", it is
"a refusal at `n <= 3` across four solver modules, one of which has no guard of its own".
