## The four silent-corruption sites, with magnitudes

### SITE 1 — `newton_gamma2`'s free unknown `a` cannot reach the module's own Y_0/Z_2 (**severe**)

`newton_gamma2`'s docstring is explicit that it solves "with the advection `a` as the free
unknown", and it returns that `a`. But `y0_measure` and `z2_quadratic_constant` **have no `a`
parameter** — they re-evaluate `residual` and `jacobian_Omega` at the *constructor's* `dp.a`,
and `z2` uses `abs(dp.a)` in `‖B‖`. There is no way to express the correct call, and nothing in
the returned value records which `a` was used.

At leg 125's own M7 setup (`DissipativeProfile(a=0.39)`, `a0=0.386`, `nu=0.3`):

| n | solved `a` | drift | defect at solved `a` | defect at `dp.a` | inflation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 201 | 0.36235319 | −2.765e−02 | 4.716e−14 | 5.395e−02 | **1.144e+12×** |
| 401 | 0.38208549 | −7.914e−03 | 5.934e−14 | 1.432e−02 | **2.413e+11×** |

The reported `Y_0` is 4.27e−01 (n=201) / 4.78e−01 (n=401) — a norm of **the wrong operator's
defect**, for a solve whose true defect is ~5e−14. No error, no warning.

Across the a\*-bracketing sweep (G7, 21 cases) the inflation runs from 1.0 to **5.10e+12×**.

### SITE 2 — the zero profile echoes `c_l0` back at `residual_rms` exactly 0.0 (**severe**)

`R(0; c_l, c_omega, nu) == 0` identically for **every** `c_l`, and with `scale_gauge=None` the
dilation-gauge target is read off the initial guess (`target = float(g @ Om)`), which is 0 for
a zero profile — so the gauge row is satisfied too. Newton breaks on **iteration 1** and never
touches `c_l`.

| `c_l0` in | `c_l` out | `residual_rms` | iterations | Δ reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1/3 (leg 125's own) | 1/3, unchanged | **0.0** | 1 | **−0.33333333333333337** |
| 0.30 | 0.30, unchanged | **0.0** | 1 | −0.4 |
| 7.5 | 7.5, unchanged | **0.0** | 1 | 14.0 |

The honest solve on the same grid gives `c_l = 0.33334150`, Δ = −0.33331700, at
`residual_rms` 7.52e−16. So at leg 125's own starting value the module hands back **Chen's
exact headline Δ = −1/3, from a profile that is identically zero**, with a residual that is not
merely small but *exactly zero* — better than the genuine solve's. **No health indicator the
module exposes can distinguish this from convergence.** This contradicts the module header's
own sentence, "Δ is measured here, not assumed … free to land anywhere".

### SITE 3 — `z2_quadratic_constant` silently substitutes the ℓ¹ norm (**moderate**)

`ordr = np.inf if norm == "sup" else 1`. Any unrecognised string becomes `ord=1`. Its sibling
`y0_measure` **raises `ValueError` on exactly the same input**, and leg 125's own
`test_y0_measure_refuses_an_unknown_norm` gates that — there is no sibling test for `z2`. The
returned dict has **no `norm` key**, so the substitution is unrecoverable downstream.

6/6 unrecognised strings tested (`"frobenius"`, `"Sup"`, `"inf"`, `"linf"`, `""`, `"2"`)
returned a dict **bit-identical to the ℓ¹ result**. Inflation of `Z_2` over the `sup` value,
**growing with n**:

| n | `Z_2` (sup) | `Z_2` (silent fallback) | inflation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 201 | 2.585320e+09 | 2.971116e+09 | 1.1492× |
| 401 | 2.361007e+11 | 3.593837e+11 | 1.5222× |
| 801 | 2.234069e+13 | 4.523746e+13 | **2.0249×** |

### SITE 4 — degenerate dilation-gauge parameters are accepted (**moderate**)

`newton`'s docstring says `scale_gauge` "kills the second [symmetry]" and that "`c_l` is then a
genuine OUTPUT". Measured at n=401 against the honest solve (`c_l` = 0.33334150,
Δ = −0.333317, `residual_rms` 7.52e−16):

| `scale_gauge` | `c_l` out | Δ reported | abs err | rel err | `residual_rms` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 (exactly degenerate) | −0.17241991 | −1.34484 | 1.012 | 304% | 2.752e+00 |
| 1e−14 | 0.61549820 | 0.230996 | 0.564 | 169% | 1.220e+01 |
| −g (sign-flipped) | −0.52695019 | −2.05390 | 1.721 | 516% | 8.536e+01 |
| 1e6 (out of scale) | −710.7936 | −1422.59 | **1422** | **426697%** | 1.021e−08 |
| NaN / Inf | — | — | — | — | **raise `LinAlgError`** ✓ |

4/6 accepted with a wrong Δ; 2/6 correctly rejected. `newton` **returns no convergence flag** —
only `residual_rms` — so a caller reading `c_l` (the documented output) without separately
policing `residual_rms` against a ~1e−16 floor gets a wrong Δ with no signal. The 1e6 row is
the sharpest: it returns `residual_rms` **1.02e−08**, small enough to read as convergence, with
Δ wrong by a factor of 4267.
