## 1. What "independent" has to mean here

Leg 248 wrote the patch and its own "independent" reachability re-run **in the same commit**
(`49eb889f`). The post-repair discipline (the 228/231–234 family) exists precisely to produce
numbers nobody who wrote the patch produced. Four things this leg does that leg 248's artifact
structurally cannot:

1. **The verdict is recomputed from first principles, not read.** For all 41 cases the correct
   verdict is derived here from the returned `Omega`/`c` alone — my own `relres`, my own
   `gauge_defect` from the formula in the module's docstring — and compared against what the
   module *reported*. A bug in how the module computes-and-returns `gauge_defect` (as opposed
   to a bug in the gate that consumes it) is invisible to a check that reads the field.
2. **The calibration pair is re-derived from `solver/profile_newton.py` directly**, not read
   from leg 202's or leg 248's JSON.
3. **Gate clause (c) is new relative to leg 248's own gate**, which had only clauses (a) and
   (b). Leg 248 never claimed a dependent sweep by that name.
4. **Every control here is one that can come out differently** (lesson 90): each is paired with
   a planted case that makes the control fire the other way, and the JSON banks both arms.
