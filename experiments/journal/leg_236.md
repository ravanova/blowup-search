## D1 — the provenance map (35 banked numbers, AST-checked)

Every banked v11 number classified by what produced it, and the classification **checked
against the anchor script's own AST** rather than asserted from reading. The AST agrees with
the hand map (`ast_check_agrees_with_map: true`), and the **only direct call site of
`continuation` in the entire v11 anchor script is line 78, inside `v2_sweep`**.

| class | count | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `continuation` | 8 | value came out of `profile_newton.continuation` |
| `gated_by_continuation` | 3 | value is a **cold** solve, but *inclusion* is decided by continuation's `relres` |
| `cold_solve` | 18 | `TwoScaleNewton.solve(om0=None)`; never touches `continuation` |
| `literal` | 5 | hardcoded constant |
| `prose` | 1 | carries no number |

**This corrects leg 202's grading.** Leg 202 named
`a_max_machine`/`GA_boundary`/`grid_converged_a_max` together as the exposed trio. Only the
**first** of the three touches `continuation`:

- `v3_boundary.GA_boundary` is a **hardcoded literal** (`anchor:112`, `[0.5, 0.55]`) — it is
  not computed by the script at all, so no solver change can move it. Leg 202's grading was
  wrong about this one, and it cannot be repaired by leg 226 either.
- `v4_grids.grid_converged_a_max` is **cold-solve only**.

The subtle class is `gated_by_continuation`, which nobody had named: `v5_budget`'s weighted
defects are **cold-solve values** (`anchor:81-82`), but *which of them enter the min/max* is
decided by **continuation's** `relres < 1e-8`. The number is continuation-**gated** without
being continuation-**valued**. That distinction is what makes the third exclusion axis below
necessary rather than decorative.

## D2 — faithful capture (the honesty precondition)

`continuation` discards `Omega`, so leg 202's decay-class classifier cannot be applied to its
rows from outside. A recording subclass retains it, changing no arithmetic. The module's real
`continuation` was then called **unmodified** over v11's own a-grid at v11's own `n = 801`
(21 solve calls, 5 of them cold retries, 383 s), and its selection logic replayed.

**`replay_is_faithful: true`, 0 field mismatches over 17 rows × 6 fields.** So the profile
attached to each row below is the profile that row's numbers actually came from — not a
re-solve that might have landed elsewhere. Had this failed the leg would have claimed nothing.

### A correction to this leg's own mid-flight reading

An earlier in-flight note on this leg recorded that *both* `a = 0.45` **and** `a = 0.35` were
SILENT_DEGRADED. **That is wrong under the frozen classifier and is corrected here.**
`a = 0.35` does hit the 40-iteration cap, but its `relres = 6.3012e-12` is **below** leg 202's
`1e-10` SILENT_DEGRADED threshold, so the classifier — frozen verbatim in the novelty pass
before any number was read, and *not* retuned here — calls it **CLEAN**.

This matters, because it means **leg 247's fallback figure survives**: its "over the 10 rows
that do hold, the worst-case margin is 10.656× at `a = 0.35`" rests on a row leg 202's
taxonomy **accepts**. The concern that 10.656× was itself built on a rejected row resolves
**negative**. Reported as such rather than quietly dropped.

## D4/D5 — three exclusion policies, because the gate has two non-equivalent readings

`v5_budget`'s defects are cold-solve values while inclusion is gated by continuation's
`relres`, so "exclude the false-convergence cases" is genuinely ambiguous. Reporting one
policy would let the answer be manufactured. All three:

| policy | rows kept | `a_max_machine` | `newton_weighted_defect_max` | `margin` (v11, min-based) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none excluded (reproduction) | 17 | 1.0 | 1.5196780e-02 | 2.5219397e+09 |
| exclude SILENT_WRONG | 13 | **0.55** | 1.5196780e-02 | 2.5219397e+09 |
| exclude SILENT_WRONG + SILENT_DEGRADED | 11 | **0.55** | **2.3741350e-05** | 2.5219397e+09 |
| exclude bad **cold** profile (the on-point axis for `v5_budget`) | 11 | **0.55** | **2.3741350e-05** | 2.5219397e+09 |

Excluded `a`: strict `{0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0}`; wide and cold-axis both
`{0.45, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0}`. **The two independent axes agree** — the wide and
cold-profile policies exclude the identical set and produce identical numbers, which is the
strongest available evidence that the move is not an artefact of one reading.

### Move 1 — `a_max_machine`: 1.0 → 0.55

Robust across **all three** policies, and it is the larger finding. v11 banks "machine
precision reached all the way to `a = 1.0`". That claim is carried **entirely by the four
off-branch roots** — remove them and the last genuinely machine-precision `a` is **0.55**.

**The blast radius here is unusual and worth stating plainly: the corrected value agrees with
v11's own prose, and the banked scalar was the thing that disagreed.**
`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTED_V11.md:69` already concludes `a* ≈ 0.5–0.55`. So this move does not
overturn a written conclusion — it removes a banked scalar that silently contradicted the
document banking it. `a_max_machine` appears in the JSON and one stdout line
(`p2_route_d_v11_anchor.py:86,233`) and is quoted in `TECHNICAL_P2_PUB3_V1.md:134`'s consumer
table; no prose conclusion rests on `1.0`.

### Move 2 — `newton_weighted_defect_max`: 1.5196780e-02 → 2.3741350e-05 (640.1×)

The banked max is carried by a **single row, `a = 0.45`**, which is SILENT_DEGRADED *and*
passes v11's `relres < 1e-8` gate. It is the only row in the good set that the taxonomy
rejects, and it is exactly the row leg 247 escalated on.

**Consequence for leg 247 (not yet on `main`; reported, not acted on — 247 is not this leg's
territory).** Leg 247's repair makes the margin `Y0_max / max(weighted_defect)`. Recomputed
here at v11's own call sites:

| | margin | reading |
|---|---|---|
| no exclusion (reproduces leg 247) | **0.01612184** | budget **MISSED by 62.03×** at `a = 0.45` |
| exclude SILENT_DEGRADED (either axis) | **10.319548** | budget **HOLDS with 10.32× slack** |

Leg 247's own 62.02373957× reproduces to 0.016122 (leg 247: 0.016122858874141922) — agreement
to 5 significant figures. **The entire 62× budget violation rests on one row that leg 202's
frozen classifier rejects.** Whether that row should be excluded is a *repair* decision and
belongs to leg 226; this leg measures the magnitude and stops.

### What did NOT move, and why that is informative

`best_relres`, `orders_below_GA`, `newton_weighted_defect_min` and v11's own min-based
`margin` are **identical to all digits under all three policies**. The reason is specific and
checkable: the minimising row is `a = 0.10`, which is CLEAN (5 iterations, `relres` 1.0e-14,
far-field inflation 2.7e-10). **v11's min-selection accidentally insulated its own headline
margin from the defect** — the favourable selection leg 247 corrected is the same selection
that made this number robust. Both statements are true at once and neither excuses the other.

## D6 — the non-vacuity control (LESSON 90)

A clean result is worthless if the instrument cannot fire. Same classifier, same
`continuation` code path, at leg 202's own grid `n = 201`, over an `a`-ladder reaching leg
202's range:

**Fires: 6 CLEAN, 1 SILENT_DEGRADED, 4 SILENT_WRONG of 11.** First SILENT_WRONG departure at
`a = 0.60`; **worst far-field inflation 1.39e+06** at `a = 1.35` (at `relres = 6.0e-16` — a
machine-zero residual on a profile six orders outside the anchor's far field).

So the instrument fires hard on this code path, and the n = 801 result is a statement about
v11's operating point, not about a classifier that never fires. Note the control is *not* a
weaker version of the main run: its worst inflation (1.4e+06) exceeds the main run's (2.4e+03)
by three orders, i.e. the pathology **worsens** off v11's grid.

## Realization (LESSON 91) — the answer holds here and nowhere else

`GCLMResidual` sinh-graded grid at `rho_max = 8.0`; the two-gauge least-squares Gauss-Newton of
`solver/profile_newton.py` at its defaults `tol = 1e-13`, `max_iter = 40`, `damping = True`;
plain float64 on scipy-openblas **with BLAS threads pinned to 1**; v11's own `n = 801` and
v11's own a-grid; off-branch detection by **leg 202's classifier, verbatim, thresholds
unchanged**. This is not a proof that the banked numbers are wrong — it is a measurement that
they are **not invariant** to a defensible exclusion of rows the repository's own audit
classifies as false convergence.
