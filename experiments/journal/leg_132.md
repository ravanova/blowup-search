# Leg 132 — Route-OLB: the op_lower repair, re-run by an independent party

**Gate: YES on both clauses.** `solver/op_lower.py` returns a true lower bound on all 209 of
leg 101's gate-deciding adversarial cases in a re-run built from scratch, at a max
`L/N_true` of **0.999999999998994804**, and every validated magnitude reproduces — the seven
banked v10 bracket rows move **down only**, by at most **1.648e-12** relative measured against a
git-reconstructed pre-repair module, with all seven `argmax` unchanged and the 307.878-decade
headroom **bit-identical** between the two modules at J = 200/300/400.

`solver/op_lower.py` was read-only throughout, as the leg's territory required under **both**
branches.

---

## 1. Why an independent re-run was not redundant

The repair (`147e8c2`) rests on two magnitudes, and as banked, both were self-reported:

- **"0 violations of 209"** was measured by `experiments/p2_route_ola_v1_adversarial.py` — the
  runner written by the leg that found the bug, re-run by the branch that fixed it, grading
  against a reference that lives *in the same file*. A defect shared between the reference and
  the fix is structurally invisible to it.
- **"the banked rows moved down by 1.648e-12"** was measured by
  `experiments/p2_route_ola_v1_bench_check.py` against `PRE` — a dict of **literals the repair
  itself wrote** (`:49–56`), because "the pre-repair module no longer exists on this branch".

Neither is a criticism of the repair; both are the ordinary consequence of a bench branch
grading its own work. This leg re-derives all three instruments — zoo, reference, grader — and
adds a fourth thing nobody had: **the pre-repair module itself**, recovered from git
(`ef0ea2e:solver/op_lower.py`, blob `5021ff3608dd`) and imported alongside the current one, so
the A/B is a measurement in one process on one matrix rather than a comparison against text.

### 2.3 The positive control, and why it is the load-bearing line

Lesson 90: a control that cannot come out differently is not a control. The same grader over the
pre-repair module reports **47 violations under leg 101's rule — 46 overflow + 1 finite — and 50
under this leg's stricter 0-ULP rule**, the extra 3 being *exactly* the cases leg 101 recorded as
`sound_within_one_ulp`. The decomposition reconciles to the case, in both directions, which is a
much stronger statement than the totals agreeing.

If both modules had scored 0, the correct reading would have been "the grader is broken", not
"the repair is confirmed". They did not.

### 2.4 The reference, derived twice

Leg 101's exact reference is the closed form `N = max_{i,j} t_i|A_ij|/u_j`. This leg instead
**enumerates the extreme points** `±e_j/u_j` of the weighted-`l1` ball, forms the image and takes
an exact weighted sup — same theorem, different derivation, and it validates the closed form
instead of inheriting it. Over the **208** cases carrying an exact reference the two routes
**disagree on 0**, in exact rational arithmetic. That is a quiet result and it is the precondition
for reading §2.2 at all, which is why it is checked first.

## 3. The finding this leg adds: clause (b) has a noise floor, and nobody had measured it

The reconstructed pre-repair module does **not** reproduce the banked literals bitwise — **0 of 7**
on `lower`, **0 of 7** on `sign_patterns`. It lands within **2.2588e-14** relative of them, and the
deviation is **two-sided** (+1.656e-16, −3.387e-15, +6.661e-15, +2.259e-14, +3.735e-16,
−2.776e-15, −1.623e-15), which is the signature of `np.linalg.inv`'s last-bit reproducibility
across BLAS builds and not of anything in `solver/op_lower.py`.

That number bounds what clause (b) can ever prove. `p2_route_ola_v1_bench_check.py` grades a
downward move against literals without measuring the floor beneath them. Here:

> repair's own deflation **1.648e-12** ÷ environment floor **2.259e-14** = **72.9×**

**The conclusion survives, and the ratio is the honest magnitude, not the raw move.** 72.9× is
comfortable but it is not infinite, and it is *not* what a reader would infer from "moved by
1.6e-12 against a 1e-9 budget" (which reads as 600× of margin). The gap between those two
readings is the whole point of measuring the pre-repair module instead of quoting it. Gate 10 of
`test_op_lower_postrepair.py` pins the floor so a future environment change that ate the margin
could not pass silently.

## 4. The two instruments this leg had to repair mid-run, both recorded

Leg 101 recorded two of its own; this one had two, in the same spirit — a pass that only reports
its hits is not a pass.

**(a) The independent `l2→l2` reference excluded 16 cases that belong in the battery.** Taking
`sigma_max` from `eigvalsh(MᵀM)` instead of `svd(M)` was chosen for LAPACK-path independence —
but the **Gram matrix squares `M`**, so it dies a full factor of two in exponent sooner. On this
zoo `M` reaches ~1e300 and `MᵀM` overflows to `inf` on **7 operators at the `decay_huge_entry`
weight** where leg 101's `svd` is perfectly happy. First run: **193 gate-deciding, not 209**, and
the positive control reported **37 violations instead of 47** because 13 of the missing cases
were pre-repair overflow violations. **The count mismatch caught it, which is exactly why the
count was made a gate magnitude before the run.** One more exact power-of-two rescale of `M`
itself, exponent carried in the return, and the battery lands on 408/209/199 and the control on
47/50. This is a defect of *this leg's* reference, not of the module under test, and it is the
same shape of error as leg 101's own: an instrument that fails in the regime it was built to
probe.

**(b) The first grader was stricter than this leg's own pre-committed rule.** Clause (b)'s
implementation initially required `0` adversarial headlines to have risen against the pre-repair
module, and 15 had, so it reported NO. But §3 of the novelty pass enumerates **six** validated
magnitudes and §2.3 states *in advance* that adversarial-case headlines are not among them.
Grading on it would have been deciding the gate on a rule invented after the data. Corrected to
the pre-committed rule; the 15 are reported as a magnitude instead (§5).

## 5. The 15 headlines that ROSE, and why that is admissible

DIRECTION.md's asymmetry: *"a deflation that moves the bound down is admissible, one that moves
any validated magnitude is not."* **15 of 408** adversarial headlines are *larger* post-repair
than pre-repair. Every one is still **under the true norm** (`ratio ≤ 1`, checked case by case).

They are the repair **recovering** bounds the pre-repair module lost:

- `huge_1e300` / `tiny_all` / `l2→l2`: **0.0 → 1.0545e+301** against a true norm of 1.3294e+301;
- `huge_1e300` / `tiny_all` / `l1→linf`: **0.0 → 2.7561e+300** against 3.2514e+300;
- four `denormal_1e-320` rows where the power-of-two rescale buys back mantissa bits, moving the
  value *closer to* but still below the exact norm (e.g. 5.524084e-318 → 5.524143e-318 against
  5.524148e-318).

This is not sharpening, which would be the banned act: no candidate direction, shape family,
ascent length, step law or operator changed — the repair's `ascend` docstring records that the
step law was deliberately left alone for precisely this reason. Only the *evaluation* changed,
and on these cases the evaluation previously produced nothing at all. It is also the reason
`sound, nonzero` runs 118 → 161 while `sound_but_zero` runs 41 → 48: the module ends up **more**
informative than before, not less, which is not the direction a soundness repair is usually
expected to move.

## 6. Scope, and what this leg does NOT claim

No mathematical novelty. Nothing here is discovered about `‖A‖`, about Route-D's bracket, or
about blow-up, and **no link of the L1→L4 chain moved.** The result is a software fact about one
module plus one previously unmeasured number (§3). `capabilities.py`'s clause for
`solver/op_lower.py` — "brackets the dense operator norm from below wherever both are computable"
— which leg 101 measured **false** pre-repair, is now independently measured **true** on all 209
cases; correcting or confirming its wording remains the orchestrator's call, and this leg read it
only.
