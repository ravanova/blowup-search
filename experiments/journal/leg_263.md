## Clause (b) — the re-scope, and what over-read what

**Leg 111 read a measurement made on ONE trial space as a statement about a REALIZATION.** The
weight exponent `γ` and the trial space are two halves of the same degree of freedom: the trial
space's vanishing order `p` sets the membership threshold `γ < 2p+1`, while damping at the
origin needs `γ > 3`. Leg 111 swept `γ` over seven weights, held the trial space fixed at
`span{sin kθ}` (`p = 1`), found the two thresholds coincide, and banked *"a third dead
realization."* At `p = 1` they do coincide. At `p ≥ 2` they do not.

| | leg 111, `p = 1` | leg 141, `p = 2` | leg 178, `p = 3` (`T2_egm`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| admissible window `(3, 2p+1)` | `(3, 3)` — **width 0.0** | `(3, 5)` — width **2.0** | `(3, 7)` — width **4.0** |
| membership `‖·‖²` ratio / refinement at `γ = 4` | **`1.677722e+07`** | `1.0000000000000002` | **`1.000000`** |
| exponent margin at `γ = 4` | **`−1.0`** | — | **`+3.0`** |
| largest admissible gap | **`−0.4999241`** | — | **`+0.499999667`** |

Leg 141 reproduced leg 111's `p = 1` column at **`abs_diff 0.0`** — same quadrature, same code,
only the trial space moved. Leg 178's grid stability at `p = 3`: relative change over the last
two refinements **`5.890e−07`** and **`7.409e−08`**. Three constants agree to **`0.000e+00`**
(leg 111's `D_φ(0) = (3−γ)/2` at `γ = 4` = `−0.500000`; EGM's `−(1/2 − C|a|)` at `a = 0` =
`−0.500000`; Xu's modulated gap = `0.500000`). And EGM's published weight **is** leg 111's own
family `B` at `γ = 4` — `φ^E/φ^B4` constant **`32`**, relative spread `1.998e-15` — the single
member the seven-weight ladder excluded.

**Why this is the fourth over-read closure and not a repeat of the first.** Leg 165 reached the
same conclusion from **banked data and the literature**; leg 178 reached it **by measurement**,
on a construction named before any number existed, and closed the exact gap leg 165 left open
in its own words (*"the coercivity half at `p = 2` has been recomputed by nobody"*). Two
independent instruments, same answer.
