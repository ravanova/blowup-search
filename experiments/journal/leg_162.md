## 2. THE STRUCTURAL FINDING: THE CORNER IS TWO CORNERS, AND THEY CLASSIFY OPPOSITELY

"Global Chebyshev basis on the support interval" is not one object. `L` is one operator, but
`Z_1 = ‖I − AL‖` is a norm on a **chosen pair of coordinates**, and the compact-support
problem admits two pairings that both close under `L`.

| | domain → codomain | unbounded part | `certificate_shapes.py` label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** — this repo's own ansatz | `(1−v²)T_n → T_m` | bidiagonal, **exactly zero diagonal**, off-diagonal `~n/2` | `SHIFT` |
| **B** — Olver–Townsend airfoil pairing ([arXiv:1507.00596](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00596)) | `√(1−v²)U_{n−1} → T_m/√(1−v²)` | **exact `diag(−n)`**, zero off-diagonal | `MULTIPLIER` |

Realization A's `n/2` is not merely *like* the compactified whole-line tail block
(`spectral_certificate.tail_block`, entries `1−k/2` and `k/2`) — it is the **same shape and
the same `n/2`**, measured by the same classifier.

**Why B is different, mechanistically.** The first integral (FI) is what makes the leading
coefficient constant: on the whole line the unbounded part is the *degenerate transport*
`(c_l X + aU)∂_X`, and that degeneracy is why `solver/finite_support.py`'s direct build never
converged. (FI) integrates the transport away, leaving a plain `c·d/dv`; the airfoil pairing
then diagonalises it. **And (FI) is available only because the profile has compact support.**

## 3. WHY THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT LEG 58's THEOREM

Proposition NG says `Z_1 ≥ 1` for **every** bounded `A` with `A21 = 0`. Every sub-1 number
below has `A21 = 0`. There is no contradiction, and `spectral_certificate.nogo_hypotheses`
is the file that says why: the proposition has three hypotheses, and **(H2) is "the tail
block has a kernel in `ℓ¹_w`"** — the proof sets `x = (0; h)` with `T h = 0`.

Realization B's tail is `diag(−n)`, which **has no kernel at any `n ≥ 1`**. (H2) fails
outright — the same way it fails for `μ > 0` in leg 58's own dissipative control. **The
theorem is untouched; it simply does not reach this realization.** That is exactly the sense
in which the corner is new.

## 6. THE ENUMERATION GAP, NAMED EXACTLY

axis with `BX2_n_uncovered = 0`. **A global Chebyshev basis on a compact support interval is

different labels from two different pairings.
