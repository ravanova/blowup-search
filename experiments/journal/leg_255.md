## The screens, and why (i) and (iv) had to be independent

The dispatch built the caution into the gate, and the table makes it visible rather than
argued. **Viscous Burgers is Breden–Chu's own home ground** — their worked example, l.1669 of
the md5-pinned extraction: *"We focus on a generalised viscous Burgers equation on ℝ₊ with
Neumann boundary condition"*, `∂_t v + v²∂_x v = ∂_xx v`. It is parabolic, it is **certified**,
it has a self-similar profile — and it **does not blow up**. In the table it PASSES screen (iv),
PASSES screen (ii), and is killed by screens (i) and (iii). `assert_probe_is_live()` fails the
run if that row ever stops looking like that.

**Screen (iv) is quoted, not paraphrased.** It is Remark 40 of `arXiv:2404.04054` (line 1687,
extraction md5 `ff7a34b776bfe5edf97397e5eabdbb7a`, located by leg 245):

> We deal with a one-dimensional example here for simplicity, but terms like **(u·∇)u** could in
> principle also be handled **in dimension d ∈ {2,3}**, as u ∈ H²(μ) is then still enough to
> guarantee that (u·∇)u ∈ L²(μ) since u ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d).

**The reason is the whole clause**, and that is what makes the screen operational rather than a
vibe: the reach is exactly what `H²(μ) ↪ L^∞(ℝ^d)` buys — a **local polynomial** nonlinearity in
`u` and its first derivatives, over a Gaussian-weighted `H²(μ)`, with a Laplacian /
Ornstein–Uhlenbeck principal part on `ℝ^d`, `d ≤ 3`. Remark 40 does **not** name the Hilbert
transform, Biot–Savart, the **Leray projection**, Fourier restriction, averaging, `(-Δ)^α`,
sequence ladders, free boundaries, or quasilinear geometric principal parts — so the census
records those as **outside the stated reach** rather than admitting them silently.

**The sub-flag that is not a screen but is the point.** Leg 240's own words for Phase 1's goal:
move the viscous term from DOMINATED to ENCLOSED. Screen (ii) as dispatched asks only for a
self-similar/DSS profile — and several candidates have one whose *profile equation carries no
dissipation at all* (BCG/CGSS/MRRS inherit an inviscid Euler profile; fractal Burgers approaches
the inviscid Burgers shock). Enclosing those re-occupies leg 174's **Grade-B** cell. So
`profile_carries_dissipation` is carried **per row** and reported alongside, and the gate is
still answered on the four dispatched screens. **The screen is not silently redefined.**

## The survivor, named precisely, with its screen (i) and (iv) locators

**`KS3D-NONEXPLICIT` — the parabolic–elliptic Keller–Segel system in d = 3, at its NON-explicit
self-similar blow-up profiles.**

* **Screen (i) locator.** Finite-time blow-up is proved. Glogić–Schörkhuber `arXiv:2209.11206`
  prove nonlinear **radial** stability of an explicit self-similar blow-up solution in d = 3,
  resolving a conjecture more than two decades old; `arXiv:2501.07073` extends to the nonradial
  setting. The dissipation is the plain Laplacian and it is **present in the blowing-up
  equation** — this is not a model whose blow-up survives *despite* a dissipation that scaling
  already defeated.
* **Screen (ii), with the sub-flag.** `arXiv:2503.02263` constructs **countably many** self-similar
  profiles in d = 3…9 by matched asymptotics + Banach fixed point; Collot–Zhang `arXiv:2406.11358`
  give their finite-Lipschitz-codimension stability, codimension = number of unstable eigenmodes.
  The blow-up is **Type I**: the diffusion is scale-invariant under the blow-up scaling, so the
  profile equation carries `Δ` at the *same order* as the drift. That is structurally the
  **DF-CGL position, not the BCG one** — **ENCLOSED**, not DOMINATED. This is exactly the
  property leg 240 said Phase 1 exists to obtain.
* **Screen (iii).** No interval-arithmetic enclosure found, in either half of the screen. The
  nearest apparatus anywhere on this candidate — and it is recorded rather than rounded to zero,
  per leg 246's discipline — is that the **nonradial** stability work uses computer assistance to
  **evaluate two integrals**, not as a validated enclosure of the profile. The profiles of
  `arXiv:2503.02263` are built **analytically**, explicitly not by computer assistance.
* **Screen (iv) locator.** Remark 40, line 1687. Written as the system
  `u_t = Δu − ∇·(u∇c)`, `0 = Δc + u`, the nonlinearity is
  `∇·(u∇c) = ∇u·∇c − u²` — **local, quadratic in `u` and first derivatives**, at **d = 3**,
  inside Remark 40's own `d ∈ {2,3}`, and covered by precisely the `H²(μ) ↪ L^∞` argument
  Remark 40 gives its reason in terms of. Radially it reduces further to a 1D ODE in the mass
  variable. **Recorded honestly as the one extension involved:** the second equation is
  *elliptic*, where Breden–Chu wrote a parabolic profile problem — an extension of the setting,
  but **not** the introduction of a nonlocal term, which is what the reach rule actually tests.

**`KS3D-EXPLICIT` survives the same four screens and is banked as a survivor — and it is a
vacuous target.** Its profile is **closed form**. There is nothing for an interval enclosure to
do. It is in the table because leaving it out would have made the survivor count look cleaner
than the evidence is.

## The part that matters more than the YES

**Every survivor is non-fluid.** Certifying `KS3D-NONEXPLICIT` would put a **third** occupant
into leg 174's `(fluid = False, grade = A)` cell — the cell DF-CGL occupies and, newly located
by this leg, Biernat–Donninger occupies **eight years earlier**. **Leg 174's empty
`(fluid = True, grade = A)` cell would stay empty.** Phase 1's goal in leg 240's words is the
viscous term ENCLOSED *for a fluid self-similar object*, and this census does not deliver a
fluid one.

**The one fluid-adjacent candidate that clears (i), (ii) and (iii) is killed by (iv) alone.**
`KS-NS-LI-ZHOU`, `arXiv:2404.17228` (Li & Zhou, *Comm. Math. Phys.* 2025,
DOI `10.1007/s00220-025-05371-w`): 3D **Keller–Segel–Navier–Stokes with buoyancy**, abstract
verbatim —

> *"…we use a quantitative method to directly construct a smooth finite-time blowup solution for
> the Keller–Segel–Navier–Stokes system with buoyancy in 3D. The heart of the proof is to
> establish the non-radial finite-codimensional stability of an **explicit self-similar blowup
> solution to 3D Keller–Segel equation** …"*

— a genuine viscous fluid system, a proved finite-time singularity from smooth data with
non-negative density and finite mass, and no certification apparatus in the fetched abstract.
**It dies on the Leray-projection clause.** Remark 40 names `(u·∇)u` and gives its reason; it
says nothing about the **pressure**, and boundedness of the Leray projector on the Gaussian-
weighted `L²(μ)` does **not** follow from the `H²(μ) ↪ L^∞` embedding Remark 40 argues from.
**This is the single most consequential judgement in the table, and it is a strict reading made
deliberately.** A leg that wanted this row to survive could get it by *arguing the Leray
projector into the reach*, which is a real mathematical question and is **not** something a
census may decide. It is written into the JSON as exactly that: the highest-value question leg
257 could scope.

A second honest caveat on the same row: the self-similar object in Li–Zhou is the **Keller–Segel
profile**; the Navier–Stokes velocity is carried along, not the blowing-up self-similar object.
Even with the Leray clause relaxed, the thing enclosed would be a chemotaxis profile inside a
fluid system, not a fluid profile.

## Why the verdict is computed and not asserted (lesson 90)

`screen_verdicts()` derives each row's four verdicts from its **evidence fields**; `classify()`
derives the gate from the resulting table. `self_test()` perturbs the evidence and reaches **all
four** outcome codes, **including both gate branches**:

* certify the two Keller–Segel rows → `NO_MODEL_PASSES_ALL_FOUR`, gate **NO**;
* relax the Leray clause on the fluid row → `SURVIVOR_IS_FLUID_ADJACENT`, gate **YES** on a
  fluid model — i.e. **the answer this leg would most have liked is reachable on evidence and
  was not reached**;
* certify only the non-explicit profiles → `SURVIVOR_ONLY_VACUOUS_TARGET`;
* remove the blow-up proof from the survivor → screen (i) alone kills it.

`assert_probe_is_live()` fails the run outright if the table degenerates: if **no** row is
killed, if **every** row is killed, if any screen kills nothing, if any screen passes on
nothing, or if the viscous-Burgers caution row ever stops passing (iv) while failing (i).
