## X1 — the passage ledger: Xu never admits `ν ≤ 0`

Twelve passages, every place in the paper where the sign of the dissipation coefficient is
or could be fixed. **0 of 12 admit `ν ≤ 0`.** The three that decide it:

* **(6.1)** — `wt + a u wx = ux w − ν Λs w, Λ = (−∂xx )1/2 , ν > 0, s ∈ (0, 2].`
* **(A.1)** — `wt = w Hw − ν Λs w, Λ = (−∂xx )1/2 , ν > 0, s ∈ (0, 1),`
  the only two places in the paper a dissipative equation is written, both stipulating
  `ν > 0`; and the appendix restricts further to `s ∈ (0,1)`, strictly below the `s = 2`
  Laplacian leg 185 works at.
* **Appendix A, structural fact (ii)** — `−νe−γτ |k|s ≤ 0) and carries a coefficient that
  vanishes as τ → ∞`. The sign-definiteness of the dissipative form — which the whole
  persistence program of §8 rests on — is **derived from** `ν > 0`. Flip `ν` and it reverses.
  Xu does not consider that case.

And §8 (iv) closes the door explicitly: *"the supercritical regime s > s∗ (a), where the
dissipation is relevant, is outside the present framework."* Leg 185's negative-`ν` half
sits on exactly that side at `s = 2`.

## X3 — the mechanism is the same object, exactly

Xu's (6.2) defines `γ := 1 − s c_l`. `solver/dissipative_profile.py` (lines 35–44) defines
`Δ := 2 c_l/|c_ω| − 1`. Under Xu's own time normalization — his rescaling `w = (T−t)⁻¹W` **is**
`c_ω = −1` — these are the same quantity up to sign:

    Delta(c_l)  ==  - gamma_Xu(c_l, s = 2)

**Worst deviation over all 8 Table 1 rows: `0.0e+00`.** Not approximately — exactly, as an
algebraic identity. `γ` changes sign once on Xu's table, in the bracket `a ∈ [0.3, 0.4]`.

**Negative control (lesson 90), and it fires:** the identical code path against decoy maps
`γ|_{s=1}` and `γ|_{s=4}` misses by **1.000**. A check that cannot fail is not a check.

The consequence, stated as a reading and not as a reconciliation: **leg 185 imposed `Δ = 0`,
i.e. it sat exactly on Xu's marginal curve `γ = 0`, and then let `ν` float.** The `a` at
which the recovered `ν` changes sign is the `a` at which the *inviscid* branch meets that
same curve.

## X5 — honest limit: the two numbers are not resolved apart
