## What the negative control was, and that it could fail

For a literature leg the queries **are** the experiment, so the control has to be inside the
query set. Two were built in:

- **C1 is leg 174's own net, verbatim.** If the environment, the API, or my query hygiene had
  drifted, C1's counts would not have reproduced. They did, exactly: **0/12 grew, every delta
  0.** That is a live control that could have failed and didn't.
- **The mechanical screen is kept in the record.** Every hand adjudication appears as
  `overrides_mechanical`, so the four overrides are visible as overrides. The screen was not
  retuned to produce the answer.

The controls also caught a real defect: **6 of 35 queries came back HTTP 429/503/timeout on
the first pass.** Recording those as zeros would have produced a false null across the entire
C4 model-name axis, including one of the two channels that surfaced the hit. Fixed by
`--retry-unavailable` at 25 s spacing; final 35/35 OK.

## Decisions I made, and why

**1. I did not do the full-text read.** The dispatch assigns it to reserve leg 309 in terms
that leave no room. The temptation was real — a claimed 3D NS blow-up is the single most
consequential thing this project could encounter — and the discipline that says the finder is
not the judge is exactly the discipline that matters most when the stakes are highest. What I
recorded from the abstract and the arXiv metadata is a *pointer plus four credibility flags*,
labelled as observations, not as a verdict.

**2. I landed to `main` rather than parking.** The dispatch's yes-branch escalates "if a hit
actually FILLS the cell". Whether it fills the cell is precisely what cannot be settled at the
depth I was scoped to, so "actually" is unmet; and none of the four §8 escalations fires (no
`plan_of_record.py` change, no ban lifted, no Clay-chain movement claimed — odds stay ~0.05% —
no banked result rewritten). Under §8's "everything short of these four is decided, not asked",
I decided. The escalation *candidacy* is written at the top of the finding and into the JSON's
`escalation` field so the orchestrator can route it without reading the whole quartet, and
**leg 309 is now load-bearing and should be prioritised.**

**3. Territory extension, declared.** The dispatch named three paths; the §6 quartet needs a
runner, prose pair, evidence script and a registered figure. I added, all under the
collision-free `gaf`/`GAF` prefix: `experiments/p2_route_gaf_v1_sweep.py`,
`experiments/p2_route_gaf_v1_adjudications.py`,
`experiments/p2_route_gaf_v1_sweep_evidence.py`,
`writeup/data/p2_route_gaf_v1_raw.json`,
`writeup/4_p2_lottery/{BLOG,TECHNICAL}_P2_ROUTEGAF_V1.md`,
`writeup/figures/fig71_route_gaf_v1_cell.png`, plus a **one-line append** to
`writeup/build_figures.py::P2_EVIDENCE` (shared file, append-only, no existing line touched).
No shared ledger was edited: `solver/viscous_novelty.py` is **not** modified by this leg —
banking `2604.09949` as a `PRECEDENTS` row would be asserting an occupancy the leg cannot
establish, and is properly leg 309's call.

## Lessons this leg would hand forward

- **MF1 — `Navier--Stokes` with a LaTeX double hyphen defeats a `Navier-Stokes` regex.** My
  own mechanical screen scored the one hit a near-miss for exactly this reason. A systematic
  false-negative channel pointed at the project's central model. Use a hyphen class.
- **MF2 — leg 174 banked counts, not links, and it cost exactly what the leg-53 lesson said
  it would.** Its query #12 returned the hit, its count is unchanged, and whether leg 174 read
  and rejected the paper or never opened it is **unrecoverable**. 10 links total are
  unattributable. Every one of my 35 rows carries its links.
- **MF3 — rate-limited is not empty.** Separate transport failure from a null, always.
- The two hand-adjudicated failure modes (5× no certificate, 4× inviscid object) are a
  one-line portrait of the field, and they confirm leg 174's phrasing under re-test: the cell
  is empty **for want of a target, not a method** — the newest method paper in the sweep
  (`2607.15256`, 26 days old) sharpens the machinery and aims it at 3D Euler.
