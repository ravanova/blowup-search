Every **admissible** weight's modulated gap is negative and converges, over `n = 32 … 256`, to

| weight | `n = 32` | `64` | `128` | `256` | limit `-(3-γ)/2` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `A0_flat` (γ=0) | −1.492304 | −1.498135 | −1.499541 | **−1.499886** | −1.500 |
| `A1` (γ=1) | −1.003329 | −1.005822 | −1.006152 | **−1.006201** | −1.000 |

`A1` is the only member the limit does not capture to 4 digits (−1.00620 against −1.000): there

## The mechanism, registered before the ladders ran and then confirmed

Integrating the transport term by parts puts the **entire local part of the form** into a
single multiplier — the damping factor

```
D_φ(θ) = (3/2) cos θ + (1/2) sin θ (log φ)'(θ) ,       D_φ(0) = (3 − γ)/2
```

for both named families (checked against a central difference to `2.3e−10` over all seven
weights). Chen–Hou's move is to choose `φ` making this negative where the profile is singular,
i.e. at the origin. That needs **γ > 3**. But `sin kθ ~ kθ` there, so the integrand of
`‖sin θ‖²_φ` is `~ θ^(2−γ)`, and the basis is in `L²_φ` only for **γ < 3**.

**The two thresholds are the same number.** The window is not empty by a margin — it has
**zero width**, closing exactly at `γ = 3`, where the weighted norm log-diverges. That is
measured, not argued: at equally spaced grading depths the norm's increments are equal to
**1.000000** at `γ = 3` (the logarithmic signature) and grow by **4096 = 64²** at `γ = 4` (a
power), while every admissible member's norm ratio is `1.000000` to six places.

This is a **new instance of lesson 88's shape in a realization that shares no quantity with
it**: the exponent at which the method would start working is exactly the exponent at which
the space stops existing.

## The one positive number, and why it is a statement about the code

`A3` (γ=3) reports a *positive* gap. It fails on three independent grounds, any one of which
would be enough:

1. **It collapses.** `+0.042609 → +0.012644 → +0.003640 → +0.000961 → +0.000121` over
   `n = 16 … 256` — an 87 % relative drop at the last refinement alone.
2. **It moves with the quadrature cutoff.** At fixed `n = 64`, sweeping the grading depth
   `12 → 24 → 48 → 96` gives `+0.002938 → +0.003640 → +0.003908 → +0.004005` — relative
   spread **2.665e−01** — while admissible `A2` sits at `−0.498757` across the same sweep with
   relative spread **6.678e−16**, i.e. machine precision. The number is finite only because
   the innermost panel is (lesson 86).
3. **Its space does not contain the basis** (the admissibility precondition, above).

Its relative change per refinement is `0.7033, 0.7121, 0.7361, 0.8736` — *increasing*: the
sequence is not merely failing to settle, it is coming apart faster the further it is refined.

`A4_chen_hou` (γ=4 — Chen–Hou–Huang's own exponent) is worse and in the same direction: the
gap wanders `−2.447 → −3.181 → −1.279 → −0.193 → −0.072` while the whitening drops
`0 → 2 → 13 → 45 → 109` directions and `cond(G)` climbs `4.88e3 → 6.15e7`. **Their exponent is
the one this object cannot afford.**

**Disclosure on the tolerance.** The gate's tolerance had to be declared in the driver; the
ladders were run exploratorily first. The declared form is **relative** (`≤ 5 %` per
refinement) because an absolute tolerance is passed trivially by a sequence collapsing to
zero — which is precisely `A3`'s behaviour. `A3` is the only weight the two readings judge
differently, and it is disqualified twice over by criteria fixed independently of it, so the
answer does not turn on the choice. Both readings are recorded per weight in the JSON.

## Controls, and both can report the other answer

- **Positive control (lesson 90).** `L − μΛ` turns the unbounded part from a shift into a
  multiplier and changes nothing else. `A0_flat`: `−1.49814 (μ=0) → −0.17271 → +0.79960 →
  +2.53778 → +5.73584 (μ=2)`; `A2`: `−0.49876 → +5.82045`. Monotone, and **the sign flips** —
  so the negative gap at `μ = 0` is a measurement and not a property of the code.
- **External known-answer window (lesson 84).** Xu arXiv:2607.19762 publishes this
  linearisation's spectrum: point spectrum exactly `{0,1}`, essential spectrum on
  `Re λ = −1/2`, modulated gap `1/2`. Both point modes reproduce here to `2.2e−16`
  (`L sin 2θ = 0`; `L(sin θ + sin 2θ/2) = itself`); the **raw** gap is `≤ −1` for every
  admissible weight at every `n`, as the published eigenvalue `1` requires (at `n = 16`:
  `−2.1015, −2.6735, −4.0801, −1.4830, −1.1180`); and the largest admissible modulated gap,
  **−0.499924**, is inside the published ceiling `+0.5`. The instrument agrees with the
  literature everywhere the literature has an answer.
- **Quadrature validated where the answer is known.** The flat-weight Gram matrix is exactly
  `(π/2)I` to `1.4e−15` at `n = 64`. This gate earned its place: a purely geometric mesh —
  the obvious choice for a singular weight — reported `cond(G) = 7.1e10` for that exactly
  diagonal matrix, because its interior panels were wider than a wavelength of `sin 64θ`. The
  first draft's `n = 64` gaps (`−4.0e6`) were entirely that bug.
