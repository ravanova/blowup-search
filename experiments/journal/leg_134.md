## What the novelty pass changed about the leg, before construction

**One thing, and it decided the leg.** A scoping probe run while the pass was being written
showed that the gate's clause (b) — *"reproduce the banked `s_c` measurement bit-identically"* —
is **not identifiable as a two-arm comparison**. Comparing the 2026-08-02 JSON against a run
today cannot distinguish *"the repair moved the number"* from *"the environment moved the
number"*, and the bitwise-reproducibility literature (Q2) is explicit that bitwise identity is
a property *within* one implementation and environment, its legitimate use being comparison
against *"a previously trusted (validated) implementation"* — **re-run**, not read from a file.

So the leg was pre-registered with **three arms**, not two:

| arm | what it is | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| `BANKED` | `writeup/data/p2_route_f_v1_viscosity.json`, generated `2026-08-02T01:15:27Z` | everything since |
| `POST` | `solver/fractional_gclm.py` on `main` today (with the guard) | — |
| `PRE` | the same module at `8d4fdec^`, `git show`n into a temp file, run in **this** environment | **the repair, and only the repair** |

`PRE` is a control that can come out differently (lesson 90): it is a real second computation
of the same 16 quantities, and had the guard touched the numerical path the two arms would
disagree. Environment recorded as data: Python `3.12.3`, NumPy `2.5.1`,
`Linux-7.0.0-28-generic-x86_64-with-glibc2.39`.

neither edited nor overwritten. Nothing here reads the repair's own bundled test, and nothing

|---|---|---|

| **A3** full pipeline, invalid `s` | **2 of 5** returned a silent finite `p` (`s = -0.5 → +2.1864`, `s = -2.0 → +3.2481`); 3 refused | **0 of 5** silent; **5 of 5** refuse |

| **A5** negative `nu` | **1 of 1** silent, `p = +0.5264` vs control `+0.4948` (**+6.38 %**) | **0 of 1**; raises |

**And the other direction — the guard did not overshoot.** `6 of 6` non-negative `s` values in

## B2 — the banked `s_c` line, three arms (`n = 8192`, `a = 0`, `nu = 1e-3`, 7 `s` values)

**The repair arm: `PRE` vs `POST`, 16 of 16 quantities at 0 ULP.** Every `p(s)`, every `T_est`,
the slope and the zero crossing are **bit-identical** between the pre-repair and post-repair
modules run in the same environment. The guard is a **bitwise no-op** on the validated line —
established independently of the repair's own claim, and by a control that could have said
otherwise.

**The archive arm: `BANKED` vs `POST`, 0 of 16 at 0 ULP.** The banked line does *not* reproduce
to the bit across the four days since `2026-08-02T01:15:27Z`:

| quantity | banked | today | ULP |
|---|---|---|---|
| `p(s=0.15)` | `0.73309161483923158` | `0.73309161483918961` | −378 |
| `p(s=0.65)` | `-0.30888753017629811` | `-0.30888753017633491` | **−663** (worst) |
| `slope` | `-2.0675856861670567` | `-2.0675856861670172` | +89 |
| **`s_zero`** (the banked `s_c` measurement) | **`0.50330538502014555`** | **`0.50330538502013789`** | **−69** |

Worst relative move on any `p`: **1.191e-13**. The shift in `s_zero` is **7.661e-15 absolute**
(**1.522e-14 relative**).

**"Small in which norm?" — the scale, from Route-F's own banked error bars.** That shift is
**1.694e-13** of Route-F's dominant systematic (the fit window, `F7` half-spread `0.0452249`,
swept not chosen) and **1.002e-13** of its resolution spread (`F5`, `0.0764378`). For contrast,
the banked `s_zero` sits `0.003305385` from the predicted `s_c = 0.5` — i.e. the drift is a
factor **4.3e11** (11.6 orders of magnitude) below the measurement's own agreement with its
prediction. Nothing about the banked result changes.

## B3 — the known-answer gate, and it is the discriminator

Route-F's `F1` (the exact `a = 0` CLM solution, the solver-vs-exact error at `t = 2.5`, the
run's own recovered singular time) is **5 of 5 at 0 ULP on BOTH arms** — bit-identical to the
2026-08-02 archive as well as to the pre-repair module.

That split is not decoration, it localizes the drift. `F1` runs at `nu = 0`, `s = 1`, so
`|k|^{2s}` is an exact integer power and `exp(-nu·visc·dt) = exp(0) = 1`: **neither
transcendental kernel is exercised non-trivially.** `F2` runs at `nu = 1e-3` and fractional
`s`, so **both are**. Every quantity that touches `pow`/`exp` drifted; every quantity that does
not is exact. The archive arm is a NumPy/libm difference, not a repository event.

## B4 — the mechanism, measured rather than asserted (lesson 85)

"A library kernel moved by an ULP" is an explanation only if an ULP is **enough**. So the same
line was rebuilt with `visc` displaced by exactly **one ULP** (`np.nextafter`) and nothing else
changed — the smallest difference a different `pow` could make — against this leg's own `POST`
line, same environment, same driver:

| quantity | move from a 1-ULP displacement of `visc` |
|---|---|
| `p(s=0.15)` / `p(s=0.35)` | 0 ULP |
| `p(s=0.25)` | −2 ULP |
| `p(s=0.45)` / `p(s=0.55)` / `p(s=0.65)` | +37 / +119 / +186 ULP |
| `p(s=0.75)` | **−484 ULP** |
| `slope` | −112 ULP |
| `s_zero` | **−38 ULP** |

A **single** one-ULP displacement of the multiplier therefore reproduces **484 of the 663 ULP**
(73 %) of the worst archive-arm distance on `p`, and **38 of the 69 ULP** (55 %) on `s_zero`;
the archive arm is `1.37×` the single-ULP response. Since the archive arm involves ULP-level
differences in **two** kernels (`pow` and `exp`) across **4096 wavenumbers** and ~40 000 RK4
steps, a factor `1.37` is squarely within what one ULP of input already buys. **The archive
arm needs no explanation beyond floating-point kernel evolution**, and this probe could have
said otherwise: a response of a handful of ULPs would have refuted the localization and made
the drift something to chase. It did not.

## The gate, answered in its pre-committed wording

> *Post-repair, does `solver/fractional_gclm.py` (a) reject every `s<0` / `nu<0` case in leg
> 91's original battery at construction, and (b) reproduce the banked `s_c` measurement
> bit-identically on the validated line?*

**YES**, with clause (b) answered precisely and its caveat stated rather than hidden.

* **(a) YES.** 7 of 7 gate-scoped `s < 0` / `nu < 0` silent-acceptance cases are closed, all of
  them **at construction**, before `visc` is built — which is the right place, because the
  defect was that `visc[0] = 0.0` overwrote the `inf` that says the operator is ill-defined.
  0 remain. And no overshoot: 6 of 6 admissible constructions and the control `p` are unmoved
  to the bit.
* **(b) YES on the repair arm — 16 of 16 quantities on the `s_c` line and 5 of 5 on the
  known-answer gate at 0 ULP between the pre-repair and post-repair modules.** The repair is a
  bitwise no-op on the validated line, verified against the pre-repair module re-run here, not
  against the repair's own claim. **The archive arm (0 of 16 vs the 2026-08-02 JSON) is not the
  repair**: it is confined to the quantities that exercise `pow`/`exp`, it is reproduced to
  within `1.37×` by a one-ULP displacement of `visc` alone, and it is `1.7e-13` of Route-F's
  own dominant systematic.

**Disposition: NOT an escalation.** The gate's no-branch is for *"an incomplete fix or a repair
regression"*, and the measurements say it is neither: the fix is complete on its declared scope
and provably bitwise-inert on the validated line. None of `ORCHESTRATION.md` §8's four
escalations is touched — no `plan_of_record.py` change, no ban lifted or weakened, no claim of
movement on the Clay chain, and no banked result deleted or rewritten (`p2_route_f_v1_viscosity.json`
and `p2_route_fga_v1_adversarial.json` were opened read-only and are byte-identical to
`origin/main`).

**The yes-branch's instruction is executed: leg 91's battery is banked as a permanent
regression suite** — `test_fractional_gclm_postrepair.py`, 8 checks, 17 s. It pins the four
guard directions, the no-overshoot control, the positive dissipation-monotonicity control, the
five already-correct refusals leg 91 found the module already handled, `F1` **bitwise**, and
the `F2` line at `1e-12`. The two halves are pinned differently on purpose, and the asymmetry
is B3's finding: `F1` is environment-independent and is held to the bit; `F2` is not, so a
bitwise pin there would assert something measured to be false — `1e-12` is 130× above the
measured 7.66e-15 drift and still 1.7e-13 of the measurement's own error bar. **A failure at
`1e-13` is an environment change; a failure at `1e-3` is a regression.**

## What is explicitly NOT claimed

* **No patch, and no edit.** `solver/fractional_gclm.py` is byte-identical to `origin/main`.
  The one gap this leg re-measured and did **not** close — the undomained pure closed forms,
  6 of 10 malformed arguments still silently finite — is left exactly where leg 91 and the
  repair left it, pinned by gates 2/3/5 of `test_fractional_gclm_adversarial.py`.
* **No novelty.** Pre-empted by the novelty pass: a reproduction-and-regression claim about
  this repository's own code, capped there, as legs 86, 87, 94 and 103 were.
* **Nothing here is a statement about the value of `s_c`.** `s_c = alpha/2` remains validated
  against XU eq (6.3) and PRE-EMPTED under Route-J, exactly as before this leg ran. The
  `n = 512` audit crossings are clean-vs-contaminated differences only, and are not comparable
  to Route-F's measurements.
* **The archive arm is not evidence of a defect anywhere.** It is a measured property of
  float64 transcendental kernels, quantified, localized, and mechanism-checked. Reading it as
  instability in Route-F's result would be wrong by a factor 4.3e11.
