# Leg 247 — Route-VBR: repairing Route-D v11's `v5_budget` min/max selection

**Role: LEG.** Branch `leg/247-vbr-v1`. Repairs the defect leg 235 (Route-CDAP)
diagnosed and deliberately did not patch.

## The gate, in its pre-committed wording

> "Does using the correct selection (per leg 235's own diagnosis) for
> `v5_budget`'s margin computation at `a=0.45` bring the block back within its
> own declared Y0 budget, and does re-running the other 195 banked JSONs confirm
> they remain unaffected (matching leg 235's own 0/195 isolation finding)?"

**NO — and it is a conjunction whose two clauses answer differently.** Clause 2
answers YES (0 of 196 others affected, one *more* file than leg 235 screened).
Clause 1 answers **NO**: the corrected selection does not bring the block back
within its budget, because the budget was never met. The repair is correct and
lands the right number; the right number is a **62.02× violation**.

Escalated per the dispatch's NO branch. Branch pushed, `main` not touched.

## The repair

`experiments/p2_route_d_v11_anchor.py :: v5_budget`, and only that function
(plus the V5 line of `main()`'s printout, which quoted the same selection —
leaving it pairing the *min* with a *max*-derived margin would have been a new
inconsistency, not a preserved one).

`v5_budget`'s claim is universally quantified over the `a`-range it declares
(`a_range_used = 0.5`; its own prose says "for a < a\*"). A universal budget is
certified by its **worst case**. So:

```
margin := Y0_max / max(weighted_defect)      # was: / min(...)
```

**Why not a min/max combination.** No average of the two ends — mean, geometric
mean, median — certifies a universal statement: each one still admits a single
over-budget row. The worst case is the only defensible selection. The min is
kept, renamed `margin_at_best_row`, because it is a genuine diagnostic once it
is not masquerading as the headline. Alongside it the block now enumerates
`rows_over_budget`, `worst_violation_x`, `budget_holds_uniformly` and
`margin_over_rows_within_budget`, so a scalar below 1 is no longer the whole
story a reader gets.

## R2 — the `a = 0.45` row re-solved from scratch

The defect is not a stale bank. Fresh `TwoScaleNewton` solves at `n = 801`:

| `a` | fresh `weighted_defect` | banked | rel. difference | `× budget` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.40 | `2.376162e-08` | `2.374541e-08` | `6.83e-04` | `9.70e-05` |
| **0.45** | **`1.532023e-02`** | `1.519582e-02` | `8.19e-03` | **`62.53×` over** |
| 0.50 | `7.452778e-07` | `7.452803e-07` | `3.42e-06` | `3.04e-03` |

The `a = 0.45` defect reproduces to `0.82%` on an independent solve, and it sits
**~6 decades above both immediate neighbours** — which is precisely why a
min-selection hid it so completely: the min is taken over a set where 10 of 11
members are `1e-7` or smaller.

The two clean rows reproduce to `6.8e-04` and `3.4e-06` while `a = 0.45`
reproduces only to `8.2e-03`; that row also needs 40 Newton iterations to reach
`relres = 1.6e-9` where its neighbours reach `~1e-14`. Recorded as a fact, not
interpreted here (see residue 2).


## R3 — the load-bearing regression check

Leg 235's `R-SELECT` rule, re-implemented independently here (a `_min`/`_max`
sibling pair in one dict, a budget-named sibling in the same dict that the
unfavourable end violates, and a third scalar in that dict equal to
`budget/favourable` and not to `budget/unfavourable`), run over every other
banked JSON.

- **196 other banked JSONs screened, 25,598 dicts walked, 187 `_min`/`_max`
  sibling pairs seen — 0 affected.**
- Leg 235 screened 195 others and found 0. The corpus has grown by one since; the
  isolation finding holds on the larger set.
- **Positive control fires**: the rule recovers the banked pre-repair
  `v5_budget` block exactly (`headline_value = 1.0467862586e+10`,
  `violation_x = 62.0237`, `headline_if_corrected = 1.6122858874e-02`). A null
  elsewhere from an instrument that did not fire on the known instance would be
  worthless.
- **The named limit (lesson 91).** The realization is: banked
  `writeup/data/*.json`, screened by this one rule. It can only speak about
  dicts carrying a `_min`/`_max` sibling pair **and** a budget-named sibling in
  the **same record**. It is *silent about*, not *clearing*, every other shape —
  in particular any favourable-end selection where the two ends are not named
  `_min`/`_max`, or where the budget lives in a different block. 187 pairs is the
  honest denominator, not 25,598.

## Does the corrected value change any conclusion Route-D v11's headline draws?

This is Route-D v11's **second** confirmed headline-adjacent correction this
cycle (after leg 226/236's, which is a different file and a different mechanism
— `profile_newton.py`'s convergence flag). Applying leg 236's discipline to this
one, checked at source rather than asserted:

- `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTED_V11.md` §4 **already** prints the
  `a = 0.45` weighted defect as `1.5e-2` in bold and says in terms: "*not
  uniformly under the budget (2.45e-4): `a = 0.45` is above it*". Its stated
  conclusion is a *change of binding constraint* ("`Y₀` is no longer
  **search**-limited, it is **discretization**-limited"), which the corrected
  margin does not disturb — that reading rests on the RMS collapse from `1e-2`
  to machine precision (V2), not on the `1.05e10` margin.
- `writeup/4_p2_lottery/BLOG_P2_ROUTED_V11.md` likewise quotes `1.5e-2` as
  *above* the budget.
- `writeup/4_p2_lottery/p2_route_d_v11_evidence.py` (fig29) prints **both** ends
  already, min and max, and does not use `margin`.
- `test_profile_newton.py` mentions the artifact once in a NOTE, with no
  assertion on `margin`.

**So: no written conclusion of Route-D v11 changes.** The exposure was confined
to the JSON field `v5_budget.margin` and the runner's stdout V5 line — the two
places a reader gets a *number* rather than the prose. That is a narrower blast
radius than leg 235's escalation had to assume, and it is stated here as a
measurement of the four consumers, not as a reassurance.

It also sharpens what leg 235 found: the defect was never that the project
*believed* the profile cleared the budget uniformly — the prose says otherwise
in two places. It was that the machine-readable artifact said something the
prose contradicted, and the artifact is what the next automated consumer reads.
