## 2. Reading the machinery before writing any of my own

`solver/energy_coercivity.py` is read-only to this leg. The chain that produces a gap:

1. `graded_quadrature(n_unif, n_grade, order)` — composite Gauss–Legendre, `n_unif` uniform
   panels with the first and last geometrically refined `n_grade` levels at ratio `1/2`
   toward `0` and `π`.
2. `wes_constrained_basis(n, "T2_egm")` — an **exact integer** `(n, n−2)` basis. Its columns
   are `w_m = u_{m+1} v_m − u_m v_{m+1}` with `v_j = (j+1)e_j − j e_{j+1}`, so each column
   has at most **3** nonzeros. That sparsity is what makes an MP patch affordable.
3. `wes_form_matrices_constrained` — contracts `F = Vᵀ E` **pointwise** (before the weight),
   then `G = (F·pw) Fᵀ`, `B = (F·pw) LFᵀ`.
4. `wes_coercivity_gap_exact` — whitens with `G`'s eigendecomposition at `rcond = 1e-12` and
   returns `gap = −λ_max(W ᵀ Sym(B) W)`.

The float64 loss is at step 2/3's *pointwise* contraction: at `θ ~ 1e-31` (which
`n_grade = 96` reaches) `F_m(θ) = Σ V[k,m] sin kθ` is an `O(θ³)` cancellation between
`O(θ)` terms, so float64 returns dust. **Neither the weight `φ` nor the quadrature weights
`qw` are where the loss is** (§7a), so they are not patched — patching only where the
cancellation is, is leg 312's own protocol and it is inherited unchanged.

The patch is therefore: recompute `F`, `LF`, `HF` at the unsafe nodes in `Decimal`, leave
everything else identical, re-assemble. That is leg 312's `experiments/p2_route_apia_v1.py`
`leg178_remeasure()` exactly; this leg re-uses its `_mp_sin_cos` / `_mp_recurrence_all_k` /
`_spot_check_against_rigorous_mp` **by import**, not by copy (§4 of the novelty pass), and
generalises the surrounding driver from one cell to twenty-eight.
