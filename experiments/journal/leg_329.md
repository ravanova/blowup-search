## 2. Reading the machinery before writing any of my own

`solver/energy_coercivity.py` is read-only to this leg. The chain that produces a gap:

1. `graded_quadrature(n_unif, n_grade, order)` — composite Gauss–Legendre, `n_unif` uniform
   panels with the first and last geometrically refined `n_grade` levels at ratio `1/2`
   toward `0` and `π`.
2. `wes_constrained_basis(n, "T2_egm")` — an **exact integer** `(n, n−2)` basis. Its columns
   are `w_m = u_{m+1} v_m − u_m v_{m+1}` with `v_j = (j+1)e_j − j e_{j+1}`, so each column
   has at most **3** nonzeros. That sparsity is what makes an MP patch affordable.
3. `wes_form_matrices_constrained` — contracts `F = Vᵀ E` **pointwise** (before the weight),
   then `G = (F·pw) Fᵀ`, `B = (F·pw) LFᵀ`.
4. `wes_coercivity_gap_exact` — whitens with `G`'s eigendecomposition at `rcond = 1e-12` and
   returns `gap = −λ_max(W ᵀ Sym(B) W)`.

The float64 loss is at step 2/3's *pointwise* contraction: at `θ ~ 1e-31` (which
`n_grade = 96` reaches) `F_m(θ) = Σ V[k,m] sin kθ` is an `O(θ³)` cancellation between
`O(θ)` terms, so float64 returns dust. **Neither the weight `φ` nor the quadrature weights
`qw` are where the loss is** (§7a), so they are not patched — patching only where the
cancellation is, is leg 312's own protocol and it is inherited unchanged.

The patch is therefore: recompute `F`, `LF`, `HF` at the unsafe nodes in `Decimal`, leave
everything else identical, re-assemble. That is leg 312's `experiments/p2_route_apia_v1.py`
`leg178_remeasure()` exactly; this leg re-uses its `_mp_sin_cos` / `_mp_recurrence_all_k` /
`_spot_check_against_rigorous_mp` **by import**, not by copy (§4 of the novelty pass), and
generalises the surrounding driver from one cell to twenty-eight.

## 3. What the runner does, and what it deliberately does not do

`experiments/p2_route_egmf_v1.py`. Three layers:

1. **`mp_patch(class, n, n_grade, n_unif, tau, prec)`** — recomputes `F`, `LF`, `HF` in
   `Decimal` at every node with `min(θ, π−θ) < τ`, from one `sin/cos` base pair per node via
   the angle-addition recurrence (leg 312's `_mp_sin_cos`, imported). Cached, because the
   patch depends on `(class, n, n_grade)` **and not on the weight** — so `B4_egm`, `E_egm`
   and `A4_chen_hou` share one patch each and the C7 ceiling control is free.
2. **`assemble_gap(patch, family, gamma, which, rcond)`** — mirrors
   `wes_form_matrices_constrained` + `wes_coercivity_gap_exact` line for line, on either the
   float64 or the patched contraction. The mirror is not trusted: control C1 checks it
   against leg 178's banked numbers.
3. **`five_clauses(...)`** — leg 178's predicate, its five constants unchanged.

**Not patched, on purpose, and each for a stated reason:**

- **`φ` and the quadrature weights** — cancellation-free by inspection (§7a); patching where
  the cancellation is not is how a patch starts manufacturing answers.
- **the mesh.** The object being re-measured is *leg 178's quadrature rule, evaluated
  exactly*. Moving the nodes would measure a different rule and C1 would then have nothing
  to reproduce.

### 3a. Diagnostic D1 — a mesh failure I did not go looking for

Counting nodes before patching them turned up something not in leg 178's record and not in
leg 312's: at `n_grade ≥ 48`, **exactly 12 nodes have `min(θ, π−θ) == 0.0` in float64.**
The geometric refinement at the **right** endpoint descends below the float64 spacing near
`π` (`4.44e-16`), so the innermost right-hand panel collapses onto `π` itself. The left
endpoint has no such problem — `θ` near `0` is representable down to `7.1e-34` at
`n_grade = 96`, and the mesh does reach that. So leg 178's grading is **asymmetric in
practice while symmetric in intent**, and the asymmetry appears at `n_grade = 48`, one rung
*below* the depth everyone has been discussing.

This is a **mesh** representation failure, categorically different from the **pointwise
contraction** failure this leg patches, and it is not repaired here. Control D1b sizes it:
the gap recomputed with those 12 nodes deleted outright, against the gap with them kept.
Deleting them is a different quadrature rule, so D1b is a ruler, never the answer.

### 4d. A defect in my own pre-registration, recorded and not self-adjudicated

The sign of the C4 residual was not anticipated when C4 was written, and it matters.
`−R(x)` is not a second opinion about the gap; it is a **one-sided upper bound** on it. The
exact bound lands **below `1/2`**, by 5.19e-18 and 1.42e-18. Clause 5 is
`gap ≤ 1/2 + 1e-9`, which is also one-sided, and on the same side. So:

- read as the **agreement test it was registered as**, C4 fails → gate `no`;
- read as the **bound it actually computes**, C4 does not merely fail to refute clause 5 —
  it *establishes* clause 5 more firmly than the float64 eigensolve ever could, eight orders
  of magnitude inside the clause's own slack, and the gate would answer `yes`.

The two readings disagree about the gate. **This leg does not resolve that**, for a reason
written down in §7d of the novelty pass before any of this was measured: overriding a
pre-registered control in the exact direction everyone already expects the answer to move is
the failure mode this leg was constructed to guard against, and the literal pre-registration
is the only guard I have against my own motivated reasoning. Suppressing the finding would
be the opposite failure, so it is banked in full, with its exact digits, in
`gate_answer.C4_pre_registration_defect`. **The adjudication is the DM's.**

C5 is *not* rescued by the bound reading, and this is the load-bearing distinction: what the
C4 bound would rescue is the **clause**, not the **number**. The reported gap remains a
property of the truncation whichever way C4 is read — although it is worth recording that
every rung of the C5 ladder, and every MP number this leg produced for these two rows, lies
below `1/2`.

### 4f. C2's literal failure, taken at the stronger reading
