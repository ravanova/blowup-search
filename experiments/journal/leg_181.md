## The answer to "what is being modulated"

**The two free parameters of the self-similar ansatz — the collapse time `T` and the spatial
scale.** Not the operator, not the space, not the norm. Xu, §3.2 p. 10, verbatim:

> *They are modded out by the standard modulation (choosing the collapse time and spatial
> scale) and do not represent instability.*

Those two parameters generate the two exact eigenmodes at `λ = +1` (time shift) and `λ = 0`
(scaling), namely `y Ω′` and `Ω + c_l y Ω′`, which Xu proves exact by direct substitution for
**every admissible profile**, not only at `a = 0`.

## The answer to "what does the technique cost"

**Nothing, and it buys nothing beyond deleting two already-known eigenvalues.** Three findings,
all new to this repository:

- **It is not Xu's technique and Xu says so.** He writes "the standard modulation" at every one
  of its three appearances and credits the method to Elgindi–Ghoul–Masmoudi
  ([arXiv:1906.05811](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05811) / *APDE* **14** (2021) 891) at §1 p. 2.
  There is no "Xu modulation technique" to transfer.
- **As an operator it is a rank-2 projection onto the two origin Taylor functionals**, eq
  (A.13), §A.4 p. 37: `P v = v(0) + v′(0) y`, `Q v = v − v(0) − v′(0) y`, with the reading
  "*Thus Q is the regular part at the collapse point: it removes the zeroth and first Taylor
  coefficients of v at y = 0*". No file in this repository had written that down. Its **one**
  structural prerequisite is that those two functionals be **bounded** on the realization —
  Xu supplies his own worked failure of it (`Y_θ`, Definition A.2; **banked by leg 171, cited
  not claimed here**), and the repair costs a transfer map `J_X` carrying `C_Q ≠ 1` and forcing
  `θ ∈ (1,2)`.
- **"after modulation" is not a hypothesis, and the clause that would carry weight is OPEN.**
  §8 p. 33: "*Three inputs are still missing, and none is supplied here: … and the modulation
  closure that removes the two symmetry directions along the flow*"; restated independently at
  §6.1 p. 31. Legs 127/163 have cited the headline as though "after modulation" carried a
  hypothesis. It does not — it is a two-point deletion — and the **dynamic** closure is
  explicitly not supplied. Worth a one-line correction wherever the phrase is used.

## The criterion (this repository's own abstraction, not Xu's)

> Modulation is available on a realization `Z` **iff** (a) the two exact symmetry modes lie in
> `Z`, **and** (b) the two origin Taylor functionals `v ↦ v(0)`, `v ↦ v′(0)` are **bounded**
> on `Z`.

## The finding

**`bordered_linearization`'s gauge row IS `v′(0)` — character for character — and has been
since leg 51.**

In the odd sine basis `v = Σ b_k sin kθ`, the first origin derivative is `Σ_k k b_k`. That is
exactly `solver/spectral_certificate.py:260`, `M[K][k-1] = k`, whose own docstring already says
it "*removes the exact zero eigenvalue the dilation symmetry puts there*" — Xu's `λ = 0`
scaling mode. Checked, not asserted: max deviation from `(1,2,…,K)` is **0.0**.

So **leg 127's `Z₁ ≥ 1` is a theorem about an already-partially-modulated object.** Nothing
banked is reversed; what changes is what leg 127's theorem is a theorem *about*.

And that half of the projection is **unbounded on the space in exactly the range leg 127 lives
in**:

| check | measured | predicted |
|---|---|---|
| C1 dual norm of `v′(0)` on `ℓ¹_w`, `w_k=(1+k)^s`, `= sup_k k/(1+k)^s`, ladder to `K=4096` | diverges at `s = 0, 0.3, 0.7, 0.9`; saturates at `s = 1, 1.5` | **finite iff `s ≥ 1`** — HOLDS |
| C1 at `s = 0.3`, `K = 4096` | 337.8 and still growing | — |
| C2 `fredholm_sides` kernel exponent | **−2.0024** | ~ −2 |
| C2 `fredholm_sides` cokernel exponent | **+1.0012** | ~ +1 |
| C2 crossing | **s = 1.0** | 1.0 |
| gauge-row identity, max deviation | **0.0** (exact) | exact |
| locators | **11/11** quotes located, **0/5** negatives wrongly located | 11/11, 0/5 |

The cokernel functional grows like `m`, i.e. **the same growth class as `v′(0)`**. So `s = 1` is
not two separate facts that happen to coincide; it is one fact seen twice. **Modulation is
unbounded where the obstruction is (`s < 1`), and defined only where the obstruction has already
left the space (`s ≥ 1`).**

## A pre-registered check failed, and is kept (lesson 76)

`writeup/novelty/leg_181.md` §4.3 pre-registered **C3**: the worst column of `M_K⁻¹` would carry
negligible `ℓ¹_w` mass on the modulation span, and removing the span would leave the constant
unchanged. **Both predictions are wrong as stated** — measured **65.65%** mass (predicted < 5%)
and a **92× collapse**, 187.568 → 2.030 (predicted ~1×).

Interrogated rather than banked (lesson 90; leg 127's own `μ = 0` coincidence is the precedent),
and the check does **not** survive interrogation:

- the domain-only quotient is **overdetermined** (two fewer unknowns, same equations), so `pinv`
  returns a **least-squares** constant that solves nothing and is not comparable to `‖M_K⁻¹‖_w`;
- the dimensionally comparable form, the two-sided compression `Q L Q`, is **exactly singular**
  (verified by rank) — i.e. the one form that *is* comparable says modulation destroys
  invertibility, which is also not a repair;
- and identifying `‖M_K⁻¹‖_w`'s argmax column with leg 127's `σ_min` near-null direction would
  need leg 127's own instrument, which is outside this leg's territory.

**C3 therefore decides nothing in either direction, and this leg's verdict does not rest on it**
— it rests on C1, C2 and the gauge-row identity, all three closed-form or exact. C3's real
output is a new, properly-posed question, escalated below as **T2b**.

## Escalations, with what each would require precisely

**T2b.** The literature already knows the answer's shape and this repository already uses it:
the `ℓ¹` way to remove a symmetry direction is a **phase condition** — give the direction its
own unfolding parameter and its own column, keeping the system **square** — which is exactly
what leg 52's bordering does and what the gauge row already does for `λ = 0`. *Precisely:* add a
**second** border column/row pair for `e_1 + e_2/2`, keep the system square, re-measure
`‖M_K⁻¹‖_w` and `Z₁` on leg 127's own ladder. Cheap, decidable either way in one leg.
**Strong prior against, recorded now so nobody is sold a hope:** leg 127 measured that the
existing border's coupling column is supported on a *single* row, the truncation edge, so
"the bordering repair has nowhere else to reach"; a second border of the same kind has no
obvious reason to reach further.

**T3.** The only space this repository has built that is (i) **not** `L²`-equivalent, so Xu §8's
coercivity no-go does not cap it as it caps T1; (ii) not at `s < 1` in the `ℓ¹_w` sense, so T2's
unboundedness argument does not transfer verbatim; and (iii) grades **smoothness** explicitly,
which is the only property criterion (b) asks about. *Precisely:* (1) evaluate the dual norm of
`v ↦ v′(0)` in the two-grading norm and report whether it is finite — v7's provisional optimum
sat at `(α, γ) = (1.4, 0.15)` and `α > 1` is the condition, but **what `α` indexes in that map
is not verified by this leg**; (2) if finite, check the two modes lie in the space under the
decay grading; (3) **only then** ask whether the Route-D obstruction is a symmetry-mode object
at all — because T2 is the cautionary case, where modulation was both available and did not
help. **Strong prior against:** in every case this repository has measured, the obstruction has
been a **far-field** object, and modulation touches only the origin.
