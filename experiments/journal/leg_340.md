## 3. The instrument, and why it removes the thing the DM objected to

The DM's cycle-8e ruling was not that leg 329's C4 number was wrong. It was
that a margin of `5.19e-18` cannot carry a clause when the same leg's control C5
measures a truncation sensitivity of `3.85e-05` — thirteen orders larger. The
only honest reply is to compute the bound **without a truncation at all**.

With `X = tan(θ/2)`, `u = 1+X²` and `(1+iX)^{2k} = R_k(X) + i P_k(X)`:

| object | exact form |
|---|---|
| `sin kθ`, `cos kθ` | `P_k/u^k`, `R_k/u^k`, `R_k, P_k ∈ ℤ[X]` |
| `h = Σ c_k sin kθ` | `A/u^n` |
| `Hh` (module convention `H(sin kθ) = −cos kθ + (−1)^k`) | `C/u^n` |
| `L h` (the `a=0` CLM linearisation) | `[(1−X²)A − 2X(C+D)]/u^{n+1}` |
| `φ_B4` | `u³/(64X⁴)` |
| `φ_E` | `u³/(2X⁴)` = **exactly** `32 φ_B4` |
| `φ_A4` | `u²/(16X⁴)` |
| `dθ` | `2 dX/u` |

Every integral collapses to `∫₀^∞ X^a (1+X²)^{−M} dX = ½B((a+1)/2, M−(a+1)/2)`,
which is **rational** for odd `a` and **rational·π** for even `a`. So

> `R(c) = (r₁ + q₁π)/(r₂ + q₂π)` with `r_i, q_i ∈ ℚ`, computed exactly.

No mesh, no quadrature rule, no `rcond`, no eigensolver, and **no float ever
enters the exact path**. `π` is enclosed rigorously by leg 312's `mp_pi` at 60
digits. A divergent monomial is a hard error, never silently dropped — dropping
one would manufacture a finite answer for an inadmissible trial function, which
is exactly what control K3 exists to catch.

**Validity on the operator quantity (gate part i), fixed in the novelty pass
§7d before any number existed.** `gap := −sup_h R(h)`. A truncation restricts
the supremum to a *subspace*, so `sup_trunc R ≤ sup_op R`, hence

> `gap_op ≤ gap_trunc ≤ −R(x)` for **any** admissible trial vector `x`.

The chain runs one way only. It can never certify that a gap is *achieved* —
only that it is not exceeded. Clause 5 is a one-sided ceiling, so this is
precisely the side that can decide it.

## 5. The gate, answered in its pre-committed wording

> Does the truncation-controlled one-sided bound hold `gap ≤ 1/2 + 1e-9` at
> every ladder rung, with the margin's dependence on the truncation parameter
> measured and reported (magnitudes, not booleans)?

# **YES.**

At every one of the 19 rungs the bound is **exactly `1/2`**, the margin to the
ceiling is **exactly `1e-9`**, and the margin's dependence on the truncation
parameter is **exactly `0`** — against a measured eigensolve sensitivity of
`1.927e-05` / `2.697e-05` on the same rungs. The bound is valid on the
**operator** quantity, by the one-sided chain fixed in §7d before the run, and
the structural control upgrades that bound to an identity.

This clears the pre-declared `yes` criterion of §1b: an enclosure strictly
inside `(−∞, 1/2 + 1e-9]` at every rung, of width (`1.4e-59`) far smaller than
the distance to the ceiling (`1e-9`). It is neither TRUNCATION-LIMITED nor
KNIFE-EDGE: the value does not move under the ladder, and the margin does not
shrink toward zero with precision.

### 5a. The mandatory second reading — pre-registered in the novelty pass §7e, and it is not optional

**Because `R = −1/2` is an IDENTITY on `T2_egm`, clause 5 is a TAUTOLOGY on this
class.** It cannot come out any other way, for any admissible trial function, at
any truncation, in any arithmetic. The quantity therefore carries **no
information about the operator** beyond the two constraints themselves — lesson
90 in its purest form, met not as a suspicion but as an exact matrix identity.

**A flip of leg 178's `NO` on this clause would be a flip ON AN IDENTITY, not a
measurement of a coercivity gap.** This leg is forbidden by its own
pre-registration from reporting the `yes` without this sentence beside it, and
does not report it without.

Note also what §4b says about leg 178's original `NO`: the reason the float64
sweep landed at `+0.4999930` rather than `0.5` is now fully explained. The
continuum answer is exactly `1/2`; `cond(G) ≈ 2.55e+11` at `n=256`; the deficit
is arithmetic, not mathematics. Leg 329's artifact explanation therefore stands
**refined, not overturned** — it was right, and this leg supplies the exact
identity it was reaching for.

### 5b. What this does NOT establish

- **Not a coercivity gap in any useful sense.** The estimate is *saturated*, not
  strict: there is no room left for the perturbation argument a blow-up proof
  would need. An identity gives no margin to spend.
- **Nothing about `a ≠ 0`**, which is where EGM's `−C|a|` term lives and where
  the estimate actually does work.
- **Not a Stage claim.** No `L1`–`L4` link moves. `plan_of_record.py` is
  untouched and byte-identical to `origin/main`.
- **Not a resolution of parked escalation #3.** Per the DM's cycle-8e ruling
  that decision is the **user's**. This leg reports and stops.
- **Nothing new mathematically.** EGM arXiv:1906.05811 Prop. 2.1 already asserts
  the `−1/2` coefficient at `a = 0`; Xu arXiv:2607.19762 §3.1 supplies the
  ceiling. What is this leg's own is only the *measurement that the quantity is
  an identity*.
