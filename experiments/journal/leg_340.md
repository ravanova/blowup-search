### 1b. The trap this leg has to walk past

It is dispatched at reserve rank 1 *because* a `yes` would resolve parked
escalation #3. That is exactly the pressure §7d of leg 329's novelty pass names
and exactly why leg 329 refused to re-read its own fired control. The
protection here is the same one the repo already uses: **pre-register the
instrument and its failure modes in `writeup/novelty/leg_340.md`, on `main`,
before any number exists**, and make the pre-registered prediction *sharp
enough to be wrong*. Written down before running anything:

- If the exact bound lands **strictly above** `1/2 + 1e-9` at any rung, the
  answer is `no`/TRUNCATION-LIMITED and the 5.19e-18 "margin" was noise.
- If it lands **at or below** `1/2` by an amount smaller than the instrument's
  own enclosure width, the answer is `no`/KNIFE-EDGE.
- Only an enclosure that is **strictly inside** `(-inf, 1/2 + 1e-9]` at every
  rung, with a width smaller than the distance to that ceiling, is a `yes`.

The third case is a real possibility and this leg does not get to decide which
of the three it wants.
