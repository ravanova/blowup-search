# Leg 172 — Route-W2L: is Wall 2 this repository's wall, or the field's wall?

**Date:** 2026-08-06. **Kind:** light, literature-only. No model computation, no solver
module imported, no ledger touched. **Answers a question the user asked directly.**

## The question, as the user put it

Wall 2 says validated/interval numerics today reaches only 1D/2D models and that 3D
Navier–Stokes is out of reach. Is that a property of **this repository's approach**, or of
**the field**? The distinction matters because this repository's own EXT-family freshness
legs (74, 77, 82, 90, 93, 123) only ever asked *"has anyone certified THIS repository's
target object"* — a question that is structurally blind to a computer-assisted 3D result
in celestial mechanics, combustion, chemotaxis, pattern formation, or compressible fluids.

## Gate, verbatim

> "Does any published work — in fluid dynamics, PDE theory, or any other
> computer-assisted-proof tradition — establish a rigorous (interval-arithmetic, validated
> numerics, or otherwise machine-certified) singularity/blow-up result for a genuinely 3D
> PDE model (not an ODE, not a 1D/2D reduction)?"

## Answer: **NO** — and the wall's usual wording is wrong in a way worth fixing

**NO.** Across 15 queries and four primary-source reads spanning fluid mechanics
(incompressible and compressible), parabolic blow-up, dispersive PDE, chemotaxis, pattern
formation, and the dynamical-systems/celestial-mechanics CAP lineage, **the largest number
of spatial variables in any machine-certified singularity object is 2** (Chen–Hou). Not
one record clears all four gate clauses.

**Wall 2 is the field's wall.** This repository's 1D/2D scope is the field's scope.

## The measurement, and the control that keeps it honest

`experiments/p2_route_w2l_v1_lit.py` encodes each surveyed work with a decidable field —
`certified_dims`, the number of spatial variables of the object **the machine actually
bounds** — separately from `statement_dims`, the number in the theorem's own statement.
The gate then becomes arithmetic rather than rhetoric.

| clause | records satisfying |
|---|---|
| is_singularity | 8 |
| is_time_dependent | 10 |
| is_machine_certified | 8 |
| **certified_object_is_3d** | **1** (van den Berg–Williams) |
| **all four** | **0 → GATE: NO** |

The single record with `certified_dims = 3` is not a singularity result. That is the whole
finding, in one line.

**Control (lesson 90 — a code path that can only ever say one thing has said nothing).**
Drop exactly one clause, `is_singularity`, and re-run the same predicate: *"has validated
numerics ever certified a genuinely 3D PDE object of any kind?"* → **YES**, van den Berg &
Williams, *Rigorously computing symmetric stationary states of the Ohta–Kawasaki problem
in three dimensions*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 51(1):131–158 (2019), giving *"the first
existence proofs for both the double gyroid and body centered cubic packed sphere
solutions."* Two different answers out of one code path.

**So the naive form of Wall 2 — "validated numerics cannot reach three spatial dimensions"
— is FALSE, and is corrected here.** The barrier is **time-dependent singularity
formation**, not dimension. The sourced form:

> **Wall 2, sourced.** No published work machine-certifies a singularity for an object
> with three spatial variables. Every 3D singularity theorem carrying a computer-assisted
> ingredient obtains its 3D-ness from a symmetry reduction, or from an ODE profile plus
> analysis. This repository's 1D/2D scope **is** the field's scope, not a self-imposed
> limit.

## The lift gap — the leg's real content

Four surveyed works state a 3D theorem and carry a machine-certified ingredient. In every
one, the distance from the certificate to the theorem is bridged **by analysis**:

| work | certified | stated | gap | bridged by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chen–Hou (arXiv:2210.07191 + 2305.05660; PNAS 2025) | **2D** | 3D | 1 | axisymmetric reduction — 3D Euler unknowns depend on `(r,z)` only |
| Buckmaster–Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano (arXiv:2208.09445) | **1D** | 3D | 2 | spherical symmetry; the profile is an **ODE** |
| Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano–Shi–Staffilani (arXiv:2310.05325) | **1D** (inherited) | 3D | 2 | **pure analysis** on the imported 1D profile |
| van den Berg–Williams (SIAM JMA 2019) | 3D | 3D | 0 | — (not a singularity) |

The BCG read is the sharpest, because the paper says in its own words what the machine
did (§1.4, verbatim):

> *"In our concrete case, we will use the computer in two different parts of our strategy:
> 1. Computing (with rigorous bounds) a high amount of Taylor coefficients of a solution of
> an **ODE** at a singular point … 2. Validating the sign of polynomials of degree 7-11
> with coefficients depending on **2 parameters** using a branch and bound method …"*

and §1.5 opens *"Let us begin by rewriting (1.2) **under spherical symmetry**"*. A paper
titled *"Smooth imploding solutions for 3D compressible fluids"*, in Forum of Mathematics
Pi (Jan 2025), whose certified objects are an ODE and a two-parameter polynomial sign
condition. **This is the Tucker/Lorenz pattern occurring inside fluid mechanics itself** —
which is precisely what `DIRECTION.md`'s thesis suspected but no landed leg had checked.

**The closest thing to a YES**, and why it isn't one: Cao-Labora–Gómez-Serrano–Shi–
Staffilani prove *"smooth, **non-radial** solutions of the compressible Euler and
Navier-Stokes equation that develop an imploding finite time singularity"* in `T³` and
`R³` — genuinely 3D, genuinely non-symmetric. But the paper introduces **no** interval
arithmetic of its own; its certified content is inherited from BCG's 1D ODE profile. The
field's one route to a genuinely-3D non-symmetric certified singularity puts the
certificate at the bottom, on a 1D object, and does the dimensional lifting by hand.

## False positives, cleared

Exactly one record in the literature asserts a machine-certified singularity for genuinely
3D Navier–Stokes: arXiv:2604.09949 (Shahmurov). It is disqualified **twice, independently**
(the driver refuses to emit unless every claimant carries ≥2 disqualifiers):

1. **On its own title** — *"via 5D-Lifted **Axisymmetric Reductions**"* is a symmetry
   reduction, so its certified object is not genuinely 3D even taken entirely at face
   value, before any question of validity arises.
2. **On this repository's banked audit** (legs 90, 93) — `CLAIMED_UNUSABLE`: no
   verification package; the reconstructed exactly-backward self-similar solution is
   excluded by Nečas–Růžička–Šverák; the author has since posted six papers claiming the
   logically opposite result in the same lift, none citing it. This leg's queries
   re-confirm no independent confirmation, refutation or retraction has appeared.

Also cleared as non-instances: 3D Keller–Segel blowup (genuinely 3D, but **fully
analytic** — no computer assistance, so it says nothing about validated numerics' reach);
*Discovery of Unstable Singularities* arXiv:2509.14185 (objects are IPM 2D, Boussinesq 2D,
CCF 1D, and it is a PINN discovery framework, not a proof); Palasek arXiv:2605.13827
(a **shell model** of 3D NS); Tao's averaged 3D NS (not a CAP).

## Where the frontier of the enabling technology actually is

Worth recording, because it bounds what any future 3D attempt would be building on:

- **Rigorous forward-in-time integration of PDEs** is demonstrated in **one** spatial
  dimension as of January 2026 (van den Berg–Breden, arXiv:2601.05146: Swift–Hohenberg,
  Ohta–Kawasaki, Kuramoto–Sivashinsky).
- The high-dimensional CAP literature reaches higher dimensions by *"generat[ing] sharp
  **one-dimensional** analytic estimates which are then used to produce high-dimensional
  estimates"*; the direct tensor-product route is *"deemed to be practically infeasible
  beyond a relatively small number of dimensions (three or four)"* — the curse of
  dimensionality, in the CAP community's own words, not this repository's inference.
- Even the one genuinely-3D certificate (Ohta–Kawasaki) buys its 3D-ness with space-group
  symmetry, for *"an enormous reduction in computational cost."*

## What it informs (never determines)

`DIRECTION.md`'s Status note wonders whether a future leg on a genuine 3D reduction — the
2D-Boussinesq / axisymmetric-swirl-free-3D-Euler exact correspondence — would be worth
anyone's time. This leg **raises** that route's standing without making it a chain link,
for a specific reason: that correspondence is **precisely the mechanism by which the only
existing 3D-fluid CAP result reaches 3D** (Chen–Hou). It is the field's established and
only demonstrated route, not a dodge or a weakening of the target. Any 3D ambition in this
repository would be building the same shape of thing the field builds.

Equally, the leg supplies the reason **not** to expect more: nobody has certified a
three-spatial-variable time-dependent object, rigorous time integration is at 1D, and the
dimensional obstruction is named by the CAP community itself. Wall 2 stands, sourced.
