### 0.2.1 The test, stated as the predicate I will apply

**W2's 2D-LIFT PREDICATE (as banked).** A certified profile on `ℝ³` (or `T³`) is a **2D LIFT** when,
in the certificate's own Cartesian coordinates:

- (L1) it has **no dependence on one Cartesian coordinate** — zero modes in `x₃`, extent 1 in `x₃`; and
