## §3. Defects of the pre-registration and the runner, recorded, none acted on as a widening
1. **G4's scale was mis-transcribed** in `leg_430_prereg.md`: it normalises `M, J` by `X_p e_b f` at
   the pulse start; the paper normalises the closure rows at the bump centre (A.15). Both are
   reported; the pre-registered answer stands as `NO`.
2. **G5's measure is a 10⁸-fold cancellation**: `∫(H − H_pow)dX` over `∫|H − H_pow|dX` at float64
   bottoms out near `10⁻⁶`; the identity holds in Lemma A.8's equivalent form to `10⁻¹⁵`. The
   pre-registered `10⁻⁶` is not widened; the answer is `NO` with this diagnosis.
3. **G6's exponent window was an upper bound transcribed as a slope window.** (A.14) says
   `e_b ≤ C_pre λ^{30}`; the schedule gives `e_b ∝ λ^{30 + 60λ}`, whose log-slope between the
   pre-registered `λ` is 23.0 and 25.0 — **the measurement matches the schedule's own exponent to
   0.04** and satisfies the paper's inequality (`λ^{60λ} < 1`), but lies outside `[30, 36]`, so the
   sub-gate is `NO`. `gates()` omits this cross-`λ` check (a per-run function cannot make it); it is
   computed in `arc6_profile_evidence.py`.
4. **Two planted controls could not fire.** **C5** (drop the (A.11) bumps): the `30 log(1/λ)` hold
   relaxes `I/(XH)` to `1/(1−λ)` to `6·10⁻¹⁴` before the bumps act, so their fitted coefficients are
   `≈ 10⁻¹⁴` and dropping them changes nothing measurable — **DID NOT FIRE**. **C6** (`R_0` cut at 5):
   the `e^{−2ξ}` weight makes `ξ ∈ [5, 10]` worth `3.2·10⁻⁴` of `K_b` (0.2447 vs 0.2450) — **DID NOT
   FIRE**. Both are recorded as refuted control expectations; `test_arc6_profile_v1.py` asserts the
   measured reason. C1 (flip `λ`: G6, G7, G8 fail) and C3 (drop `c_1, c_2`: G4 fails, `M = 3.9·10⁻³`)
   **fired**.
5. **C2 was not run** (`Amp = 0.5` must fail G3): G3 fails at every pre-registered `λ`, so the control
   has no passing twin to be compared with. Omitted for that reason, recorded as an omission.
6. **The runner was rewritten log-scaled after the first numbers were seen**: the first build
   overflowed float64 beyond ~680 e-folds and underflowed `E` on the hold. No gate or tolerance
   changed; the pre-registered quantities were re-implemented in logs (`log X, log E, log H`,
   normalised moments, a backward ODE for `∫_y^∞ E²/E²`).
7. **Grid deviation:** the pre-registration says 24 Chebyshev points; the runs use 17 (`n_eta = 16`)
   after the rewrite, for runtime. A grid choice, not a tolerance; the `η`-profiles are polynomial
   of degree ≤ 4 in the schedule and the answers above did not move between 9, 13 and 17 points.
8. The pre-registered `λ` set is far from where the paper's closure works; **that distance is the
   finding**, not a reason to move the set.
