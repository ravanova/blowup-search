## §3. CRITERIA — fixed here, applied mechanically

### §3.1 What counts as a "TECHNIQUE" for this gate

A **technique** is a published argument that converts, or claims to convert, a statement about an
**infinite-energy or non-decaying model object** into a statement about a **solution with the decay
/ energy the target requires**, or that supplies the **decay exponent** such a conversion consumes.
It must be:

- **published** and **located by identifier** (arXiv id, DOI, or journal reference);
- carrying at least one **explicitly stated hypothesis** on the object's decay, integrability,
  energy class, propagation, or symmetry, quotable verbatim and locatable by section or page;
- **applicable in shape** to a nonlinear evolution PDE — not a statement purely about a linear
  problem or purely about a numerical scheme.

A paper that is only a *statement of the difficulty* — including this repository's own record — is
**not** a technique and is not entered in the table.

### §3.2 What counts as "THE NAMED HYPOTHESIS THAT FAILS"

For each technique exactly one **`decisive_hypothesis`** is banked: the hypothesis, **quoted
verbatim from the located source**, whose truth-value for **this object** decides whether the
technique can be run against it. Each technique is then banked with exactly one verdict:

- **`FAILS`** — this object provably does **not** satisfy the quoted hypothesis, and the reason is
  a **banked measurement** (an artefact number) or a **banked property of the object** (backward
  DSS, `ℝ³`, `α = 1`, infinite global `L²`). The failing clause is named.
- **`FAILS-BY-CONSTRUCTION`** — the hypothesis is about a **different equation or a different
  direction of time** (forward vs backward self-similar; Euler vs Navier–Stokes; compressible vs
  incompressible), so the technique cannot reach this object without new mathematics that the
  technique itself does not supply. The mismatch is named.
- **`OPEN-FOR-THIS-OBJECT`** — this object's status against the quoted hypothesis is **not
  decidable from anything banked**. This is a **candidate** under pre-committed reading (b) and is
  reported as a lead, never as a broken wall, and it must additionally state **whether leg 381's
  bill is paid** (`α > 1.5` supplied for THIS object) — which, absent a certified exponent, is
  **NO** by default and must be argued, not assumed.
- **`SATISFIED`** — this object **does** satisfy the quoted hypothesis. If this ever fires, the
  technique is a candidate method and the unit says so in the gate's own words.
- **`UNREACHABLE`** / **`THROTTLED`** — the source could not be read at the level required. Banked
  with its reason and a cost. **Never a confirmation, never a zero.**

### §3.3 What would count as PAYING LEG 381's BILL

Pre-committed so it cannot be relaxed later. The bill is paid **only** by all three of:

1. a **certified** (not fitted, not a-priori-only) two-sided enclosure `[α_lo, α_hi]` for the far
   field of **route 4's own profile**, with
2. **`α_lo > 1.5`** — the artefact's `L2_threshold_alpha`, so that the discarded tail is finite in
   the energy norm and the critical `L³` tail is not log-divergent; and
3. a cutoff whose introduced perturbation is controlled **in a scaling-invariant norm**, not merely
   shown to vanish as `ρ → ∞` in a subcritical one — because the artefact measures every
   subcritical residual shrinking while the critical tail does not.

**Anything less is `NOT PAID`, and a technique that pays it for a different object has not paid it
for this one.**
