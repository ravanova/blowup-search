## §3. WHAT MAKES MY IMPLEMENTATION INDEPENDENT — pre-committed, and I will be held to it

I have READ `experiments/p2_route_l6_v1.py` in full (I cannot check what I have not read).

1. **Representation of the differential operator.** `L6` works in vector-spherical-harmonic
   radial components (`_vsh_V`, `_vsh_w`, `_vsh_lap_w`), with hand-derived scalar radial
   recursions per component and a pseudo-spectral tangential-gradient matrix `Dop` built by
   analysis/synthesis against `Y_lm`. **I work in CARTESIAN coordinates on real solid

   which matters, because `L6`'s own `tail_cumfrac` puts 0.32% of the integrand's mass
   beyond `r = 1000`.
4. **Angular quadrature.** `L6`: tensor product of Gauss–Legendre in `cos θ` (`2Lmax+8`)

5. **`s` quadrature and the branch normalisations.** `L6`: uniform trapezoid on the period

is refuted by measurement.**

Take MY independent operator — my basis, my term algebra, my jets, my curl — and evaluate
it at L6's own quadrature nodes, contracting with L6's own weights. Nothing of my
quadrature survives; nothing of L6's operator survives.

| point | `J` (my operator, L6's rule) vs `J_L6` |
|---|---|
| P1 | rel `1.101e-14` |
| P2 | rel `2.460e-15` |
| P3 | rel `5.897e-15` |
| P4 | rel `1.864e-14` |

**So `L6`'s operator `W[V]` is right, at all four points, to machine precision, as judged by
a genuinely independent second implementation.** That is the thing `V-W6` asked to have
checked, and it checks out.

The entire 43% is the **quadrature rule**. And a difference that lives entirely in the rule,
survives every convergence test of each rule separately (X5; L6's own grid is likewise
internally converged), and shrinks toward the minimiser, is the signature of one thing:

**`J(c)` as written is a DIVERGENT integral for generic `c` in this trial space.** Two
correct programs disagree about it because there is no finite number for them to agree on.

**At `r → ∞`.** `F_lm(r,s) → F_lm(∞,s)` finite (the `α = 1` branch), so `w ~ A(ŷ,s)/r²`.
The DSS term `a(2w + y·∇w)` annihilates a degree `−2` homogeneous `w` **exactly**; `Δw` and
both nonlinear terms are `O(r^{-4})`. But `w_s ~ ∂_s A(ŷ,s)/r²` **survives**. Then
`|W|^{3/2} r² ~ 1/r` and `∫^∞ dr/r` diverges. Branch B cannot escape it: its own
normalisation forces `Σ_lm F_lm(∞,s)²` to average 1, and the `k > 0` `s`-modes make
`∂_s A ≠ 0`.

**At `r → 0`.** The radial basis `u^l T_n(2u−1)` with `u = r/(r+2) = r/2 − r²/4 + …` has a
nonzero `r^{l+1}` Taylor coefficient, and `Δ²(r^{l+1} Y_lm) = −4 l(l+1) r^{l−3} Y_lm`. At
`l = 1` that is `r^{-2}`, so `Δw ~ r^{-2}`, `|W|^{3/2} r² ~ 1/r`, and `∫_0 dr/r` diverges.

**The measurement that settles it — mass per decade of `r` is FLAT.** A flat integrand mass
per decade IS a logarithmic divergence.
- at P3, decades `1e-6 … 1e-3`: `0.199, 0.181, 0.202, 0.185`
- at P4, decades `1e3 … 1e7`: `0.139, 0.156, 0.173, 0.155, 0.170`

**Cutoff sensitivity at the banked B point** (`r_min = 1e-6` fixed): `r_max = 1e3 →
1.6038780729`; `1e4 → 1.6134514480`; `1e5 → 1.6137830344`; `1e6 → 1.6138484012`; `1e8 →
1.6139729157`; `1e11 → 1.6141602832`; `1e14 → 1.6143466387`. A near-constant increment per
decade. It does not converge; it creeps, forever, and the creep rate is the residue.

**L6's grid reach is `nq_r = 72`, `r ∈ [5.502e-4, 7.270e+3]`.** Every finite route-4
residual in the record is a value of that 72-node truncation. Near a minimiser the
divergent tail is small (the optimiser has pushed it down — that is *why* the rel error
shrinks along the rungs), which is exactly why nobody saw it: **the functional is best
behaved precisely where it was always evaluated.**

## §12. WHAT THIS DOES **NOT** SAY

Written out because the temptation to over-read it is large.

1. It does **not** say either program is miscoded. X7, X7b and X9 say the opposite, at

2. It does **not** say the minimum of the truncated functional is a wrong number. `c_mod =
   869.288`, `ρ = 1.613811232`, `ρ = 1.504851895` are all correct values **of the
   72-node-truncated functional**. What is now known is *which* functional they are values
   of.
3. It does **not** move any `L1→L4` link, in either direction. Nothing here is progress.
4. It does **not** say a blow-up profile does or does not exist. A divergent objective is a
   statement about the trial space and the norm as coded, not about Navier–Stokes.
5. It does **not** license repairing `L6`. The brief forbids it and I have not touched
   `experiments/p2_route_l6_v1.py` or any banked artefact.
