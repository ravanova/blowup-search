## §3. WHAT MAKES MY IMPLEMENTATION INDEPENDENT — pre-committed, and I will be held to it

I have READ `experiments/p2_route_l6_v1.py` in full (I cannot check what I have not read).
I import nothing from it, call nothing in it, and copy no line of it into my implementation
module. The five choices below are pre-committed as GENUINELY DIFFERENT, and §7 will state
for each whether I actually kept it.

1. **Representation of the differential operator.** `L6` works in vector-spherical-harmonic
   radial components (`_vsh_V`, `_vsh_w`, `_vsh_lap_w`), with hand-derived scalar radial
   recursions per component and a pseudo-spectral tangential-gradient matrix `Dop` built by
   analysis/synthesis against `Y_lm`. **I work in CARTESIAN coordinates on real solid
   harmonic POLYNOMIALS** `R_lm(y) = r^l Y_lm(ŷ)`, and differentiate by exact symbolic
   term algebra on objects `c · y^α · r^p · G^{(k)}(r)`, using only
   `∂_i(y^α r^p G^{(k)}) = α_i y^{α−e_i} r^p G^{(k)} + p y^{α+e_i} r^{p−2} G^{(k)} +
   y^{α+e_i} r^{p−1} G^{(k+1)}`. No VSH component ever appears in my code. No formula of
   `L6`'s is transcribed: `Δw`, `y·∇w`, `(V·∇)w`, `(w·∇)V` are all produced by the same
   generic `∂_i` above, applied to `V = ∇×∇×(f y) + ∇×(g y)` written as
   `ε_{cij} ∂_i(·) y_j` compositions.
2. **Radial derivatives.** `L6` uses the analytic chain rule through `u(r)` up to
   `d⁴/dr⁴` (`radial_basis`), with hand-written `up1..up4`. **I use CAUCHY'S INTEGRAL
   FORMULA**: the radial functions are analytic, so `G^{(k)}(r) = k! c_k / ρ^k` from the
   FFT of `G` on a circle of radius `ρ` about `r` in the complex plane. Different
   mechanism, different failure modes; a sign or factorial error in `L6`'s chain rule
   cannot be reproduced by mine.
3. **Radial quadrature.** `L6`: Gauss–Legendre in `u = r/(r+2)`, `nq_r = 3Nr+12` nodes,
   which at `J4` places its outermost node near `r ≈ 6.6e3`. **Mine: exp-sinh
   (double-exponential) quadrature in `r` on `(0,∞)`**, nodes spanning `~1e−6` to `~1e8`,
   a different node family, different weights, and a different treatment of the far tail —
   which matters, because `L6`'s own `tail_cumfrac` puts 0.32% of the integrand's mass
   beyond `r = 1000`.
4. **Angular quadrature.** `L6`: tensor product of Gauss–Legendre in `cos θ` (`2Lmax+8`)
   with uniform `φ` (`4Lmax+12`), i.e. a polar grid. **Mine: a CUBE-SPHERE rule** — six
   gnomonic patches, Gauss–Legendre in each of the two equiangular patch coordinates, with
   the exact `(1+X²)(1+Y²)/δ³` Jacobian. Different nodes, no polar axis, no `1/sin θ`
   anywhere.
5. **`s` quadrature and the branch normalisations.** `L6`: uniform trapezoid on the period
   with `4Ks+6` nodes for `J`, and its `N_B` by that same quadrature. **Mine**: a
   half-step-offset uniform rule at a different count for `J` (checked against
   Gauss–Legendre on `[0,T_s]`), and `N_B` computed **exactly in closed form** from Fourier
   orthogonality, with no `s` quadrature at all.
