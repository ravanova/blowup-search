# Leg 203 — Route-RSA: adversarial audit of `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`

**Branch** `leg/203-rsa-v1`. **Route** RSA (exploration, not critical path). **Difficulty**
standard. **Gate answered YES.** **No solver file edited, under either branch.**

## The gate, in its pre-committed wording

> Under adversarial and degenerate inputs, does `rescaled_spectrum.py` ever silently return a
> wrong value rather than reject or visibly propagate the defect?

**YES.** Eight mechanisms, named and measured below. Per the yes-branch: the mechanisms and
magnitudes are named, claim-adjacency was checked *before* deciding, and **the module is not
patched by this leg**.

## What was run

`experiments/p2_route_rsa_v1_adversarial.py` — nine batteries, deterministic (seed 20260806),
25.3 s, no logging, no new gCLM measurement. Every number quoted here is in
`writeup/data/p2_route_rsa_v1_adversarial.json`, and every one is re-asserted as an executable
pin in `test_rescaled_spectrum_adversarial.py` (lesson 68: a check that is not executable
decays at the rate of memory).

**Resolution, and why it is small.** One continuation to `a = 0.5` at `K = 96` costs **371 s**
on this machine, so the batteries run at `K <= 64` and are anchored at `a = 0`, where the fixed
point is exact (`Omega_0 = -sin theta` nulls the residual to 1.1e-16 at every `K`) and the
analytic spectrum is known in closed form to be exactly `{0, -1}`. **That is a stronger truth
reference than a converged solve at a generic `a`, not a weaker one** — every R1/R2/R6
magnitude is a distance from a value known exactly. The natural-failure census (S6) recomputes
nothing; it reads the banked JSONs.

## The normative anchor — the module's own contract

This module implements no published algorithm (the filter, the structural pair, the gauge `(N)`
and the closed-form continuum are this repository's construction), so leg 120's approach of
citing an external standard does not transfer. The anchor is instead the module's **own written
contract**, quoted verbatim, which makes each battery a test of a sentence somebody actually
wrote. `writeup/novelty/leg_203.md` §3 records the four clauses — and records one citation
(Boyd's eigenvalue rule-of-thumb) that I intended to use, **could not verify in session, and
therefore did not cite**. No number here rests on it. Leg 57's BDL episode is why that is
written down rather than quietly dropped.

## The eight findings, magnitudes not booleans

| # | Mechanism | Magnitude | Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | `converged_spectrum` has **no guard on `K_fine` vs `K_coarse`** | `n_kept` 2 → **all K** (24/24, 32/32, 48/48 — **24×** at K=48); every match distance **identically 0.0**; largest retained is a pair at **±40.4623i** | LATENT |
| **R2** | `spectrum`/`converged_spectrum` **discard `newton`'s `converged` flag** | worst silently-accepted iterate sits **2.632e-01** from the exact −1 and puts **44** eigenvalues in the right half plane (max Re **+0.3437**) where the truth has none | **NOT LATENT** |
| **R3** | `continuation` **silently skips the continuation** on a sign-mismatched `da` | at a=0.5, K=48: alpha **+3.00384222** followed vs **−0.98782818** skipped, \|Δalpha\| = **3.991670** — a **sign change** in the far-field exponent | LATENT |
| **R4** | `continuum_defect` has **no admissibility content** | rejects nothing over 20 λ; dropped term reaches **6.320e+32×** the normalising scale while it reports **8.296e-05** — a **36.9-decade** gap | LATENT |
| **R5** | non-integer `K` **silently floor-truncated** | 5/5 (96.9 → 96) | LATENT (R1's reachability path) |
| **R6** | filter's kept-**count** blind to a planted wrong profile | count unmoved to delta **1e-01**, residual **1.801e-01** = **7.3 decades** above the 1e-8 threshold | LATENT |
| **R7** | `match_filter(tol=nan)` keeps 0 silently | reproduces the module's headline conclusion; `tol=inf` keeps all | LATENT |
| **R8** | planted control's **docstring** criterion is wrong | count unchanged (2 vs 2); test 6's actual criterion (position shift, Re **+1.0831**) does hold — **prose only** | none |

**R1 is the sharpest, and it is lesson 90 in the module's own code.** Line 116 states the
specification — *"the discrete eigenvalues are the ones that stop moving under refinement, and
the continuum is the part that never does"* — and `converged_spectrum` never checks that
refinement occurred. At `K_fine == K_coarse` the filter compares a spectrum with itself, every
distance is exactly zero, and the whole discretized continuum is returned as "the
grid-independent part of the spectrum", **including a purely imaginary conjugate pair — exactly
the Hopf-crossing signature this module exists to rule out.** Four identical numbers should read
as a bug, not a finding (lesson 90); here it is 24 of them.

**The control that can come out differently** (lesson 90's own demand): `K_fine < K_coarse` — a
*coarser* "refinement" — still returns 2. So the trap is **equality specifically**, not any
mis-ordering, and the filter is not simply broken.

## What decided the escalation: the exposure census (S6)

**R1, R3, R4, R5, R7 are LATENT** — measured, not assumed. Every `converged_spectrum`-shaped
call site passes `96/144` (`test_rescaled_spectrum.py:132`, `p2_route_e_v1_spectrum.py:158`,
`p2_route_h_v1_critical.py:224`, `critical_dissipation.py:576`); Route-E passes `a >= 0` with
`da = +0.02` and Route-G passes `a < 0` with `da = -0.02`, so **both signs match**; every `tol`
passed anywhere is in `1e-4 .. 1e-1`.

**R2 is NOT latent.** 5/7 banked Route-E `E5_sweep` rows (`a` = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.55;
residuals 6.187e-04 … 1.135e-02) and 7/7 banked Route-G `g4_cross_model` rows (residuals
9.094e-05 … 2.275e-03) were computed at points whose residual exceeds the module's own 1e-8
threshold, and the module hands its consumers no flag.

**Two things cut the other way, and are stated because they do:**

1. **Both routes banked their residuals alongside their values.** The condition is discoverable
   from the JSON and was not hidden. What this leg adds is that the *module* offers no flag, so
   discoverability depended on each caller choosing to record it.
2. **The 1e-8 test is hard-coded and conservative.** The banked `a = 0.5 / K = 96` row is
   flagged `converged=False` at residual 3.319e-08 while its `alpha` is **3.00000002 against an
   exact 3**. So **`converged=False` is not by itself evidence a banked spectrum is wrong, and
   this leg does not claim it is.** The claim is about the missing flag and about which rows are
   affected — the *intermediate* `a`. The two analytic points the module's docstring says
   quantitative statements are quoted at (`a = 0`, alpha=1 and `a = 1/2`, alpha=3) are exactly
   the two `E5` rows that **are** converged, both giving `n_kept = 2`, `n_unstable = 0`.

**The origin-H² spectral gap is NOT downstream** — checked before deciding, as the gate
requires. `solver/origin_h2_certificate.py` (the strict realization, the one *with* the gap,
legs 176/186) imports only `numpy` and `math.comb`. Legs 176/186 cite this module's
**docstring** on the loose realization (`experiments/journal/leg_176.md`:135,
`p2_route_h2c_v1_construction.py`:50/287, `writeup/novelty/leg_177.md`:30); **no banked
origin-H² gap number is computed by any code path in this module.**

**Decision: claim-adjacent via Route-E / Route-G, so ESCALATE.** Branch pushed, `main` not
touched, reported as parked. The module is not patched — the yes-branch forbids it, and R2's
repair is a design choice (propagate the flag, or refuse) that belongs to whoever owns the
module, not to an audit leg.

## The passes, reported as loudly as the defects (leg 91)

- **Non-finite propagation is clean end to end.** 18/18 coefficient poisons (nan/±inf × 3 slots
  × K=16,32) stay visible as nan through `residual`, `c_omega`, `jacobian` and `generator`, then
  hard-stop at `eigvals` with `LinAlgError`; `newton` flags 18/18; a poisoned model parameter
  `a` is flagged 3/3. **Nothing is erased** — the failure mode legs 66/79/120 found in
  `spectral_utils` is absent here.
- One detail worth recording: **`np.linalg.solve` does *not* reject a non-finite matrix**, so
  `generator()` returns all-nan silently; the rejection happens one frame later inside
  `eigvals`. The chain is still honest, but the guard is not where a reader would expect it.
- `K = 0` builds empty operators and `anchor()` raises `IndexError`; negative `K` raises
  (`K=-1` → `IndexError`, `K=-4` → `ValueError` — different doors for the same invalid input,
  leg 120's D7 shape, but both visible); `da = 0` raises `ZeroDivisionError`; an empty fine
  spectrum raises `ValueError`.
- **The positive control is a real control.** With the potential it returns an eigenvalue at
  Re **+1.0831**, shift **1.0831** from anything the plain operator has; with the potential
  removed it does not. **Both outcomes occur.** (Discovering *that* is what exposed R8: my first
  pass used the docstring's count criterion, got "no gain" on both rows, and would have shipped
  a control that never came out differently — lesson 90, nearly self-inflicted. The criterion
  was corrected to test 6's, which discriminates.)
- **On valid input the module is exact.** At K = 16, 48, 96 the `a = 0` anchor still nulls the
  residual to <1e-14 with `c_omega = -1` and reproduces `{0, -1}` to <1e-12. **Every pin is
  about degenerate input, not about the module's arithmetic.**

## Ceiling, and what this is not

**No link of the L1→L4 chain moved, and nothing here touches one.** Every number is a statement
about *this repository's code*, not about gCLM, DSS, or blow-up. No new gCLM measurement was run
(banned, leg 42 — the model is exhausted); S6 reads banked JSON rather than recomputing. The DSS
re-ask stays banned and nothing here reopens it. Plain float64 throughout; **nothing is
interval-enclosed and nothing here is rigorous.**

This leg does **not** claim any banked number is wrong. R2's census is a statement about which
rows sit on non-fixed points and about a missing flag — the sign and size of any resulting error
in those rows is **not measured here**, and the one place it could be checked cheaply (a = 0.5)
comes out right to 8 digits.
