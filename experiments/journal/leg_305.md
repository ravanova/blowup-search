## 5. The ledger, defined before it is computed

Every constant is a **named symbol appearing in a displayed BCG equation**, with its locator.
Each is perturbed **one at a time**, multiplicatively, `c → c(1+ε)`, everything else held fixed,
and the window `W(γ) = r*(γ) - 2γ/(γ+1)` is recomputed at `γ = 7/5`.

**Lower endpoint** — from the viscous prefactor exponent `2 - r + \frac1α(1-r)`, `\eqref{eq:main}` l.483:

* `C1  c_lap = 2` — the Laplacian's scaling weight (two spatial derivatives)
* `C2  c_r = 1` — the coefficient of `r` in the exponent
* `C3  c_dens = 1` — the multiplier on `\frac1α(1-r)`, i.e. the power of `S^{1/α}` dividing the Laplacian
* `C4  α = (γ-1)/2` — the ideal-gas exponent

**Upper endpoint** — from `D_{Z,1} = -4 + (1+γ)\frac{r-1}{γ-1} + \mathcal R_2`, `\eqref{eq:k_asquotientDZ1}` l.600, and the `\mathcal R_2` radicand, `\eqref{eq:def_R2}` l.548–550:

* `C6  c_lin = (1+γ)/(γ-1)` — the linear-in-`r` coefficient in `D_{Z,1}`
* `C7  \mathcal R_2` overall scale
* `C8  ` radicand `r²` coefficient `-(3γ-5)((γ-5)γ+2)`
* `C9  ` radicand `r` coefficient `(γ(γ(18γ-52)+50)-8)`

* `C11 ` radicand `\mathcal R_1`-coupled group `\mathcal R_1(9(γ-2)γ + ((2-3γ)γ+5)r + 5)`

**Metrics reported per constant:**

* `M1  dW/dln c` — absolute width change per unit *relative* change in `c`
* `M2  width_credit_1pct = W(1.01c) − W(c)`

**Costliest constant** `:=` the constant with the **smallest `|move_to_close|`** among those with
a finite `move_to_close`. (Ties broken by larger `|M1|`.)

## 14. Sharp or slack, and on what evidence

**SHARP_FOR_BCG_ARGUMENT_AS_STATED.** All eleven constants classify `EXACT_IDENTITY`; the
pre-registered SLACK branch requires at least one `ESTIMATE`, and there is none. Evidence
beyond the classification:

* **The upper endpoint is a discriminant, not an estimate.** `R₁` radicand at `r*` is `3E-59`

## 15. The `r=1` conditioning trap — hit, and diagnosed before believed

Hit exactly where the dispatch warned. Float `R₂` radicand at `r=1` is `1.2212453270876722e-14`
(matching leg 302's reported `1.22e-14`) against `0.000` exactly in 60 digits (BCG l.603), and
float `|k(1)−1| = 1.381374710174299e-07` vs leg 302's `1.29e-07`, failing `1e-9` as
pre-registered. The 60-digit value is `0` exactly. The float number is emitted under
`conditioning_trap`, decides nothing, and is labelled diagnostic everywhere.

**A second, self-inflicted instance, recorded because it is the same failure mode.** T5 first
came back FALSE at `1.0954e-22`. Diagnosis: I was probing `k` at `r = 1 + 1e-45`, and because the
radicand vanishes *linearly* at `r=1`, the square root halves the exponent — so I was measuring
**my own probe offset**, not BCG. Probing at `r = 1` exactly gives `k(1) − 1 = 0` exactly. Had I
reported that `1.0954e-22` as a property of BCG's algebra it would have been precisely the error
the dispatch named. Diagnosed by high-precision re-evaluation before concluding anything.
