## 5. The ledger, defined before it is computed

Every constant is a **named symbol appearing in a displayed BCG equation**, with its locator.
Each is perturbed **one at a time**, multiplicatively, `c → c(1+ε)`, everything else held fixed,
and the window `W(γ) = r*(γ) - 2γ/(γ+1)` is recomputed at `γ = 7/5`.

**Lower endpoint** — from the viscous prefactor exponent `2 - r + \frac1α(1-r)`, `\eqref{eq:main}` l.483:

* `C1  c_lap = 2` — the Laplacian's scaling weight (two spatial derivatives)
* `C2  c_r = 1` — the coefficient of `r` in the exponent
* `C3  c_dens = 1` — the multiplier on `\frac1α(1-r)`, i.e. the power of `S^{1/α}` dividing the Laplacian
* `C4  α = (γ-1)/2` — the ideal-gas exponent

**Upper endpoint** — from `D_{Z,1} = -4 + (1+γ)\frac{r-1}{γ-1} + \mathcal R_2`, `\eqref{eq:k_asquotientDZ1}` l.600, and the `\mathcal R_2` radicand, `\eqref{eq:def_R2}` l.548–550:

* `C5  c_4 = -4` — the constant in `D_{Z,1}`
* `C6  c_lin = (1+γ)/(γ-1)` — the linear-in-`r` coefficient in `D_{Z,1}`
* `C7  \mathcal R_2` overall scale
* `C8  ` radicand `r²` coefficient `-(3γ-5)((γ-5)γ+2)`
* `C9  ` radicand `r` coefficient `(γ(γ(18γ-52)+50)-8)`
* `C10 ` radicand constant `γ((76-27γ)γ-71) + 18`
* `C11 ` radicand `\mathcal R_1`-coupled group `\mathcal R_1(9(γ-2)γ + ((2-3γ)γ+5)r + 5)`

**Metrics reported per constant:**

* `M1  dW/dln c` — absolute width change per unit *relative* change in `c`
* `M2  width_credit_1pct = W(1.01c) − W(c)`
* `M3  move_to_close` — the relative change in `c` for which `W = 1/6`, i.e. the dominance
  window becomes as wide as the target window (deficit → `1×`); `unreachable` if the
  one-parameter family never attains it
* `M4  class ∈ {EXACT_IDENTITY, ESTIMATE}` — argued term by term against BCG's text, not asserted

**Costliest constant** `:=` the constant with the **smallest `|move_to_close|`** among those with
a finite `move_to_close`. (Ties broken by larger `|M1|`.)

## 7. Discrete sub-ledger, also pre-registered

BCG's admissible speeds are **not** an interval: Lemma `\ref{lemma:k}` (l.583) makes
`k : [1, r*) → [1, ∞)` a bijection and the profiles live at `r_n := k^{-1}(n)` for odd `n ≥ 3`.
So the width deficit has a **counting** form:

* `n_lost := ⌊k(7/6)⌋` at `γ = 7/5` — how many of BCG's admissible speeds `r_n` fall at or below
  the dominance threshold and are therefore lost to the non-dominated region.

Computed in 60 digits. **This is where the `r=1` conditioning trap lives** and it is expected.
