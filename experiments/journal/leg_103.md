## Why this is not just re-running leg 92's script

`experiments/p2_route_gla_v1_adversarial.py` and its JSON are frozen as the *pre-repair*
evidence and are leg 92's territory — untouched here. They also cannot simply be
re-executed: the repair converted several cases into `ValueError` and leg 92's families
call the solver bare, so the script aborts at the first one. `experiments/p2_route_glb_v1_postrepair.py`
re-runs **the same 54 cases** — same probe profile, same ladders, same parameters — with a
raise-aware wrapper, classified under **leg 92's own pre-registered definitions**, quoted
rather than restated. Its rule *"a raise counts as FLAGGED, not silent"* is what makes the
comparison well-posed: the count is directly comparable to its 19/54.

No threshold was re-tuned in the direction that flatters the fix. The amplitude family is
scored **twice** — against a freshly measured root-finder floor (leg 92's *procedure*) and
against leg 92's frozen `2.353e-03` (leg 92's *number*) — and the stricter verdict is the
one reported. Both give 0.

**The G1 residual is the floor, not a shrunken defect.** The worst surviving violation,
`2.353088e-06`, is equal to the bisection noise floor leg 92 itself measured
(`2.353087958e-06`) — and it is *constant* across `eps = 1e-300 … 1e-30`, i.e. amplitude
independent, which is precisely the signature of root-finder noise rather than of a
scale-dependent bug. Leg 92's defect did the opposite: it grew monotonically as `eps` shrank
and saturated at exactly 1/3. Checked far past leg 92's own ladder, over **1e-300 …
1e+100**.

**The closed form's own amplitude law is reproduced on both sides.** For `w0 = A sin x`,
`T* = 2/A` exactly; measured to **6.10e-16** worst relative error over 60 decades
(`A = 1e-30 … 1e+30`). G1's defect was never that `T*` depends on amplitude — it genuinely
does — but that below `1e-12` it depended on it the *wrong way*.

corruption in any case**: measured `outcome = "diverged"` with a non-finite payload, i.e.
FLAGGED through the module's documented channel. Recorded at full strength; no ban lifted,
no repair proposed, nothing patched.
