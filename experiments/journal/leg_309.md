## The finding

**H11 breaks the paper**, and it is a theorem-level break, not an arithmetic slip. Theorem 12.1
("Singular solution recovery"), equation (18):

> `u(x,t) = (1/√(T*−t)) · ū(x/√(T*−t))`

is, verbatim, the definition of an **exact backward self-similar solution** of 3D Navier–Stokes
on `ℝ³` (this is stated *before* the torus transfer — the self-similar core is built on the
lifted/meridional `ℝ³`-type domain first). The profile space `𝒳` (H12) forces `ū` to be
smooth, axis-regular, and Gevrey-decaying — i.e. certainly in `L³(ℝ³)` and much stronger classes.
**That is exactly the object Nečas–Růžička–Šverák (Acta Math. 176, 1996) prove must be
trivial** (nontrivial `U ∈ L³(ℝ³)` backward self-similar ⟹ `U ≡ 0`), extended by Tsai (ARMA 143,
1998) to weaker decay/local-energy hypotheses. The manuscript's own certified profile is
nontrivial by construction (leading spectral coefficient `+5.0`, appendix A) — direct
contradiction with a 30-year-old exclusion theorem the manuscript never cites, engages, or
attempts to evade anywhere in its 818 lines. Its own bibliography cites Jia–Šverák on *forward*
self-similar solutions (which do exist) in the spot where the relevant *backward* non-existence
literature would go.

**Nothing in the paper's own arithmetic is where it fails.** Every constant that could be
independently recomputed from the stated definitions checked out: `K = C_rec^map · C_conv ≈
1.0998×10⁴ ≈ 1.1×10⁴` (matches, l.481); `C_rec^map`'s maximizer at `k=2500` reproduces
`2.5652×10⁷` exactly by direct calculus; the NK closure `2δMK ≈ 8.9×10⁻⁵ < 1` matches this
repository's own **prior, independent** recomputation in `solver/target_selection.py::
ns_preprint_closure_audit()`, which checks it in both the printed and the Kantorovich-corrected
forms and finds **both close**. That prior module's own conclusion, reached before this cycle:
*"the arithmetic is not where that manuscript fails … the ansatz is the backward self-similar
one excluded by Necas-Ruzicka-Sverak and Tsai."* This leg reaches the identical verdict
independently, from the pinned primary source with page-image cross-checks the earlier module
did not carry out, plus three additional secondary breaks the earlier module did not enumerate
(H1, H9/torus-persistence, H10) — recorded in the JSON as non-load-bearing corroboration.

**Three secondary, non-load-bearing breaks**, recorded for completeness (JSON
`secondary_independent_breaks_recorded_but_not_load_bearing`): (i) Theorem 2.1 (main) claims
validity for the *entire interval* `ν ∈ (0, ν_c≈0.00582)`, but every certified constant is
computed at the single fixed value `ν=0.005` (Arb log header, l.681) with no continuation
argument across `ν`; (ii) the torus-transfer theorem (§7) only shows the *initial data* `u₀` is
close to the local self-similar core in norm — it never argues the actual time-dependent
periodic IVP continues to blow up, which is a separate, unaddressed question, and is arguably
incoherent on its face since exact self-similar rescaling is incompatible with a fixed periodic
domain as `t↑T*`; (iii) the stated bound `‖∇𝒲_p‖ ≤ 10⁻¹⁷¹⁴` (item (ii) of the torus theorem) is
not obtainable at the paper's own stated 128-bit (~38-digit) interval precision and is
unsupported — corroborating evidence (not proof) that the numerical claims are not the product
of an actual running verification.
