## The read: Chae-Wolf's Theorem 1.3, traced line by line

Chae-Wolf's Theorem 1.3 (v2, `arXiv:1610.09464`) is stated for a genuine `λ`-DSS solution
(period `T = 2 log λ`, `λ ∈ (1, λ*)` fixed) — this is exactly the DSS ansatz, not the SS ansatz —
and concludes `u ≡ 0` under the Type-I bound `|u(x,t)| ≤ C*/√(-t+|x|)`. **This is a genuine
Liouville-type rigidity theorem for DSS solutions.** It is the same theorem Pineau-Vicol restate
as their own "Main result for DSS solutions" (leg 330's `T2`), confirmed here by direct
side-by-side reading rather than trusting PV's own citation of it: PV's Theorem 2 conclusion
("There exists `λ̄ = λ̄(C_{U,0}) > 1`, such that if `1 < λ < λ̄`, then `U ≡ 0`") and Chae-Wolf's
Theorem 1.3 conclusion are the same statement up to notation (`λ*` vs `λ̄`).

**The proof mechanism, read at Section 3 (pdftotext l.~440–680):**

1. Argue by contradiction: suppose the theorem is false, so there is a sequence `λ_j → 1` and
   nontrivial `λ_j`-DSS solutions `u_j` satisfying the uniform Type-I bound.
2. Extract a weak limit `u` (Banach-Alaoglu + Calderón-Zygmund pressure decomposition to control
   compactness of the pressure).
3. **The compactness step that reaches DSS at all**: for every fixed `µ > 1`, using
   `λ_j → 1`, construct `k_j = k(λ_j, µ) ∈ ℕ` with `λ_j^{k_j} → µ`, and show via the discrete
   self-similarity of each `u_j`, Arzelà–Ascoli compactness of the vorticity away from the origin,
   and a five-term triangle-inequality estimate, that `µ²u(µx, µ²t) = u(x,t)` for every `µ > 1`.
   Equivalently: `u_µ − u` is a harmonic vector field, hence (classical Liouville theorem for
   harmonic functions) `u_µ = u` for every `µ`. **This forces `u` to be exactly, continuously,
   self-similar for every scaling factor — the DSS ansatz has degenerated to the SS ansatz in the
   limit.**
4. Verbatim, the paper's own next sentence (l.~669–676): *"u [is] backward self-similar ... In
   particular, u satisfies local energy estimate (3.4). Thus, [we are] in [a] position to apply
   Tsai's result [15, Theorem 2], u [is] identical[ly] zero."* This contradicts the
   uniform-non-triviality lower bound the proof established earlier, closing the argument.

**So Tsai's Theorem 2 (the local-energy-estimates mechanism) is invoked as an unmodified black
box, applied only to the emergent SS limit object — not generalized, re-derived, or extended to
work directly on a genuinely periodic (fixed `λ > 1`) DSS profile.** The mechanism that reaches
DSS solutions at all is the compactness/harmonic-Liouville argument of Chae-Wolf's own Section 3,
which is a DIFFERENT mechanism from Tsai's local-energy-estimates machinery (Tsai's own Section 4,
the `A_p`-weight/CKN-type partial-regularity argument) — it is not a DSS-native version of that
machinery. And this is exactly why the compactness argument needs `λ → 1`: only then can the
`k_j` construction force `u_µ = u` for every `µ`. For a fixed `λ` significantly larger than 1
(this repository's screened regime) there is no sequence `λ_j → 1` to run compactness over, and
the degenerate-to-SS step has no analogue — the mechanism is structurally incapable of reaching
fixed-large-`λ` DSS objects, independent of how tight the non-explicit constant `λ*(C_{U,0})`
could in principle be made.

## Classification against the dispatch's exact ask

The dispatch's gate asks for: *"a theorem that proves Liouville-type rigidity for discretely
self-similar (not just self-similar) weak solutions using a local-energy-estimates mechanism
analogous to Tsai's, or that explicitly extends/generalizes Tsai's Theorem 2 to the DSS ansatz."*

Chae-Wolf's Theorem 1.3 satisfies the **first disjunct** (it proves DSS rigidity, and the route
terminates in Tsai's own local-energy mechanism) but **not the second** (it does not extend
Theorem 2's proof to the DSS ansatz — it eliminates the DSS-ness first, via an unrelated
compactness argument, then applies Theorem 2 unchanged). **This is a class (iii) PARTIAL RESULT**,
not a class (i) clean extension: the reach is real but structurally confined to `λ` near 1 by a
mechanism that has nothing to do with local energy estimates, and that confinement is not a slack
constant that a sharper proof could plausibly widen to "significantly larger than 1" — it is
built into the only step that lets the argument touch DSS at all.

## Reach against this repository's screened object — unchanged, now explained

**DOES NOT REACH.** This is the *same* conclusion leg 330 already banked (adjudicating the
identical theorem via Pineau-Vicol's restatement): the screened object `NS3D-DSS-NONAXI-
LAMBDA-LARGE` is specified with `λ` significantly larger than 1, and Chae-Wolf/PV's theorem only
reaches `λ` forced near 1. Leg 330's clause-by-clause ledger (H1–H6, deciding clause H5) is reused
here without modification — it is the identical theorem under two names, and re-running the
clause-by-clause check against the object would only reproduce leg 330's own table.

**What this leg adds is not a new verdict but a new reason for the same verdict**: the lambda-near-1
confinement is not an artifact of a weak or non-optimized constant `λ*(C_{U,0})` (which one might
hope a future paper could widen) — it is forced by the ONLY mechanism in the literature that
reaches DSS solutions at all (compactness-degeneration-to-SS), and that mechanism has no version
for fixed large `λ`. So "wait for a sharper constant" is not a live hope for this route; a genuine
reach into large-`λ` DSS would require an entirely different mechanism, most plausibly a DSS-native
generalization of Tsai's local-energy machinery (his Section 4, not his Theorem 2's citation) that
works directly on a periodic-in-`s` profile without first collapsing it to a stationary one — and
no such paper exists in the literature searched here.

## Gate verdict

**Class (iii), PARTIAL RESULT.** Chae-Wolf's Theorem 1.3 (`arXiv:1610.09464v2`) is a Liouville-type
DSS rigidity theorem whose proof invokes Tsai's Theorem 2 as its finishing move — the closest thing
in the literature to a "DSS extension" of Tsai's local-energy route — but it reaches DSS only by
degenerating it to SS via an unrelated compactness argument confined to `λ → 1`, not by extending
Theorem 2's own local-energy machinery to the DSS ansatz directly. It does not reach this
repository's screened object (same conclusion as leg 330, now mechanistically explained). No paper
extending Tsai's Theorem 2 *directly* to a fixed-`λ`-large DSS profile was located, and the
controlled searches give no positive reason to think one exists, though the searches are scoped
(arXiv abstract field; Semantic Scholar refused).
