## §4. WHY (a3) FAILS **ANYWAY** — the counterfactual, and the finding with reach

### 4.3 What §4 does and does **not** establish

**Establishes, for this object:** the manuscript's mechanism applied to a cut `α = 1` profile
produces a force that is **not bounded** and whose time derivative **diverges like `τ^{-1}`**. Such
an `f` is not in `C∞_c(ℝ³×(0,∞))` and does not satisfy Fefferman **(5)**.

**Does NOT establish:** that **every** `α = 1` profile does this. The logarithm here traces to
`arcsinh` in the closed form of `G4`, which is specific to this witness's algebraic vorticity
`S(r) = (1+r²)^{-3/2}`. **Its universality is open and this unit does not claim it.**

**What IS structural, and is the useful part.** Because `α = 1` means `|u| ~ 1/|x|` in the
transition annulus **independently of `τ`**, the cut field's annulus values have a `τ`-independent
leading term, and **the entire admissibility question there reduces to the `τ`-dependence of the
SUBLEADING far-field term** — i.e. to `δ`, and to whether it carries a log.

**That reduction turns "does the mechanism port?" into "what is the far-field expansion of the
profile?", which is a question about the profile and is measurable.** `M4` measures it here for the
first time in this repository: **`δ = 1.974126`** (ray `(1,0,0)`: `1.961633`; ray `(1,1,1)`:
`1.986619`). **The shortfall below `2` and the ray-to-ray spread are themselves the log's signature
under a power fit.**
