`Y_0, Z_1, Z_2, ‖A‖` **all positive and all finite**, and converts the honest *no root* into

constants negative?" would pass it. This is the strongest available statement of why the
guard belongs at the weight and not at the returned value, and it is this leg's own finding,
not a re-quote.

```
np.all(np.isfinite(w) & (w > 0))          # confirm-good, not detect-bad
```

each clause measured necessary. Confirm-good polarity is SEI CERT NUM07-J and leg 152's
one-token flip: ordered comparisons all return false on NaN, so `any(w <= 0)` fails **open**
on NaN while `all(w > 0)` fails **closed**.

**Two corrections to leg 198, both to its diagnostics and neither to its verdict:**

1. **"Zero weights are REJECTED (`ZeroDivisionError`)" is path-specific.** Inside
   `induced_sup_norm` the division is a NumPy array op returning `+inf` with a
   `RuntimeWarning`; the `ZeroDivisionError` leg 198 saw comes from
   `certificate_constants`'s *scalar Python* `Xmax/w_l` and `1.0/w_r`. A guard written to
   leg 198's literal text would have left the `w = 0` seminorm reachable through every
   direct `induced_sup_norm` call. Leg 198's framing — "the module already halts at the
   boundary of the admissible set and walks straight through it just past" — is true of the
   `certificate_constants` path and false of the mechanism site.
2. **Its two `Z_1` ratios do not reproduce; its `‖A‖` and `Z_2` columns reproduce exactly.**
   `‖A‖` 6.6712e+09× and 6.0663e+10× and `Z_2` +1.188608e+17 / −1.779456e+07 match leg 198
   to 4–6 significant figures. `Z_1` is **2.2867e+08×** here against its 1.198e+09 (5.24×
   apart) and **1.5048e+12×** against its 2.651e+12 (1.76×). The mechanism is identifiable:
   `Z_1 = ‖I − A·DF‖` with `A = inv(DF)` in float64 is a near-total cancellation whose
   honest and flipped values (`5.518e−09`, `5.099e−15`) both sit at the round-off floor, so
   the **ratio** is environment-dependent while the **verdict** is not. Standing-discipline
   lesson 86, applied to the finding leg's own headline column. It is also precisely why the
   differential must be same-process and bytewise.

This is the **fifth consecutive repair leg** whose finding leg's prescribed predicate did not
