## Finding 1 — the root is LOCKED TO THE MESH, and the law was predicted in advance

The pre-registration (§5) fixed, as numbers, before this code existed:

> `|nu(n)| = c_l (2h)^2 = 128/(n-1)^2`, i.e. **`nu(1601) = -5.000e-05`** and
> **`nu(3201) = -1.250e-05`**; amplitude `= 87.8/(n-1)`, i.e. **0.05557** and **0.02778**.
> Scored as PASS iff observed/predicted ∈ [0.80, 1.25] (P1) and [0.85, 1.18] (P2).

Observed at Chen's `a`:

| n | `nu` predicted | `nu` observed | ratio | amp predicted | amp observed | ratio | `ell/h` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 401 | -8.000e-04 | **-8.292691e-04** | 1.037 | 0.22226 | 0.22603 | 1.017 | 2.036 |
| 801 | -2.000e-04 | **-2.024637e-04** | 1.012 | 0.11113 | 0.10921 | 0.983 | 2.012 |

**Both advance predictions land inside their pre-registered windows**, on `nu` and on amplitude.
The law also holds at `a = 0.45` (`nu(3201) = -1.239014e-05`, ratio 0.991) and — the sharpest
version — **across the truncation ladder**, where changing `rho_max` changes `h` at fixed `n`:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 0.004375 | -3.828e-05 | **-3.779832e-05** | 0.987 | 1.987 |

| 9 | 0.005625 | -6.328e-05 | -7.374292e-05 | 1.165 | 2.159 |

`nu` follows the **mesh spacing**, not the domain. Enlarging the box by 7.4× in `X_max`
(274 → 2026) moves `nu` by 95%, in the direction `h` moves it.

**So the naive ladder does "converge" — to `nu = 0`, at rate `h^2`.** That is exactly the
coincidental plateau lesson 84 warns about, and clause K1 was written before the run precisely
to refuse it: a genuine continuum length has `ell/h` doubling, a mesh-locked one has it pinned.
It is pinned at 2.0.

**Scoring honesty, stated plainly.** The runner prints **"H1 REFUTED ON ITS OWN POINT
PREDICTIONS (0/4)"**, and that flag is *wrong about the magnitudes and right about the rule*.
P1/P2 as pre-registered required the advance rungs to ALSO meet K2's residual bar; they do not
(relres 5.31e-03 at n = 1601, 1.25e-07 at n = 3201), so the boolean short-circuits to False
before the ratios are consulted. **The ratios themselves — 1.110, 0.990, 1.016, 0.957 — are all
inside the windows.** I am not rewriting the rule after the fact: the flag stays False in the
artifact. What I am recording is that the sub-condition which fails it is *itself a consequence
of the hypothesis being right* — the residual cannot reach 1e-10 there because the system is
singular (Finding 2), which is what H1 says. The magnitudes confirm H1; the pre-registered
boolean does not, because I wrote a conjunction I should have written as two clauses.

## Finding 2 — the augmented Jacobian is SINGULAR TO WORKING PRECISION at `a = 1/2`

Smallest over largest singular value of the odd-reduced, `nu`-bordered Jacobian **at the root
the solver found**:

| n | `a = 0.30` control | `a = 1/2` |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | 3.404e-04 | **7.745e-13** |
| 801 | 8.206e-05 | **7.352e-16** |
| 1601 | 2.009e-05 | **3.454e-19** |

At Chen's `a` it falls by **2.24e+06×** across the ladder and is **below machine epsilon from
n = 801 onward**. The control drifts by 16.9× — a trivial `O(h^-2)` growth in `sigma_max`,
since `||D2|| ~ h^-2`. A system that singular has no isolated root; it has a numerically
indistinguishable family, and every member passes a residual test.

The near-null direction is **the origin layer**: its mass inside `|X| <= 4 ell` is
**0.9973, 0.9994, 0.9997** at `a = 1/2` against 0.623, 0.611, 0.603 at the control.

**P3 as pre-registered SCORES FALSE, and I am reporting why rather than moving the line.** I
required (i) ≥4× decay at Chen's `a` — observed 2.24e+06×, passes hugely; (ii) <2× drift at the
control — observed 16.9×, **fails**; (iii) localisation <0.5 at the control — observed 0.603,
**fails**. Clauses (ii) and (iii) were badly chosen: `sigma_min/sigma_max` carries an
`O(h^-2)` trend for *any* root, and `4 ell` at `a = 0.30` is 0.19 — a window that covers Chen's
profile's own width `b = 0.61`-scale core, so of course most of the vector is in it. The
**discriminating** magnitude, which I flag as post-hoc, is the mass inside `4h` (the mesh
scale): **0.383, 0.388, 0.436 at `a = 1/2` versus 2.5e-08, 1.1e-06, 2.3e-08 at the control** —
seven orders of magnitude apart, and *increasing* with refinement on the negative side.

## Finding 3 — `nu(1/2)` is not determined even at floating-point associativity (V6, post hoc)

**Not pre-registered.** Found while cross-checking this leg's operators against leg 210's
parked code, when the two produced identical `nu` at `a = 0.30` and different `nu` at `a = 1/2`.
The operator matrices were then compared entry by entry: `D`, `D2`, `H`, `VH` all agree to
**0.000e+00**, and the gauge is identical. The **only** difference is that leg 210 wrote
`- c_l X Om_X - a U Om_X` and this leg first wrote `- (c_l X + a U) Om_X` — the same expression
in exact arithmetic, ~1e-15 apart in float64. Both orderings are now run on the whole ladder:

| n | `a = 0.30`: relative difference | `a = 1/2`: relative difference |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | 5.784e-16 | 9.861e-11 |
| 801 | 1.967e-14 | **1.913e-01** (both converged, relres 2.9e-15 / 5.2e-12) |
| 1601 | 6.768e-14 | 2.477e-02 |

**At `a = 1/2`, `n = 801`, reordering two floating-point additions moves `nu` by 19.1% — from
-2.024637e-04 to -2.503447e-04 — with both solves converged.** The control's worst is
**6.77e-14**. That is a discrimination ratio of **2.8e+12**, and it is a stronger statement than
non-convergence: no amount of grid refinement can fix a quantity whose value moves when the
compiler-level association of a sum changes.

**A negative that I went looking for and did not find (V7).** Having seen my own stagnation
break change a reported `nu`, I built a stopping-rule probe: solve at iteration caps
{15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90} and measure the spread. **Result: 0.0 at `a = 1/2` and 0.0 at the
control.** The effect did not reproduce; the earlier discrepancy is attributable to a
configuration (cap 80, no stagnation break) that no longer exists. The probe is also weaker than
intended, because once the stagnation break fires the larger caps are inoperative. Reported as
the null it is.

## Finding 4 — the branch DOES NOT REACH `a = 1/2`. It folds, and that re-pins `a*`

The decisive methodological change, pre-registered in §3. Legs 185 and 210 both restarted Newton
from the Chen profile at every `(n, a)`, so which root they found was a basin accident. This leg
continues the **converged, independently confirmed `a = 0.30` root** by pseudo-arclength —
unknowns `(Omega_odd, nu, a)`, so folds in `a` are traversable rather than mistaken for failure.

**On all four grids the branch turns around before Chen's `a`. None reaches `a = 1/2`.**

| n | steps | `a_max` reached | fold bracket | fold estimate | `nu` at fold | amplitude at fold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 401 | 60 | 0.39259 | [0.38771, 0.39259] | 0.39259 | -6.787e-04 | 1.4836 |
| 801 | 57 | 0.38626 | [0.38122, 0.38521] | 0.38521 | -1.763e-04 | 1.5020 |
| 1601 | 57 | 0.38592 | [0.37988, 0.38387] | 0.38387 | -4.475e-05 | 1.5059 |
| 3201 | 32 | **0.38568** | [0.38010, 0.38410] | 0.38410 | -1.114e-05 | 1.5057 |

`a_max` converges: **0.39259 → 0.38626 → 0.38592 → 0.38568**, and the amplitude at the fold is
**stable at ~1.506** — this is a real branch turning point, not a collapse. **P5 PASSES**: the
fold-location spread across the ladder is **0.008719**, inside the pre-registered 0.02.

**The re-pin.** Leg 125's `a* = 0.3864963972206034` came from a completely different computation
(the `Delta(a)` sweep at `nu = 0`). This leg's `a_max` ladder brackets it and converges to
**0.3857 ± 0.004**, offset **+0.0024** from the n = 3201 fold estimate. **Leg 210 could not
reproduce `a*` at all** — its sign-change bracket was `[0.36, 0.37]`, which excludes leg 125's
value. This leg reproduces it to ~2e-3, by using an instrument that can see what the object
actually is.

**And that changes what `a*` MEANS.** It is not a zero-crossing of `nu(a)` along a branch that
continues to Chen's `a`. It is a **turning point**: the Object-B branch **ceases to exist** as
`a` increases past it. So the question "what is `nu` at `a = 1/2` on this branch" has no answer
because there is no branch there — which is a different and stronger statement than "the
computation does not converge". The `nu = 0` crossing bracket is reported as `None` on every
grid, because no two consecutive K1-and-K2-passing continuation points straddle zero.

**Corroboration from the dilation covariance (P4).** Seeded `kappa^2` recovery error:

| n | `a = 0.30` | `a = 1/2` |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | **3.184e-05** (reproduces leg 210's 3.184e-05 exactly) | 6.008e-04 |
| 801 | 8.429e-06 | 3.650e-02 |
| 1601 | 7.351e-06 | 4.976e-02 |

At the control the error **falls** under refinement; at Chen's `a` it **rises**, to 6.8e+03×
the control at n = 1601. A root whose length scale is set by the equation returns to its own
dilation image; one set by the mesh cannot, because the mesh does not dilate. (P4 scores False
only because its `a = 1/2` minimum, 6.008e-04 at n = 401, sits under the 1e-2 floor I
pre-registered; the two finer rungs are 3.7e-02 and 5.0e-02.)

## Two defects in this leg's own instruments, found and recorded

**(1) The continuation corrector had an unreachable tolerance (lesson 67).** It required
`rms(R) < 1e-13` **absolute**. The n = 1601 grid's own residual floor is **2.39e-13** — the
`a = 0.30` ladder solve reports exactly that — so the test could never be satisfied there. Tier
C died at step 0 after **348 s** with `ds` halved to **9.14e-10** and reported
`corrector_failed`, i.e. a branch failure that was a property of the tolerance and not of the
branch. `n = 801` passed only because its floor happens to sit below 1e-13. Repaired to a
**relative** test (`rms/scale < 1e-11`, or `rms < 1e-13`), matching what `solve_nu` itself calls
converged, and tier C re-run on the whole ladder. **A convergence test a grid cannot satisfy
manufactures a negative result** — the same failure mode this repository keeps finding in its
own instruments, here in mine.

**(2) The repair changed a headline number, and that is the reason it mattered.** Before the
repair, the n = 401 continuation appeared to sail through to `a = 0.5518` with
`nu(1/2) = -1.2704e-03` and **no fold**. With the corrector fixed and the first predictor step
capped, n = 401 **folds at 0.39259 like every other grid**. The earlier "branch reaches Chen's
`a`" was the continuation taking one oversized step across the turning point on the one grid
coarse enough to hide it. Had I stopped at the first version, this leg would have banked a
`nu(1/2)` on the branch that does not exist.

**Numerical settings declared, not hidden.** BLAS is pinned to one thread — this machine spends
**1.27 s** on a 200×200 `solve` that takes **0.0013 s** single-threaded (a 1000× thread-thrash
penalty at load ~50), and single-threading also makes every number here bit-reproducible, which
Finding 3 requires. A Newton **stagnation break** (10 iterations without a 10% residual
improvement) was added for cost; it cannot fire on a solve that converges, and every solve it
stops is reported with its non-converged residual.
