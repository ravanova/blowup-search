### §0.3 VERDICT RULES — fixed here, applied mechanically, never adjusted after reading

For each paper exactly one verdict is banked.

* **`confirm`** — the full text was obtained, and it contains no statement contradicting or
  materially narrowing leg 348's recorded classification of that paper. **CONSISTENCY IS NOT
  STRENGTHENING** (pre-committed reading (b)): a full text that merely fails to contradict the
  abstract is `confirm`, and this is the default landing place for a reachable paper. A `confirm`
  still requires a deciding sentence quoted verbatim and located; absent one, the paper is
  `UNREACHABLE`, never `confirm`.
* **`strengthen`** — the full text was obtained, contains nothing contradicting leg 348, **and**
  supplies at least one of the following that the abstract did **not**:
  * **S1** — an explicit statement that the viscous / dissipative term is *inside* the certified
    equation (not merely present in the model being discussed);
  * **S2** — an explicit dimensionality of the certified object;
  * **S3** — an explicit statement of what the computer-assisted part covers;
  * **S4** — an explicit statement of the domain and boundary conditions the abstract did not carry.
  The specific S-code(s) fired are banked per paper. **S-codes are checked against the ABSTRACT
  TEXT ITSELF, fetched in the same run**, so "the abstract did not say it" is measured, not
  remembered.
* **`UNDERCUT`** — the full text was obtained and contains a statement that **contradicts,
  materially weakens, or narrows** leg 348's recorded classification, or the aggregate claim of
  §0.2 as it rests on that paper. Pre-registered, non-exhaustive triggers, each with the paper it
  would bite:
  * **U1** — the paper's domain is *generalized beyond compact/periodic in the body* (e.g. an
    unbounded-domain section, an `ℝ^n` theorem, a whole-space variant). Bites the domain census and
    therefore `obstruction_named` directly. Any paper.
  * **U2** — the certified equation does **not** contain the viscous/dissipative term (it is
    dropped, projected away, or the certified object is for a truncated/inviscid surrogate). Bites
    P3 and W2's crack hardest.
  * **U3** — the certified object is not what leg 348 recorded: e.g. P3's object is not a genuine
    periodic orbit, or is a lift/2D reduction carried up, or the "3D" is a symmetry-reduced ODE.
    Bites P3 and W2.
  * **U4** — the computer-assisted part does not cover what leg 348 asserted (e.g. the CAP covers
    only a sub-step, or the existence proof has a non-rigorous ingredient).
  * **U5** — the apparatus is misattributed: e.g. P1's method is not Galerkin-plus-tail as
    described, or P3's is a radii-polynomial/`Y₀ Z₀ Z₁ Z₂` contraction that leg 348's own
    apparatus-distinction language mis-sorts.
  * **U6** — the paper's own text asserts the technology *has* been applied to an unbounded domain
    / algebraic weight for a time-periodic target (in itself or in a cited work it characterises),
    which is the direct negation of `obstruction_named`.
* **`UNREACHABLE`** — the full text was not obtained, or was obtained in a form that could not be
  read. **`UNREACHABLE` IS NOT A ZERO AND IS NOT A CONFIRMATION** (pre-committed reading (c)). The
  reason, the URL or identifier tried, and the HTTP status / parse outcome are banked.
* A query or fetch that fails to reach the service is banked **`THROTTLED`** or **`FAILED`**,
  **never as a zero** (pre-committed reading (e); leg 387's fabricated zero).

### §0.4 CONTROLS — they can fire in BOTH directions, and a control that cannot fail is not a control

Ten controls, pre-registered here, all executed by `experiments/p2_route_t6_v1_evidence.py` and
gated **by exit code**, never by a printed line (lesson 68).

* **K1 — instrument-reached-the-service positive datum, every fetch.** Every full-text record banks

  arXiv-API abstract fetches additionally bank the **served opensearch namespace verbatim**. **No

  over-eager pass on a stub is refused.

### §1.2 UNDERCUT 1 — `arXiv:1902.00384`, trigger U3

Three sentences from §1, p.3, all verbatim and all machine-re-found on that page:

> "While all of the analysis is performed in full generality on the 3-torus, the solutions we present
> in Theorem 1.1 below are two-dimensional (in space) time-periodic solutions."

> "Indeed, they are homogeneous in one spatial variable and can thus be interpreted as solutions on
> the 2-torus."

> "they are independent of x3 and the third component of the velocity vanishes. We call such a
> solution an (essentially) 2D solution."

`WALLS.md`'s W2 sets a pre-committed test — "name the certified equation, show the viscous term is
inside it, and **show the certified object is not a lift of a lower-dimensional one**." The paper
answers the third clause itself, against the crack: `u₃ ≡ 0` and `∂/∂x₃ ≡ 0` on the certified
solution. This is the precise failure mode W2 exists to name — *the 3D-ness comes from somewhere
other than the certificate* — and it is **invisible from the abstract**, which says only "on the
three-torus". Leg 348 could not have caught it at abstract level. That is exactly what leg 348's own
§6 ceiling said might happen, and it is why the Conductor ordered this unit run early.

**What is NOT undercut, kept explicit so the undercut is not overclaimed.** The viscous term *is*
inside the certified equation: eq. (1.1) carries `−ν∆u`, the zero-finding problem is built from it,
and the tail of the approximate inverse is diagonal with `λₙ = 1/(ν ñ² + iΩ̄n)` — viscosity is what
makes that tail bounded. Leg 348's parenthetical "(viscous term inside the certified equation)"
**stands**. The domain really is `𝕋³`. **The undercut is on three-dimensionality, and on nothing
else.**

**The reason is a price, not a theorem.** §1, p.3: *"The only reason for this reduction is that the
physical memory requirements [for a three-dimensional solution are, for now, prohibitive in our
current implementation]."* The analysis is built in full generality on `𝕋³`; what is missing is
memory. A price is a number, and this leg does **not** claim the 3D case is impossible.

**Second finding, material to Ruling C1 and to `T4`.** Theorem 2.15 (§2.4, **p.13**) is literally a
`Y₀ / Z₀ / Z₁ / Z₂` radii-polynomial contraction, with `Z₀ ≥ ‖I − AA†‖` requiring a **bounded
approximate inverse `A`**. C1's own words: *"A unit that reaches for a `Y₀/Z₀/Z₁/Z₂` contraction, in
any space, is inside the ban whatever it calls itself."* So the paper's apparatus is the banned one,
not the Zgliczyński-style Galerkin-plus-tail dynamical closure C1 placed outside the ban. This
**confirms** leg 348's own apparatus sorting ("the same NK-in-exponentially-decaying-coefficient-basis
family already measured DEAD in this repository"), and it is a fact `T4` needs, since `T4` is
chartered to reproduce this paper under a C1-compliant apparatus.

### §1.3 UNDERCUT 2 — `arXiv:2409.09234`, triggers U4 and U1

> "our results, depending on numerical approximations, do not guarantee that the Navier-Stokes
> solutions exhibit chaotic behaviour in the sense of Devaney" — §5 Conclusion, **p.9**

> "the theorems hinge upon an imperative condition: the map must be one-dimensional and continuous"

The rigorous content is a Li–Yorke / Sharkovskii statement about a **1-D return map fitted to DNS

*"Periodicity is enforced to the rest of boundaries of the parallelogram domain"*, with **no-slip
rotating-wall** conditions at `r = rᵢ, r_o`.

Leg 348's per-paper `method` field is accurate. What breaks is the **aggregate**: leg 348's §3 counts
this paper among the instances of the technology that "closes its tail-domination (neglected-mode)
estimate against a COMPACT domain". **It closes no tail-domination estimate at all.** The
`domain_census` over-counts by one.

**The obstruction is not thereby refuted.** No counter-instance — a tail-domination estimate closed
against an algebraic far field for a time-periodic target in >1D — was found at full text either. The
statement in `obstruction_named` still stands; its **evidence base is thinner than the record says**.
