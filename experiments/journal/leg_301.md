**Answer: YES.** The named fourth space is the **Malmquist–Takenaka / Christov rational
orthonormal basis of the line**, `φₙ(x) = iⁿ√(2/π)(1+2ix)ⁿ/(1−2ix)^{n+1}`, `n ∈ ℤ`, with its
Hardy splitting. The structural arguments, one per mechanism, are in
`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEFSB_V1.md` §4 and rest on computed quantities, not hopes: the Hilbert
transform is exactly diagonal (`∓i`), the differentiation matrix's diagonal is `i(2n+1)` with
`lmin = 1.0` and linear growth (M1); both operators are exact in closed form on the basis, so
the `(H,D)` defect is identically zero rather than convergent (M2); completeness and the Hardy
splitting reference no profile, and the uniform bound `|φₙ(x)| = √(2/π)/√(1+4x²)` puts algebraic
decay in the basis functions rather than in a weight (M3).

Per the yes-branch, the scoping-leg spec is written (`TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEFSB_V1.md` §8,
proposed as **Route-MT v1**, with a gate that can come back NO and a stage S3 on the
nonlinearity that is capable of killing it), and this leg **ESCALATES**.

Counterweights are stated at full strength in §5 of the TECHNICAL and are not buried: `δ = 1`
exactly (against BDL's `< 1/2`, the same wall leg 158 reached at 1.0039, scored as not-a-death
only because leg 62's test 14 refuted `δ` as the coordinate at `µ = 0.25`, `δ = 2`, still
boundedly invertible, `K`-exponent −0.849); no validated MT transform exists anywhere, so it
must be built; and the nonlinearity is entirely UNMEASURED by this screen.
