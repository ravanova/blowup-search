## Method

Two independent, cross-checked instruments (full detail and counts in
`writeup/novelty/leg_293.md`):

1. A file-existence census: `ls experiments/journal/leg_*.md` filtered to `N >= 102`, sorted
   — **206** files, legs 102 through 364 (57 numbers in that integer range have no journal
   file at all, and are outside this gate's scope — never dispatched, parked on an unmerged
   branch, or, for leg 293 itself, this leg in progress).
2. A mention census: every occurrence of `leg[s]? N` (`100 <= N <= 400`, case-insensitive,
   word-boundary) anywhere in `experiments/JOURNAL.md`, by line, so incidental prose mentions
   count as coverage and not just formal `**Leg N**` bullets.

Cross-referencing (1) against (2) identified the 63 orphans. Nothing was re-derived — every
verdict appended to the journal is read directly out of that leg's own landed
`experiments/journal/leg_N.md` file (quoted verbatim or paraphrased to one line, never
re-computed).
