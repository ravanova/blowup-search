## 1. Identifying the exact solver call (dispatch requirement)

`solver/rescaled_spectrum.py:333`, inside `spectrum()`:

    ev = np.linalg.eigvals(flow.generator(out["b"]))

`flow.generator(b) = np.linalg.solve(S, jacobian(b))` — a dense, real, `K x K` matrix, general
(non-symmetric, non-Hermitian, non-normal — confirmed non-normal directly by leg 367's own
skew-symmetry-defect measurements, all far from zero). Read `numpy.linalg.eigvals`'s own
docstring directly in this environment (numpy 2.5.1): "implemented using `_geev` LAPACK
routines" — i.e. LAPACK's `dgeev`, the standard Hessenberg-reduction-plus-implicit-shift-QR
routine for the real general eigenproblem (Golub & Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*, 4th ed.,
Ch. 7; original backward-stability source: Wilkinson, *The Algebraic Eigenvalue Problem*).
Critically, this is `dgeev`, **not** `dgeevx` — the driver `dgeevx` additionally computes and
returns the reciprocal eigenvalue condition numbers (`RCONDE`, denoted `s_i` below) that LAPACK's
own error-bound theorems require as an input. The solver's call site does not request them.

## 2. The literature bound (fetched live, quoted, not from memory)

Fetched `www.netlib.org/lapack/lug/node91.html`, `node92.html`, `node93.html` (LAPACK Users'
Guide, Anderson et al., 3rd ed., "Further Details: Error Bounds for the Nonsymmetric
Eigenproblem") directly rather than relying on a remembered paraphrase. Extracted the equation
images' ALT text verbatim (these are the actual formulas the page renders as GIFs):

- Backward stability (all algorithms for the nonsymmetric eigenproblem): `||E|| <= p(n) eps
  ||A||`, `p(n)` a "modestly growing function of n" (the guide gives no closed polynomial form —
  this is honestly its own posture, not a gap this leg introduced).
- **Table 4.5 (asymptotic bounds)**, simple eigenvalue: `|lambda_hat_i - lambda_i| <~ ||E||_2 /
  s_i`. Quote: "true for sufficiently small `||E||`, which is why it is called asymptotic."
- **Table 4.6 (global/guaranteed bounds)**, eigenvalue cluster (a simple eigenvalue is the
  size-1 special case): `|lambda_hat_I - lambda_I| <= 2||E||_2/s_I`, captioned explicitly
  "assuming `||E||_F < s_i * sep_i / 4`" — i.e. this table's bound is *conditionally* licensed,
  not unconditional.
- An always-true (no precondition) disk theorem: every computed eigenvalue lies in a disk of
  radius `n*||E||_2/s_i` centered on the corresponding true eigenvalue — this is the one bound
  that needs no smallness assumption to invoke.

`s_i` is LAPACK's `RCONDE`: `s_i = |y_i^H x_i|` for unit-norm right/left eigenvectors `x_i, y_i`,
i.e. the reciprocal of the classical Wilkinson eigenvalue condition number
`kappa(lambda_i) = 1/s_i`.

## 4. The two-sided failure this measurement establishes

1. **The tight (asymptotic) bound is not licensed to apply.** With `p(K)=10K` (a large,
   pessimistic-for-the-theory estimate of the backward-error constant), the measured
   `‖E‖/threshold` ratio is `1.8e4` to `7.4e4` — the precondition fails badly. Checked robustness
   in the OTHER direction too: even taking the most favorable-to-the-theory value `p(K)=1` (i.e.
   `‖E‖ = eps*‖A‖` with no `K`-growth at all, the smallest any defensible reading of "modestly
   growing" permits), the ratio only drops to `22`-`77` — still a clean, unambiguous violation of
   Table 4.6's stated precondition (`||E||_F < s_i*sep_i/4`) at every one of the four `K` values,
   independent of which end of the plausible `p(K)` range is used. The asymptotic bound is simply
   not licensed here, regardless of the backward-error constant's exact value.
2. **The unconditional (disk) bound is licensed but vacuous.** `1.4e5` to `1.4e7` sits `11`-`13`
   orders of magnitude above leg 356's `1e-6` threshold and `18.8`-`19.7` orders of magnitude
   above leg 367's measured floor. True, but uninformative at either scale — it neither
   contradicts nor derives the observed tightness.

So neither of LAPACK's own two eigenvalue error-bound regimes converts the (genuinely true)
backward-stability property of `dgeev` into a derivation of the specific `K*machine_epsilon`
shape leg 367 measured. That is the resisting step, named precisely and sourced to the primary
NLA reference rather than asserted.
