## 1. Part (i) — the criterion, made executable

`2p+s>d` at `d=3`:

| field | `p` | criterion | `s` needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| velocity `V`, `|V|~|y|^-1` | 1 | `2+s>3` | `s>1` (a weight is mandatory) |
| vorticity `Ω=∇×V`, `|Ω|~|y|^-2` | 2 | `4+s>3` | `s≥0` — **none** |

Closed-form crossing for `p=1`: `s = 3-2 = 1`, exactly. A shell-ratio quadrature
(`Int_{2R}^{4R} / Int_R^{2R}` of `|V|² (1+|y|)^{-s} y² dy`, `R=1e4`, mirroring leg 313's own
diagnostic) gives ratio `1.0` exactly at `s=1` in closed form; leg 313's own measured ratio
(branch `leg/313-sdss-v1`, read-only, quoted verbatim by the plan §2.2 and by leg 341's
journal) is `1.000216400535569` — this script's closed-form recomputation agrees with leg
313's own quoted closed form (`2^{1-s}=1.0`) to machine precision, and leg 313's *measured*
value differs from that closed form by `2.16e-4`, exactly the gap leg 313 itself reported.
**Reproduces leg 313's crossing: YES.**

Leg 331's exponent table (`writeup/data/p2_route_algw_v1.json`), predicted `1+2α` (`m=0`) vs.

## 2. Part (ii) — the spectrum, one eigenvalue solve

**Operator.** `L f = -Δf + ½(y·∇)f + f`, restricted to the `ℓ=0` radial channel (per the plan
§5.2's own sizing: *"quadrature + one eigenvalue solve on an existing-style 1-D operator;
comparable to leg 331's own scope"*). In the radial variable, `y·∇ = r ∂_r` and
`Δf = f''+ (2/r)f'` for `ℓ=0`.

**Discretisation.** Chebyshev collocation on `X = r/(1+r) ∈ [0,1]` (leg 313's own compactified
variable). Boundary conditions: Dirichlet `f(X=1)=0` (decay at `r=∞`, required for `L²`
membership) and the standard `ℓ=0` regularity condition `f'(X=0)=0` (`r=0`). No `scipy` is
available in this environment (checked directly: `ModuleNotFoundError`), so rather than form a
singular generalized eigenproblem, both boundary unknowns are eliminated algebraically by
direct substitution (a Schur complement), reducing to a plain `(N-1)×(N-1)` eigenvalue problem
solvable with `numpy.linalg.eigvals` alone.

**Validation control, run FIRST.** Setting the drift coefficient to `0` gives the bare operator
`-Δ+1`, whose essential spectrum on `L²(R³)` is analytically `[1,∞)` with **provably no
discrete eigenvalues** (a textbook fact, independent of this leg's numerics). Running the
Route-E `match_filter` (imported, coarse/fine pairs at increasing resolution, `tol=1e-2`) on
this control shows the **kept count growing** with resolution — `6, 9, 13, 18, 23, 30` across
resolution pairs `(40,60)` through `(240,320)` — because a genuine continuum, discretised on a
finite domain, produces a densely accumulating cluster near the band edge that a fixed-tolerance
filter partially (and increasingly) mistakes for "converged." **This calibrates what
"continuous" looks like inside this exact numerical scheme before the operator the gate
actually asks about is measured.**

**The operator itself, drift coefficient `1/2`.** The same filter, same resolution pairs, shows
the identical qualitative signature: kept count `1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3` — also growing, not
saturating. The two lowest real eigenvalues drift slowly toward `1.0` and `1.25` respectively as
`N` grows (`fig91`, log-scale deviation plot): at `N=60` they sit `~5e-5` and `~1.4e-4` away
from their own `N=320` estimates; by `N=240` they are still `~1e-8` and `~1e-6` away — shrinking
only algebraically, never flattening. The lowest value's limit (`1.0`) coincides exactly with
the analytically known bottom of `-Δ+1`'s continuous spectrum, which is itself independent
confirmation this is a band edge, not an accidental isolated point.

**Planted-eigenvalue positive control.** A localized attractive Gaussian well
(`V(X) = -8·exp(-((X-0.35)/0.08)²)`) is added to the *same* discretized operator. Its lowest
eigenvalue is `0.12816778` — strictly below the continuum threshold `1.0` — and is reproduced
to **8 significant digits already at the coarsest tested resolution (`N=60`) and does not move
at all through `N=320`** (spread `0.0` to the reported precision). This is the sharp contrast
`fig91` plots directly: the planted state sits at the numerical noise floor from the first
resolution tested, while the plain operator's near-threshold values are still visibly shrinking
after six resolution doublings. **The filter can find a genuine isolated eigenvalue when one is
there — "nothing isolated in the plain operator" is a measurement, not a broken instrument.**

**Verdict: CONTINUOUS.** Both the accumulation-count diagnostic (calibrated against the
provably-continuous drift-0 control) and the high-precision drift-vs-flat contrast (calibrated
against the planted control) agree. **Reproduces Route-E §26's structural finding for the
analogous gCLM operator: YES.**
