## 3. The magnitudes — the pair that pulls in opposite directions, and by how much

`σ_min` at its own ideal (`κ*=0.1`, `N=256`) is `0.135799`. Pushed to `κ=10⁸`, it collapses to
`1.0879e-05` — a **`12,478×` collapse**. Equivalently, `‖R‖_X = 1/σ_min` (`A2`, the resolvent
operator norm the certificate needs *bounded*) **blows up by the same `12,478×`**, from `7.367`
at `κ*` to `91,924` at `κ=10⁸`. That limit (`κ→∞`) is exactly the direction `Z₁` and `‖ℓ‖_{X*}`
prefer: `Z₁` has only `0.0348 %` headroom left to its floor at `κ*=0.1` and only `0.00055 %`
headroom left to its floor at `C_xu_full` (`κ=2π`) — a `63×` tighter approach to the floor —
while `‖ℓ‖_{X*}` heads toward exactly `0` in that same direction with no bound at all.

No candidate escapes this. At every one of the 4 candidates, at least one of `{σ_min-family,
Z₁/D1}` is measurably off its own ideal (min deviation `3.5e-06` relative, i.e. **zero rows have
both families simultaneously at their ideal to any candidate's precision**). The tension is not
a small numerical wobble that some fifth convention could split the difference on: `σ_min`'s
ideal is an **isolated interior point** (`κ*=0.1`, confirmed a real local max, not a plateau),
while `Z₁`/`D1`'s ideal is **the unreachable limit `κ→∞`** — the two "ideal sets" `{0.1}` and
`{+∞}` do not merge, and every point on the line between them trades one family's stability for
the other's, monotonically, in the measured direction, over 8 decades.

## 4. Why the caveat family kept recurring (legs 270/276/277/281) — this leg's answer

Not an oversight: **the certificate's own headline margin quantity (`σ_min`, `‖R‖_X`) and its
own dual-norm/Z₁-battery quantities are structurally anti-correlated in the one convention
parameter that moves them.** Any convention chosen to make `σ_min` look good (near `κ*=0.1`)
makes `Z₁`/`‖ℓ‖_{X*}` further from their best case than the repository's own default (`κ=1`)
already is; any convention chosen to make `Z₁`/`‖ℓ‖_{X*}` look best (large `κ`, e.g. `xu_full`)
drives `σ_min` toward the certificate's own unbordered float-noise floor (leg 281's `E1`,
`~1e-14`) — i.e. toward the resolvent norm the certificate needs bounded becoming unbounded.
A single "convention C" citation for the whole record is not a missing convenience; it does not
exist for this axis.
