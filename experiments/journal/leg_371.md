## 3. Candidate mechanism: the real Schur form, not the eigenvector channel

`numpy.linalg.eigvals` on a real matrix calls LAPACK `dgeev` (leg 369's own read of
numpy's source). `dgeev` internally reduces to Hessenberg form (orthogonal, backward
stable) then runs the implicit-shift QR algorithm to a real Schur form
`T = Q^T A Q`, block upper-triangular with 1x1 real blocks and 2x2 blocks for
genuine complex-conjugate pairs. Fetched and read LAPACK's own source directly
(Reference-LAPACK GitHub mirror, `raw.githubusercontent.com/Reference-LAPACK/
lapack/master/SRC/`):

- `dlanv2.f` (standardizes a real 2x2 block): quote --
  "DLANV2 computes Schur factorization of a real 2-by-2 nonsymmetric matrix in
  standard form: [A B;C D] = [CS -SN;SN CS][AA BB;CC DD][CS SN;-SN CS] -- 1) CC=0
  => AA,DD are real eigenvalues, 2) AA=DD and BB*CC<0 => AA +- sqrt(BB*CC) are
  complex conjugate eigenvalues." The pair's real part is the SAME scalar `AA=DD`
  for both eigenvalues -- extracted once from a small orthogonally-similar block,
  not computed independently per eigenvalue via left/right eigenvectors.

- `dlahqr.f` (small-matrix QR iteration; used directly since `NMIN=75` is the
  documented `dhseqr.f` crossover to `dlaqr0`, and K=48 < 75): quote -- "conservative
  small subdiagonal deflation criterion due to Ahues & Tisseur (LAWN 122, 1997) ...
  IF( ABS(H(K,K-1)) .LE. ULP*TST )". `dlaqr3.f` (aggressive-early-deflation window
  inside `dlaqr0`, used for K=96/144/192, all > 75): same family, "IF(
  ABS(S*V(1,NS)) .LE. MAX(SMLNUM, ULP*FOO) ) THEN [deflatable]" with `FOO` built
  from the block's own diagonal magnitude. Both are LOCAL, ULP(=machine epsilon)-
  relative tests against the block's own entries -- not tests against the
  eigenvalue's separation from the rest of the spectrum or its eigenvector
  condition number.

- Confirmed via `www.netlib.org/lapack/lawnspdf/lawn122.pdf`, read directly (title
  page): Mario Ahues and Francoise Tisseur, "A New Deflation Criterion for the QR
  Algorithm," 14 January 1997.

**Consequence:** for THIS real matrix, Re(lambda) of a genuine conjugate pair is
governed by an ULP-times-local-block-norm-scale quantity (through `dlanv2.f`'s
shared `AA=DD` and `dlahqr.f`/`dlaqr3.f`'s local deflation gate), structurally
decoupled from the eigenvector-condition-number channel (kappa_i ~ 1e15) that leg
369 measured and correctly found inapplicable. Since matrix entries scale with
`||A||_2` (near-linear in K here), the predicted scale is `O(K * machine_epsilon)`
-- matching leg 367's own reported `K*eps` ratios (1.70, 4.04, 5.89, 8.17).

## 4. Numerical verification against leg 367's four points

Independent computation via `scipy.linalg.schur(A, output='real')` (LAPACK
`dgees` -- a different driver entry point than `dgeev`/`eigvals`, sharing the same
core Hessenberg-QR machinery) of the same generator matrix at K=48/96/144/192,
`a=0`. Located the 2x2 diagonal blocks (nonzero subdiagonal in T), computed each
block's half-trace `(T[i,i]+T[i+1,i+1])/2 = AA = DD`, excluded the block containing
the isolated structural eigenvalue near `lambda=-1` (matching leg 367's own
statistic), took the max over what remained:

| K | ||A||_2 | measured max\|Re(lambda)\| (leg 367) | max\|half-trace of Schur block\| | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 48 | 41.403 | 1.8138e-14 | 1.6875e-14 | 0.930 |
| 96 | 87.622 | 8.6042e-14 | 1.4470e-13 | 1.682 |
| 144 | 134.379 | 1.8829e-13 | 2.1672e-13 | 1.151 |
| 192 | 181.391 | 3.4817e-13 | 3.4817e-13 | 1.000 |

All four ratios are O(1) (0.93-1.68x) -- the same order of magnitude at every K.
Structural cross-check: `trace(A)` is exactly `-1.000000000000000e+00` at all four
K (an exact structural invariant: 0 dilation + -1 amplitude + 0 from continuum
conjugate-pair cancellation, per leg 367's closed form); `|trace(A)-trace(T)|`
grows with K (1.998e-15, 6.839e-14, 2.607e-13, 1.046e-12) in the same
`O(K*eps*||A||)` family, an independent check of the same mechanism rather than a
second fit.

## 5. What is and is not claimed

This is a mechanism-derived, numerically-verified match at checkable precision,
not a closed-form theorem with a rigorously proven universal constant. Ahues &
Tisseur (LAWN 122) documents the deflation criterion's design and convergence
motivation, not a stated-and-proven inequality of the form
`max|Re(lambda)| <= C*K*eps*||A||_2` for a general real matrix. What is
established: (a) the matrix is manifestly real (source-verified); (b) LAPACK's own
source shows Re(lambda) for a conjugate pair is extracted via a shared, locally-
deflated channel structurally distinct from the eigenvector-based channel leg 369
ruled out; (c) this specific, independently-computed quantity reproduces all four
of leg 367's measured points to within a small O(1) factor, where the generic
mechanism missed by 4-19 orders of magnitude.
