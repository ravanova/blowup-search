## §1. THE GATE

> **Does the extracted Theorem 1.1 match the charter's verbatim text, character for character
> modulo whitespace?**

## **`NO-AND-HERE-IS-THE-DIFF`** — strictly. **After six named typographic normalisations, MATCH, with no word, symbol or quantifier differing.**

The strict comparison fails on exactly these, each listed in the manifest as a named rule:

| rule | charter | text layer |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | — | the displayed-equation label `(1.1)` is present in the PDF |
| R2 | `R³`, `L²` | `R3`, `L2` (superscripts flattened) |
| R3 | `C∞_c`, `∂_t`, `sup_{…}` | `C∞c`, `∂t`, `sup` (TeX markers absent) |
| R4 | `Δ` U+0394 | `∆` U+2206 |
| R5 | `‖` U+2016 | `∥` U+2225 |
| R7 | `sup_{0≤t<1}`, `limsup_{t↑1}` inline | the limits are **present** but rendered on the next line by the layout |

R6 (`lim sup` vs `limsup`) did not fire: whitespace removal already equates them. **Rule R7 removes
information, so the manifest separately records that both limit strings are present in the
extraction** (`sup_and_limsup_limits_present_in_extraction: true`). **The residual diff after the
six rules is empty.**

**The honest reading:** the charter's text is a TeX-style transcription and the PDF's text layer is
a glyph stream; they cannot match strictly, and a gate that only said `NO` would be a gate about
fonts. The six rules are the entire difference and a reader can reject any of them.

## §2. THE STATEMENT INDEX — 79, reconciled to the charter exactly

A first strict regex (`Kind S.n.`) found **73**, with **0 Definitions** and §6 one short. Two
causes, both found and both fixed in the runner rather than by hand:

1. **Titled statements** — `Lemma 6.3 (Number of relevant labels).`, `Definition 6.4 (Mean and
   moment coefficients).`, `Definition 6.5 (Labelled wave coefficients).` — carry the title before
   the period. The regex now admits an optional `(Title)`.
2. **Eleven identifiers begin a line more than once.** Every one is a **cross-reference that
   happened to wrap onto a line start**, not a restatement: p.117's *"Theorem 1.1. Rescaling gives
   arbitrary positive viscosity…"* is *"Proof of Theorem 1.1."* wrapped; p.155's bare *"Lemma
   4.4."* is a citation; p.69's bare *"Proposition 6.6."* likewise. The statement line is chosen by
   rule (a titled header, or a period followed by a hypothesis word — *For, Let, There, Fix, …* —
   and never inside §3's outline pp. 6–23, whose forward references are prose). **The rule
   resolves nine of the eleven; it gets two wrong** — `Lemma 7.7` (p.73, §7's opening paragraph)
   and `Lemma 8.2` (p.88, §8's opening paragraph), section-opening forward references whose first
   word is a hypothesis word. **Those two are overridden by name, to p.86 and p.90, with the reason
   written in the runner**, and the evidence script asserts exactly those two overrides and no
   others. Every choice, rule or override, is banked beside its alternatives.

**Result:** **79** = Theorem **3**, Proposition **26**, Lemma **38**, Corollary **7**, Definition

charter states**, from the text rather than from the charter.

## §3. Sizes, and why they are committed

`manuscript_pages.txt` is **599,585 B** and the JSONL **876,552 B** (PyMuPDF's `sort=True` keeps
layout spacing, which is why the text is larger than the charter's ~473 kB pypdf estimate). Both
are committed: the repository already tracks a 4.9 MB ledger, and **provenance beats size** — every
quotation in R2's ledger will resolve to a page and a line in a file under version control.
