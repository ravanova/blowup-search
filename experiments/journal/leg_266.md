## 1. The defect, restated

`writeup/novelty/verify_251.md` §C (on `main`) reported one load-bearing gap in leg 251.
Obligation 1 read:

> *"**Existence, in interval arithmetic, of a solution of the self-similar profile system *of
> the dissipative equation*** — the enclosed object itself carries the viscous term."*

**At BCG's scaling that object does not exist.** The stationary system BCG's Thms 1.1/1.2 solve
is the **Euler** system; dissipation never enters a stationary profile equation.

profile equation therefore cannot carry the viscous term at all — which is exactly why BCG
handle it as a decaying forcing inside the stability step and buy that decay with an
`r`-restriction. The verifier's finding is confirmed independently.
