## 1. Source material read

**Leg 285's spec** lists 15 absent terms (A1–A15, A15 split into A15a/A15b for 16 spec
records), each with a definition, an I/O contract, a dependency position, anchored to BCG
(`arXiv:2208.09445`) / CGSS (`arXiv:2310.05325`). No term carries a precision or conditioning
field.

**Leg 302's ledger** built and instrumented exactly **one** of those 15 terms — A4, the
adiabatic/γ-law pressure — in IEEE double, plus a validation-only "consumer layer"
(`δ_dis`, `r_min`, `r*`, `R1`, `R2`, `k`) transcribed from BCG to check A4's constants. Eleven
known-answer probes (KA1–KA11), five negative controls, two plants (`PLANT-α`, `PLANT-FORMULA`),
each probe's detection threshold `ε*` found by bisection over `log10 ε ∈ [−16, 0]` and banked in
`writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json` under `plants.alpha.*` / `plants.sqrt3.*`. Ten of eleven
probes pass at pre-stated tolerances; **KA8 fails** — `|k(1)−1| = 1.2904784973954975e-07`
against a pre-stated `1e-9` tolerance (129.048× over) — and leg 302 diagnosed the mechanism to a
60-digit `decimal` recomputation, banked verbatim in the JSON's
`post_hoc_conditioning_diagnostic.verdict` field:

> "TRANSCRIPTION CORRECT, EVALUATION ILL-CONDITIONED: at 60 digits k(1)=1 and k(7/6) reproduces
> legs 265/266's banked value; the double-precision defect is localised at the cancellation
> point r=1 and decays as r moves away."

Sensitivity range across all 11 probes and both plants: **5.55e-16** (KA11, comparing two
algebraically distinct closed forms of `δ_dis`, one ULP) to **8.32e-03** (KA10, an
integer-valued probe on the odd-branch count, structurally insensitive below ~1% error) — the
5.55e-16-to-8.32e-03 range named in this leg's dispatch, confirmed against the JSON directly
(`writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json` → `plants.alpha.KA11.detection_threshold` =
`5.551115123125788e-16`; `plants.alpha.KA10.detection_threshold` = `0.00832492332931534`).

No other term in leg 285's 15 was built, instrumented, or planted by leg 302 or by any other
landed leg found in this leg's search — confirmed by `writeup/novelty/leg_324.md` §1.
