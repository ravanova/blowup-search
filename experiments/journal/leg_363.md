## §2 — Constant 1: `epsilon_*` (CKN)

**Search run (arXiv API, abs-field queries):** `"Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg" AND "explicit"`,
`"quantitative partial regularity" AND "Navier-Stokes"`, `"epsilon-regularity" AND
"constructive"`, `"effective" AND "Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg"`, author search `Vasseur AND
"Navier-Stokes"`, title search `"A new proof of partial regularity"`.

**Immediate hazard, banked:** the phrase "Caffarelli-Kohn-Nirenberg" is heavily overloaded —
most hits under that name are the CKN *functional/interpolation inequalities* (a Hardy-Sobolev
type inequality literature), a completely different body of work from the 1982 Navier-Stokes
*partial regularity* paper PV actually cite. I screened all hits by title/abstract and discarded
the interpolation-inequality literature as off-topic (it does not reach PV's usage — different
theorem, different mathematical object, same author-name coincidence only).

**Load-bearing find:** Zhen Lei, Xiao Ren, "Quantitative partial regularity of the Navier-Stokes
equations and applications", `arXiv:2210.01783v1` (4 Oct 2022) — fetched the PDF, converted with
`pdftotext` (4155 lines), read it directly rather than trusting the abstract. Two quotes settle
this constant:

> *"Note that the methods in `[20, 17]` rely on certain (non-quantitative) compactness
> arguments, while the method in `[35]` based on the De Giorgi iteration is quantitative."*
> (`[35]` = Alexis F. Vasseur, "A new proof of partial regularity of solutions to Navier-Stokes
> equations", NoDEA 2007 — **not on arXiv**, confirmed absent by direct title search returning
> zero hits and by a full scan of Vasseur's arXiv-listed papers finding no match. `[20]` = Lin,
> `[17]` = Ladyzhenskaya-Seregin — two of PV's own four citations.)

> *"We point out that the proof of Theorem 4 in `[3]` is still non-quantitative... it is clearly
> impossible to give an explicit dependence of delta on epsilon unless higher integrability of g
> is assumed."* (`[3]` = the original 1982 CKN paper.)

And, critically, Lei-Ren's **own** headline theorem — the most recent published work in the
one genuinely "quantitative-derivation" line (built on Vasseur's De Giorgi method) — still only
asserts existence:

> *"Theorem 6 (CKN criterion)... There exists a universal positive constant `epsilon**` such
> that `limsup (1/r) int_{Q(r)} |grad v|^2 dxdt <= epsilon**` implies `(0,0)` is not a singular
> point."*

**Reach check:** the equation class matches PV's usage exactly (suitable weak solutions of 3D
NS; two of Lei-Ren's non-quantitative citations, `[20]` and `[17]`, are literally two of PV's
four citations). But (a) PV's own citation list cites *none* of the quantitative-derivation
route — only the non-quantitative/compactness-based proofs — and (b) even that quantitative
route's own literature, traced to its 2022 state of the art, has never itself produced a number;
"quantitative" there describes the *derivation technique* (De Giorgi iteration avoiding
compactness/contradiction), not that a numeric value was ever written down.

**Verdict: NO-EXPLICIT-VALUE-LOCATED.**

## §3 — Constant 2: `K_H` (Harnack, Gilbarg-Trudinger Corollary 9.25)

**Search run:** `"explicit" AND "Harnack" AND "elliptic"`, `"quantitative Harnack"`, `"explicit
Harnack constant"` (zero hits), `"Krylov-Safonov" AND "explicit"` (zero hits), `"Krylov–Safonov"`
(em-dash, broader — 15 hits scanned by title), `"Harnack constant" AND "non-divergence" AND
"explicit"` (zero hits), `"Harnack inequality" AND "explicit dependence"` (zero hits),
`"Krylov-Safonov" AND "ellipticity ratio"`.

Four of these eight queries returned **zero results** — each banked individually as a
refusal-to-locate (the search executed against a live, working endpoint and genuinely returned
nothing, distinguishable from a fetch failure, per MF1 discipline: measure, don't assume).

**Closest find, checked and rejected on reach:** `arXiv:2512.21025v1`, "Quantitative bounds for
Holder exponents in the Krylov-Safonov and Evans-Krylov theories". Abstract:

> *"We establish quantitative bounds for Holder exponents in the Krylov-Safonov and Evans-Krylov
> theories when the ellipticity ratio is close to one."*

Two independent reach failures: (1) it bounds a **Holder exponent**, not the multiplicative
Harnack constant `K_H` that PV's proof actually needs; (2) it is restricted to ellipticity ratio
"close to one", a hypothesis PV's proof never establishes for their operator
`L_bar* - lambda_Rbar` (re-checked the surrounding text this leg; no such restriction is stated
or derivable from what's given). Recorded as a near-miss, not counted toward
EXPLICIT-VALUE-EXISTS.

I did not have independent access to a physical copy of Gilbarg-Trudinger to check whether
Corollary 9.25's own proof (rather than its statement) is constructive-in-principle — that is
out of scope for an arXiv-based literature search and PV in any case cite it only generically,
never extracting or restating a constant from it themselves.

**Verdict: NO-EXPLICIT-VALUE-LOCATED.**

## §5 — The independent, separate blocker: pressure decay (restated, not re-derived)

Leg 360's Condition 1 (its journal §3) is a **separate, independent** open condition from
delta_0's non-explicitness — logically AND'd into T4's hypotheses, not resolved by anything
found here. Quoted for completeness:

> Substituting PV's own uniform-in-time DSS/RDSS pressure-decay estimate into T4's own
> annulus-boundedness hypothesis via PV's own change of variables gives
> `|p(x,t)| <~ C_{P,0}(sigma) * (-t)^{-sigma/2}` — **divergent** as `t -> 0-` for every fixed
> `sigma > 0` the paper's estimate ever attains (`sigma = 0` is never reached; the estimate is
> quantified over the strict open interval `sigma in (0,1)`).

**Even in a counterfactual world where a future search located fully explicit numeric values for
both `epsilon_*` and `K_H`, making delta_0 numerically COMPUTABLE, the T4-derived screen would
still be blocked by this pressure-decay divergence**, unless that condition is separately
resolved — which is not this leg's task and was not attempted here. I restate this explicitly so
a reader of this leg's record alone does not mistake "delta_0's constants searched" for "the
screen is closer to operational" — it is not, on either count.
