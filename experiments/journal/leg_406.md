## §4. THE OPTIMISER, AND THE DEFECT CARRIED FROM `leg_401.md` §7.3

**Carried, not rediscovered.** `L6`'s objective is invariant under `c ↦ t c`, because the
normalisation is applied *inside* the AD tape. Hence `∇J ⊥ c` and `‖∇J‖` falls like
`1/‖c‖`, while L-BFGS-B's stopping test is on `‖proj ∇J‖_∞`. Once the iterate drifts
outward, **the stopping test fires while the objective is still falling** — measured by
`L6` at rung `J0`, branch B, seed 401: `CONVERGENCE: NORM OF PROJECTED GRADIENT <= PGTOL`
at iteration 1041 with `|g|_∞ = 6.85e−13`, `J` still dropping 2.7% per 25 iterations,
`‖c‖ ≈ 185`; continuing past that point took the same start from `J = 3.7734727962` to
`J = 1.4447691808`, a factor 2.6.

`L6`'s fix is used **verbatim**, by importing the same module constants and calling the
same functions: on every stop the iterate is rescaled onto the normalisation surface
(changing no function value) and the solver is restarted with the remaining budget; the
loop ends only when a restart buys less than `REL_STALL = 1e−10` relative improvement, or
the budget is spent. `ftol = 1e−16`, `gtol = 1e−12`, `maxfun = 2 ×` remaining, exactly as
`L6`. `L6`'s scale-invariant stationarity measure `‖x‖‖∇J‖/|J|` is recorded per start.

**Pre-committed now: if a start reports convergence before the cap, I must prove it is not
this defect recurring, with the same check.** The proof required, fixed before any run: the
artefact carries, per start, every restart round's `nit`, `fun`, `status` and `message`; a
recurrence of the defect is visible as a round that stopped on PGTOL and whose **successor
round bought a large further decrease**. A start is declared genuinely stalled only if its
**last** restart bought `< REL_STALL` relative improvement — i.e. renormalising and
restarting from the reported point does **not** continue to descend. Both the round table
and the scale-invariant gradient are banked so a verifier can check this without rerunning.

**The same two tests re-evaluated at THIS unit's actual starting point** — the banked `J4`
minimiser at `n_dof = 6720`, which is what *"reproduce at your starting point"* means:

| test | on `L6`'s fixed field | at the starting point (`n_dof = 6720`) | threshold | pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `T_A` | `1.969e−11` | **`1.2166e−10`** (6.2× worse) | `< 1e−6` | **yes, by 4 orders** |
| `T_D` | `1.2203e−04` | **`3.1174e−04`** (2.6× worse) | `< 2e−3` | **yes, by 0.8 of an order** |

**Both are worse, and that is the expected direction, not a defect.** Stated plainly so it
is not glossed:

1. **Neither test measures the representation; both measure finite differences.** `div V = 0`
   holds **identically** in the poloidal–toroidal trial space — it is an algebraic identity
   of `V = curl curl(f y) + curl(g y)`, not a numerical property. `T_A` is therefore a pure
   measurement of central-difference truncation plus float64 cancellation in
   `(V(p+he_j) − V(p−he_j))/2h` at `h = 1e−5`. It cannot detect a divergence error, because
   there is none to detect.
2. **The starting-point field is far richer, so its higher derivatives are far larger.**
   `L6`'s fixed test field has `Lmax = 3, Nr = 8, Ks = 1` (a smooth low-order random field);
   the starting point has `Lmax = 4, Nr = 20, Ks = 3` — 20 radial Chebyshev modes instead of
   8, and 7 `s`-Fourier modes instead of 3. Central-difference truncation is `O(h² ∂³V)` and
   cancellation error is `O(ε‖V‖/h)`; both grow with the field's derivative content. A
   factor of 6 on `T_A` across that jump is small.
3. **`T_D` is a doubly-nested finite difference** — a finite-difference curl of an `R[V]`
   that is itself assembled from finite-difference `∂_s`, Jacobian and Laplacian — so it
   inherits truncation from two levels and degrades faster than `T_A` in absolute terms but
   by a *smaller factor* (2.6× vs 6.2×), because its step is already coarse (`3e−4` relative)
   and it is truncation-dominated rather than cancellation-dominated at both resolutions.
4. **The direct, non-finite-difference check agrees.** `L6`'s `diagnostics` computes
   `div_V_max_abs_checked = 5.42e−13` at this very coefficient vector (banked in `L6`'s
   artefact, `ladder_results.B[-1].diagnostics`), i.e. the field really is divergence-free
   to round-off; the `1.2166e−10` is the finite-difference instrument, not the field.

**The stop condition of §6 therefore did not fire, and I record explicitly that it was
armed and could have:** the run refuses to proceed unless `T_A < 1e−6` and `T_D < 2e−3` at
the starting point **and** `objective` reproduces `1.613811231995397` to relative `1e−12`.
It reproduced it to **exactly zero difference** — `1.613811231995397` against
`1.613811231995397`. This unit is on `L6`'s object.

### §7.8 A REPORTING DEFECT IN THIS UNIT'S OWN PROGRESS NUMBERS, caught by the Conductor mid-run and recorded rather than quietly fixed

## §9. CEILINGS ON THIS UNIT'S OWN RESULT — **written BEFORE the answer exists**, because the specific failure that created this unit was a unit leaving its ceilings for the Conductor to find

These bind whichever way the gate answers. They are not hedges: each names a thing this
unit **cannot** establish, and each would be true of a `YES` and of a `NO` alike.

1. **20,000 L-BFGS-B iterations is still a BUDGET, not convergence.** This unit replaces a
   cap of 800 with a cap of 20,000. It does not replace a cap with a limit. If the run ends
   with starts still at the cap and still descending, **the resulting number is again a
   stopping point**, and the correct reading of it is the same one the landing audit
   applied to `L6` — a fact about this construction *at this budget*. **A residual reported
   at a cap is never an infimum**, and `this_is_not_an_infimum` is banked in the artefact's
   `chain` block for exactly this reason. Scale is not evidence: 25× the iterations is not
   25× the argument.
2. **Three starts is not a basin survey.** `L6` ran six starts per rung and 133 in total;
   this unit runs three, at one resolution. Two independent seeds cannot map a basin
   structure, cannot bound the number of basins, and cannot establish that any basin found
   is the lowest. **A seed that fails to reach `≈1.6` shows that seed did not reach it, not
   that the basin is unreachable**, and the pre-committed reading is worded that way.
3. **`n_dof = 6720` is ONE truncation.** The gate fixes it, correctly — changing it would
   stop the measurement being an answer about `L6` — but the consequence is that **this
   unit says nothing whatever about refinement.** It cannot confirm `L6`'s
   `decreases_under_refinement = NO` and it cannot overturn it. If the budget turns out to
   have been the binding constraint, **the entire five-rung ladder is unmeasured at an
   adequate budget** and must be re-run before any refinement statement is made; this unit
   does not re-run it and does not price it as done.
4. **The load-bearing norm's integrand is Hölder-½, not Lipschitz, where `W` vanishes**
   (`L6` §7.4). A quasi-Newton method has no convergence theory on it. So slow descent is
   *expected* and cannot be read as evidence of proximity to a minimum, and fast descent
   cannot be read as evidence of a well-conditioned problem. `L6`'s measured `ε`-smoothing
   homotopy — 1.4× better on an equal budget — is **deliberately not used**, because using
   it would change the apparatus and break the comparison; the cost of that fidelity is
   that this unit is running the *slower* of two known methods, on purpose, and says so.
5. **Branch A is not run.** The gate names branch B. Nothing here bears on branch A's
   `7.5834`, and no statement about "route 4's residual" that does not name the branch is
   supported by this unit.
6. **float64, Tier 2, and no certificate.** Every number is a float64 quadrature of a
   residual field on a fixed grid. There is **no bound**, no enclosure, no interval
   arithmetic, no contraction, no `Y₀/Z₀/Z₁/Z₂`. A small residual is **not** a solution and
   **not** a blow-up; the distance from "small measured residual" to "certified profile" is
   `CLAY_OBLIGATIONS.md` §6, which has **no known method**.
7. **NO `L1 → L4` LINK MOVES, EITHER WAY.** A `YES` re-opens a *price* (the `L7`/`L4`
   estimates) and invalidates a *reading* (`L6`'s refinement `NO` as a statement about the

   ~0.05%, and neither breaks W4 — a wall breaks when a clause *succeeds*.
8. **What this unit cannot establish at all, stated so nobody has to discover it:** whether
