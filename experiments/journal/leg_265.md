## 2. Deliverable 1 — the term, and exactly what breaks outside the window

**The stationary system is Euler's.** Confirmed independently and from a *second* paper: CGSS
writes it out at `(eq:ss_profiles)`, l.319–322, explicitly for `ν = 0`. Leg 266's correction is
right, and it is now corroborated rather than merely re-checked.

**The term.** `F_dis`, BCG's own words *"the dissipative forcing"* (l.2142), defined l.2130,
entering the **right-hand sides** of the dynamically rescaled system at l.2138–2139. In `(U,S)`
coordinates (l.3595, `eq:cortazar`):

```
    |F_dis|  ~  e^{-delta_dis s} * Delta U / S^{1/alpha}
```

CGSS states the same object independently at l.382: `ν C_dis e^{−δ_dis s} ΔU/S^{1/α}`.

**Where the certificate has to reach — two loci, and they are different.**

* **The top-order energy term `J`** (l.3898). BCG's own words, l.3804: *"we treat the term
  coming from the dissipation, **which one cannot expect to bound** (since it has more
  derivatives than our energy). Thus, the strategy is to **extract the correct sign**."* They
  show `|(α^{1/α}/r^{1+1/α}) e^{δ_dis s} J + G²| ≲ G` with
  `G² = Σ ∫ (∇Δ^K U_i)²/S^{1/α} φ^{2K} ≥ 0`. So `J` is dominated by `−G²`.
* **The stable-projection forcing bound** (l.4062, `eq:tambor1`):
  `‖F_dis(·,s)‖_X ≤ δ₁ e^{−δ_dis s/2} ≤ δ₁^{3/2} e^{−(9/10)δ_g(s−s₀)}`. This is what makes the
  Duhamel integral at l.4078 converge.

### The single most important structural fact, and it is BCG's own sentence

**l.2994**, verbatim:

```
 1/s0 << delta_0^{3/2} << delta_1 << delta_g*delta_0 << delta_0 << 1/Ebar
      << 1/K << 1/m << eta_w << delta_g << delta_dis = O(1)
```

**`δ_dis` is the TOP element of an eleven-element ordered chain, and the other ten are
calibrated against it.** The obligation asks for `r` with `δ_dis ≤ 0`. That does not violate one
inequality — it removes the element every other smallness parameter is defined relative to.

### What inverts, and what survives — measured against the papers, not asserted

| | at `δ_dis > 0` | at `δ_dis ≤ 0` | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| the smallness knob (l.3990, l.4062) | every dissipative bound carries `e^{−δ_dis s₀/2}`, and *"s₀ sufficiently large"* makes it **small** | the same factor is `e^{+|δ_dis|s₀/2}`: the proof's **only** smallness knob makes it **large** | **the monotonicity in the free parameter flips sign** |
| Duhamel (l.4072–4082) | converges, `9/10 δ_g > 1/2 δ_g` | `‖F_dis‖_X` grows; the integral behaves like `e^{+|δ_dis|s/2}` | **the estimate is false, not merely unproved** |
| the sign extraction (l.3943–3990) | `J ≈ −(r^{1+1/α}/α^{1/α})e^{−δ_dis s}G² ≤ 0` | **still `≤ 0`** — `e^{−δ_dis s} > 0` for every real `δ_dis`, and the damping **grows** | **SURVIVES** |

That third row is the reason the obligation is statable rather than absurd: **viscosity is
stabilising at top order at every `r`**. What is lost outside the window is not the dissipation's
sign — it is the *budget* that let everything else be treated as an error.
