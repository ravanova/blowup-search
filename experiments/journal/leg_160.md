## Why this is the third failure, and what it re-prices

Legs 50 and 59 repaired the weight **box** (1-D then 2-D conditioning wall). P2 went
`0.775 → 0.875 → 0.975`. P3 went `0.3656058258949979 → 0.3421493449940881 →
0.3421493449940881` — **the second repair moved it by exactly zero**, which is why leg 59's own
forward-pointer said the *definition* had to change. This leg changed the definition, twice, in
the two directions `DIRECTION.md` named, and P3 got worse both times.

Three repairs have now been tried and the property has not come within an order of magnitude of
its floor:

- **the box** (legs 50, 59) — moves P2, moves P3 by 0.0000000000;
- **the definition** (this leg, WVR-1 and WVR-2) — moves P3 by +0.658 and +0.692, i.e. away;
- **the realization** (this leg's `hiprec` control) — moves P3 by −0.0102, i.e. toward, and it
  is the *only* thing that has ever moved it toward.

That pattern is the leg's substantive result, and it points somewhere none of the three
declared repair routes go: **P3 is not measuring the fitness.**

## What P3 is measuring, quantified

Leg 59 already banked this and left it out of the frozen gate on purpose (its `D_probe_window`
block: per-weight `eps_min_noise_floor = Y_0/max_i w_i|d_i|`, `Spearman(window width,
|slope−1|) = −0.8779220779220779`, and a `reading` field that already says "this is a defect in
the FITNESS's definition, not in the box"). The general principle is older still and belongs to
Moré–Wild (ECnoise) and the Berahas/Shi derivative-free-optimization interval literature: a
finite-difference probe has a **two-sided** window, capped above by truncation and floored below
by the function's own noise. `defect_ladder` has the upper cut (leg 50's
`DEFECT_WINDOW_C = 0.1/‖A‖_w`) and **no lower cut** — its grid runs to `eps = 1e-11`.

This leg's contribution is to *reconstruct* rather than correlate. Writing

    A F(z* + eps d) = eps d + rho + O(eps^2),   rho := A F(z*),

the ladder can be rebuilt from `rho` alone, **with no PDE evaluation at any perturbed state**.
Over the 21 resolved weights that reproduces the measured slopes to `max |pred−meas| = 0.058`
(median `0.021`) and reproduces P3's headline as `0.3057` against the measured `0.3421` —
under-predicting by `0.0365` (11%), which is the first-order model's own missing `O(eps^2)`
term. A correlation of −0.878 is consistent with several mechanisms; a reconstruction is
consistent with one.

And the magnitudes line up exactly where the mechanism says they should:

    ||rho||_inf = 4.790972900601766e-11      at n = 201   -- ABOVE the grid bottom 1e-11
    ||rho||_inf = 4.640571829290985e-13      at n = 101   -- BELOW it, by 21x

**The unmodified, unrepaired leg-49 fitness PASSES the frozen six-property gate 6/6 at

Nothing was repaired to achieve that. The grid was coarsened, `‖A‖_w` fell, the floor `rho` fell

sense, and this leg reports it rather than using it: the frozen configuration is `n = 201/401`,

## Why each candidate failed, measured rather than argued

**WVR-1 (coercivity-only), `f = log10(2 Z_2/(1−Z_1)^2)`.** Behaved exactly as pre-registered in
the runner's docstring before it ran: dropping `Y_0` makes the score nearly independent of the
injected defect, so the frozen P3 reads a slope of ≈ 0 and `|slope−1| ≈ 1`. This is not a
tuning failure. **P3, as frozen, hard-codes that the fitness must be a defect-magnitude proxy**,
so it rejects *any* definition that scores the norm's quality rather than the state's residual —
including the one that arguably makes the most sense for a searcher, given leg 49's own gauge
ablation putting the whole 5604× of the incumbent in the border rows of `Y_0`.

**WVR-2 (floor-quotiented).** Subtract the state's own arithmetic floor componentwise, add it
back once as an anchor so the score stays finite at the converged state. It fails for a reason
the run measures: the floor **estimate** a single-state definition can compute is
`tau = 8·|A| @ |noise|`, an absolute-value bound carrying **no cancellation**, while the floor
actually present is `rho = A F(z*)`, which cancels. Measured: `‖τ‖_∞ = 1.2999035590373247e-09`
against `‖ρ‖_∞ = 4.790972900601766e-11`, a ratio of **27.1×**, over-subtracting on **99.5%** of
components. `‖τ‖_∞` sits *inside* the probe window (top `≈ 2.8e-08`), so about two of its ~3.4
decades are annihilated before any floor is removed and the fitness goes defect-blind
(`P3 = 1.0339`). At `n = 101`, where there is signal to spare, the same definition leaves some
(`P3 = 0.2744`) — still `3.8×` worse than doing nothing. The idea of subtracting is not what
fails; the estimator is, and structurally: any floor estimate a single-state fitness can build
bounds a *signed* quantity by an absolute-value expression, and the cancellation it discards is
exactly what makes it unusable as a subtrahend.

## C3 — the tension, stated because nothing in the record states it

P1 and P5 require ≥ 1 and ≥ 2 decades of fitness spread **at the converged state**. P3 requires
exact linearity in an injected defect **down to `eps = 1e-11`**. A fitness positively
homogeneous of degree 1 in the defect vanishes at the converged state and has no spread there,
so any fitness satisfying P1/P5 carries a nonzero floor, and any floor saturates the ladder's
small-`eps` end and costs P3. The two are jointly satisfiable only when the floor sits *below*
the window's bottom decade — which is a statement about arithmetic and grid, not about the
fitness. WVR-2 is the attempt to have both (subtract for P3, anchor for P1/P5) and the anchor is
what caps it.
