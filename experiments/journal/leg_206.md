## What the novelty pass changed about the leg

It **weakened three of the four findings**, which is what a novelty pass is for.

* **DEAP** keeps validity in a dedicated flag (`fitness.valid`), never inferred from the
  objective's magnitude. So "an extreme value is not an invalid individual" is an
  **established convention**, and the `-inf` finding is *our code failing a known standard*,
  not a discovery about genetic algorithms.
* **numpy documents** that `clip(x, lo, hi)` with `lo > hi` returns `hi` (manual; issues
  #15693, #27960). So the inverted-bounds collapse is **our unchecked precondition**, and this
  leg must not — and does not — report a numpy bug.
* **SciPy's `differential_evolution`** derives the dimension from *paired* bounds, so the
  length mismatch `ga_search` accepts is not even representable there. "Validate the box" is
  standard practice, not taste.
* Method novelty was already discharged at leg 98 (Arguzz, arXiv:2509.10819).

The leg therefore claims **only measurements**: which inputs produce a wrong return, and by
how much.

## Why this module, and why nobody has asked this before

Three legs have touched the GA lane — 49 and 59 scored the **fitness** against the
six-property viability gate (NO 4/6, twice), and 160 measured why a coarser grid fakes a pass
on that same **fitness**. All three asked *is the fitness valid?* None asked *does the
optimiser underneath it handle bad input?* The engine is precisely the component the standing
ban does not cover: the ban freezes **what may be optimised**, not **whether the optimiser is
sound**. Confirmed zero prior name-matched adversarial entry: no `test_ga_search*` file existed;
`test_ga.py` tests the unrelated `ga/` package (the mis-citation leg 71's Route-CAP self-audit
already corrected in `capabilities.py`); the module's only exercise, `test_gclm_family.py`, is
well-formed input throughout.

## The pre-committed corruption criterion

Fixed before the battery ran, `SILENT_RATIO = 100x`. A case is SILENT CORRUPTION when
`ga_minimize` **returns** — no exception — and (a) `best_fitness` is finite but worse than a
well-formed control at the identical seed/pop/generations by more than `SILENT_RATIO`, or a
known `-inf` optimum was sampled and discarded; (b) `best_fitness` is finite while
`best_genome` carries a non-finite gene; (c) `best_genome.size != len(lower)`; (d)
`best_genome` lies outside the declared box; (e) `generations` reports the full budget over a
provably frozen population. Returning `+inf`, raising, **or reporting fewer generations than
requested**, all count as FLAGGED — disclosed, not silent.

**Two refinements were made after the first run, and both NARROWED the finding** (16 silent →
12); both are recorded here rather than quietly applied. Clause (e) was restricted to
populations that had more than one distinct genome to breed from, because freezing a
zero-width box or a one-member population is honest arithmetic, not a lost search (C8, C11
moved to CLEAN). Clause (a) was suppressed for visibly truncated runs, because
`generations = 1 < 40` discloses the early stop (D1 moved to FLAGGED). Clause (d) was made
broadcast-aware, because a length-1 `upper` against a length-3 `lower` is a well-defined box
and the answer returned for it is correct (B4 moved to CLEAN).

`writeup/data/p2_route_gsa_v1_adversarial.json`. Runner:
`experiments/p2_route_gsa_v1_adversarial.py`. Regression suite:
`test_ga_search_adversarial.py`, 18 gates, all passing.

The error is not a ratio, it is the whole real line |
| 2 | a non-finite bound on a gene the fitness does not read; `np.clip` does not remove NaN, and `+inf` (the natural way to write "no upper bound") makes BLX compute `inf - inf = nan` | `best_fitness = 6.761311476498164e-06` — finite, converged-looking — with **1 of 2 genes NaN**, on all four poisonings (NaN/±inf, upper/lower), and **zero Python warnings** reach the caller |

**What the module already does right, and the suite now pins** (HOLDS gates): a NaN or `+inf`
fitness propagates to a visible `best_fitness = inf`; a poisoned bound on an **active** gene
*does* surface (finding 2 exists only because the defence runs through the fitness, so it
cannot cover a gene the fitness ignores); eight malformed configurations raise; four honest
degeneracies (zero-width box, one-member population, zero/negative budget, oversized
tournament) return correct answers and must not become exceptions under a repair; early stop
is disclosed in `generations`; determinism per seed holds exactly — the one property
`capabilities.py` claims for this module.

**Two honest prediction misses**, recorded rather than deleted: A10 (`mutation_prob = NaN`
switches mutation off entirely, but BLX alone still converges — CLEAN) and B4 (`lower` dim 3
against `upper` dim 1 broadcasts to a well-defined `[0,1]^3` and the answer is correct for
*that* box — an unvalidated input, not a wrong value).

## Why this does not escalate — orthogonal to the standing GA ban

**The ban and this finding are about different objects.** The ban on *"any GA compute on an
unvalidated fitness"* governs **which fitness may be optimised** — legs 49/59's six-property
gate, still NO, lift condition untouched by this leg. These are defects in the **optimiser's
input handling**, found with closed-form fitnesses whose minima are known on paper. No banned
fitness was evaluated, no search was run or repaired, and `solver/ga_search.py` was not edited.

**And no banked artifact is corrupted**, which is checked executably rather than asserted, in
two halves that are re-run by the regression suite:

* `test_no_live_call_site_is_exposed` — a static `ast` audit (the experiment modules are
  parsed, never imported, so nothing executes) of **all 9 live `ga_minimize` call sites**
  across `p2_two_scale_sweep.py`, `p2_two_scale_kladder.py`, `test_gclm_family.py` and
  `p2_ga_framework_evidence.py`. Every one has equal-length, finite, correctly oriented bounds
  and the default `elite_frac = 0.1`. Boxes reached through the `best_of(...)` helper are
  resolved from the module-level `LO_*`/`HI_*` literals and from `even_bounds(K)`, whose
  pure-literal body is exec'd in an empty namespace for K = 1,2,3,4,6,8.
* `test_production_fitness_cannot_emit_minus_inf` — the only production fitness,
  `gclm_family.residual_two_scale_relnorm`, is a **ratio of norms**, structurally in
  `[0,+inf) u {nan}`, so it can never take the `-inf` value finding 1 exposes. Asserted on the
  source, with no gCLM compute.

So these are **latent** defects in infrastructure. The two-scale kladder result and every other
banked GA-derived number stand unaffected. If a future call site ever fails that audit, the
gate flips and the leg's orthogonality conclusion no longer holds — which is exactly why the
check is a test and not a sentence in this file.

## For the DM

The repair, if authorised, is small and entirely at the module's entry: validate
`len(lower) == len(upper)`, all bounds finite, `lower <= upper`, `0 <= elite_frac < 1`, and
finite operator parameters; and separate *validity* from *magnitude* in `evaluate` so a `-inf`
objective is kept rather than demoted (DEAP's `fitness.valid` is the reference design). All
six GAP-PIN gates are written to **fail** when that lands — that failure is the repair's
acceptance test, and each gate's docstring carries the pre-repair magnitude it is protecting,
so the conversion to a refusal-asserting gate is mechanical.
