## The repair, in full

`solver/nk_bounds.py`, at the `farfield_modelling_error_bound` guard (the `:430` site), one
clause after the existing `unit_range_violation` call and before the existing `raise`:

```python
    if bad is None and np.isinf(float(alpha)):
        bad = (f"alpha is {float(alpha)!r}, outside the required range [-inf, 2.0): a "
               f"non-finite decay exponent makes the far-field integrand NaN, and NaN "
               f"in an upper-bound slot passes the contraction test silently")
```

`np.isinf` rather than `math.isinf` because `numpy` is already imported here and `math` is
not; adding an import would have been a second change to a file whose diff is meant to be
one clause. The clause **cannot fire on a finite alpha**, and `+inf`/`NaN` were already
refused at this site (by `f >= 2.0` and by the guard's `isnan` test), so it fires on `-inf`
**alone**. That is why clause 2 is not merely observed to pass but is structurally
guaranteed to.

`solver/certificate_guards.py` is byte-identical to what this leg inherited.

**0 of the 15 is addressable by an `isinf` test at any site, and 0 of the 15 has a surface
this leg may edit.** No `isinf` clause anywhere rejects a `Decimal` radius, a `bool` in a
norm slot, or an empty weight vector. The gate is unsatisfiable by the repair it authorises.

Per the NO branch this is reported rather than papered over: the honest move was available
and refused. Widening the diff into `certificate_guards.py` would have closed most of the 15
and turned the gate YES — and would have violated the territory, re-litigated leg 199's
closed ground, and made a four-mechanism repair out of a leg dispatched for one.

### One measurement that had to be corrected mid-run, recorded because it would have inflated the result

a `str` gamma dies of an unnamed `TypeError` from the power operator three lines into
