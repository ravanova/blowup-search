# Leg 119 — Route-HHA: adversarial audit of `hilbert_holder.py`'s bound direction

**Branch** `leg/119-hha-v1`. **Agent** LEG-P. **Date** 2026-08-06.
**Gate (verbatim).** *Under adversarial/degenerate inputs, can any bound
`hilbert_holder.py` reports be exceeded (fail to be a true bound), or a wrong value be
silently returned?*

## GATE ANSWER: **YES.** Escalated, not patched.

`solver/hilbert_holder.py` is untouched by this leg, under the gate's own instruction. Two
entirely NaN-free configurations of `gamma` produce a finite, plausible-looking
`(u_S, u_T)` pair from `increment_pair_bound` that an explicit, computable adversarial `h`
exceeds — a direct structural analogue of leg 106's finding on the sibling module
`hilbert_pointwise.py`.

| | configuration | returned pair at θ1=1.0, σ=0.05 | first violation | worst measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | `gamma = 0.0` | `(0.0, 27.580869)` | feature width `1e-45`, ratio **1.0994** | **1.2239** at width `1e-50` |
| **V2** | `gamma = -0.5` | `(13.089262, 0.041444)` | feature width `1e-45`, ratio **1.1059** | **1.2311** at width `1e-50` |

At the low-level function, **all three** of `gamma = 0.0, -0.5, -1.0` return silently at
default `(n_quad, eps)` — no warning, no exception. The public assembly API
`hilbert_holder_constant` behaves *differently* depending on the exact value: `gamma = 0.0`
**crashes** (`ZeroDivisionError` inside `solver.nk_seminorm.hilbert_split_bound`'s own
`/gamma`), while `gamma = -0.3` returns a finite `(46.7564, 4.9190)` across a full 24×14
pair-grid sweep with only non-fatal `RuntimeWarning`s, never raising. Neither polarity is
safe; they are just differently unsafe.

## The mechanism, in the module's own construction

`_increment_env`'s per-point rule `take_T = (cT <= cS)` picks, at every point in the E_N/E_F
quadrature, between a seminorm-account envelope `cT = dphi^gamma * cos^(alpha-gamma)(inner)`
and a sup-account envelope `cS = cos^alpha(a_phi/2) + cos^alpha(a_ref/2)`. As the pair
separation `dphi -> 0`, `cS -> 2cos^alpha(inner/2) != 0` — the sup account does **not**
vanish — so only `cT -> 0` (which needs `gamma > 0`) tames the near-pole singularity in
`E_N`. For `gamma <= 0`, `cT` fails to vanish either (it diverges for `gamma < 0`, stays
`O(1)` for `gamma = 0`), so the near-pole mass ends up charged to whichever account is
finite-looking at the quadrature's own cutoff, and `increment_pair_bound`'s log-graded mesh
starts at `s = eps*lo`, **never** `s = 0`. The returned `(u_S, u_T)` is finite only because
of that truncation:

| eps | 1e-6 | 1e-8 | 1e-10 | 1e-12 | 1e-14 | 1e-16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `u_T` at `gamma=0.0` | 16.71 | 22.15 | 27.58 | 33.02 | 38.45 | 43.89 |
| `u_S` at `gamma=-0.5` | 7.90 | 10.49 | 13.09 | 15.68 | 18.28 | 20.88 |
| `u_S+u_T` at shipped `gamma=0.5` | converged after `1e-8` (moves **0.008%** over the same 10 decades) |

Growing without a plateau over 10 decades of `eps`, against a converged shipped control
under the identical sweep — the signature this leg pre-registered as the reason to proceed
(novelty pass §3).

## The adversary, and why its |psi(th1)-psi(th2)| is trustworthy

`h(phi) = cos^alpha(phi/2)*clip(-(phi-center)/delta, -1, 1)`, centred at `theta1 = 1.0`
(the same clipped-ramp shape leg 106 used, now attacking the two-point `E_N` pole rather
than a single-point one). Its `H(h)` at any evaluation point is computed by the *same*
subtracted p.v. identity the module bounds, parameterised by the exact signed offset from
the ramp's own centre — the offset trick that keeps `delta = 1e-50` free of floating-point
cancellation (both in the quadrature and in the `T`-seminorm sup search, which needed the
identical trick once `delta` fell many decades below machine epsilon relative to
`theta1 = 1.0`). Gate 1 known-answer-checks this quadrature machinery against
`solver.hilbert_holder.conjugate` (cos k th -> sin k th, exact) to **2.1e-09** over 18 cases
(p.v. of a constant: exactly `0.0`), and *separately* checks the module's own
`decomposition_exact` against the same exact answer, to **5.0e-07** — before any verdict is
read from either.

## Blast radius: shipped AND production configurations are NOT affected

`(alpha=1.5, gamma=0.5)` (shipped) and `(alpha~1.4, gamma~0.15)` (the map's own production
optimum, per the module's docstring) were hunted with the identical adversary at 4 pairs
(near-0, mid-range, and near-π) and the same delta ladder:

| | shipped 1.5/0.5 | production 1.4/0.15 |
|---|---|---|
| worst true/bound over 4 pairs × 6 deltas | **0.1121** | **0.1081** |

Domination holds everywhere, and — unlike the pointwise sibling's far-field saturation — the
ratio here falls **toward zero, not toward one**, as the adversary's feature narrows: for
`gamma > 0`, `T` grows *faster* than the true increment as `delta` shrinks (the opposite
asymptotics from V1/V2, where `T` plateaus while the true increment keeps growing). No
production call site (`p2_route_d_v8/v9/v10`) ever passes `gamma <= 0`.

## What is NOT a finding (pre-declared in the novelty pass)

`alpha = inf` returns a silent `(0.0, 0.0)` from `increment_pair_bound` — but the decay
weight `(1+X^2)^(alpha/2)` is `+inf` at every `theta != 0` when `alpha = inf`, so the only
function with finite `S` there is `h == 0` a.e.; a `(0, 0)` bound on an admissible set that
contains nothing but the zero function is **vacuously true**, not a soundness gap. Confirmed
directly (gate 7): the weight is `+inf` at every sampled `theta != 0`.

## Disposition

**ESCALATE. Do not patch under this leg's authority.** The repair, when authorised, is a
*guard* on `increment_pair_bound` and `hilbert_holder_constant` — reject `gamma <= 0` (and
non-finite `gamma`/`alpha`) — **not** a change to the majorant, which is correct wherever the
payer rule selects the seminorm account on a neighbourhood of the near-pole singularity. The
soundness predicate is exact: **`gamma > 0`**. The eps-drift diagnostic separates the two
regimes with a clear gap here (max `1.34×` when sound — `gamma = 0.05` is the closest
approach — vs. min `1.98×` when not), unlike leg 106's pointwise sibling, which had a
genuinely marginal `gamma = 0.01` where the diagnostic and the predicate disagreed.

Gates 2, 3, 4, 6 (the unsound rows) and 8 of `test_hilbert_holder_adversarial.py` are
**GAP-PINS**: they assert the current unsound behaviour so it cannot decay at the rate of
memory, and **they must be INVERTED, not weakened, the day a guard lands** (leg 84's
precedent, its own words, re-applied here as leg 106 did before).
