defects that ordinary use never triggers" is **established prior art** — silent-bug incidence

**Claimed.** The ledger: twelve legs ran a silent-wrong gate; **twelve answered YES**; graded by

cycle's largest magnitude (198, `Z₂` to `1.189e17×`) contaminates nothing; the item that reaches
