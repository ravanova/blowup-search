## Three decisions worth keeping

**The criteria are computed on the module's own trajectory, not a lookalike.** `integrate`
records `mu` at sampled steps and `b` only at the end, so C1–C5 cannot be evaluated from its
record. `instrumented_trajectory` mirrors `integrate`'s accepted-step loop and calls the
module's own `_implicit_step`, recording per-block state every step. All 27 runs agree with
`integrate`'s `mu_end`/`state_growth` to `< 1e-12` relative — but that check is **reported as
plumbing, not quoted as a control**, because it is close to a tautology of the code (lesson 90:
ask what would have had to change for it to say otherwise).

**The real control is the one that inverts.** The same five criteria were run against the four
cases the landed `state_growth` clause already closed, with the identical must-not-flag
population: **C2 admits a threshold at 1.143e3× and C4 at 2.989e4×**, where on the open five
they fail at 4.17e-3× and 8.86e-4×. The instruments are not inert — the failure is a property
of the residual.

**The fifth open case is a different object and is scored separately.**
`osc_stiff_underresolved` is *fidelity-excluded*, not missed: BDF2's L-stability damped it to
**3.479e-14** of its exact amplitude, so the trajectory the predicate saw genuinely converged.
No state predicate can see a divergence the stepper removed. Every ratio is therefore reported
twice, over the 4 scored misses and over all 5.

## Mechanism, measured rather than inferred

* `SUB_THRESHOLD_UNBOUNDED_GROWTH` — `mu_linear` (2.01e2), `b_quadratic` (3.73e3). The hard fact:
  `mu_linear`'s growth is **0.495× the module's own largest legitimate growth**. That is an
  *ordering*, not a threshold-placement problem; no bound of this shape can separate them.
* `BOUNDED_NON_CONVERGENT` — `osc_sustained` (growth 1.00, tail range 0.278 of scale),
  `osc_growing` (1.99, 1.04). A magnitude clause is the wrong instrument: there is no magnitude
  to exceed.
* `INTEGRATOR_ERASED_THE_DIVERGENCE` — `osc_stiff_underresolved`, above.

Half the pre-registered prediction was **falsified**: C2/C4 were predicted to fail on
`damped_oscillation` and in fact fail on `gate6_p5` too, because normalizing by the *initial*
scale makes `mu0 = 2e-3` a very small denominator. Reported as falsified, not re-worded.

## What is NOT claimed

Not "no detector exists". Five instruments of **one shape** (end-of-window magnitude and rate
statistics) were measured against **this** well-behaved battery. The new information the
no-branch would need is named in the findings note §6: **the separating variable is not on the
trajectory — it is the caller's expectation of it.** Gate 6 knows its run is supposed to grow at
`lambda_mu = +2`; `integrate` does not. No link of the L1→L4 chain moved.
