# Leg 353 — Route-DSSP brick B5: DSSP-NKBASIN

**Gate (drafted at `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_P2_ROUTEDSSP_V1.md` §5.1,
brick B5).** Does a Newton-Krylov + phase-condition layer recover a
PUBLISHED relative periodic orbit (RPO) of 2-D Kolmogorov flow, and what is
the MEASURED radius of its basin (how far can the published initial guess be
perturbed before Newton fails)?

**Answer: NO.** The extraction layer, run with the sign-corrected recurrence
search (see §2), fails to converge on any of 5 independently-selected
Newton attempts at published RPOs, from seeds whose relative near-recurrence
residual was 0.18-0.26. A dedicated control run of the IDENTICAL
Newton-Krylov + phase-condition code, started from a 1%-perturbed exact
laminar fixed point, converges cleanly (99.3% monotone residual reduction) —
so the failure is not "the solver is broken," it is "this leg's affordable
DNS/recurrence-search budget cannot produce a seed inside the method's local
basin." **Basin radius: NOT MEASURED** — nothing converged to perturb, per
the plan's own branch structure (basin radius is conditional on recovery).

## 0. Novelty pass, before any construction

`writeup/novelty/leg_353.md` (committed first, SHA 9774b7f) pinned the
target: Lucas & Kerswell 2015, *Phys. Fluids* 27, 045106 (arXiv:1406.1820v2),
Table IV row "UPO 37" — the paper's own highlighted example RPO of 2-D
Kolmogorov flow at Re=60, forcing wavenumber n=4, unit-aspect torus, with
published period T=19.334 and x-translation shift s=0.375 (m=0). Verdict:
PROCEED — no arXiv paper measures a Newton-Krylov basin radius for a 2D
Kolmogorov RPO.

## 1. Construction

`solver/kolmogorov2d_nkbasin.py` builds, independently (no import from
`solver/boussinesq.py`, disjoint territory):

- `Kolmogorov2D`: vorticity-streamfunction pseudospectral DNS (fft2, 2/3-rule
  dealiasing, RK4 on the nonlinear+forcing terms, EXACT integrating factor
  `exp(-Ksq*dt/Re)` for the linear viscous term), matching Lucas-Kerswell
  eq. 3-6 exactly (same Re, n, domain, symmetry group).
- `shift_x` / `optimal_shift_residual`: the recurrence-flow near-return
  criterion (paper's eq. 14/15 form), FFT cross-correlation over discrete
  shifts plus a parabolic sub-grid refinement.
- `newton_krylov_rpo`: the state residual `shift_x(Phi_T(w0),s) - w0` plus
  two phase-condition rows (kill time-translation slip and x-translation
  slip via a moving Poincaré section evaluated at the current Newton
  iterate), solved by Newton's method with the correction found via a
  from-scratch matrix-free GMRES (`gmres_matrix_free`, full non-restarted
  Arnoldi + Givens rotations — no scipy in this environment). Plain-Newton
  step-halving line search substitutes for the paper's own
  Newton-GMRES-hookstep; this simplification is recorded here rather than
  silently presented as identical to Viswanath's/the paper's algorithm.
- `measure_basin_radius`: perturbs a converged (w0,T,s) at increasing
  relative magnitudes and re-runs Newton, checking same-orbit reconvergence.

**Physical validation** (`test_kolmogorov2d_nkbasin.py`, 9/9 pass): the
laminar profile `w_lam=-(Re/n)cos(n y)` is an exact fixed point of
`rhs_physical` to 7.0e-15 and is integration-invariant to <1e-3 relative
drift over `T=1`; the energy-balance identity `dE/dt` (finite-diff) vs `I-D`
matches to 1.4% relative; `shift_x` is a genuine group action (identity at
`s=0`, checked on a band-limited field — full-band random noise produces a
Nyquist-bin aliasing artefact under fractional phase shift and was excluded
from the test); `gmres_matrix_free` matches `np.linalg.solve` on a small
dense system to 2.9e-16 relative.

## 2. A genuine sign bug, found by the leg's own test suite

`test_optimal_shift_residual_is_zero_for_a_shifted_copy` (constructs a
synthetic pair via `shift_x` with a known true shift, then asks
`optimal_shift_residual` to recover it) initially FAILED with
`rel=1.966` — nowhere near zero. Diagnosis: for
`C[j] = sum_{x,y} w1(x,y) w2(x+j,y)`, and `shift_x(w,s)(x)=w(x-s)`, if
`shift_x(w2,s)=w1` then `C[j]` is maximised at `j=-s`, not `j=+s` — the
original code read `s_best = (j_best+frac)*(2*pi/N)`, the wrong sign. Fixed
to `s_best = -(j_best+frac)*(2*pi/N)`; after the fix the same test recovers
a true shift of `1.1` as `s_found=-5.1950` (`-5.1950 mod 2*pi = 1.088`,
`rel=3.424e-3`).

This bug is load-bearing: it means every recurrence-search candidate's
`s_guess` fed to Newton BEFORE this fix carried the wrong sign, so an
earlier (pre-fix, not committed) round of Newton failures could not have
been trusted as evidence toward the gate's "no" branch — they may simply
have been fed corrupted seeds rather than genuinely too-far ones. The run
reported below is the sign-CORRECTED run; it is the one the gate verdict
rests on.

| UPO37[0] | 0.177 | 24.19 | line_search_failed | 24.19 | 0 | 90.2s |

that reduces the residual at all); the other three make modest partial

**monotonically**, a 99.3% reduction, wall 5.7s. The identical
Newton-Krylov + phase-condition code — same `extended_residual`, same
`gmres_matrix_free`, same line search — works correctly when the seed is
genuinely close to a true solution. This is the control the gate's own
branch structure calls for: it rules out "the extraction layer is broken"
as the failure mode, and pins the failure to seed quality instead.

**Basin radius: NOT MEASURED.** `any_converged=False`,
`result["basin_radius"]=None` (nothing converged to a real target to
perturb around).

## 4. Mechanism (why NO, precisely)

The recurrence-search seeds this leg's affordable DNS (T_total=2000,
N=24) can find have relative near-recurrence residual 0.18-0.26. The
control shows the machinery works fine once inside its local basin. The
gap is that Newton's local basin for this stiff, chaotic-flow extended
residual is evidently much smaller than "0.2 relative residual in field
space" — consistent with why Lucas & Kerswell (and the wider
recurrence-flow literature, e.g. Viswanath 2007, Chandler & Kerswell 2013)
mine DNS on the order of `T~1e4-1e6` (this leg's `T_total=2000` is 1-3
orders of magnitude short of that) AND use a hookstep/trust-region
globalisation rather than plain Newton with step-halving specifically
because plain Newton is known to fail from moderate-residual near-returns
on chaotic attractors. Both budget shortfalls (shorter DNS, no hookstep)
are named up front in the module's own docstring as the leg's declared
simplifications, not discovered after the fact.

Per the plan's own drafted branch: **no → the extraction layer fails where
the answer is known, refuting the "mature engineering" premise of leg
334's clause (b) — this STOPS route 4 before any 3-D cost is committed.**
The failure mode identified here (seed quality / globalisation, not a
broken solver) is the specific, actionable form of that refutation: a
future attempt would need either a much longer recurrence-mining DNS or a
genuine hookstep/trust-region Newton-GMRES implementation (or both) before
retrying — this is reported, not silently absorbed into a vaguer "it
didn't work."
