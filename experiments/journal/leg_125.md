## What the full-text read changed, which is everything

Leg 63 put this candidate at the top of the ledger as *"gCLM with full Laplacian dissipation
(γ=2) … blow-up PROVED analytically"*. The abstract supports that reading. **The full text does
not**, and the sentence that settles it is the last one of the proof of Theorem 1.1:

> *"Since ν(t) converges to 0, such profile is the same as the inviscid profile associated
> with a."* — Chen §2.6, p.12

with Remark 2.1 (p.11) saying the same thing from the other side: *"(2.43) implies that the
diffusion term in (2.6) vanishes as t → +∞."* And §1.5's own roadmap already announces it:
*"In Section 2, we construct the self-similar profile for the **inviscid** gCLM (1.1) with
a = 1/2."*

**There is no γ=2 dissipative self-similar profile in the paper.** The object Theorem 1.1's
solution converges to is the explicit *inviscid* a=1/2 pole-dynamics solution, eq (2.2) p.4 —
a closed-form rational function. That is the third time this repository has been caught by
abstract-depth reading (legs 53, 64, and now the ledger entry leg 63 wrote), and the first
time the check was run *before* the construction was built on it rather than after.

**The γ tension leg 64's review surfaced is resolved, and it was never a contradiction.**
Chen's `γ = |a|⁻¹` critical dissipation is §1.2 p.2 and Theorem 1.5, stated **only for
a ≤ −1**, where the a-priori `L^{|a|}` estimate exists. For **a > −1** his §1.2 gives `γ = 1`
critical, from `‖ω‖_{L¹}` conservation. The two statements have disjoint ranges of `a`, so they
never met. Neither produces a γ=2 profile, and Theorem 1.1 does not need one — its blow-up data
is **class 3** (Remark 1.4), the one class where `L¹` is *not* conserved (Lemma 3.1(b),
eq (3.5)), so the `L¹`-based criticality of §1.2 does not even classify the blow-up data.

**The a-neighbourhood is confirmed UNQUANTIFIED**, as leg 63 suspected: eq (2.41) p.11 picks
`δ, ν₀` only through `(1 + C₂ + C₃ + C₂C₃ + C₂²)(δ + ν₀) < 1/1000` with `C₂, C₃` unnamed
universal constants. Recorded as `None` with the provenance string, never bounded (lesson 73).
And `0 ≤ ν ≤ 1` in Theorem 1.1 is **not** a large-viscosity claim: eq (2.41)–(2.42) reach it by
choosing the initial length scale `C_l(0)` large so that `ν(0) ≤ C_l(0)⁻² ≤ ν₀`.

## What was built, and what it measures

`solver/dissipative_profile.py` (new, sole owner). It **reuses** `gclm_family.GCLMResidual`
(grid, whole-line Hilbert matrix, the a-family residual convention), `gclm_rescaled.sinh_grid`
and `nk_bounds.budget`; it adds only the four things that did not exist: a 4th-order cumulative
velocity operator, the dissipative steady residual and its exact Jacobian,
`diffusion_consistency`, and the Y₀ measurement.

**The one piece of new mathematics, and it is small but it is the leg's spine.** From Chen's
eq (2.7), a *steady* effective viscosity requires `(2c_l + c_ω)ν = 0`. Removing the
time-normalisation gauge gives the invariant

    Δ  :=  2 c_l / |c_ω| − 1 ,        Δ = 0  ⟺  a γ=2 profile is admissible

(`Δ = 0` is exactly the heat scaling `c_l/|c_ω| = 1/2`). At Chen's eq (2.2), `c_l = 1/3`,
`c_ω = −1`, so **Δ = −1/3 exactly** — and Chen's own bootstrap (2.40) independently bounds the
same exponent *"above by −1/3 and a small term"*, tightened to `≤ −1/4` in (2.43). The
algebra and the paper agree.

**Lesson 90 was applied before the number was quoted**, not after: `diffusion_consistency`
returns exactly `0.0` on the heat pair `(1/2, −1)` and exactly `+1.0` at the a=0 CLM anchor
`(1, −1)`, so it varies and *can* report the other answer. Both are permanent tests.

**Lesson 86 decided which Y₀ is the honest one.** After Newton the *discrete* defect sits at
the Newton floor (~1e−15). That number is a statement about the code, not about the
mathematics, and it is reported as such and never as the headline. The honest `Y₀` is the
**consistency** defect — the discrete solution's defect in the *continuous* equation — proxied
at each resolution by the exact profile's residual. Both are in the JSON; which is which is
stated at both.

## The thing that came out that nobody asked for, reported as a lead and not a result

`Δ(a)` was swept over `a ∈ [0.30, 0.70]`, every point converged to machine precision, and it
**crosses zero** — at `a* ≈ 0.3865`, determined two independent ways that agree. So there *is*
an advection value at which the γ=2 self-similar scaling is admissible, and it is **not**
Chen's `a ≈ 1/2`, which sits `1/3` away in `Δ`. Chen's theorem says nothing about `a*`.

`Δ = 0` is **necessary, not sufficient**. M7 imposes it and asks whether `ν > 0` can then be
solved. Non-trivial solutions do appear at every tested `ν ∈ [1e−3, 3e−1]`. **But the branch is
not resolved, and this leg says so rather than banking it:**

* The `ν = 1` solve **collapsed to the trivial null `Ω ≡ 0`**, which solves the equation
  exactly and therefore has *absolute* residual zero. Only the scale-invariant residual caught
  it. That is lesson 90 in its sharpest form and it is the reason M7 reports a relative
  residual at all.
* **Dilation covariance fails as a check.** `Ω(X) → Ω(X/μ)` maps a solution at `ν` to one at
  `μ²ν` with `a` *unchanged*, so a genuine one-parameter branch must have `a` independent of
  `ν`. The measured `a` is not: it moves across the tested `ν`. In the odd subspace the system
  is short by one equation, so Newton lands on an arbitrary point of a one-parameter set.

So: an interesting corner exists at `a*`, it is unclaimed by Chen, and **this leg has not
established that a γ=2 profile lives there.** It is written into the artifact as an open lead
with its magnitudes and its failed check attached (lesson 76 — keep the negative construction
in the artifact), and explicitly *not* into the ledger as a result.

## The gate, answered in its pre-committed wording

> *"does Y_0 come in under the radii-polynomial budget at any tested resolution?"*

**NO**, at every tested resolution, and the gap **widens** under refinement — because `Z₂`
grows faster than `Y₀` falls. The no-branch's second clause also fires, and independently:
*"If the full text does not support the abstract as read (no explicit profile at γ=2 …), that
lands here too"* — it does not, and the verbatim quote is above.

The honest reading of the `Y₀` number itself, stated so it is not over-read: the measured `Z₂`
is dominated by `‖A‖` on a Jacobian that is rank-deficient by the two gauge directions, so it
is substantially an artifact of a gauge-unbordered discretisation rather than a statement about
the operator. A real certificate would border those directions — which is precisely what legs
51–53's bordered certificate does, and precisely where leg 53 found the margin runs out at
`Z₁`'s block coupling. The candidate does not clear the budget, and the reason it does not is
not the reason the ledger expected.

**And the reason the candidate leaves the ledger's top slot is the literature one, not the
numerical one.** Even a `Y₀` under budget would have certified the *inviscid* a=1/2 profile —
which Chen writes down in **closed form** (eq (2.2)), and which `Papers/MANIFEST.md`'s
exclusion list already records as covered analytically for the whole smooth gCLM branch
`a ≤ 1` (HQWW arXiv:2308.01528 / 2305.05895). A computer-assisted proof of a rational function
that is already an exact solution is worth nothing. This is the *same* degeneracy as the banned
leg-51 clause (`Y₀` exactly zero because the a=0 CLM profile **is** one basis mode) arriving
for a **new reason**, and it is flagged here so it cannot be re-read as progress later.
