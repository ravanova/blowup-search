## The one binding thing the novelty pass changed

**The organising concept is published, named, and on this exact operator, so none of it is
claimable.** Xu, [arXiv:2607.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19762), **Proposition 2 of
§3.1**, fetched at full text on 2026-08-06:

> "The essential spectrum of `L₀` depends on the realization. On the maximal `L²`
> realization…, the full vertical strip between the two indicial lines; on the origin-`H²`
> realization `X` of Theorem 1, the open strip contains no spectrum beyond the two symmetry
> eigenvalues `0` and `1`."

So "the obstruction belongs to the realization, not the operator" is **Xu's realization
dichotomy**, not this leg's insight, and not this repository's. This leg uses it and does not
claim it — exactly the discipline leg 127 applied to `Z₁ ≥ 1 − ‖A‖σ_min`. What is this leg's
own is only the **classification** of four banked findings under someone else's dichotomy.

The second finding: **no in-repo leg has asked this question.** Leg 126 is the near miss and
it is a genuinely *different predicate* — it enumerated stage `B`'s five axes into **1,686
configurations** and asked *is any configuration UNCOVERED*, answering **1,686 of 1,686
covered, 0 uncovered**. Coverage and realization-dependence are not the same test, and leg 126
found that out inside its own instrument: its covering predicate initially answered "covered"
at the `μ > 0` control *where a certificate demonstrably closes*, because "not one of the
twelve clauses referenced `μ`". Leg 126 scoped its clauses to `μ = 0` and shipped. **It scoped
them; it never asked whether the scoping was load-bearing.** Leg 141 banked the decisive

the Xu overlap and explicitly declined to act on it — "recorded so a future `NG` leg does not
rediscover the overlap". This is that leg.

## The predicate, and why it could come out the other way

Pre-registered in `writeup/novelty/leg_165.md` §5 **before the runner existed**, three tiers
never conflated:

| tier | meaning | answers the gate YES? |
|---|---|---|
| **1** VERDICT-DEPENDENT | a different choice *within the same degree of freedom* is banked in which the certificate/gap/bound **closes** | **yes** |
| **2** MAGNITUDE-DEPENDENT, VERDICT-INVARIANT | a different choice moves the headline by orders of magnitude, verdict survives | no |
| **3** REALIZATION-INVARIANT | every choice the record actually tried agrees | no |

Three controls, all pre-registered, all three passed:

- **C1 — the predicate must be able to say NO.** Required at least one gated row back at tier
  3. **Two came back tier 3** (52, 53). Had all four returned tier 1 the audit would have been
  a tautology of this file and **withdrawn, not shipped**.
- **C2 — the predicate must be able to say YES.** Leg 127's own row, through the identical code
  path, must return tier 1, since that is the finding the gate takes as given. It does:
  `σ_min → 0` in `ℓ¹_w` at `s < 1` against Xu's modulated gap `1/2` on origin-`H²`.
- **C3 — no borrowing across degrees of freedom.** A `μ > 0` dissipative operator is a
  different **operator**, not a different realization of the same one. All three banked `μ > 0`
  witnesses (leg 53's `Z₁ < 1` at `μ = 2.0`; leg 111's sign-flipping `A2` gap; leg 127's
  `σ_min` saturating, `|exponent| ≤ 2.6248e−03` against `0.6985` at `μ = 0`) are collected into
  their own block and **structurally barred** from tier 1 — no tier assignment reads that
  block.

**And the prediction was recorded before the code was written: 52 → 3, 53 → 3, 56 → 2,
111/141 → 1. Observed: 4/4 as predicted.** That is worth stating plainly as a *weakness* as
well as a strength: a 4/4 prediction means this leg's reading was already formed from the

### Leg 111/141 — TIER 1. This is the finding.

**Degree of freedom:** the weighted-energy realization = (weight exponent `γ`) × (**trial
space**). Leg 111 swept `γ` over seven weights and held the trial space fixed at
`span{sin kθ}`, vanishing order `p = 1` at the origin. The trial space is the *other half of
the same degree of freedom*: it is what sets the membership threshold `γ < 2p + 1`.

**Both sides, side by side, both banked:**

| | leg 111's choice: `p = 1` | the other choice: `p = 2` |
|---|---|---|
| admissible window `(3, 2p+1)` | `(3, 3)` — **width 0.0** | `(3, 5)` — **width 2.0** |
| contains `γ = 4`, the published exponent | **no** | **yes** |
| membership `‖·‖²` ratio per refinement at `γ = 4` | **1.6777e+07** (divergent) | **1.0000000000000002** (convergent) |
| increment ratio at `γ = 4` | **4096 = 64²**, a power | — |
| verdict | **0 of 7 weights pass the gate**; largest admissible modulated gap **−0.4999241** | **a coercivity gap of −1/2 is certified in print** |

The right-hand column is not an extrapolation. It is **Elgindi–Ghoul–Masmoudi,
[arXiv:1906.05811](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05811), Prop. 2.1**, re-located by leg 141 in the
actual PDF at run time (10/10 statements LOCATED, four papers):

> "There exists a universal constant `C > 0` so that if `a` is small enough and if `f` is odd,
> `f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0` and `∫_R |f|² φ(y) dy < +∞`,
> `∫_R f M_a f φ(y) dy ≤ (−1/2 − C|a|) ∫_R f(y)² φ(y) dy`"

with `φ = (1 + y²)²/y⁴` — `γ = 4` at the origin, **exactly the exponent leg 111 measured as
inadmissible** — and, from EGM's own §1, *"when `a = 0` we get CLM model"*: **this
repository's own object.**

**The two sides are not two calculations disagreeing. They are one calculation read on two
trial spaces**, and the record proves it three ways: leg 111's own damping formula
`D_φ(0) = (3 − γ)/2` at `γ = 4` gives **−0.500000**; EGM's constant `−1/2 − C|a|` at `a = 0`
gives **−0.500000**; Xu's published modulated gap gives **0.500000**. **Max absolute
discrepancy `0.000e+00`.** And leg 141's `p = 1` column reproduced leg 111's banked norms at
`abs_diff 0.0` — same quadrature, same code, only the trial space moved.

Xu names the mechanism himself, §8, and hands over the classification:

> "(The small-`a` coercivity estimate of Elgindi, Ghoul, and Masmoudi [15, Prop. 2.1], for odd
> `f` with `f′(0) = Hf(0) = 0` in a singular-weighted space, **is not a counterexample but an
> instance of the realization mechanism of Section 3.1**: the singular weight and origin
> constraints define their own realization, **in which a gap is certified**…)"

**Reading: leg 111's zero-width window is a property of the trial space `span{sin kθ}`, not of

**The caveats, carried verbatim and not softened.** Leg 141's own scope line: the trial-space

### Legs 52 and 53 — TIER 3, and they are also not independent rows

**Leg 53** (degree of freedom: the split — `K`, class, gauge, normalisation, border direction).

must be under **1**. **Nothing on the axis crosses 1.**

**One nuance this row must carry, and it sharpens the tier definition.** The border-direction
axis is *not* flat — it is the axis lesson 90 was written about. Leg 53 first reported

**And the structural point about both rows.** Legs 52 and 53 vary parameters *inside* the

names four rows; the record contains **two genuinely independent realizations** beyond leg

## What the classification actually says, stated carefully

The gate offered a clean dichotomy — either one is realization-dependent, or all four are
invariant and the operator-level reading is strengthened. **The record answers neither
cleanly**, and pretending otherwise would be the error:

- **Leg 127's reframing is not a general pattern**, which is the no-branch's reading and it
  survives: 3 of 4 rows are not verdict-dependent.
- **But it is not unique either**, which is the yes-branch and it fires: the weighted-energy
  realization dies for a reason that a different trial space demonstrably removes, and the
  literature certifies the gap on the other side of that choice, on this repository's own
  object, with the constants agreeing to `0.000e+00`.
- **Twice now**, the thing this repository measured as an operator-level obstruction has turned
  out to be a statement about a choice it made. Both times the correction came from **outside**
  — Xu for leg 127, EGM via Xu for leg 111 — and both times **the repository had the reference
  in hand before it drew the conclusion** (leg 111's own novelty log §2 recorded the EGM
  neighbourhood via an NSF-PAR landing page, without the full text). That is the pattern worth
  escalating, and it is a process pattern, not a mathematical one.

**A tier-1 classification is not a licence to try the other side.** This leg proposes, prices
and recommends no realization. Two things stand between the `p = 2` trial space and anything
resembling a route, and both are banked above: the coercivity half at `p = 2` has been
recomputed by nobody, and EGM buy their origin conditions with two modulation parameters.
