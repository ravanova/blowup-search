## 1. Reading the ground first

`experiments/journal/leg_356.md` §2 and `writeup/CORRECTIONS.md` §25(a) both state the mechanism
as: "the discretized ESSENTIAL/continuum spectrum ... sits on the imaginary axis in exact
arithmetic (`Re lambda = 0`)". Both cite `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s own module docstring as
the source, not an external theorem, and leg 356's own text frames this as read off the module's
stated theory, not independently derived or sourced. `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py` itself states
the claim in its "WHAT THE SPECTRUM CONTAINS BEFORE YOU COMPUTE IT" section, immediately after a
closed-form derivation whose actual conclusion is narrower (see §3 below).

## 2. Identifying the exact operator and domain (dispatch requirement)

`RescaledFlow.generator(b) = S^{-1} @ jacobian(b)`, a dense `K x K` real matrix, `S` the odd-sine
collocation matrix on `theta in (-pi, pi)` (`X = tan(theta/2)`, the whole real line, compactified
with no truncation). At `a=0` the closed-form linearization is stated in the variable
`s = delta Z`, `Z = H(Omega) + i Omega`, `w = e^{-i theta}`:
`L s = w s - ((w^2-1)/2) s_w - s(w=1)(1+w)`.

**This is not leg 343's operator.** `experiments/journal/leg_343.md`'s brick B1 measured
`L f = -Delta f + (1/2)(y . grad) f + f`, the `ell=0` radial channel of the BACKWARD-rescaled
Navier-Stokes/heat operator, on a compactified `[0,1]` domain (`r/(1+r)` variable) — a different
PDE (backward heat/NS vs. dynamically-rescaled gCLM), different domain (compactified half-line
vs. compactified whole line), and a different discretization signature: leg 343's continuous-
spectrum band edge sits at `Re(lambda) = 1.0`, and the count of eigenvalues surviving
`match_filter`'s convergence test GROWS without saturating across resolution pairs
(`6, 9, 13, 18, 23, 30` for `(40,60)` through `(240,320)`) — a discretized continuum that never
converges. Leg 356's E5 finding is qualitatively different: a STABLE 16-entry multiset that
reorders under noise rather than a growing unconverged count. Confirmed distinct before treating
either as evidence for the other.

## 3. Theorem search

Read `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s closed-form section clause by clause. Its actual proof:
admissible eigenfunctions `s = (w-1)^{1-lambda}(w+1)^{1+lambda}` (vanishing at `w=-1`, bounded at
`w=1`) exist for the WHOLE STRIP `-1 < Re(lambda) < 1` — both signs of `Re(lambda)`, not just 0.
Only when ADDITIONALLY requiring analyticity at `X=0` (the class compatible with the sine trial
space) does admissibility collapse to `1-lambda` a non-negative integer, i.e. exactly
`{lambda=0, lambda=-1}`. **This proves the exact operator, restricted to the analytic-at-origin
subspace, has ONLY the two structural eigenvalues — it says nothing about the `K-2` other
eigenvalues a finite `np.linalg.eigvals` call on the truncated matrix actually returns**, since
those necessarily approximate continuum eigenfunctions (branch points at `X=0`) that are outside
the sine basis's span entirely. The theorem and leg 356's claim are about different objects; one
does not imply the other by clause-matching.

Checked `experiments/journal/leg_70.md` (Route-RC realization audit): a DIFFERENT diagnostic
(count of "unstable" eigenvalues at `mu=0` in Route-I's related `CriticalDissipativeFlow`
subclass) finds the deficit `K - count` is exactly `3` at `K=48,96,144` — the signature of a
discretized continuous spectrum for the maximal-`L^2` realization (Xu, `arXiv:2607.19762`, Prop
2's dichotomy). This corroborates the qualitative "discretized continuum" phenomenon but is
silent on whether `Re(lambda)` is exactly 0 versus merely small-and-noise-dominated (both would
produce a count near `K` with a noise-decided sign).

Checked Xu's paper as read by legs 111/127/141/163/173 (the same paper leg 70 cites). Leg 163's
reading quotes Xu's own abstract explicitly declining to extend closed-form spectral results to
norm-level statements in the un-transformed variable, BECAUSE the linearization `L0` is
non-normal ("a spectral gap does not by itself give a decay rate in the `X` norm"). No
proposition quoted anywhere in this repo's Xu-reading legs proves the finite discretization's
real part is exactly zero. Conclusion: **no theorem located** whose hypotheses match the specific
object (the `K x K` sine-collocation matrix) and specific claim (exact `Re=0` for every finite
`K`).

## 4. Derivation attempt

If the pinning were exact for every finite `K` (not just asymptotically), the cleanest mechanism
would be: the generator matrix `A` restricted to the complement of the 2D structural invariant
subspace is skew-symmetric (or skew-adjoint under a natural weighted inner product), which forces
purely imaginary eigenvalues by elementary linear algebra, independent of any continuum limit.
Tested directly against `solver/rescaled_spectrum.py`'s own `RescaledFlow` class (`K=48`, `a=0`,
read-only import):

- `S^T S` (the sine basis's Gram matrix): off-diagonal/diagonal Frobenius ratio `5.5e-15` — the
  basis genuinely is discretely orthogonal, confirming the natural weight candidate is honest.
- Plain jacobian `J`: symmetric-part/total Frobenius ratio `0.891` — far from skew.
- `A = S^{-1}J`, Gram-weighted skew defect `(A^T G + G A)` relative to `G A`: `0.477` — far from
  skew-adjoint under the natural `L^2(d theta)` inner product either.
- Five diagonal reweightings of `A` (by `1, k, k^2, 1/k, 1/k^2` — the natural mode-scalings for a
  derivative-type operator): symmetric-part ratios `0.135, 0.155, 0.183, 0.606, 0.714` — every
  one full rank, none isolates a low-rank symmetric residual attributable to the structural
  directions alone.

**No elementary skew-symmetrization mechanism explains the observed pinning.** `A` is genuinely
non-normal and not close to skew-adjoint under any tested weight, yet its non-structural
eigenvalues still cluster at machine-noise-level real parts — a striking, reproducible fact this
leg could not reduce to a checkable identity in the time available. Flagged as open for a future
leg, not resolved here.

## 5. Empirical corroboration (independent of leg 356's own run)

Measured `max|Re(lambda)|` over the non-structural eigenvalues (dropping the isolated eigenvalue
near `-1`) at four resolutions, `a=0`:

| K | n_nonstructural | max\|Re(lambda)\| | max\|Re\| / (K * machine_eps) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 48 | 47 | 1.814e-14 | 1.70 |
| 96 | 95 | 8.604e-14 | 4.04 |
| 144 | 143 | 1.883e-13 | 5.89 |
| 192 | 191 | 3.482e-13 | 8.17 |

Grows roughly linearly in `K`, staying within single-digit multiples of `K` times machine
epsilon at every resolution — tracking accumulated float64 roundoff, not any `O(1)`-scale genuine
real part. Consistent with exact `Re(lambda)=0` at every finite `K` tested (not merely a
`K -> infinity` asymptotic claim), but "consistent with" is not "proven."

Independently reproduced leg 356's own `K=96/K=144`, `tol=0.1` run: 16 kept eigenvalues,
`Re(lambda)` range `[-1.0000000000000, 6.27e-14]`, `|Im(lambda)|` multiset (each value twice, a
conjugate pair) `{0, 0.271, 0.840, 7.156, 8.135, 14.828, 23.008, 45.174}` — bit-for-bit match to
leg 356's own reported values.

## 6. Classification and consequence

**(iii) UNVERIFIED-ASSUMPTION, empirically corroborated.** No theorem located; no derivation
constructed despite a genuine, documented attempt that ruled out the obvious mechanism (skew-
symmetry under six natural weightings). The claim is robustly reproduced by three independent
measurements (leg 356's own run, leg 70's unrelated `K-3` diagnostic on a related subclass, this
leg's fresh four-resolution run) and contradicted by nothing found.

**Consequence for leg 356's canonical ordering scheme: HOLDS, with a caveat, not undermined.**
`canonical_order()`'s `round_dp=6` (`1e-6` threshold) only needs the strictly weaker fact that the
noise floor sits far below `1e-6` and far below the smallest genuine isolated real part this
module has ever reported (`>1e-3` per its own docstring). Margin re-measured directly:
`1e-6 / 3.48e-13 ~ 2.9e6`, roughly 6.5 orders of magnitude, at every resolution `K=48` through
`K=192` — over-confirmed, not merely adequate. The repair's correctness does not depend on the
"exactly 0" claim being a theorem. The caveat is textual only: leg 356's prose states the ground
as settled fact ("in exact arithmetic") where it should read as a strongly-corroborated but
unproven empirical regularity. Recorded here and in `writeup/data/p2_route_e5p_v1.json` only —
leg 356's own files were not edited (verified via `git status --short` before and after: only
