## 1.3 The plants — controls that can come out differently

**PLANT-α** (the primary plant): `α ← α·(1+ε)`, everything downstream recomputed from the
perturbed constant. Sweep `ε ∈ {0} ∪ {10^-12 … 10^-1}`, 12 decades, plus a bisection for the
**detection threshold ε\*** — the smallest `ε` at which each probe crosses its own tolerance.
**Pre-stated requirement for the gate's second half:** at `ε = 1e-2`, probes **KA2, KA3, KA4,
KA7, KA9 must FAIL**; at `ε = 0`, **all** probes must PASS.

**PLANT-FORMULA** (the second plant, aimed at the probes PLANT-α cannot reach): the `√3` in
`r*`'s `γ ≥ 5/3` branch `← √3·(1+δ)`. **Pre-stated:** at `δ = 1e-6`, **KA5 and KA7 must FAIL**,
while **KA2 and KA3 are expected to be BLIND** because they never touch `r*`.

**Blindness is a reported result, not a hidden one.** The deliverable is a full
**probe × plant sensitivity matrix** with a detection threshold in every cell, and every
`ε* = ∞` cell named explicitly — a probe that cannot come out differently under a given plant is
recorded as such (lesson 90's exact instruction: ask what would have had to change in the code).

**Negative controls that can fail:**

* **NEG-1** `γ ≤ 1` must raise (α ≤ 0 destroys the pressure law and the `S^{−1/α}` multiplier).
* **NEG-2** `ρ ≤ 0` must raise.
* **NEG-3 (wrong-γ discrimination)** — the γ=7/5 window probe KA4, evaluated at γ=5/3 instead,
  **must NOT** reproduce leg 240's γ=7/5 numbers. If it did, the probe would be measuring the
  code and not γ.
* **NEG-4** finite-difference `dp/dρ` vs analytic, max rel error `≤ 1e-6` on `ρ ∈ [1e-2, 10]`.
* **NEG-5** `1/α → ∞` as `γ → 1⁺`: measured `1/α` at `γ = 1+10^-k` must grow by one decade per
  decade of `k` (slope `1.000 ± 1e-6` in log–log).
