## 1.3 The plants — controls that can come out differently

**PLANT-α** (the primary plant): `α ← α·(1+ε)`, everything downstream recomputed from the
perturbed constant. Sweep `ε ∈ {0} ∪ {10^-12 … 10^-1}`, 12 decades, plus a bisection for the
**detection threshold ε\*** — the smallest `ε` at which each probe crosses its own tolerance.
**Pre-stated requirement for the gate's second half:** at `ε = 1e-2`, probes **KA2, KA3, KA4,
KA7, KA9 must FAIL**; at `ε = 0`, **all** probes must PASS.

**PLANT-FORMULA** (the second plant, aimed at the probes PLANT-α cannot reach): the `√3` in
`r*`'s `γ ≥ 5/3` branch `← √3·(1+δ)`. **Pre-stated:** at `δ = 1e-6`, **KA5 and KA7 must FAIL**,
while **KA2 and KA3 are expected to be BLIND** because they never touch `r*`.

**Blindness is a reported result, not a hidden one.** The deliverable is a full
**probe × plant sensitivity matrix** with a detection threshold in every cell, and every
`ε* = ∞` cell named explicitly — a probe that cannot come out differently under a given plant is
recorded as such (lesson 90's exact instruction: ask what would have had to change in the code).

**Negative controls that can fail:**

* **NEG-1** `γ ≤ 1` must raise (α ≤ 0 destroys the pressure law and the `S^{−1/α}` multiplier).
* **NEG-2** `ρ ≤ 0` must raise.
* **NEG-3 (wrong-γ discrimination)** — the γ=7/5 window probe KA4, evaluated at γ=5/3 instead,
  **must NOT** reproduce leg 240's γ=7/5 numbers. If it did, the probe would be measuring the
  code and not γ.
* **NEG-4** finite-difference `dp/dρ` vs analytic, max rel error `≤ 1e-6` on `ρ ∈ [1e-2, 10]`.
* **NEG-5** `1/α → ∞` as `γ → 1⁺`: measured `1/α` at `γ = 1+10^-k` must grow by one decade per
  decade of `k` (slope `1.000 ± 1e-6` in log–log).

# PART 2 — WHAT WAS BUILT

`solver/p2_apparatus_term1.py` (NEW file, no dependency beyond `math`, `dataclasses`, `decimal`;
scipy is not installed in this environment and is not used). A frozen dataclass
`GammaLaw(gamma, alpha_scale=1.0, sqrt3_scale=1.0)` carrying **term A4** —

* `alpha = (γ−1)/2 · alpha_scale`, `one_over_alpha`
* `pressure(ρ) = ρ^γ/γ`, `dpressure(ρ) = ρ^(γ−1)`, `sound_speed(ρ) = ρ^((γ−1)/2)`
* `sigma(ρ) = α⁻¹ρ^α`, `dissipative_multiplier(S) = S^(−1/α)`, `vacuum_pole_order() = 1/α`

and, clearly separated below a banner comment, the **consumer layer** used only to validate those
constants: `delta_dis`, `r_min_published`, `r_min_via_alpha`, `r_min_bisected`,
`r_star_branch_low/high`, `r_star`, `R1`, `R2`, `k`, `k_high_precision`, `summary`.

Two realization details are load-bearing and are named here (lesson 91):

1. **`sound_speed` deliberately uses the thermodynamic exponent `(γ−1)/2`, not `self.alpha`.**
   Were it routed through the planted `alpha`, PLANT-α would silently rewrite the pressure law
   itself rather than the derived constant, and the plant would be testing a different question.
2. **`R2`'s leading factor is written `1/(2α)`, not `1/(γ−1)`** (algebraically identical at
   `alpha_scale = 1`), *so that* PLANT-α propagates into `k`. Symmetrically,
   `r_min_published = 2γ/(γ+1)` is **not** routed through α, so it stays fixed under the plant and
   KA3's bisected-vs-published comparison has something to detect.

`experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1.py` is the runner: 11 probes, 5 negative controls, 14 self-tests,
2 plant sweeps with per-probe bisected detection thresholds, a 3-panel figure, and a **derived**
gate (`gate = YES iff half_one and half_two`, computed from the results, never asserted).

**Arithmetic: IEEE double throughout.** `solver/interval.py` exists and is deliberately unused —
this is the float dress rehearsal the dispatch asked for, and the point of a dress rehearsal is to
find out where the float run hurts *before* paying for balls.

## 3.2 KA8 — the one failure, diagnosed rather than described

The residual `129.048` is **not** 129 monotonicity violations. It is
`|k(1) − 1| / 1e-9 = 1.2904784973954975e-07 / 1e-9`. The other two clauses of BCG's `lemma:k`
**pass outright**: **0** monotonicity violations on 200 points, `k` rising monotonically from
`1.0000001290478497` to `3.5632e+05`, and `k(r*−1e-6) = 1.1054e+04` against the `1e3` blow-up
threshold. So `k` has the right shape everywhere; it is wrong by `1.29e-07` at the single point
`r = 1`.

A 60-digit `decimal` recomputation (post-hoc, labelled `NOT_PRE_REGISTERED` in the JSON, and it
**does not enter the gate**; KA8's tolerance was **not** moved) settles which side is at fault:

* `k(1) = 1` **exactly** at 60 digits (deviation `0.0`), and `k(7/6) = 16.347921051661395`
  reproduces legs 265/266 — **the transcription of `R₁`/`R₂` is correct**.
* At `r = 1` the `R₂` radicand's five terms are `−24.5280, −2.4320, +9.4720, −0.5120, +18.0000`;
  largest term `24.5280`, **exact sum 0** (BCG l.603 say the radicand vanishes there), and the
  double-precision sum is `1.2212e-14` — pure rounding residue, consistent with the predicted
  `24.528 · ε_mach ≈ 5.4e-15`.
* `sqrt()` then converts a residue `ε` into an absolute error `√ε`: `√1.22e-14 ≈ 1.1e-07`, which
  is the observed `1.29e-07` to within a factor of 1.2. **Square root costs half the digits.**
* The loss curve confirms it is local: rel error `1.29e-07` at `r=1`, `2.62e-09` at `r=1+1e-12`,
  `2.36e-11` at `1+1e-8`, `5.15e-14` at `r=7/6`.

**Named realization of the failure:** *the naive IEEE-double evaluation of BCG's printed `R₂` in
its published algebraic form, at the cancellation point `r = 1`, where the radicand is exactly
zero.* Not the mathematics, not the paper, not leg 265's constant — the arithmetic realization.

# PART 4 — THE GATE

> **Gate.** Does the implemented term reproduce its pre-stated known-answer/planted control within
> a pre-stated tolerance AND report the failure when the plant is perturbed (a control that can
> come out differently, lesson 90) — magnitudes, not booleans, realization named?

**Both halves were pre-registered as required for YES (§1.4). Answer: NO.**

Taking the gate's **no** branch, in its own words — *"Name the term and the mechanism; 285's spec
for that term gets a rework FLAG, never a silent edit"*:

* **Term:** A4, adiabatic / γ-law pressure — head of leg 285's critical path
  `A4 → A1 → A2 → A9 → A8 → A13 → A14`.
* **Mechanism:** A4's own constants are clean (`α`, `1/α`, `p`, `c`, `σ`, `S^{−1/α}`: KA1, NEG-4,
  NEG-5, and 14/14 self-tests). The failure is in the **consumer layer's realization**: naive
  double-precision evaluation of BCG's printed `R₂` at `r = 1`, where the radicand is exactly zero,
  loses half the mantissa to `sqrt` of a cancelled sum — `1.22e-14` residue in, `1.29e-07` error
  out.
* **Rework FLAG on 285's spec** (recommendation only; 285's files are outside this leg's
  territory — see §5.3): 285's I/O contracts carry no **precision / conditioning column**. A term
  whose consumers evaluate a radical at a point where the radicand vanishes cannot be specified in
  reals alone; the contract needs a stated working precision or a stated re-arrangement.

**This is a useful NO, and it is exactly what a dress rehearsal is for.** It is a *measured*,
*localised*, *independently motivated* argument for term **A15a (ball arithmetic)** — which 285
ranked as the cheapest term overall but off the critical path. This leg's numbers say A15a (or a
targeted 60-digit path) is a **prerequisite of the critical path at `r = 1`**, not an optional
rigour upgrade. That reordering is a finding about 285's dependency graph that no boolean gate
would have produced.

**Clay odds: ~0.05%, unchanged.** No link of the L1→L4 chain moved. Building an apparatus term is
not movement; failing to build one is not movement either.

> stated working precision, a cancellation-free re-arrangement of R₂, or term A15a. This makes A15a
> a prerequisite of the critical path near r = 1, not an off-path convenience.`

# PART 6 — BLOG PROSE

**Registered home: `writeup/4_p2_lottery/BLOG_route_p2t1_v1.md`**

## The cheapest brick, and the first thing that broke

Leg 285 wrote down the shopping list: fifteen terms, seven layers, a critical path seven deep,
everything you would need to check by machine whether the imploding-fluid construction of
Buckmaster, Cao-Labarbe and Gómez really does what its authors say. This leg built exactly one
item on that list — the cheapest one that anything else depends on — in ordinary floating-point
arithmetic, as a dress rehearsal for the expensive rigorous version.

The term is the gas law. For a compressible fluid the pressure is `p = ρ^γ/γ`, and everything in
the construction is phrased through a single derived constant, `α = (γ−1)/2`. For air, γ = 7/5 and
α = 1/5. That number then shows up all over the paper: the sound speed is `ρ^α`, the dissipative
weight is `S^(−1/α)` — a fifth-order pole for air — and the admissible range of the self-similar
exponent is fixed by α too. It is the least glamorous thing in the paper and it is upstream of
almost everything.

Before writing a line of the module we wrote down, and committed, eleven checks with their
tolerances attached, plus two deliberate sabotages. The sabotages matter more than the checks. It
is easy to write a test suite that agrees with your code; the only way to know a test can say *no*
is to break the code on purpose and watch it. So we perturbed α by a factor `(1+ε)` and asked, for
each of the eleven checks, **how small an ε it can still see** — bisecting until the check crosses
its own tolerance.

The answers are the useful part. The best check notices an error of `5.6e-16` — one unit in the
last place of a double. The worst needs the constant to be wrong by nearly **1%** before it
complains, because it is a check on an *integer* (how many "odd branches" fall below a certain
threshold: seven, as it happens), and integers do not budge for small errors. Between those two
extremes lies a factor of ten trillion. If you had only run the integer check you could have shipped
an apparatus with a constant wrong in the third decimal place and never known. And a second
sabotage — moving a `√3` that appears in a different formula — is completely **invisible** to eight
of the eleven checks. Two plants, near-complementary; exactly one check sees both.

Ten of the eleven checks passed, several with room to spare. The eleventh failed, and it is the
interesting one.

There is a quantity `k(r)` in the paper — a ratio of two Taylor indices — which the authors prove
equals exactly 1 at `r = 1` and increases from there. Our code gave `1.000000129`. Off by `1.3e-07`
where we had demanded `1e-09`: a hundred and twenty-nine times worse than required, and not a typo,
because everywhere else the same function reproduced an independently banked value to fourteen
decimal places.

The cause is a small classic. `k` is built from a square root, and at `r = 1` the thing under that
root is exactly zero — the paper says so. In exact arithmetic, fine. In floating point, a sum of
terms of size ~24 that should cancel to zero instead cancels to about `1.2e-14` of rounding dust,
and then the square root of dust is not dust: `√(1.2e-14) ≈ 1.1e-07`. **A square root costs you
half your digits.** We checked by redoing the whole thing at sixty digits, where `k(1)` comes out
as exactly 1 — so the transcription from the paper was right all along, and what failed was the
arithmetic we chose to do it in.

So this leg's verdict is a **no**, on a gate we wrote down before we started, and the no is worth
more than a yes would have been. It says the mathematics is fine, the transcription is fine, and
the *realization* — plain double precision — is not good enough at one specific point. Leg 285's
shopping list ranked "ball arithmetic" as cheap but optional, sitting off to one side of the
critical path. Our measurement moves it: near `r = 1` it is not optional, it is a prerequisite. The
spec gets a flag, not a quiet edit.

None of this moves the Clay problem. The odds stay where they were, around 0.05%. What it does is
tell the next leg which brick to lay, and why — which is what a dress rehearsal is for. Better to
discover that the floor is soft while you are still carrying a cheap brick.

# PART 7 — TECHNICAL PROSE

**Registered home: `writeup/4_p2_lottery/TECHNICAL_route_p2t1_v1.md`**

## 7.1 Scope

Implementation of a single record from leg 285's fifteen-term apparatus specification — **A4, the
adiabatic/γ-law pressure term** — in IEEE double precision, with a pre-registered validation
battery. A4 was selected from 285's own banked fields, not by judgement: it is the head of the
measured critical path `A4 → A1 → A2 → A9 → A8 → A13 → A14`, the only in-degree-0 term on that
path, and the cheapest layer-0 term requiring no pre-existing substrate. (A15a, ball arithmetic, is
cheaper still by 285's ranking but is off the critical path and has substrate `solver/interval.py`
already; it is therefore not the cheapest *load-bearing* term. See `writeup/novelty/leg_302.md` §1.)

The novelty pass (`writeup/novelty/leg_302.md`) confirms A4 is not `ℓ¹`-Fourier/radii-polynomial
machinery in the banned sense — it is a thermodynamic constitutive law — and records that the
scalar constants validated here were previously banked by legs 240, 265/266 and 275 through
different code paths, so they enter as *known answers*, never as re-claimed results.

## 7.2 Implementation

`solver/p2_apparatus_term1.py`. Frozen dataclass `GammaLaw(gamma, alpha_scale, sqrt3_scale)`;
`α = (γ−1)/2 · alpha_scale`. Term proper: `pressure`, `dpressure`, `sound_speed`, `sigma`,
`dissipative_multiplier`, `vacuum_pole_order`, with domain rejection for `γ ≤ 1` and `ρ ≤ 0`.

Consumer layer (validation only; transcribed from the pinned BCG TeX, md5
`45ea63c45a1a199ecfb4dc4a15431600`, sixth bit-identical download):

| function | BCG anchor | form |
|---|---|---|
| `delta_dis(r)` | `eq:delta:dis` l.489–491 | `δ_dis = r − 2 + (r−1)/α` |
| `r_min_published(γ)` | `eq:r:restriction` l.493–495 | `2γ/(γ+1)` |
| `r_star(γ)` | `eq:rstar` l.353–357 | two branches, split at γ = 5/3 |
| `R1(r)`, `R2(r)`, `k(r)` | l.526, l.547, `eq:k_asquotientDZ1` l.600 | `k = (base − R₂)/(base + R₂)`, `base = −4 + (1+γ)(r−1)/(γ−1)` |

Realization choices with consequences (lesson 91):

* `sound_speed` uses the thermodynamic exponent `(γ−1)/2` rather than `self.alpha`, so PLANT-α
  perturbs the *derived* constant and not the constitutive law.
* `R2`'s prefactor is written `1/(2α)` rather than the algebraically identical `1/(γ−1)`, so
  PLANT-α propagates into `k`; `r_min_published` is *not* routed through α, so KA3's
  bisected-vs-published comparison retains discriminating power.
* `k_high_precision(r, digits=60)` reimplements the same expression under `decimal.localcontext`.
  It exists only as a **post-hoc diagnostic**, added after KA8 failed, and is excluded from the
  gate; KA8's pre-stated tolerance was not modified.

No ODE is integrated, no Jacobian assembled, no `P_s` located, no `F_dis` evaluated, no profile
constructed. Terms A1–A3, A5–A15b are not built and no claim is made about them.

## 7.3 Battery and pre-registration

Eleven probes with tolerances committed before implementation (journal §1.2), split between
**class E** (external: BCG's own printed closed forms) and **class I** (internal: constants banked
by legs 240, 265/266, 275 via different code). Five negative controls, of which NEG-3 is the
discriminating one: the γ=7/5 window probe re-evaluated at γ=5/3 must *not* reproduce leg 240's
numbers. Measured discrimination ratio **2.469e+06** (residual `1.6667e+06` vs `0.675`).

Fourteen self-tests, including a corrupted-reference test asserting the battery can return FAIL, a
bit-identical determinism test, plant-inertness at scale 1.0, and a test that the 60-digit path
*disagrees* with double at `r = 1` while agreeing away from it.

Two plants, each swept for a per-probe detection threshold by bisection on `log10` magnitude over
`[1e-16, 1]`:

* **PLANT-α**: `α ← α(1+ε)`. Required to break KA2/KA3/KA4/KA7/KA9 at ε = 1e-2 — all five do.
* **PLANT-FORMULA**: `√3 ← √3(1+δ)` in `r*`'s γ ≥ 5/3 branch. Required to break KA5/KA7 at
  δ = 1e-6 — both do; KA2/KA3 pre-stated BLIND and measured BLIND.

Thresholds are tabulated in §3.5 and in `writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json` under `plants`. Blind
cells are reported, never suppressed: KA5/KA6 are blind to PLANT-α; KA1–KA4 and KA9–KA11 are blind
to PLANT-FORMULA; only KA7 detects both, since it alone couples the α-side restriction `2γ/(γ+1)`
to the `r*`-side formula. Sensitivity spans **5.55e-16 (KA11) to 8.32e-03 (KA10)** — ten and a half
orders of magnitude — and KA10's weakness is structural: it is an integer-valued probe
(`⌊k(7/6)⌋ = 16` ⇒ seven odd indices `3,5,…,15`) and therefore cannot resolve sub-percent errors in
α. It must not be used as a standalone check.

## 7.4 The KA8 failure

`|k(1) − 1| = 1.2904784973954975e-07` against a pre-stated `1e-9`: residual **129.048**. The other
two clauses of BCG's `lemma:k` pass — **0** monotonicity violations on 200 points
(`k` rising `1.0000001 → 3.5632e+05`) and `k(r*−1e-6) = 1.1054e+04` above the `1e3` blow-up
threshold.

Diagnosis. At `r = 1` the `R₂` radicand's terms are
`{−24.52799999999999, −2.432000000000002, +9.472000000000005, −0.5119999999999989, +18.0}`; largest
magnitude `24.528`, exact sum **0** (stated at BCG l.603), double-precision sum `1.2212e-14`. The
predicted rounding residue `24.528 · ε_mach ≈ 5.45e-15` is the right order. Since
`k − 1 ∝ R₂/(base + R₂)` and `R₂ ∝ √(radicand)`, a residue `ε` in the radicand produces an error
`O(√ε)` in `k`: `√1.2212e-14 = 1.105e-07`, versus the observed `1.290e-07` — agreement to a factor
of 1.17.

At 60 digits, `k(1) = 1` exactly and `k(7/6) = 16.347921051661395`, reproducing legs 265/266's
banked `16.3479210516613`. The double-precision error decays away from the cancellation point:
relative `1.29e-07` at `r=1`, `2.62e-09` at `1+1e-12`, `2.58e-10` at `1+1e-10`, `2.36e-11` at
`1+1e-8`, `2.53e-12` at `1+1e-6`, `7.48e-14` at `1+1e-3`, and `5.15e-14` at `r = 7/6`
(`8.42e-13` absolute).

**Conclusion: transcription correct, evaluation ill-conditioned.** The defect is a property of the
chosen arithmetic realization at a measure-zero point, not of BCG's formula or of any previously
banked constant. This is lesson 86 in its cleanest form — a quantity dominated by its own
evaluation error is a statement about the code — with the additional content that here the
evaluation error is *localised* and *quantified*, so the remedy is scoped rather than global.

## 7.5 Gate and consequence

Pre-registered gate, both halves required for YES. **Half one FAIL** (10/11 probes; KA8 at 129.048×
tolerance). **Half two PASS** (all pre-stated must-fail probes fail at their pre-stated plant
magnitudes; thresholds bisected and tabulated; blind cells named). **Gate answer: NO.**

Consequences, per the gate's own no-branch:

1. **Term named:** A4. **Mechanism named:** naive double-precision evaluation of the published `R₂`
   at the cancellation point `r = 1`.
2. **Rework FLAG recommended** on 285's records for A4 and its `R₂`/`k` consumers: the I/O
   contracts carry no precision/conditioning column, and a term whose consumers evaluate a radical
   at a vanishing radicand cannot be specified over ℝ alone. Recommendation only — 285's files are
   outside this leg's territory (journal §5.3); never a silent edit.
3. **Dependency-graph correction:** term A15a (ball arithmetic), ranked cheapest-but-off-path by
   285, is a **prerequisite of the critical path near `r = 1`**. The recommended re-scope of term #2
   (reserve leg 308) is A15a applied to `R₂`/`k`, gated on exactly the probe that failed here —
   `|k(1) − 1| ≤ 1e-9` with a certified enclosure. Reference values at 60 digits, the plants and the
   battery are already banked and reusable.
4. **Clay odds ~0.05%, unchanged.** No L1→L4 link moved.
