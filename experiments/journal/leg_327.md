## The debt

Leg 302's runner (`experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1.py`) emits `fig69` only under a `--figure PATH`
flag and exits nonzero, because its own gate answered **NO**
(`writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json`'s `gate.answer`). That is a correct leg outcome — the gate
is computed, not asserted — but it means a shared/CI rebuild of `writeup/build_figures.py`
(which runs every figure unconditionally and treats nonzero exit as failure) cannot produce
`fig69`. `writeup/build_figures.py` names this exact debt in a comment above its `P2_EVIDENCE`
registry, next to items 9, 10 and 13 of `writeup/INDEX.md` (explicitly out of this leg's scope,
reserved for a later batched follow-up). What is owed: a standalone
`experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1_evidence.py` that redraws `fig69` from the curated JSON alone.

## What the figure must show, and why it's claim-bearing

Leg 302 built term A4 (BCG's adiabatic/γ-law pressure) as a float dress rehearsal of leg 285's
apparatus spec, ran 11 known-answer probes, 5 negative controls, 2 planted-perturbation sweeps,
and 14 self-tests. 10/11 probes passed; **KA8 failed**, and only KA8 failed
(`writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json`'s `probes` block). KA8 tests BCG's own lemma about `k`'s
three properties; the transcription-gate framing (leg 302's own pre-registration) means a KA8
failure is read as *my transcription*, not a discrepancy with BCG or with legs 265/266. The
measured magnitude: `|k(1) - 1| = 1.2904784973954975e-07` against a pre-stated tolerance of
`1e-09` — a residual of **129.048x** the tolerance (`probes.KA8.residual`, computed as
`dev_k_at_1 / tolerance`, matching the JSON's own stated `units` convention for KA8). Leg 302's
own post-hoc diagnostic (not gate-affecting, added after the fact, tolerance unchanged) traced
this to catastrophic cancellation in `R2`'s radicand at `r=1`: in 60-digit arithmetic the
radicand is exactly 0 and `k(1)=1` exactly, reproducing legs 265/266's banked `k(7/6)` value; in
IEEE double the same computation loses ~half its digits to `sqrt()` of rounding noise. The brief
was explicit that this 129x figure must appear on the redrawn figure at its actual measured
magnitude — not smoothed, rounded away, or omitted.

## Gate

> Does the evidence script rebuild fig69 byte-stably from the curated JSON alone (no
> re-running), with KA8's failure visibly represented at its measured magnitude (129x)?

**GATE: YES.** `writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json` carries everything needed:

- **Panel A** (`delta_dis(r)` and the dominance window): the closed form
  `delta_dis(r) = 6r - 7` at gamma=7/5 is itself a curated, verified fact (probe KA2, residual
  6.66e-16 against a pre-stated 1e-13 tolerance) — evaluating it at plotting resolution is not a
  re-measurement. `KA4.detail` supplies the window's `lo`, `hi`, and `delta_dis_at_r_star`
  scalars directly.
- **Panel B** (probe x plant detection-threshold matrix): the `plants` block already carries a
  full probe x plant `detection_threshold`/`blind` matrix, read and rendered as-is; KA8's row is
  flagged separately because both plants report `detection_threshold: 0.0` — KA8 already fails
  unplanted, so plant-sensitivity is not even the right frame for it.
- **Panel C** (double vs 60-digit `k(1+h)`): `post_hoc_conditioning_diagnostic.loss_curve` is a
  ready-made array of `(h, k_double, k_hp, abs_err, rel_err)` rows, plotted directly.

The script (`experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1_evidence.py`) reads only
`writeup/data/p2_route_p2t1_v1.json`, runs 5 self-checks before drawing anything (G1: gate is
NO; G2: KA8 is the only failing probe; G3: the banked 129.048x residual reproduces exactly as
`dev_k_at_1 / 1e-9`; G4: the failure margin exceeds 100x, i.e. is not a rounding-distance miss;
G5: the closed-form line reproduces the JSON's own banked window endpoints), all 5/5 pass, and
writes `writeup/figures/fig69_route_p2t1_v1_sensitivity.png`. Re-ran it twice and confirmed the
output PNG is byte-identical both times (`sha256sum` match) — genuinely rebuild-stable, not just
visually similar.

```
.venv/bin/python experiments/p2_route_p2t1_v1_evidence.py
```

The 129.048x figure is printed on the figure itself (panel C's annotation and the figure's
suptitle both carry the literal number, not a rounded or qualitative substitute), and panel B
marks KA8's row as failing at both plants rather than folding it into the same color scale as
the passing probes.
