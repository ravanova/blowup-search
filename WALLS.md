## 0. The distinction that makes attacking walls safe

**A wall** is a gap in what anyone knows how to do — attacked, in scope, never closed on the grounds
that it is hard. **A ban** is a record of something *this repository measured dead*, with the
measurement named; `plan_of_record.py` holds 19.

**A ban is superseded by a new measurement, never by a decision.** Arithmetic, not caution: legs
51–56, 126, 260, 341 and 349 each cost real time and ended in a number, and re-walking a lane spends
it again for the same number. **The revolutionary act is to attack the wall the ban is evidence
about — in a lane the measurement does not cover.** The pattern to copy is the **DSS
expensive-entrance ban**: not lifted, *scoped* (user ruling 2026-08-11) by observing that its own
text names an **unseeded** trawl and a **seeded** search is a different object. The measurement
stayed true, the lane opened, `PROG-R4` exists.

**NOT permitted under any reading of "be revolutionary":** calling output movement toward Clay when
no link of `L1 → L4` moved; softening the three-tier win condition; calling Tier 2 a proof; retiring
a planted control because it keeps firing.
